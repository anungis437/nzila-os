# Doctrine Governance Hierarchy

> **Status:** Canonical · **Layer:** Meta-doctrine · **Precedence:** Governs all other doctrine

This document establishes the **canonical precedence** of Nzila's institutional doctrine. All other documents in `docs/nzila-ip/` and all product implementations across the Nzila ecosystem inherit from this hierarchy.

---

## 1. Canonical Precedence Order

Doctrine resolves from highest to lowest priority. Lower-order doctrine and product implementations **must never contradict** higher-order doctrine.

| Priority | Doctrine | Role |
|---------:|----------|------|
| 1 | **Continuity Ontology** | Foundational model of institutional behavior |
| 2 | **Governance-Safe Intelligence** | Philosophy of explainable, non-coercive interpretation |
| 3 | **Institutional Risk Philosophy** | Boundaries of acceptable modernization risk |
| 4 | **Role-Centered Continuity Architecture** | Operational center-of-gravity for every stakeholder |
| 5 | **Operational Legitimacy Framework** | Conditions under which institutional adoption is real |
| 6 | **Scenario Intelligence Framework** | Continuity-aware interpretive infrastructure |
| 7 | **Product Implementations** | All applications, services, packages, UI surfaces |

> **Product implementations must never contradict higher-order doctrine.**
> Where a product appears to require a deviation, the deviation is rejected; the product changes, the doctrine does not.

---

## 2. Inheritance Rules

- **Downward inheritance is mandatory.** Every doctrine layer inherits all constraints from every higher-priority layer.
- **Upward influence is forbidden.** A product implementation may not introduce concepts that retroactively redefine doctrine.
- **Lateral conflicts** between equal-priority doctrines are resolved in favor of whichever interpretation better preserves continuity, governance legitimacy, and operational trust.

## 3. Override Restrictions

- No product team may unilaterally override doctrine.
- No client engagement, pilot, or commercial pressure may override doctrine.
- Doctrine override requires:
  1. A formal doctrine review (see [doctrine-drift-governance.md](doctrine-drift-governance.md))
  2. Written justification grounded in the **Non-Negotiable Strategic Constraint** (continuity, legitimacy, trust, human-centeredness)
  3. Updates to all affected lower-priority doctrine and implementations
  4. A historical record retained under institutional memory governance

## 4. Implementation Compliance Expectations

Every product, package, service, and surface across the Nzila ecosystem must:

- Be traceable to one or more doctrines it implements
- Avoid features, copy, or behaviors that violate higher-order doctrine
- Carry doctrinal review as part of its definition-of-done
- Surface doctrine references in internal architectural records

Compliance is **structural, not aesthetic**. A product that uses correct vocabulary while violating continuity doctrine is non-compliant.

## 5. Doctrine Review Authority

Doctrine review authority is held by the **Doctrine Governance function** (institutional, not departmental). It is independent of:

- Engineering velocity
- Sales pipeline pressure
- Quarterly objectives
- Individual executive preference

Review authority operates on the timescale of **institutional integrity**, not release cadence.

## 6. Cross-Product Doctrine Alignment

All Nzila-branded products — Union Eyes, ExecutiveOS, FairCase, Trade, Mobility, ABR, Agrimo, Cora, Veridian, Trustcore, Maestria, NACP, Weekone, Zonga, and any future verticals — share **one** doctrine stack.

A doctrine accepted in one vertical is binding in all verticals unless the [cross-vertical-doctrine-mapping.md](cross-vertical-doctrine-mapping.md) explicitly classifies it as adaptable.

## 7. Conflict Resolution Pattern

When ambiguity arises:

1. Consult the highest-priority doctrine that addresses the question
2. If silent, consult the next-priority doctrine
3. If all doctrines are silent, **default to the institutional continuity-preserving interpretation**
4. Open a doctrine review to close the gap permanently

Silence is never license to optimize for productivity, automation, surveillance, or velocity.

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns This Hierarchy Prevents

- **Doctrine cherry-picking** — selecting only doctrines that support a desired feature
- **Implementation-driven doctrine drift** — letting code shape philosophy
- **Local heroics** — individual teams reinterpreting doctrine for convenience
- **Marketing-doctrine inversion** — letting external messaging redefine internal meaning
- **Technology-stack contamination** — letting stack choices leak into conceptual frameworks
