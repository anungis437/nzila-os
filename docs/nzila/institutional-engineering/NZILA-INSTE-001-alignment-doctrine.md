# NZILA-INSTE-001: Institutional Engineering Alignment Doctrine

## Purpose

This doctrine positions Nzila as a proprietary implementation profile of the emerging discipline of Institutional Engineering. It establishes coexistence boundaries without revising the existing Nzila doctrine estate.

## Hierarchy

1. Institutional Engineering is the independent emerging discipline.
2. IEBOK, pending final naming, is the governed corpus for discipline material.
3. Nzila institutional engineering doctrine is Nzila's proprietary implementation profile.
4. OCI, OCRA, CIVIC, SAGE, CLEAR, and adjacent work remain distinct Nzila proprietary methods or systems.
5. Shared platform primitives enable methods and products.
6. Domain products demonstrate bounded capabilities; they are not reference implementations unless qualified.

## Controlling Rules

### No replacement by implication

Institutional Engineering does not supersede Institutional Intelligence, OCI, OCRA, CIVIC, SAGE, CLEAR, existing product doctrine, or public-sector positioning. Explicit supersession requires a decision record and an affected-artifact register.

### Institutional Intelligence remains first class

Institutional Intelligence may be a discipline-level science when that corpus is accepted. Nzila Institutional Intelligence remains a valid portfolio and platform category where existing doctrine establishes it. These uses must be identified when ambiguity is material.

### Methods remain proprietary

OCI, OCRA, CIVIC, SAGE, and CLEAR remain proprietary Nzila methods or systems. The discipline may define general functions such as observation, assessment, modernization, intelligence, or evidence, but it does not absorb the specific Nzila method identity.

### IEBOK informs; Nzila adopts

A corpus statement cannot become a production behaviour, public claim, contractual commitment, assessment criterion, or product requirement unless Nzila records a separate adoption decision.

### Demonstration status is evidence-based

No product is a reference implementation solely because it resembles the doctrine. Qualification requires mapped principles, architecture domains, implementation evidence, known deviations, operational validation, stewardship, and version alignment.

## Current Scope

This Wave 1 document is an internal control. It changes no runtime behaviour and authorizes no external publication, product repositioning, standard, conformance claim, or implementation work.
