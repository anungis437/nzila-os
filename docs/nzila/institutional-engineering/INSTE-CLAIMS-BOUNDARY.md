# Institutional Engineering Claims Boundary

## Prohibited Until Independently Evidenced

The following claims must not be made in product material, procurement material, contracts, public documentation, or internal decision records that could be relied on externally:

- NzilaOS fully implements IEBOK or Institutional Engineering.
- Any Nzila product is a complete reference implementation.
- Curling Canada is a complete reference implementation.
- Institutional Mechanics equations or quantities are validated.
- Institutional Engineering is internationally recognized.
- IEBOK is an approved standard.
- An institutional digital twin exists where the implementation is only a model, dashboard, or state representation.

## Claim Preconditions

| Claim class | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Corpus statement | Accepted source artifact, lifecycle evidence, and provenance record |
| Nzila adoption | Adoption register entry with responsible authority and scope |
| Implementation | Traceable implementation evidence and known deviations |
| Validation | Defined validation method, results, limitations, and steward sign-off |
| Reference implementation | RI qualification package, mapped evidence, stewardship, and assigned level |
| Standard or conformance | Approved normative artifact and recorded conformance assessment |

`source_pending`, `observed`, `aligned`, and `research_only` are not sufficient bases for public or contractual claims.
