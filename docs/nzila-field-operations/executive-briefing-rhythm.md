# Executive Briefing Rhythm

**Status:** Active
**Effective:** 2026-05-09
**Authority:** [master-field-operations-index.md](./master-field-operations-index.md)

This document governs the continuity-safe executive operational
briefing rhythm.

---

## 1. Cadence

The default executive briefing cadence is **bi-weekly**, with an
ad hoc continuity-safe briefing available on escalation.

The briefing is sparse. It is not a status report. It is a
strategic-cognition aid.

---

## 2. Surface

The executive briefing is rendered in:

- Console → Rollout Readiness (always-on summary)
- Console → Field Operations Briefing (cadence-paced)

Both surfaces are calm, single-screen, and contain no live charts.

---

## 3. Briefing content

A briefing contains:

1. **Rollout interpretation** — one paragraph on rollout posture.
2. **Continuity posture interpretation** — open windows, if any,
   and their meaning.
3. **Stabilization summary** — tiers stabilizing, time remaining.
4. **Onboarding readiness summary** — phases active and pacing.
5. **Deployment legitimacy summary** — attestation chain health.
6. **Pilot posture interpretation** — pilot tier identity and
   sponsor cadence health.

A briefing is bounded to ~400 words of generated text plus the
posture data.

---

## 4. Continuity safety

If a tier is inside its continuity window at briefing time, the
briefing **must** name the window and its meaning. It must **not**
recommend cadence acceleration to "catch up."

---

## 5. Strategic-cognition optimization

The briefing optimizes for:

- enabling executive interpretation
- supporting strategic decisions
- preserving operational calm

It does **not** optimize for:

- operational visibility breadth
- adoption metrics
- velocity reporting

---

## 6. Continuity-safe escalation briefings

Escalation briefings are short, focused, and explicitly named:

- **Continuity escalation** — open window risk.
- **Legitimacy escalation** — attestation chain concern.
- **Pilot escalation** — sponsor or operator concern.

Escalation briefings do not displace cadence briefings; they
augment them.

---

## 7. Posture

The executive briefing rhythm is the rhythm of an institution that
trusts its operators and reviews its posture calmly.
