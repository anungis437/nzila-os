# Operational Rehearsal Governance

**Status:** Active
**Effective:** 2026-05-09
**Authority:** [master-field-operations-index.md](./master-field-operations-index.md)

This document institutionalizes operational rehearsal.

---

## 1. Posture

Rehearsals optimize for **operational calmness and legitimacy**, not
for performance theater. A rehearsal is an institutional act.

---

## 2. Governed rehearsals

| Rehearsal                  | Cadence       | Reviewer                 |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| Demo rehearsal             | Pre-demo      | Demo operator + sponsor  |
| Pilot rehearsal            | Pre-pilot     | Pilot operator + sponsor |
| Onboarding rehearsal       | Per phase     | Onboarding operator      |
| Executive briefing rehearsal | Bi-weekly  | Executive reviewer       |
| Rollout rehearsal          | Per release   | Rollout operator         |
| Rollback rehearsal         | Bi-monthly    | Continuity reviewer      |

---

## 3. Rehearsal scorecard

A rehearsal scorecard is **interpretive**. It records:

- whether the rehearsal completed
- continuity-safe behavior observed
- governance gaps observed
- interpretive notes

It does not record numeric scores or pass/fail flags.

---

## 4. Rehearsal review panel

The Rehearsal Review panel surfaces, per rehearsal type:

- last rehearsal date
- next due rehearsal
- last reviewer
- the interpretive note

The panel never indicates "overdue" with alarm; it indicates
"due" calmly.

---

## 5. Operational readiness rehearsal flows

Readiness rehearsals exercise the governed workflow without
producing real attestations. They are recorded in a separate
`rehearsals-YYYY-MM.jsonl` ledger and never mixed with real
attestations.

---

## 6. Continuity-safe rehearsal summaries

Rehearsal summaries fit on a single calm panel and report only
posture, not metrics.

---

## 7. Rehearsal of refusal

Refusal contracts (continuity refusal, out-of-graph refusal) are
rehearsed to ensure they remain visible. Visible refusal is itself
a rehearsal artifact.

---

## 8. Posture

Rehearsals must never become spectacle. They must remain quiet,
governed, and interpretive.
