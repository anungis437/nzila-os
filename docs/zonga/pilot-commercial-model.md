# Zonga Pilot Commercial Model

> **Status:** Active — Pilot Phase  
> **Effective:** 2025-Q2  
> **Scope:** Founding label and creator partners admitted through the Zonga pilot programme

---

## 1. Pilot Tier Overview

Zonga operates a structured pilot programme before general commercial availability. Pilot partners receive access to the full Zonga platform (streaming, rights management, analytics, payouts) under a discounted commercial model in exchange for:

- Active usage and feedback during the pilot period
- Permission to reference the partnership in Zonga go-to-market materials (with written consent)
- Participation in at least one joint review session per quarter

---

## 2. Revenue Share Structure

| Tier | Pilot Rate | Post-Pilot Rate | Conditions |
|------|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Founding Label | 72% net streaming revenue | 70% net streaming revenue | Signed before GA |
| Independent Creator | 80% net streaming revenue | 78% net streaming revenue | Signed before GA |
| MS Celebrations Partner | 75% net streaming revenue | 72% net streaming revenue | Event-based verticals |

> **Net streaming revenue** = gross platform receipts minus payment processing fees, DSP distribution costs, and applicable taxes.

---

## 3. Platform Fees During Pilot

| Service | Pilot Fee | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Monthly platform access | $0 | Waived during pilot period |
| Payout processing | 0.5% per transaction | Stripe processing pass-through |
| Rights dispute resolution | $0 | Included in pilot |
| Analytics export API | $0 | Included in pilot |

Platform fees will be published in the General Availability pricing page before the end of the pilot period. Pilot partners receive 60-day notice of any fee changes.

---

## 4. Pilot Agreement Terms

### 4.1 Duration
The pilot term is **12 months** from the partner onboarding date, renewable by mutual agreement.

### 4.2 Data Handling
- All uploaded audio, metadata, and financial data is stored in Canadian-region infrastructure (Azure Canada Central)
- Partner data is organisation-scoped with row-level security isolation
- Zonga does not share partner catalogue or financial data with other partners

### 4.3 Rights Ownership
- Partners retain 100% ownership of their sound recordings and compositions
- Zonga acquires a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to distribute the content on the Zonga platform during the term

### 4.4 Termination
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Upon termination:
- Partner catalogue is removed from the platform within 14 days
- Accrued but unpaid royalties are paid out within 30 days
- Uploaded audio files are purged from Zonga storage within 60 days

---

## 5. Payout Schedule

Royalties are calculated monthly (calendar month) and paid within **15 business days** after month end. Payouts are issued via Stripe Connect to the bank account registered in the partner's Zonga account.

Minimum payout threshold: **$25 CAD**. Balances below the threshold roll over to the next period.

---

## 6. Dispute Resolution

Commercial disputes are subject to:
1. Good-faith negotiation (14-day window)
2. Mediation through a mutually agreed Canadian mediator
3. Binding arbitration under the Arbitration Act (Ontario) if mediation fails

---

## 7. Agreement Download

The full pilot agreement document (PDF) is available for download from the Zonga Partner Dashboard under **Settings → Rights & Terms**. Partners must countersign the agreement before the platform activates payout processing.
