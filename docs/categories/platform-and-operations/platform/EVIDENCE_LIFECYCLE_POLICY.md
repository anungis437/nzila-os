# Evidence Lifecycle Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines retention, archival, deletion, and legal-hold behavior for governance and compliance evidence artifacts.

## Scope

Applies to all evidence generated by:

- CI workflows (`.github/workflows/*`)
- governance checks and attestations (`ops/outputs/*`)
- evidence packs (`evidence-output/*`)
- security/compliance outputs (`ops/security/*`)

## Evidence Classes

| Class | Examples | Default Retention | Archive Rule | Deletion Rule |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Operational | CI logs, k6 outputs, ephemeral reports | 30-90 days | Optional for active incidents | Auto-delete at expiry |
| Compliance | SBOMs, governance reports, control-test output | 365 days | Archive to compliance bucket quarterly | Delete after 7 years unless legal hold |
| Forensic | incident timelines, snapshots, captures | 30 days after incident closure (minimum) | Required for SEV-1/SEV-2 | Delete by owner approval once legal/compliance clears |
| Procurement | signed evidence pack exports | 7 years | Required, immutable storage class | Delete only after policy term and legal review |

## Legal Hold

When legal hold is active for an organization, case, or incident:

1. Automatic deletion is suspended for affected artifacts.
2. Artifact metadata must include `legalHold: true`.
3. Hold release must be approved by Legal + Security.

## Chain of Custody Requirements

All compliance, forensic, and procurement artifacts must include:

- creation timestamp (UTC)
- producing workflow or command
- cryptographic digest (SHA-256)
- actor/system identity
- evidence pack linkage when applicable

## Ownership and Review Cadence

| Area | Owner | Review Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Policy updates | Platform + Security | Quarterly |
| Retention config in workflows | Platform | Quarterly |
| Legal hold process | Legal + Security | Semi-annual tabletop |

## Minimum CI Expectations

1. Workflow artifacts must explicitly set `retention-days`.
2. Governance gate must fail if this policy file is missing or malformed.
3. Quarterly strategic report must include evidence lifecycle compliance status.

## Implementation Notes

- Workflow retention values should map to one of the policy classes above.
- Long-lived evidence should be replicated to immutable storage where available.
- Any exception requires an expiry date and named owner.
