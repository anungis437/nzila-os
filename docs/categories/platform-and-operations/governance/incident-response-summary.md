# Incident Response Summary

## Objective

Provide a concise, buyer-readable summary of how incidents are identified, escalated, and resolved.

## Severity Model

- P0: Critical service outage, security compromise, or severe data integrity impact
- P1: Major capability degradation with material customer impact
- P2: Degraded non-critical function with workaround available
- P3: Low-impact defect or quality issue

## Response Workflow

1. Detect: alerts, operator board signals, or support escalation
2. Triage: severity assignment and incident commander assignment
3. Contain: immediate mitigation and customer communication path
4. Resolve: corrective change and verification
5. Review: post-incident analysis and prevention actions

## Accountability and Communication

- Engineering lead accountable for technical remediation
- Operations lead accountable for execution and coordination
- Customer-facing updates follow support model commitments in docs/buyers/sla-support-model.md

## Evidence and Traceability

- Orchestrator and operational event records support timeline reconstruction
- Incident notes and remediation artifacts are retained for governance review
