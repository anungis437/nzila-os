# Procurement FAQ

## What is Nzila OS?

Nzila OS is a shared operating system powering multiple domain products with common identity, governance, and workflow infrastructure.

## Which products are currently prioritized for commercial rollout?

Immediate revenue focus products are Union Eyes and Flow. Strategic growth products are CFO and Partners.

## Is the platform production certified?

No unsupported certification claims are made. Readiness is represented through documented controls, evidence artifacts, and pilot outcomes.

## How is access controlled?

Through @nzila/platform-auth with role and organization scoping, plus optional Entra federation.
