# Buyer / Procurement Public-Evidence Qualification

**Named candidate assessed:** Walmart Canada (supplier requirements published
by Walmart Inc.)

**Status:** public-evidence qualification only. No buyer is selected. No
supplier relationship or buyer relationship is assumed; no supplier portal
or live supplier record was accessed.

## Finding

Walmart's public supplier materials establish a real supplier-goods
relationship and a general expectation that suppliers comply with applicable
law. Canadian organic-claim rules make certification material for products
marketed under the applicable organic regime. The public evidence reviewed
does **not** establish that Walmart requires a supplier to submit an organic
certificate as a distinct supplier-qualification artifact, which evidence
formats it accepts, or whether it accepts a certifier reference without a
certificate upload.

The important distinction is:

- **Organic product-claim compliance:** certification is required for covered
  products represented as organic under applicable Canadian rules.
- **Buyer supplier qualification:** a named buyer may require suppliers to
  comply with law, but the reviewed public material does not establish
  Walmart's organic-specific evidence intake or reuse procedure.

**Readiness:** `PROMISING_REQUIRES_DISCOVERY`, not `EVIDENCE_QUALIFIED`.
Public law plus general supplier compliance is not a publicly evidenced
same-evidence handoff.

| Decision → outcome                          | Walmart controls its commercial supplier/product decisions; regulatory compliance constrains the organic claim.                    | A Walmart-specific outcome for certificate expiry or scope mismatch is unknown.                                           |

**Current apparent mechanism:** general supplier onboarding/requirements and
regulatory product-claim compliance. Organic evidence submission and
verification are not publicly established.

**Reconstruction point:** `RECONSTRUCTION_NOT_PUBLICLY_ESTABLISHED`. No public
evidence shows a supplier re-entering facts, repeatedly uploading a
certificate, or Walmart independently checking a source record.

**Possible reusable reference point:** a certificate or stable issuer reference
for the certified operator/product scope, if one exists and Walmart accepts
it. Neither condition is publicly evidenced.

**Failure state:** a supplier cannot support a covered organic claim or meet
Walmart's applicable supplier requirements. The public evidence does not
specify Walmart's organic-specific cure, suspension, or appeal path.

## Rights and handling boundary

- Walmart's supplier-compliance materials and CFIA's public regulatory
  information do not grant Cora access to certification-body data.
- A supplier's own certificate being available or shareable is separate from
  Cora's rights to receive, normalize, retain, redistribute, or reuse it for
  a second purpose. Those handling terms are not established here.
- Whether Walmart needs a copy or accepts a source reference, whether it
  retains the evidence, and whether commercial use changes rights are
  unknown.
- Unresolved Pro-Cert/Ecocert rights still block direct source access and
  source-supported integration. An approved verification endpoint would
  simplify the technical path but would not itself establish permission.
- An operator-supplied evidence path might proceed without Cora accessing the
  source, but public evidence does not establish that Walmart accepts it or
  that it would constitute reference reuse.

## Exact discovery fact

For a covered organic product supplied to Walmart Canada, does its current
supplier/product qualification process require an operator's organic
certificate or status/scope evidence; what exact evidence and issuer
attribution are accepted; and can the buyer accept an operator-authorized
issuer reference instead of a new upload while retaining its own commercial
decision?

The public record cannot settle this. A future narrow question would belong
with Walmart's supplier quality/food compliance or supplier onboarding policy
owner, whose exact title is not publicly established in the reviewed
material. Recipient discovery is justified only as a future authorized step;
no outreach is drafted or sent. Certifier/source-rights outreach remains
separate and premature until an exact method, purpose, and handling plan are
defined.
