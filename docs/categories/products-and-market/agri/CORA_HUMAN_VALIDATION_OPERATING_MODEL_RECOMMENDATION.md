# Cora Round 1 Operating Model Recommendation

**Version:** `cora-round1-operating-recommendation-1.0`
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** UNAPPROVED RECOMMENDATION - PREPARATION ONLY

This is an operating choice proposal, not new study design or human evidence.
No source, person, sender, channel, site, platform, storage, retention period
or compensation is selected. Resolve the
[decision register](CORA_HUMAN_VALIDATION_AUTHORIZATION_DECISION_PACK.md)
and [consent blockers](CORA_HUMAN_VALIDATION_CONSENT_DECISION_REGISTER.md)
before any external action.

## Lowest-complexity compliant model

- Notes-only, no audio/video recording, participant photography,
  transcription, AI meeting assistant or automated research analytics.
- Local accessible interaction with the existing pinned synthetic reference;
  no provider accounts, production records or software installation on
  participant devices required. Local is proposed, not approved.
- One moderator plus a distinct observer/note-taker; named initial coder,
  independent second reviewer and third independent adjudicator available.
  Roles can overlap only where the accepted independence rules permit.
  The second reviewer for an observation cannot be its moderator.
- Approved encrypted research storage outside git/repository and outside
  this assistant session. Separate contact key from pseudonymous captures.
  No external research tool proposed; existing communication tools still
  require privacy review and copy/backup inventory.
- Minimal contact and broad-role screening, no employer/farm names in
  analysis, no sensitive proof of eligibility, no contact-list harvesting.
- Explicit bounded retention/withdrawal/deletion decisions per store.
  No numeric retention period is approved or supplied as a silent default.
- Internal reviewed aggregate/paraphrase output; no direct quotations or
  public disclosure proposed. No compensation proposed, but neither choice
  becomes effective without Aubert and applicable reviewer decisions.
- Manual scheduling only after separate authorization, through an approved
  channel; no new calendar/survey/recruitment platform.
- Full 61-response protocol for every participant; up to 90 minutes including
  breaks or optional two 45-minute sittings. Preserve counterbalanced orders,
  split after the fourth assigned core scenario, no teaching between sittings.

These choices minimize new processors and recording risk, not all privacy
risk. Notes and role descriptions remain potentially identifying. Local
attendance can increase travel burden and narrow recruitment; do not select
only people willing to travel without recording the resulting bias.
Remote accommodation is a separately reviewed option, not a reason to enable
screenshare or recording silently.

## Notes-only feasibility before collection

Rehearse using synthetic invented answers only, with no recruited participant.
Confirm the team can capture exact first answers, confidence, question/scenario
IDs, baseline locks, neutral prompts and navigation observations across all
61 tasks without teaching or recording. Check digest, timing/censoring,
independent coding workflow and restricted corrections/deletion handling.
Do not treat a rehearsal as human comprehension evidence.

If capture fails, collection remains NO-GO. Adjust approved staffing/mechanics
within accepted design or bring the issue to Aubert; do not solve missing notes
by covert recording, invented quotations or reduced questions.
Independent review uses the locked answers and pinned reference, not a
recording dependency. Missing material is INVALID_RETEST_REQUIRED under the
accepted QA contract.

## Recruitment sequence

| Option                         | Advantage                                                                                                      | Cost / risk                                                                                                | Recommendation                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Recruit all twelve immediately | Shorter potential elapsed time; all role pipelines available.                                                  | Commits scarce relationships before first defects are known; stop notices and extra retained contact data. | Viable only with explicit full-scope authorization; not preferred. |
| `3 -> review -> remaining 9`   | Detect fundamental defects with practical roles; fewer initial contacts and lower wasted institutional effort. | Slower, source/availability bias, and no first-wave round-pass claim.                                      | Preferred, unapproved.                                             |

1. Resolve the operating/privacy decisions, approve outreach for only the
   first-wave scope, then separately authorize scheduling and collection.
2. Approach only approved first-wave slots/sources. No substitute, reminder
   campaign or expansion is automatic.
3. Review after every session. One critical finding requires immediate review
   and blocks advancement. Same critical flag in two independent people,
   defective reference, leading moderation, material loss, misleading scenario
   or privacy/consent failure pauses recruitment and collection.
4. After three full sessions, perform the first-wave review: QA/completeness,
   independent coding of critical/high/disputed observations, burden,
   source bias and privacy handling. Incomplete sessions cannot stand in for
   all three; replacement/retest needs an explicit decision.
5. Hold release on unresolved critical/high issues or disputes; apply accepted
   revision/retest rules if needed. A clean review enables a decision, not
   automatic permission.
6. Only explicit Aubert wave release permits the remaining nine slots:
   P03, P05, P06, P07-P09 and P10-P12. Same reference/cohort unless separately
   authorized revision; never blind-pool pre/post evidence.

A mixed first wave leaves one producer, two advisers, three certifiers/program
administrators and three institutional recipients. Total remains twelve,
three per persona. No early stopping-for-success or lower advancement gate:
all twelve complete independent valid sessions are still required.

No source category establishes representativeness. Preserve source/bias
annotations in restricted study records; three per persona are formative,
not prevalence estimates. If an approved route is unavailable, bring an
alternative category to Aubert rather than silently scraping or replacing it.
