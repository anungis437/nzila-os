## Draft message

**Subject:** Clarification of evidence accepted for SCIC organic coverage

Hello,

I am seeking to understand SCIC's current evidence process for organic crop
insurance coverage. Public SCIC information states that current-year
certification by an approved certification body is relevant to organic
coverage and that proof may be requested. Public material does not specify
which evidence format or verification method SCIC accepts.

We are validating a narrow question: when proof is requested, can SCIC
consider an operator-authorized, certifier-attributed reference to current
certification and relevant scope, or does the insured need to provide a
certificate or other document? We would also like to understand the minimum
fields and currentness requirements, how often proof is requested, how
status changes or disputes are handled, and whether SCIC retains the evidence
or only its determination.

This is not a vendor pitch, partnership request, or proposal to change SCIC's
process. No access to SCIC systems or producer records is requested. No change
to SCIC's independent decision authority is proposed. We are not asking SCIC
to accept any particular evidence method or to evaluate a live case.

If you are not the accountable policy or operational owner for organic
coverage evidence acceptance, would you please direct us to the appropriate
SCIC role or function?

Thank you.
