**Status:** public-evidence research completed; no second consumer selected.

## Result

**Leading candidate:** Finance / insurance — Saskatchewan Crop Insurance
Corporation (SCIC).  
**Runner-up:** Buyer / procurement — Walmart Canada.

SCIC's public organic-coverage rule establishes current-year certification,
proof on request, and a material insured-price consequence. Walmart's public
supplier requirements establish general supplier compliance duties, while
Canadian organic-claim law makes certification material for covered organic
products. Neither public record establishes acceptance of the same
issuer-attributed reference without reconstruction.

The proof remains unresolved. The decisive missing event is recipient
acceptance of an operator-authorized, source-attributed evidence reference
under a current requirement, with the recipient retaining its independent
decision and all rights/handling terms satisfied.

See [SCIC Public-Evidence Qualification](CORA_SCIC_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_QUALIFICATION.md),
[Buyer Public-Evidence Qualification](CORA_BUYER_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_QUALIFICATION.md),
and [Handoff Reconstruction](CORA_SECOND_CONSUMER_HANDOFF_RECONSTRUCTION.md).

## Outreach decision and rights separation

Recipient outreach is **justified in principle for SCIC** because a single
narrow, answerable question about reference-only acceptance could materially
raise or lower the leading candidate. This determination does not authorize
sending outreach and does not override the user's instruction not to send it.
Walmart-specific recipient discovery is a reasonable next question only if
buyer qualification remains in scope; its evidence gap is broader because no
organic-specific Walmart requirement has been published in the reviewed
material.

**Source-rights outreach is premature and separate.** The exact source,
reference, purpose, data fields, retention, and recipient handling design are
not settled. Operator permission to provide an existing evidence object does
not grant Cora rights to retrieve or process certifier data. Unresolved
Pro-Cert/Ecocert rights remain unchanged.

## Selection and safety gate

Before selection, one named recipient must have a current requirement and
decision owner; identify accepted evidence, source/scope, freshness, and
correction rules; accept the same issuer-attributed reference without
reconstruction; retain independent accept/reject/defer authority; establish
recurrence and a bounded operator-burden baseline; and have appropriate
source/provider, operator, and recipient permissions for the purpose and
handling. A public rule alone does not satisfy these gates.
