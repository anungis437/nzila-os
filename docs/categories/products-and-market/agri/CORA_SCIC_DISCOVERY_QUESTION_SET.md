# SCIC Discovery Question Set

**Status:** `PREPARED_NOT_AUTHORIZED_TO_SEND`

**Use:** recipient-side discovery only; no live records, systems access, or
implementation request.

## Questions for the accountable SCIC owner

Please answer at the level of the current policy/process. No producer or
policy-specific information is requested.

1. **Requirement and handoff:** When SCIC requests proof of current-year
   organic certification for organic coverage, what exact evidence object or
   response satisfies that requirement, and what decision does SCIC make from
   it?
2. **Document versus issuer reference:** Must the insured submit a certificate
   or other document, or can SCIC accept an issuer-attributed certificate,
   status, or verification reference and verify it without a newly submitted
   document? If the answer differs by crop, program, or certifier, what
   governs the difference?
3. **Minimum evidence and currentness:** Which of operator identity, certifier
   identity, status, crop/product or land scope, effective date, expiry,
   acreage/parcel, certificate number, or other fields are required? What
   currentness point or freshness period applies, and how is the evidence
   matched to insured land?
4. **Recurrence and changes:** How often or under what triggers is proof
   requested (for example, enrollment, renewal, review, or a status change)?
   What must happen when certification changes, expires, is suspended, or is
   disputed; what coverage or insured-price treatment follows; and what
   correction, reassessment, or appeal route applies to certification
   evidence specifically?
5. **Handling and retention:** Does SCIC retain the evidence object, record
   only the verification/determination, or retain both? What are the applicable
   retention, access, and onward-disclosure rules?
6. **Existing verification support:** Is there an existing SCIC-supported
   process with certification bodies for checking status or scope (manual or
   technical)? If so, what evidence does it return, and who is responsible for
   operating it? This asks whether a supported process exists; it does not
   request access or propose a new integration.
7. **Same-evidence acceptance:** If an operator authorizes it, could SCIC
   consider the same unchanged certifier-attributed evidence reference/version
   that was already used for another legitimate institutional requirement,
   with provenance, scope, and currentness intact, while SCIC independently
   decides whether its own rule is met? What, if anything, would prevent that?
8. **Operator reconstruction today:** In the current process, does the
   operator have to retrieve, re-enter, re-upload, or re-verify information
   already held by the certifier? If so, which step recurs and how often, in
   general terms? No case details or burden estimate is requested unless
   SCIC already publishes one.
9. **Accountable owner:** Are you the policy or operational owner for
   organic-coverage evidence acceptance? If not, which SCIC role or function
   is accountable for the current requirement and workflow?
