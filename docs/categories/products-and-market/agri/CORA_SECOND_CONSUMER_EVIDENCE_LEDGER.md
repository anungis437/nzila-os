# Cora Second-Consumer Evidence Ledger

**Status:** public-evidence review; second consumer unresolved. This ledger
records evidence for candidate decision contexts, not institutional
endorsement, permission, a selected recipient, or authorization to contact an
institution or source.

## Evidence standard and limits

A candidate is useful only if the record supports this actual handoff:

`AUTHORITATIVE_EVIDENCE → RECEIVING REQUIREMENT → INDEPENDENT DECISION`

An organic claim, certification ecosystem, research topic, or sector
benchmark is not by itself evidence that a second institution consumes the
same operator-level certification evidence. `NOT_EVIDENCED` means the reviewed
public material did not establish the fact; it does not prove the practice
does not exist.

Evidence confidence describes support for the stated fact, not confidence
that a future institution will participate. High confidence is reserved for
an explicit primary-source statement; medium denotes a bounded inference from
primary material; low denotes an unverified or absent fact.
