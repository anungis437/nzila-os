## Independent route scorecards

Each route has six independent phase rows, a Phase 4 baseline/result,
a Phase 5 readiness baseline/result and evidence-backed portfolio indicators.
States: PASS, PASS_WITH_CONDITIONS, REDIRECT_POLICY, REDESIGN,
BLOCKED_EVIDENCE, FAIL, NOT_STARTED.
Initial scorecards: all twelve phases NOT_STARTED; A source access blocked;
B selection pending; baselines/results/indicators null. No portfolio result
is currently warranted. Missing evidence is not a negative observation.

PASS_WITH_CONDITIONS requires explicit conditions and their future
authorization effects. Unresolved conditions do not authorize execution.
No route average or pooled phase pass; A cannot complete B's row.
Terminal scorecards require every phase evidenced with a memo and closed
decision record, including redirects/failures. A REDESIGN, NOT_STARTED or
BLOCKED_EVIDENCE row prevents a final portfolio classification.

## Phase 0 - authority, source rights and legitimate baseline

For every evidence/re-entry step, record current actor, requested evidence,
source, subject, recipient, current verification method and reason.
Classify each reason independently as legally mandated, regulatorily mandated,
audit mandated, contractual, institutional risk policy, habitual/manual,
independent professional judgment or currency check; preserve unknowns.
Record actual governing access/authority mechanism, producer effort,
institutional effort, elapsed time and failure/rework points with units,
sample, observation window, source and limitations.

For Organic distinguish authority to issue/publish from Cora's acquisition/use.
The phase record must carry separately:

| Field                      | Required interpretation                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PUBLICATION_AUTHORITY      | Verified certifier/scheme publication class and scope; not licence to reuse. |
| ACCESS_METHOD              | Supported/proposed method and evidence; not an authorization.                |
| AUTOMATED_ACCESS_RIGHTS    | Exact automation permission or unresolved state.                             |
| MANUAL_ACCESS_RIGHTS       | Exact manual viewing/retrieval permission; viewing is not retention.         |
| INTERNAL_PROCESSING_RIGHTS | Processing/normalization operations, purpose and limitations.                |
| RETENTION_RIGHTS           | Exact duration/conditions/deletion or unresolved.                            |
| REUSE_RIGHTS               | Named contexts/recipients and scope; no universal grant.                     |
| REPUBLICATION_RIGHTS       | Redisplay/onward publication rights separately.                              |
| COMMERCIAL_USE_RIGHTS      | Commercial scope independently established or unresolved.                    |
| ATTRIBUTION_REQUIREMENTS   | Required credit/link/marks and supporting terms.                             |
| TIMESTAMP_SEMANTICS        | Observed/effective/modified/refresh meanings; no currentness inference.      |

Every field records state, evidence, scope, conditions, owner and effective/
review dates. Use the existing source-record vocabulary; unknown or
REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION stays unresolved. Assess access, retrieve, process,
retain, normalize, redisplay and commercial use separately.
The registry's eleven independent rights snapshots inherit those findings.
Their test applicability is initially null; Phase 0 cannot PASS while
applicability or required rights remain unresolved/prohibited. Full authority
owners/dates/conditions stay in the referenced source-access decision record.
Class-level publication authority does not establish any individual record.
This mission acquires no source content, even manually.

Compare Cora to the **best legitimate current-state workflow**, not a
deliberately weak emailed-PDF/manual-re-entry baseline. Inventory direct
certification-body directories, existing certificate validation, institutional
portals, supported exports/APIs where evidenced, and direct institutional
contact where required. None of these methods is newly authorized.
Record which is legitimate, available and sufficient for the recipient and
why; preserve mandatory steps that cannot be removed.

`DIRECT_SOURCE_BASELINE_SUFFICIENT` is the explicit falsifier: if authoritative
direct verification is already sufficiently usable and Cora adds no material
reduction in burden, risk, latency or institutional coordination, A cannot be
scored materially successful. Its software opportunity narrows or fails.
Pre-register sufficiency/materiality thresholds before observations; no
invented numerical thresholds in the pending registry.

## Phase 1 - recipient behavioural delta

Test at least one legitimate step for skip, substitute, narrow, accelerate,
less requested evidence/follow-up/re-entry/uncertainty/verification cost or
better decision latency. Bind baseline, comparable task, recipient behaviour,
metrics/units, conditions and observation evidence. Stated enthusiasm alone
does not establish observed behaviour. Zero meaningful delta is a strong
negative result, not hidden by acceptance wording. Recipient decides its
own conclusion; Cora cannot instruct it to waive a mandated verification.

## Phase 2 - access and coercion

Use the actual mechanism (not presumed consent) to test conditional access,
purpose limits, expiry, revocation, future-access withdrawal, deletion rights,
lawful retention, completed analyses, audit evidence, repeated requests/fatigue
and power imbalance where applicable. Explain NOT_APPLICABLE with evidence.
Revocation need not erase lawful completed analyses or audit duties.
Do not promise legally/technically unavailable retroactive deletion.
Unknown/prohibited required rights block the route operation.

## Phase 3 - distributed governance and liability

Tabletop the responsibility chain: originator -> transmitter -> Cora/platform
-> recipient. Responsibility can be distributed, never undefined.
Assign evidenced duty, operational owner, escalation, correction/notification,
retention and liability question for incorrect/stale evidence, compromised
participant, conflicting sources, source correction/withdrawal, participant
exit, Cora outage/compromise, governance dispute, recipient misuse and
out-of-purpose use. Missing liability ownership is BLOCKED_EVIDENCE.
Contrast Nzila-operated infrastructure with federated institutional evidence
authority; corporate ownership does not transfer issuer authority.

## Phase 4 - producer net value

For each route, baseline and result cover time, direct cost, administrative
burden, risk, optionality, ability to change provider, market access, decision
latency, repeated-document burden and privacy/liability exposure. Record units,
who benefits/bears cost, uncertainties and pre-registered trade-off rules.
No cash-only proxy or unapproved aggregate weighting. Institutional efficiency
with worse producer net value cannot be a material route success.
Producer net value UNKNOWN blocks classification; it is not silently zero.

## Phase 5 - readiness and capture burden

Baseline/result separately cover API/system-accessible, structured digital,
unstructured digital, email/adviser-held, paper and mixed producers.
Do not exclude less-ready people to manufacture feasibility. If digitization/
capture is prerequisite, record CAPTURE_WORKSTREAM_REQUIRED with its cost,
time, burden and authority separately from portability. Evidence of capture
cost belongs in Phase 4 trade-offs; readiness is not a new live connector
authorization.

## Preregistration and executable contract

Registry seeds contain twelve pending test records, one per route/phase.
They bind hypothesis, metric dimensions, owner/participant class, evidence
required, comparator-based pass/redirect/kill criteria, decision dates/result,
deviations and approved threshold-change records. Nulls are explicit unknowns,
not approved defaults. Approval/locking is impossible until owner, thresholds,
evidence plan, date and preregistration approval are provided.

Criteria use named numeric metrics with explicit units and comparison
operators. All metric observations/evidence and observation dates are required
for evaluation; no missing value may be treated as zero. Kill has precedence,
then all pass criteria, then all redirect criteria, otherwise REDESIGN.
The numeric evaluator is only a threshold check, not a substitute for
authority/legal judgement, qualitative evidence or the phase decision memo.
Pre-register those constraints too; they can block a metric pass.

Thresholds are locked before evidence. Any change requires a new version,
old/new snapshot, dated approver/rationale and prospective cohort/evidence
scope; do not retroactively rewrite the original result. Deviations remain
visible. Decisions bind the locked test hash, actual evidence, evaluator result
and separate human disposition. Change records do not authorize live work.

The local CLI validates schema, route/phase completeness, inherited gates,
pending B, locks and decision evidence, and reports a fail-closed portfolio.
It does not contact sources, collect data, write a DB or activate capabilities.
See commands in the [tooling README](../../../../tooling/cora-stress-test/README.md).

## Deterministic portfolio landing matrix

Evaluate in this order only after both routes are selected and all six phases
on each route have terminal evidence/memos plus all four indicators:
recipient delta, producer-positive net value, justified problem magnitude,
and direct-source baseline sufficiency. Conditions need closure for final
classification. Until then portfolio disposition is null with
BLOCKED_EVIDENCE, never a success-shaped default or premature NO_GO.

Material success requires all phases PASS or closed PASS_WITH_CONDITIONS,
meaningful recipient delta, positive producer net value, justified magnitude
and baseline not already sufficient. Policy viability allows REDIRECT_POLICY
in place of pass but no FAIL. Policy dependency is separately evidenced.

| Priority | Evidence-backed condition                                                                                                             | Portfolio disposition                                            |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1        | Neither route justifies intervention or yields positive producer net value.                                                           | NO_GO                                                            |
| 2        | Neither route changes recipient behaviour materially, while at least one has justified problem magnitude and positive producer value. | POLICY_STANDARDS_ONLY                                            |
| 3        | At least one otherwise viable route requires standards/contracts/policy change.                                                       | INFRASTRUCTURE_PLUS_POLICY; name viable routes and dependencies. |
| 4        | Both routes materially succeed without policy dependency.                                                                             | APPLICATION_CONFIRMED                                            |
| 5        | Exactly one materially succeeds without policy dependency.                                                                            | NARROWER_PRODUCT; name surviving ROUTE_A or ROUTE_B.             |
| 6        | Complete evidence but neither route qualifies under the above.                                                                        | NO_GO; preserve route-specific reasons.                          |

Priority 3 avoids calling policy-dependent success a ready application.
One successful route never hides the other; failed/nonviable routes stay
named with reasons. POLICY_STANDARDS_ONLY is not proof that a policy solution
will work. NO_GO is an intervention decision, not deletion of source facts.
Even APPLICATION_CONFIRMED does not automatically clear source rights,
production activation or commercial pilot gates; those require separate
human authorization and runtime proof.
