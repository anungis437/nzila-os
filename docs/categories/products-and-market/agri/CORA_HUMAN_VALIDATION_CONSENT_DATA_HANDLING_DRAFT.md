# Cora Human Validation Consent and Data Handling Draft

**Version:** `cora-round1-consent-draft-1.0`
**NOT LEGALLY APPROVED. NOT PARTICIPANT-READY UNTIL PLACEHOLDERS ARE RESOLVED.**

Preparation for the personal, voluntary synthetic comprehension study only.
No legal approval, lawful basis, participant consent or collection authority
is established by this draft. Aubert must approve logistics/contact, and
privacy/legal reviewers must resolve jurisdiction-specific obligations.

## Purpose and minimum information

Study whether people understand an early invented evidence-workflow concept.
Not research into a person's farm, finances, employer, certification,
eligibility or institutional decisions. Findings may inform product wording
and design proposals, subject to authorized research use.

Collect only pseudonymous slot, broad experience category/persona, necessary
access accommodations, scenario/question first explanation, confidence,
manual navigation observations, terminology notes and neutral follow-up.
Names/contact details needed for authorized logistics are kept in a separate
restricted contact-key store, never in captures or this repository.

Do not request production records, customer data, certification files,
financial details, confidential employer material, provider accounts,
credentials, or real evidence. Interrupt/redact volunteered sensitive
information; do not retain it merely because it was offered.

## Recording and screenshare design

**Default proposal: local participant interaction, written notes only; no audio
or video recording.** No screenshare is currently planned. Remote sessions
require explicit choice of approved platform and privacy review first.
If authorized remote viewing is necessary, show only the synthetic window;
avoid desktop, notifications and unrelated documents. Do not copy data to
third-party AI services or enable automated transcripts/meeting assistants.

Recording is optional only if separately justified, approved and consented:

- Distinct affirmative choices for participation, audio, video, screenshare
  recording and quote use; no bundled consent or covert recording.
- Offer notes-only participation if observation remains feasible. Recording
  opt-out does not exclude a participant solely for refusing.
- If a requested format cannot support independent observation without
  unapproved recording, explain alternatives and defer rather than force consent.
- Stop recording immediately on request; retain only according to approved
  handling and separately reviewed withdrawal/deletion process.

## Operational data lifecycle

1. After outreach authorization, screen with category-level experience only.
   Store contact mapping separately using least privilege.
2. Before collection, confirm policy and voluntary consent; log actual
   authorization/policy references in a new 1.0 human capture.
3. During session, preserve locked first answers and restricted observation
   logs. Log corrections with reasons, never overwrite a scored response.
4. Redact accidental personal/confidential material under privacy-owner review.
   Preserve an audit marker of redaction without retaining the sensitive text
   in analytical exports.
5. Review pseudonymous captures; independent reviewers do not need contact key.
   Minimize quotes, especially distinctively identifiable role descriptions.
6. On withdrawal before approved cutoff, stop collection and locate contact
   key, responses, recordings, review copies and exports. Execute approved
   deletion/withdrawal steps; log scope/date and actual limitations.
7. At retention event, remove/restrict contact key and data under the approved
   lifecycle including backups. Do not claim physical deletion without evidence.
8. Publish only separately authorized de-identified summaries/quotes; no real
   participant data committed, pushed or sent to external AI systems.

Privacy/legal review must settle consent wording, employment power imbalance,
accessibility accommodation data, accidental sensitive disclosures, cross-border
services, rights requests, retention and quote re-identification risk.
No study-specific policy implementation is added to Cora runtime.
