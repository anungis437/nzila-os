# Union Eyes CLC Booth Operations Runbook

Purpose: Operationalize booth execution so institutional perception quality remains consistent across live interactions.

Operating principle: At current maturity, physical execution quality and narrative discipline matter more than adding software depth.

## Objectives

- Route visitors to the right narrative quickly.
- Protect pilot scope and institutional confidence.
- Keep demos short, calm, and role-appropriate.
- Convert interest into structured follow-up.

## Booth Roles

1. Greeter
- Qualifies visitor role in first 30-60 seconds.
- Routes to Executive, Governance, Steward, or Procurement flow.

2. Presenter
- Runs one scripted demo path only.
- Stays within frozen script and pilot boundaries.

3. Recorder
- Captures lead context, objections, and requested follow-up.
- Tags conversation type for post-event pipeline routing.

4. Floater
- Handles queue continuity, resets environment, and timekeeping.

## Physical Setup Checklist

Signage:
- Clear role-based entry points
- Pilot boundary messaging visible
- Proof and trust language legible at distance

QR placement:
- One QR for pilot interest intake
- One QR for proof/trust collateral
- Place at eye-level and table-level

Demo hardware:
- Primary and backup device ready
- Browser tabs preloaded to correct start routes
- Network fallback available

Environment readiness:
- Seeded accounts verified
- Demo reset procedure rehearsed
- Recovery script for interruptions prepared

## Conversation Routing Matrix

Executive visitor:
- Start with institutional outcomes and continuity posture.
- Keep depth at decision-confidence level.

Governance visitor:
- Lead with explainability, boundaries, and audit confidence.
- Avoid speculative future modules.

Steward/operations visitor:
- Show daily workflow calmness and case continuity.
- Emphasize practical coordination and handoffs.

Procurement visitor:
- Keep to proof, controls, pilot framing, and adoption plausibility.
- Avoid deep product roadmap discussion at booth.

## Live Demo Standards

- Start on time, end on time.
- Keep one narrator voice at a time.
- No interface wandering outside script.
- If asked out-of-scope question, acknowledge and park to follow-up.

Approved response pattern for out-of-scope asks:
- "For CLC, we are intentionally showing the pilot operating scope. We can log that for post-pilot review and follow-up."

## Lead Capture Protocol

For each conversation capture:
- Organization
- Visitor role
- Priority topic (executive/governance/steward/procurement)
- Interest level (Low/Medium/High)
- Primary concern
- Requested next step
- Owner and due date

End-of-day processing:
- De-duplicate contacts
- Prioritize High-interest governance/procurement leads
- Assign follow-up owners within 24 hours

## Shift Choreography

- Open: 30-min pre-brief and environment checks
- Midday: 10-min recalibration on script fidelity
- Close: 20-min debrief and lead triage

Debrief questions:
1. Which narrative entry worked best today?
2. Where did confusion recur?
3. Which objections need tighter handling language?
4. Did anyone push us out of pilot scope?
5. What single script refinement improves tomorrow most?

## Incident Response

If demo issue occurs:
1. Pause and reset calmly.
2. Shift to proof narrative while resetting.
3. Resume from nearest stable script checkpoint.
4. Record incident and prevent recurrence for next shift.

## Success Indicators

- High script fidelity across presenters
- Low off-script drift
- Strong governance/procurement confidence signals
- Clean lead routing and fast follow-up ownership
