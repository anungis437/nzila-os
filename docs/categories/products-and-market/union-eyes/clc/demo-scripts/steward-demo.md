# Steward Demo Script
## Union Eyes — CLC Convention · May 2026

**Persona:** Sofia Lemaire, Steward Lead, CUPE Local 4279  
**Duration:** 10–12 minutes  
**Audience:** Stewards, chief stewards, shop steward networks, regional reps

---

## Purpose

Show a steward-level stakeholder that Union Eyes:
- **reduces the coordination overhead** of case management across sites
- **makes assignment visibility clear** without requiring constant follow-up
- **preserves steward authority** — the platform supports your judgment, it doesn't replace it
- **respects the complexity** of steward work without adding to it

---

## Before You Begin

**Environment check:**
- [ ] Logged in as Sofia Lemaire (chief_steward → Staff experience)
- [ ] All 8 cases are seeded and visible in Sofia's workbench view
- [ ] Cases distributed across statuses: submitted×2, under_review×3, investigation×1, pending_docs×1, resolved×1
- [ ] GRV-2026-038 (harassment investigation) ready to show as active complex case

**Emotional preparation:** Stewards are busy. They don't have time for software that creates overhead. Every screen you show should *reduce cognitive load*, not add to it. Slow is bad. Confusion is fatal. Clarity is the demo.

Your job is to make them feel like Union Eyes is built for someone who has too much to track, not someone who needs a technology dashboard.

---

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Open with Recognition (1 min — verbal only)

**Do not open the platform yet.**

Say:

> "How many of you have a spreadsheet — or a notebook, or a group chat — that's doing the job that case management should be doing? Something you built because the official tools weren't working for how you actually work?
>
> That's what Union Eyes is trying to replace. Not with something more complicated. With something simpler."

---

### Step 2 — Workbench Landing (2 min)

**Click path:** Dashboard → Workbench (Sofia auto-lands here)

**What to show:**
- Sofia's open cases — the full list, all statuses
- Point to GRV-2026-041 (scheduling), GRV-2026-033 (discipline), GRV-2026-038 (harassment investigation), GRV-2026-031 (pending docs)
- The workbench overview: this is her current caseload at a glance

**What to say:**
> "Sofia lands at her workbench. Not a dashboard full of organizational metrics she doesn't need — her caseload. Active cases, status, priority. The context she needs to start the day."

**Pause, then:**
> "Notice there's no inbox of emails to check. No messages scattered across 3 platforms. The case is the container."

---

### Step 3 — Case Detail (2 min)

**Click path:** Open GRV-2026-038 (harassment investigation)

**What to show:**
- Case description, incident location, desired outcome
- Current status: investigation
- Assignment: Sofia
- Progress indicator (72%)
- Timeline of events or case notes

**What to say:**
> "This is GRV-2026-038 — a workplace harassment investigation at St. Joseph's. Sofia has had this case for 58 days. She can see where it stands, what's been documented, and what's outstanding — without opening a file folder or calling Raymond."

**Trust moment:**
> "The progress indicator is driven by case stage completeness, not by an algorithm evaluating the quality of her stewardship. It's a coordination signal, not a performance score."

---

### Step 4 — Assignment View (1 min)

**Click path:** Navigate back to Workbench → Assignments or Cases by Assignment

**What to show:**
- Which cases are assigned to Sofia, which to Raymond
- Any pending-documentation cases (GRV-2026-031 — disability accommodation)

**What to say:**
> "Sofia can see not just her own caseload, but what Raymond is working on. If he's overloaded, she can reassign. If she's managing a case with multiple staff touchpoints, everyone's looking at the same record."

---

### Step 5 — Case Requiring Action (1 min)

**Click path:** Open GRV-2026-031 (disability accommodation — pending documentation)

**What to show:**
- Status: pending_documentation
- Outstanding documentation requirement
- Member: [assigned member]

**What to say:**
> "This one needs attention — pending documentation. Union Eyes surfaces it clearly. Sofia doesn't have to remember to check. The case status does the reminding."

**Ask:**
> "Right now, how do you track which cases need documentation follow-up? For most organizations, the answer is: imperfectly."

---

### Step 6 — Communications (1 min)

**Click path:** Communications (in workbench nav)

**What to show:**
- Case-associated communications view
- Messages or notes tied to cases rather than scattered across email

**What to say:**
> "Case communications live inside the case. Not in an email thread where the subject line drifts. Not in a group chat that's 300 messages deep. In the case. Where context lives."

---

### Step 7 — Documents (1 min)

**Click path:** Documents

**What to show:**
- Documents associated with case(s)
- Upload or attachment structure

**What to say:**
> "Same for documents. CBA excerpts, incident reports, correspondence — attached to the case, not floating in a shared folder with a confusing naming convention."

---

### Closing (1 min — verbal)

> "What stewards need isn't a smarter database. It's less coordination overhead and more case clarity. That's what Union Eyes is trying to deliver: the same work, but with less of the friction that doesn't help your members.
>
> If your local is dealing with caseload growth, steward capacity strain, or losing context when a steward transitions out — this is the pilot conversation to have."

---

## Questions to Ask Them

- "How many cases are you currently tracking across your local?"
- "What's your current process for handing off a case when a steward transitions out?"
- "How do you know, right now, which member cases are stuck or stalled?"

---

## Areas to Avoid

| Area                          | Why                                                     |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Executive or governance views | Not relevant to steward audience; creates role confusion |
| Intelligence/continuity       | Stewards don't need institutional signals; operational focus |
| Pilot framework marketing     | Show this only if they ask about adoption path          |
| Raw analytics or reporting    | Operational noise for this audience                     |

---

## Recovery Flow If Interrupted

**"Will this replace stewards?"**
> "No — and that's by design. The platform handles coordination overhead. Judgment, advocacy, and relationship work remain with stewards. What we're replacing is the spreadsheet, not the steward."

**"What if I already have a case management system?"**
> "Tell me about it. Most systems weren't designed with union operational workflows in mind. The question is whether your current system reduces coordination overhead or adds to it."

**"How long does it take to set up?"**
> "The pilot is structured for a single local with realistic caseload. Setup is weeks, not months. The demo you just saw was a seeded environment — built for one local in one afternoon."
