# Executive Demo Script
## Union Eyes — CLC Convention · May 2026

**Persona:** Margaret Beaumont, Executive Director, CUPE Local 4279  
**Duration:** 12–15 minutes  
**Audience:** Executive leadership, national officers, strategic leadership at CLC

---

## Purpose

Show an executive-level stakeholder that Union Eyes is:
- a **continuity infrastructure**, not software
- **governance-safe and explainable** by design
- **operationally calm** — no chaos, no dashboards, no hype
- **built for leadership transitions** — the most common executive anxiety

---

- [ ] Logged in as Margaret Beaumont (executive experience)

- [ ] Cases seeded (8 cases visible to staff/steward views)

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Open with Context (2 min — verbal only)

**Do not open the platform yet.**

Say something like:

> "Before I show you anything, I want to name the problem we're solving. Every executive in this room has lived through — or watched — a leadership transition that was harder than it needed to be. Not because of competence. Because institutional memory wasn't there. The person who knew why a grievance was settled the way it was in 2019. The committee process that was informal for a decade. The continuity context that walked out with the last president.
>
> That's what Union Eyes is built for. Not to automate governance. To make institutional continuity visible and governable."

Then open the platform.

---

### Step 2 — Executive Overview (2 min)

**Click path:** Dashboard → Executive Overview (auto-lands here)

**What to show:**
- The landing page itself — let it sit for a moment
- Point to the nav: "Notice there's no AI dashboard. No productivity scores. No worker analytics. This is continuity and governance visibility."
- Highlight the top-level framing language: continuity posture, governance coherence

**What to say:**
> "This is what Margaret sees when she logs in. Her landing isn't a metric dashboard. It's continuity framing — where is the organization's resilience concentrated, and where is it fragile."

**What NOT to click:**
- Do not explore raw intelligence tabs
- Do not show any chart-heavy panels until you've established the narrative

---

### Step 3 — Continuity Insights (2 min)

**Click path:** Continuity Insights

**What to show:**
- Directional continuity language (not hard metrics)
- Continuity posture framing
- Governance coherence summary

**What to say:**
> "These aren't KPIs. They're directional continuity signals. Is the institution more or less resilient than it was last quarter? Where are the transition vulnerabilities? This is the conversation leadership should be having at every executive meeting."

**Pause. Let them read. Ask:**
> "Does this feel familiar? This is what most organizations know intuitively but have no structured place to review."

---

### Step 4 — Leadership Continuity (2 min)

**Click path:** Leadership Continuity

**What to show:**
- Leadership continuity framing
- Transition scenarios or continuity indicators

**What to say:**
> "This is what most union executives worry about most. When someone retires or transitions out, does the organization lose what they carried? Union Eyes makes leadership continuity a reviewable institutional practice, not a prayer."

**Trust moment to emphasize:**
> "Nothing in this screen evaluates people. It evaluates systems. There's no scoring of leaders. This is institutional architecture — built to outlive any one person."

---

### Step 5 — Operational Health (1 min)

**Click path:** Operational Health

**What to show:**
- A brief glance at operational health framing
- Keep this step short — executives don't need to dwell here

**What to say:**
> "Operations are running. We can see where coordination is strong and where fragmentation is active. You don't need a deep dive here — just confirmation that the operational layer is visible."

---

### Step 6 — Governance Visibility (1 min)

**Click path:** Governance Visibility (in nav — links to governance-center)

**What to show:**
- Governance posture framing
- Explainability indicators

**What to say:**
> "Governance oversight is preserved. In fact — it's enhanced. Every recommendation in this system comes with visible rationale. No black box. Nothing that says 'because the algorithm said so.' Explainability is a design requirement."

---

### Step 7 — Outcomes (1 min)

**Click path:** Outcomes

**What to show:**
- Continuity outcomes summary / readiness direction

**What to say:**
> "And here's how you know if it's working. Not vanity metrics — continuity direction. Is the organization more resilient this cycle than the last? That's the question this surface answers."

---

### Step 8 — Pilot Pathway (2 min — verbal + marketing)

**Click path:** Navigate to /en-CA/proof (marketing route, new tab)

**What to show:**
- Proof page: deployment walkthroughs, pilot artifacts, governance evidence
- Keep it brief — 60 seconds max on screen

**What to say:**
> "For anyone who wants to review how this works before committing to a pilot, there's an institutional proof architecture here — walkthroughs, simulation artifacts, governance evidence. You can review the deployment path before signing anything."

Then:
> "The pilot itself is scoped deliberately. We don't turn on everything. We start with what the organization actually needs — and we review together at each stage."

---

### Closing (1 min — verbal only)

> "The question isn't whether your organization needs continuity infrastructure. It does — every organization at this convention does. The question is whether you want to modernize it in a way that's explainable, governance-safe, and under your control.
>
> That's what we're offering. Not a software subscription. Institutional continuity as a governed operating practice."

Hand off a business card or QR code → `/en-CA/pilot-request?context=executive`

## Questions to Ask Them

- "What's your current approach to leadership transition documentation?"
- "How much institutional context lived with the last executive who transitioned out?"
- "If your current executive director left tomorrow, what would the next person not know?"

---

## Areas to Avoid

| Area                       | Why                                              |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| AI features / AI assistant | Not in demo scope; creates anxiety               |
| Workflow builder           | Technical — breaks strategic tone                |
| Deep analytics dashboards  | Too operational for executive audience           |
| Raw FSM / orchestration    | Never visible; do not mention                    |
| Metric-heavy panels        | Executives respond to direction, not data volume |

---

## Recovery Flow If Interrupted

If someone asks a hard question mid-demo:

**"Is this AI?"**
> "It's explainable institutional intelligence. There's no autonomous decision-making. Every output has visible rationale. Governance authority stays with your people."

**"Who sees this data?"**
> "Your organization controls access. Role-based visibility means executives see executive surfaces. Members see member surfaces. Governance officers see audit and explainability surfaces. Nothing bleeds across."

**"What's the cost?"**
> "The pilot conversation is the right place for that — and that's specifically what we're booking at this convention. The goal today is whether this is the right conversation to have."
