# Procurement Demo Script
## Union Eyes — CLC Convention · May 2026

**Persona:** Prospective buyer / institutional evaluator — no login required  
**Duration:** 8–10 minutes  
**Audience:** Procurement officers, finance directors, national operations staff, IT leadership at unions

---

## Purpose

Show a procurement-oriented evaluator that Union Eyes:
- is **an institutional operating infrastructure**, not a software product
- has a **visible, reviewable proof architecture** — not a pitch deck
- has a **structured pilot pathway** with defined scope and governance checkpoints
- **reduces institutional risk** rather than introducing it

---

## Before You Begin

**Environment check:**
- [ ] No login required — this demo uses only marketing-facing routes
- [ ] `/en-CA/proof` renders correctly (deployment walkthroughs, artifacts, governance evidence)
- [ ] `/en-CA/trust` renders correctly (governance framework, explainability language)
- [ ] `/en-CA/for-clc` renders correctly (convention-specific landing)
- [ ] `/en-CA/pilot-request` pilot CTA routes render

**Emotional preparation:** Procurement stakeholders evaluate risk and total cost — not feature sets. They've seen too many technology implementations fail. Your job is to make the evaluation *easier*, not more impressive. Evidence beats enthusiasm.

---

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Open with the Right Framing (1 min — verbal only)

**Do not open the platform yet.**

Say:

> "Technology procurement in union organizations fails for a predictable set of reasons: vendor dependency, poor fit with operational workflows, inadequate governance review, and transition cost when it doesn't work out.
>
> We built Union Eyes with procurement evaluation in mind. There's a public-facing proof architecture you can review before any commercial conversation. Let me show you what that looks like."

---

### Step 2 — Proof Architecture (3 min)

**Click path:** Open `/en-CA/proof` in browser

**What to show:**
- Deployment walkthroughs section
- Pilot simulation artifacts
- Governance evidence package
- Institutional proof artifacts

**What to say:**
> "This isn't a demo environment behind a login wall. These are public-facing proof artifacts — deployment path documentation, pilot simulation runs, governance review records. Accessible to any evaluator before signing anything."

**Point to specific sections:**
> "Deployment walkthrough: this documents the setup path for a single local. Not aspirational — this is what actually happened during validation."

> "Governance evidence package: this contains the governance review artifacts from our own deployment validation cycle. Exportable. Constitutional-committee-ready."

**Ask:**
> "When did you last evaluate a technology vendor that had this level of pre-contract transparency? We're not asking for trust. We're offering evidence."

---

### Step 3 — Trust & Governance Layer (2 min)

**Click path:** Open `/en-CA/trust` in browser (new tab)

**What to show:**
- Governance framework language
- Explainability design documentation
- Data architecture overview
- Privacy and data residency notes

**What to say:**
> "The trust architecture isn't a marketing claim. It's a design requirement. Every recommendation output includes visible rationale. There's no autonomous decision-making. Data residency is configurable and documented. Privacy compliance is addressed as a structural concern."

**Point to CTAs:**
> "Each section links to a pilot conversation pathway. Governance evaluation can happen in parallel with or before any commercial commitment."

---

### Step 4 — Pilot Framework (2 min)

**Click path:** Navigate to `/en-CA/pilot-request` or Pilot CTA on the for-clc or proof page

**What to show:**
- Pilot scope framing
- Phased governance checkpoints
- Scope transparency language

**What to say:**
> "The pilot is structured. It's not a free trial with no boundaries. You define the scope with us. The first phase covers the operational core — member representation, case coordination, continuity documentation. Everything outside that scope is gated, not active.
>
> At each governance checkpoint, you review. You have stop-authority. Nothing advances without sign-off."

**Then:**
> "From a procurement standpoint, that means risk is bounded. The institutional exposure is limited to the pilot scope. You're not committing to a platform. You're committing to a governed evaluation."

---

### Step 5 — Operational Legitimacy (1 min — verbal)

Say:

> "Three things that typically concern procurement for institutional technology:
>
> One: vendor risk. We document our deployment path and governance architecture for exactly this reason.
>
> Two: total cost of change. The pilot scope is defined to minimize disruption. Integration is phased. No single-point-of-failure dependencies.
>
> Three: exit pathway. We'll be transparent about this in any pilot conversation. The governance architecture doesn't lock you in — because it's designed to serve your institution, not ours."

---

### Closing (1 min — verbal)

> "The next step isn't a sales conversation. It's a procurement review conversation. We'll send you the governance evidence package, deployment walkthrough documentation, and pilot scope proposal in advance of any meeting.
>
> If your evaluation process requires a formal RFI or governance committee review, we support that process."

Hand off business card or QR → `/en-CA/pilot-request?context=procurement`

---

## Questions to Ask Them

- "What does your standard technology evaluation process look like for institutional platforms?"
- "What governance approvals are required before a pilot can begin at your organization?"
- "Has your organization evaluated union-specific operational platforms before? What happened?"

---

## Areas to Avoid

| Area                          | Why                                                     |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Any login / internal routes   | This demo is entirely public-facing                     |
| Feature lists or roadmap      | Procurement evaluates risk, not feature count           |
| AI capabilities framing       | Creates governance anxiety — avoid the word             |
| Competitive comparisons       | Never compare to other vendors in a live conversation   |
| Pricing discussions           | Refer to pilot conversation; don't price in the hall    |

---

## Recovery Flow If Interrupted

**"What's the actual cost?"**
> "The pilot conversation is structured specifically to scope cost proportional to the local. We don't apply enterprise pricing to a regional local evaluation. The pilot scope document includes budget framing."

**"How does data sovereignty work?"**
> "Data architecture is documented in the governance evidence package. Canadian data residency is available. We can walk through the technical architecture in a governance review meeting."

**"Can we see this running with real data?"**
> "The proof architecture includes a seeded local environment — same structure as a live deployment. The Proof page you just saw documents that. A formal evaluation demo with a national team can be scheduled."

**"What happens if we want to stop?"**
> "Exit architecture is part of the pilot contract. Your data is exportable at any point. Governance authority over continuation rests with your committee."
