# Member Demo Script
## Union Eyes — CLC Convention · May 2026

**Persona:** Teresa Nakamura, Healthcare Support Worker, CUPE Local 4279  
**Duration:** 5–7 minutes  
**Audience:** Members, grassroots delegates, members of affiliated locals at CLC

---

## Purpose

Show a member-level stakeholder that Union Eyes:
- **respects them** — the experience is calm, not bureaucratic
- **gives them visibility** into their own case without requiring follow-up calls
- **simplifies the process** of getting help from their union
- **doesn't feel like software** — it feels like a service that works

---

## Before You Begin

**Environment check:**
- [ ] Logged in as Teresa Nakamura (member experience)
- [ ] Teresa's cases visible: GRV-2026-041 (scheduling, under review), GRV-2026-044 (pay, submitted), GRV-2026-051 (verbal harassment, under review)
- [ ] Member inbox / My Cases shows her 3 active cases
- [ ] Case GRV-2026-041 shows assigned to Sofia Lemaire (steward)
- [ ] Case GRV-2026-044 shows Raymond Chen (staff coordinator) as handler

**Emotional preparation:** This is the most human demo in the set. Don't rush it. The member experience should feel *dignified*. The platform isn't impressive — it's *there for her*. Quiet competence.

Your job is to make the audience feel what a member feels: *I know what's happening with my case. My union is on it. I don't have to chase anyone.*

---

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Open with Recognition (1 min — verbal only)

**Do not open the platform yet.**

Say:

> "Teresa filed a grievance two months ago. She's not a union officer. She's not a steward. She's a healthcare support worker who worked a statutory holiday without proper notice and wants to know what's happening with her case.
>
> In most locals, she'd be calling the union office. Waiting for a callback. Getting partial updates. Not sure if her paperwork was received.
>
> Let me show you what that experience looks like in Union Eyes."

---

### Step 2 — Member Landing / My Cases (2 min)

**Click path:** Dashboard → My Cases (Teresa auto-lands here)

**What to show:**
- Teresa's 3 active cases
- Case numbers, types, statuses
- GRV-2026-041 highlighted as "Under Review" and assigned to Sofia

**What to say:**
> "This is Teresa's view when she logs in. Not the whole organization's caseload — her cases. Three active cases. She can see what's happening without calling anyone."

**Point to GRV-2026-041:**
> "Her scheduling grievance has been under review for 44 days. It's assigned to Sofia. Progress is at 55%. Teresa knows that. She didn't have to ask."

**Pause:**
> "That's not a small thing. Not knowing what's happening with your case is one of the most common frustrations members have with the grievance process. This removes that."

---

### Step 3 — Case Detail (1 min)

**Click path:** Open GRV-2026-041

**What to show:**
- Case description (in plain language)
- Current status
- Assigned steward: Sofia Lemaire
- Progress indicator
- Next step or outstanding item (if visible)

**What to say:**
> "Teresa can see her case in full. The incident description she filed. What she asked for. Who's handling it. Where it stands."

**What NOT to show:**
- Internal notes or steward communications (if accessible, navigate away — member shouldn't see staff-internal content)
- Any governance or intelligence features

---

### Step 4 — Messages (1 min)

**Click path:** Messages (within case or from member nav)

**What to show:**
- Case-associated communications from her steward or staff coordinator
- Teresa's side of any correspondence

**What to say:**
> "If Sofia needs something from Teresa — a document, a date, a clarification — Teresa receives it here. In plain language. With context. Not a phone call she might miss, not an email that gets buried."

---

### Step 5 — Documents (1 min)

**Click path:** Documents (within case or from member nav)

**What to show:**
- Any documents Teresa has uploaded or received
- Upload capability (if available)

**What to say:**
> "If Teresa needs to submit a supporting document — a pay stub, a work schedule, a message from her supervisor — she can do it here. No printing, no faxing, no scanning and emailing."

**If the screen is sparse (no documents yet):**
> "In a full pilot, Teresa would have documents attached to her case — correspondence, incident records, the collective agreement excerpt that applies to her situation. For this demo, we're seeing the structure."

---

### Closing (30 sec — verbal)

> "Teresa doesn't know what a grievance committee is. She doesn't know what Article 18.2 says. She just knows she was called in on a holiday with no proper notice and she wants her local to do something about it.
>
> Union Eyes gives her visibility without requiring her to understand the process. The process serves her — not the other way around."

---

## Questions to Ask the Audience

- "How does your local currently communicate with members about their case status?"
- "What's the most common question your office gets from members about open grievances?"
- "Has your local had members express frustration about not knowing what's happening?"

---

## Areas to Avoid

| Area                          | Why                                                     |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Executive / governance views  | Member doesn't see these; role confusion if shown       |
| Workbench / assignment tools  | Staff-facing; member experience should stay member-level |
| Intelligence / continuity     | Not relevant to member journey                          |
| Any case that's not Teresa's  | Respect member data isolation — stay in her context     |
| Anything empty/placeholder    | Seed Teresa's cases first — never demo an empty inbox   |

---

## Recovery Flow If Interrupted

**"What if the member doesn't have internet access?"**
> "The platform is mobile-friendly and accessible on any device. For members who prefer phone contact, the local still manages that relationship — the platform reduces the overhead on both sides."

**"What about privacy? Can my employer see this?"**
> "No. The member portal is accessible only to the member and authorized union representatives. Employer access is never part of the architecture."

**"Does this replace the steward relationship?"**
> "No — and that's intentional. Teresa still has a steward. Sofia still knows her case. What changes is that Teresa doesn't have to call to find out where things stand. The relationship is the same. The coordination overhead is reduced."
