## Doctrinal Posture

UE no longer gates features, utilities, AI, dashboards, or productivity tools.
UE gates **operational maturity** across four institutional tiers:

1. **Institutional Continuity Core** — Foundation
2. **Governance & Continuity Operations** — Regional / Advanced Local
3. **Institutional Operating Infrastructure** — Federation / National
4. **Institutional Sovereignty Layer** — Strategic / National Federation / Public Sector

Navigation taxonomy is therefore a **public expression of institutional maturity**, not a feature catalog.
