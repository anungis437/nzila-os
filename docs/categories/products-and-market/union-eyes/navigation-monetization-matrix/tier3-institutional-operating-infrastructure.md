# Tier 3 — Institutional Operating Infrastructure ("Federation / National")

> Full institutional operational infrastructure tier for federations and national bodies.

## Purpose

**Full institutional operational infrastructure.** Tier 3 introduces executive cognition, federation continuity, institutional memory, operational assurance, and rollout governance.

## Expanded Navigation

### EXECUTIVE — "Executive Operations"

Contains:

- institutional continuity posture
- operational stabilization posture
- governance summaries
- modernization posture
- operational risk continuity
- organizational continuity

This becomes **executive operational cognition** — calm, governance-safe, evidence-anchored, never autonomous.

### OPERATIONS — "Institutional Operations"

Contains:

- federation continuity
- cross-entity operations
- operational convergence
- rollout governance
- institutional coordination

### ASSURANCE — "Operational Assurance"

Contains:

- operational proving
- restoration legitimacy
- rollback legitimacy
- continuity assurance
- governance evidence
- auditability

This becomes **procurement infrastructure** — the surface procurement officers will inspect.

### MEMORY — "Institutional Memory"

Contains:

- precedent continuity
- institutional lineage
- governance lineage
- operational lineage
- organizational memory preservation

This becomes the **institutional continuity substrate** at federation scale.

## Tier 3 Inclusions (Cumulative)

Tier 3 adds:

- executive cognition
- federation continuity
- institutional memory systems
- operational assurance
- rollout governance
- operational proving
- cross-entity governance

All Tier 1 and Tier 2 surfaces remain present.

## Tier 3 Exclusions

Tier 3 does **not** include:

- sovereignty overlays
- federation doctrine overlays
- regional governance topology surfaces
- strategic continuity operations
- institutional resilience architecture
- the trust-center procurement surface (reserved for Tier 4)
