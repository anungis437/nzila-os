# Tier 1 — Institutional Continuity Core ("Foundation")

> Operational stabilization tier for small locals, smaller associations, pilot unions, and early adopters.

## Purpose

**Operational stabilization.** Foundation customers receive the institutional continuity baseline — enough to stabilize an institution operationally, never enough to commoditize the product as a free SaaS utility.

## Primary Navigation

### HOME — "Operational Overview"

Landing surface presents:

- continuity summary
- steward activity
- operational cadence
- unresolved continuity items
- onboarding reminders
- governance notices

This replaces the SaaS dashboard pattern — no metric overload, no productivity framing, no chaos.

### WORK — "Active Operational Work"

Contains:

- grievances
- steward work
- representation workflows
- ongoing cases
- operational assignments
- continuity-linked work items

This is **not** task management. It is **institutional operational continuity**.

### PRIORITY — "Operational Stabilization"

Contains:

- escalation items
- continuity risks
- unresolved governance blockers
- aging operational risks
- unresolved member-impact issues

This becomes **operational stabilization infrastructure**.

### OUTCOMES — "Operational Resolution"

Contains:

- completed resolutions
- settlement lineage
- precedent continuity
- operational outcomes
- institutional learning continuity

This is **not** analytics. It is **institutional memory continuity**.

### CONTINUITY — "Operational Continuity"

Contains:

- onboarding continuity
- steward transitions
- unresolved operational lineage
- continuity handoffs
- institutional memory preservation

This is UE's **core differentiator** and is present from the Foundation tier upward.

### GOVERNANCE — "Governance Operations" (Limited)

Foundation version:

- governance notices
- review queues
- compliance reminders
- operational legitimacy checks

Limited intentionally; expanded in Tier 2.

### SETTINGS — "Institution Configuration"

Basic:

- org profile
- role assignment
- basic operational controls
- notification governance

## Tier 1 Inclusions

- operational continuity core
- steward continuity
- institutional memory baseline
- governance baseline
- onboarding continuity baseline

## Tier 1 Exclusions

Foundation does **not** include:

- federation governance
- executive cognition
- operational proving
- advanced cadence governance
- cross-entity continuity

These are reserved for higher maturity tiers and are surfaced as institutional-maturity progression — never as feature paywalls.
