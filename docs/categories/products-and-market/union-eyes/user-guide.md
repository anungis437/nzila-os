# User Guide — UnionEyes

> Complete guide for members, stewards, and officers using UnionEyes.

## 1. Logging In

Navigate to your UnionEyes URL and sign in with your email address.
First-time users may see onboarding that explains the current workflow and
where to find help.

## 2. Where You Land After Login

UnionEyes no longer uses a single pilot home screen for everyone. The app
routes users to the workflow surface that matches their role.

| Role | Default landing area | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | **Inbox** | See updates, requests, and activity tied to your submitted intake or casework |
| **Steward / Chief Steward / Officer** | **Priorities** | See the next work that needs action |
| **Leadership / Federation / CLC roles** | **Intelligence** | Review trends, patterns, and strategic reporting |
| **Pilot officers** | **Pilot Program** | Review pilot health, milestones, and progress when needed |

In addition to the default landing page, the main workflow areas are:

- **Inbox** — Signals, messages, and intake-related activity for members
- **Work** — Steward-led casework and queue management
- **Priorities** — Action-oriented task view for steward and leadership roles
- **Intelligence** — Research, analysis, and reporting surfaces
- **Outcomes** — Results, resolutions, and follow-through

## 3. Submitting an Intake

Members do not directly create official cases. Members submit an **intake**,
which a steward or LRO reviews.

1. Open the intake flow from the member experience
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — Short description of the issue
   - **Category / type** — Select the closest issue type
   - **Priority** — How urgent the issue is
   - **Description** — What happened, in your own words
   - **Incident date** — When it happened
3. Submit the intake
4. UnionEyes records the submission and routes it into steward review

If the issue needs formal representation, a steward or LRO can convert that
intake into an official case.

## 4. Following Progress

Members typically follow activity from **Inbox**, the intake record, and the
related timeline or history view.

You can expect to see:

- Intake confirmation and review activity
- Requests for more information or documents
- Assignment and progress updates when work advances
- Resolution and follow-through in **Outcomes**

## 5. Steward and Officer Workflow

Stewards and officers handle official casework through **Priorities** and
**Work**.

Typical steward flow:

1. Review new intake activity
2. Decide whether to request more information, close the intake, or convert it into official casework
3. Assign or advance the case through the workbench workflow
4. Add notes, upload supporting material, and manage deadlines
5. Record the outcome and any required follow-up

## 6. Notes, Documents, and Timeline Activity

When you open a case or related record, the timeline shows the running history
of work: notes, status changes, assignments, uploads, and system-recorded
events.

Members can add updates when the workflow allows it. Stewards and officers can
add additional operational notes and supporting documents.

## 7. Outcomes

The **Outcomes** surface is where members and representatives review results,
resolutions, and follow-through. Depending on the case and role, this can
include final decisions, settlement status, next steps, or linked member-impact
items.

## 8. Feedback and Help

- **In-app help** — Use the help entry points in navigation
- **Your steward or rep** — Contact your workplace representative directly
- **Admin support** — Reach out to your union administrator for access or configuration issues

## Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Intake** | A member-submitted issue awaiting steward review |
| **Case** | Official casework managed by a steward, LRO, or officer |
| **Inbox** | The member-facing stream of updates, requests, and activity |
| **Work** | The steward-facing casework surface |
| **Priorities** | The rep-facing view of what needs action next |
| **Outcomes** | Resolution and follow-through information |
