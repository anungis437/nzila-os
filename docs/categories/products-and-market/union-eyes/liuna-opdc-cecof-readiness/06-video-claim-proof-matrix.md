# 06 - Video Claim And Proof Matrix

## Recording Position

The recording should be an executive continuity walkthrough, not a generic product demo. The safest story is:

"Union Eyes shows how an institution can preserve matters, documents, deadlines, decisions, rationale, and responsibility through leadership transition, while keeping access controlled and human decision-making intact."

## Claim Matrix

| Proposed Claim / Scene | Surface | Classification | Safe Wording | Prohibited Wording |

## Recording Decision

The recording is `READY_WITH_EXPLICIT_LIMITATIONS` if it is a qualified executive walkthrough focused on leadership-continuity concepts and demonstrable Union Eyes surfaces.

It is `NOT_READY` if it claims LIUNA endorsement, privilege/legal compliance, complete federated controls, complete legal hold, complete access revocation, or autonomous AI decision-making.
