# 28 - OCI Discovery Workshop Pack (90 Minutes, Remote-First)

## Status

`WORKSHOP_PACK_V1_READY_FOR_INTERNAL_REVIEW`
`NOT_CLIENT_VALIDATED`

This pack is a Union Eyes-authored working artifact. It is not endorsed, reviewed, or approved by LIUNA, OPDC, or CECOF. Every question below assumes the client is the source of truth; Union Eyes assumptions must never overwrite participant statements.

## Scope Lock

- Format: remote-first, video-conferencing, one shared collaboration surface for evidence capture.
- Duration: 90 minutes core; modular so a half-day extension can be composed from the same blocks.
- Participants: 6 to 10.
- Language: EN core, FR-CA equivalent materials available on request. Not simultaneously interleaved.
- Date: `DATE_TBD`.
- Purpose: discovery only. No product configuration, tenant provisioning, procurement discussion, or LiUNA-specific gate promotion happens in this session.

## Required Participant Roles

At least one person representing each of the following. Fewer than six invalidates the session; more than ten degrades depth.

| Role Category | Purpose | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsor / decision authority | Confirms scope, signs off on the discovery output. | 1 |
| Operational representative | Runs the actual grievance / case / continuity workflow today. | 2 |
| Likely end user | Would touch the system daily if it existed. | 1 |
| Bilingual / communications representative | Owns EN / FR-CA voice, plain-language register, member-facing tone. | 1 |
| IT / data / privacy representative | Owns identity, records, retention, cross-body data flow, evidence handling. | 1 |

## 90-Minute Facilitator Script

Timings are strict. The facilitator watches the clock; the scribe watches the evidence template.

| Time | Block | Purpose | Facilitator Move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-05 | Opening and consent | Confirm recording, note-taking, retention posture for the workshop artifacts themselves. | Read consent lines verbatim, get explicit go/no-go on record. |
| 05-15 | Vocabulary and register | Capture how participants actually name the work. | Ask each participant to describe one recent real matter in their own words. Scribe tags: `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`. |
| 15-30 | Workflow reality | Map today's real flow, not the intended one. | Draw the current end-to-end path on the shared surface. Scribe tags observed vs. asserted vs. proposed vs. assumption. |
| 30-45 | Authority boundaries | Who can see, act on, escalate, or close what, and under whose authority. | Walk the OPDC / CECOF / affiliated-local / central-overseer / external-counsel matrix using participant terms only. |
| 45-55 | Sensitive-data posture | Records, retention, disclosure, legal hold, evidence chain. | List every place sensitive data exists today; do not propose a Union Eyes answer. |
| 55-65 | Scenario priorities | What single scenario would justify a pilot, and what would kill trust. | Force-rank three scenarios plus one deal-breaker per participant. |
| 65-75 | Integrations and constraints | Systems of record, identity, member data, bilingual, mobile, adoption barriers. | Enumerate every current tool by name; note versions, ownership, contracts if surfaced. |
| 75-85 | Evidence-to-gate mapping | Reconcile what was surfaced today against the readiness ledger. | Read each of the 7 Sensitive Legal Pilot blockers; capture whether the session produced evidence, an assumption, or nothing. |
| 85-90 | Close and next step | Confirm decision register entries, parking lot, follow-up owners. | No commitment beyond a written summary and a follow-up scheduling proposal. |

## Question Bank

Every question is tagged with the evidence class it is designed to produce:

- `OBSERVED_FACT` - only accept if a participant can name a concrete recent instance.
- `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION` - stated as true by a participant, not independently verified.
- `PROPOSED_FUTURE_STATE` - what participants would like to be true.
- `UNRESOLVED_ASSUMPTION` - explicitly flagged as unknown.

Scribe MUST record the tag, not just the answer.

### Vocabulary and register

- What do you call a person who represents a member in a matter today? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`
- Give me one recent example where the label above was used verbatim in writing. `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Which of these terms feel wrong or out of register: grievance, case, matter, file, folder, dispute, complaint, investigation, incident? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`
- In French, what is the equivalent of the term you just used, in the register you would use with a member? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`

### Workflow reality

- Walk me from the moment you learn a matter exists to the moment it is considered closed. `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Where does the information live between steps? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Who has actually been blocked from doing their job by the current process in the last 90 days? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- What would you like the process to look like instead? `PROPOSED_FUTURE_STATE`

### Authority boundaries

- Who authorizes a case being opened? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Who authorizes a case being closed? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Who can read a case without being on it? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- What information should never cross from an affiliated local to a central body, in your own words? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`
- What information must a central body be able to see, and why? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`
- Where is that written down today? `OBSERVED_FACT` or `UNRESOLVED_ASSUMPTION`

### Sensitive-data posture

- Which of these categories exist in a real matter file today: member identity, medical, disciplinary, legal advice, financial, immigration status, family status, disability, other. `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Where is that data stored right now? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Who has ever asked for it to be deleted? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Who owns retention decisions? `OBSERVED_FACT` or `UNRESOLVED_ASSUMPTION`
- Has a legal hold ever been placed on a matter you can describe without breaking privilege? `OBSERVED_FACT`

### Scenario priorities

- If a system did exactly one thing well for you, what would it be? `PROPOSED_FUTURE_STATE`
- What is a scenario that, if the system got it wrong once, would make you stop using it? `PROPOSED_FUTURE_STATE`
- Rank these three scenarios in order of value to you: leadership continuity handover, restricted-matter access boundary, evidence export for a real request. `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`

### Integrations and adoption

- Name every tool used to run a real matter today. `OBSERVED_FACT`
- Which of those tools do you personally sign into daily? `OBSERVED_FACT`
- What is the fastest way you have ever seen a new tool fail to be adopted here? `PARTICIPANT_ASSERTION`
- What would a member on a phone in the field need to be able to do? `PROPOSED_FUTURE_STATE`

### Evidence-to-gate mapping (final block, use with the readiness ledger)

For each of the seven Sensitive Legal Pilot blockers, ask: "Did today produce anything on this?" and record one of:

- `EVIDENCED_BY_TODAY` (with quoted supporting statement or artifact reference)
- `PARTIAL_INDICATOR_ONLY`
- `NO_MOVEMENT`
- `SURFACED_AS_UNIVERSAL_PLATFORM_CONTROL_ONLY` (belongs in platform baseline, not in Legal Pilot claim)

## Facilitator Anti-Patterns

- Do not pitch Union Eyes.
- Do not offer product answers during discovery blocks.
- Do not translate participant terms into Union Eyes vocabulary in the room.
- Do not close the session without a written decision-and-assumption register.
- Do not promise timelines. Do not name a pilot date. Do not accept a pilot ask made in the room without escalating it to the sponsor in writing after the session.

## Bilingual Handling

- Materials are delivered as complete EN and complete FR-CA equivalents. Not line-by-line bilingual.
- If a participant switches to FR-CA, the facilitator matches; the scribe records the original language and the equivalent.
- The provisional vocabulary sheet (`32-opdc-cecof-provisional-vocabulary.md`) is the reference for equivalents used in the room.
