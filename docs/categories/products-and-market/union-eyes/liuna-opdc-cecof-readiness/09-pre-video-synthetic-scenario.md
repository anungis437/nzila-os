## Recording Story

1. A senior institutional user is leaving or changing responsibilities.
2. Active matters, deadlines, documents, and decisions need to remain findable.
3. An authorized successor receives access to selected records.
4. The successor reviews matter status, evidence, deadlines, and history.
5. Central leadership sees governance-level continuity signals.
6. The recording closes with discovery questions for actual LIUNA OPDC/CECOF operating rules.
