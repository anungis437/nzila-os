# 07 - OCI Workshop Opportunity

## Boundary

The OCI-informed workshop is separate from the Union Eyes product audit. This audit does not assess LIUNA's actual internal continuity risks. It identifies why Sean's leadership-change concern is a strong fit for a discovery workshop.

## Working Title

Leadership Continuity and Institutional Memory Workshop

## Potential Objectives

- Map where critical institutional knowledge is held today.
- Identify transition risks when officers, counsel, organizers, business agents, or key staff change roles.
- Distinguish documented obligations from informal memory.
- Identify sensitive or privileged matter categories requiring restricted handover.
- Define central oversight needs without collapsing affiliated-local autonomy.
- Produce a prioritized continuity action map and a bounded Union Eyes implementation scope.

## Proposed Participants

- Sean McFarling or delegated legal sponsor
- OPDC leadership representative
- CECOF leadership/organizing representative
- One or two affiliated-local operational representatives, if appropriate
- Case/matter owner or business agent
- Administrative/document owner
- Privacy/security or IT stakeholder

## Discovery Questions

- Which leadership transitions create the greatest institutional exposure?
- What matter categories are privilege-sensitive or restricted?
- Who needs successor access, who approves it, and when should access end?
- Which obligations/deadlines are most likely to be missed during transition?
- What is the current system of record for matters, documents, decisions, and rationale?
- What reporting can OPDC/CECOF see without raw access to local records?
- Which parts must be bilingual on day one?
- What is acceptable for a first bounded pilot or discovery workspace?

## Possible Outputs

- Continuity dependency map
- Leadership-transition risk register
- Restricted-matter access model
- Handover package definition
- Evidence and deadline continuity checklist
- Union Eyes pilot boundary recommendation

## Non-Claims

- The workshop is not a legal audit.
- It is not a commitment to deploy Union Eyes.
- It does not certify privilege, privacy compliance, or governance maturity.
- It should not use production LIUNA data unless separately authorized.
