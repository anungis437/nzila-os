# 08 - Executive Readiness Report

## 1. Executive Verdict

Union Eyes is `READY_WITH_EXPLICIT_LIMITATIONS` for a LIUNA OPDC/CECOF-tailored executive recording focused on leadership continuity and institutional memory.

Union Eyes is not yet ready to be represented as a fully proven LIUNA federated legal-matter platform. The safe recording must show real product surfaces with careful qualification and must avoid claims about LIUNA endorsement, solicitor-client privilege, complete legal-hold lifecycle, complete access revocation, full bilingual/mobile readiness, or autonomous AI decision-making.

## 2. What Union Eyes Can Truthfully Demonstrate Now

- A live, health-checked Union Eyes runtime.
- Case/grievance lifecycle surfaces.
- Assignment/reassignment as operational continuity primitives.
- Documents and evidence surfaces.
- Staff-level evidence export boundary.
- Audit/event history and hash-chain/immutability contracts.
- Document-level legal-hold/retention mutation guard.
- Continuity runtime components that read scoped institutional signals.
- Human-controlled advisory AI patterns with audit references.

## 3. What Can Be Demonstrated Only With Limitations

- Leadership handover: demonstrable through a synthetic runtime transition fixture and structured reassignment/context review, not as complete institutional succession.
- Restricted matters: document/search/download boundaries, staff-scoped audit-safe evidence export, and advisory copilot governance now have LIUNA-style proof, but privileged AI briefing, notification, and former-user revocation boundaries still need scenario proof.
- Central oversight: governed visibility, regional-summary behavior, and aggregate-only cross-org analytics output are now test-backed, but product dashboard/reporting flows, consent, suppression, and LIUNA taxonomy must still be configured and tested.
- Evidence export: staff-scoped governance snapshot is test-proven, not a full legal chain-of-custody certification.
- Legal hold: document mutation and destructive case-evidence deletion guards are test-proven, not a complete matter-wide hold/disposition program.

## 4. What Must Not Yet Be Claimed

- LIUNA approval, endorsement, sponsorship, pilot, or deployment.
- That Union Eyes guarantees solicitor-client privilege.
- That OPDC/CECOF can safely see all affiliated-local records.
- That access revocation is proven across sessions, direct URLs, notifications, and queued actions.
- That AI makes decisions or gives legal advice.
- That LIUNA-specific bilingual/mobile workflows are complete.
- That the current CUPE vocabulary is LIUNA-ready.

## 5. Material Leadership-Continuity Findings

- The codebase contains meaningful continuity primitives, but successor comprehension is not proven through a complete planned/unplanned transition.
- Case assignment and access delegation are promising building blocks.
- Decision rationale and exception history require stronger proof before the recording can imply full institutional memory transfer.

## 6. Material Federated-Authority And Confidentiality Findings

- Org/RLS controls are present and contract-tested.
- Restricted case/document access primitives exist.
- The audit did not prove OPDC, CECOF, affiliated-local, central-overseer, former-user, and external-counsel boundaries end-to-end.
- `/api/continuity/inheritance` now reuses the governed onboarding route contract, but broader restricted-matter and revocation proof is still required before sensitive legal pilot claims.

## 7. Tailored-Video Readiness Decision

`READY_WITH_EXPLICIT_LIMITATIONS`

Use a synthetic scenario: a senior leader or counsel changes roles and an authorized successor reviews active matters, deadlines, documents, rationale, and assignments. Keep central/local boundaries framed as configurable and discovery-dependent.

## 8. Bounded-Pilot Readiness Decision

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

A non-sensitive discovery workspace could be considered after client discovery. A sensitive legal-matter pilot needs restricted-matter, revocation, legal-hold, and cross-body negative tests first.

## 9. Required Pre-Video Remediations

Strictly speaking, none if the recording is carefully qualified and avoids the unproven claims above.

Recommended before recording:
- Create a synthetic LIUNA-style scenario script and demo-data pack.
- Use `/api/continuity/inheritance` only as a governed onboarding-continuity facade, not as proof of sensitive legal handover.
- Build the recording around case/evidence/deadline/history surfaces already present.

## 10. Required Pre-Pilot Remediations

- Prove former-user deactivation and access revocation.
- Prove matter-wide legal hold/disposition lifecycle beyond document mutation and case-evidence deletion guards.
- Prove full central aggregate reporting workflow beyond the aggregate-only API boundary.
- Validate bilingual mobile flows with LIUNA-relevant terminology.
