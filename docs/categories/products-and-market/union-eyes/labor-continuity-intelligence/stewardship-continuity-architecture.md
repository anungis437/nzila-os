# Stewardship Continuity Architecture

## Objective
Operationalize stewardship continuity support so steward transitions, grievance progress, and committee work remain coherent under real turnover and workload pressure.

## Design Goal
The system must feel stewardship-supportive, never steward-replacing.

## Continuity Domains
- Steward onboarding continuity
- Steward transition continuity
- Grievance continuity
- Committee continuity
- Operational memory continuity
- Governance continuity

## Required Components
### Steward continuity panels
In-workflow panels that show:
- Current case posture
- Open obligations
- Escalation requirements
- Governance constraints

### Continuity handoff summaries
Structured handoff packets containing:
- Active timeline and unresolved items
- Decisions made and rationale
- Required next review checkpoints
- Assigned human owner

### Institutional memory surfaces
Context surfaces with:
- Precedent references
- Prior committee decisions
- Related policy and agreement artifacts
- Continuity risk notes

### Operational continuity views
Role-aware views for stewards, officers, and reviewers:
- What changed
- What remains unresolved
- What requires escalation

### Grievance continuity context panels
Casework continuity panels showing:
- Case lineage
- Steward transition markers
- Prior action trace
- Bounded interpretation notes

## Workflow Integration Targets
- Onboarding wizards
- Grievance workbench and detail consoles
- Governance review views
- Committee operations
- Executive continuity reporting

## Governance Controls
- Every continuity summary includes non-authoritative language.
- Every panel must expose escalation-to-human options.
- Every interpretation surface must identify its bounded scope.
- No individual productivity ranking or score output is allowed.

## Implementation Notes
Current implementation anchors:
- Union Eyes continuity-intelligence and grievance intelligence panels
- Onboarding continuity support blocks for steward and officer onboarding
- Executive continuity posture projection in executive operating surfaces

Planned hardening:
- Shared continuity panel component contracts
- Standardized handoff summary schema
- Automatic escalation visibility checks in CI
