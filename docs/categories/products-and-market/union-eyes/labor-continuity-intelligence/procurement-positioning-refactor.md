# Procurement Positioning Refactor

## Objective
Position Union Eyes as institutional labor continuity intelligence infrastructure.

## Positioning Statement
Union Eyes provides governance-safe, continuity-safe, stewardship-supportive operational intelligence that stabilizes institutional labor operations and preserves continuity across transitions.

## Required Positioning Claims
- Governance-safe by design
- Continuity-safe by default
- Stewardship-supportive in operations
- Operationally assistive in real workflows
- Institutionally stabilizing in transition periods

## Must Avoid
- AI replacement language
- Labor automation language
- Autonomous interpretation language
- Workforce surveillance positioning
- Productivity optimization framing

## Procurement Narrative Anchors
- Institutional memory preservation
- Governance readability and legitimacy
- Transition resilience and continuity risk reduction
- Human-authority-preserving decision support

## Evidence Expectations
Procurement materials should include:
- Governance-safe model controls
- Anti-surveillance guardrails
- Escalation-to-human workflow patterns
- Cross-app consistency proof
