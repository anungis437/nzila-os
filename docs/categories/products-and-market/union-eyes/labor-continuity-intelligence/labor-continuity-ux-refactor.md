# Labor Continuity UX Refactor

## Objective
Refactor UX from consumer AI patterns to institutional continuity operations.

## Remove
- Chatbot primacy
- Conversational dominance
- Ask-anything framing
- AI personality behaviors
- Assistant anthropomorphism

## Add
- Continuity summaries
- Operational context panes
- Institutional lineage views
- Governance review support patterns
- Continuity interpretation blocks
- Bounded guidance copy
- Escalation pathways

## UX Principles
- Operationally embedded: continuity appears where work happens.
- Calm and legitimacy-preserving: no hype or authority theater.
- Human-governed: guidance supports human decisions.
- Traceable: context references are visible.

## Surface-by-Surface Pattern
### Onboarding
Replace assistant framing with continuity support and role transition readiness.

### Grievance
Prioritize lineage and continuity context over conversational prompting.

### Governance
Show review posture and escalation ownership.

### Executive
Show continuity posture and institutional stabilization cues.

## Validation Criteria
- No assistant-first wording in institutional continuity surfaces.
- Every critical continuity panel includes escalation path language.
- No surveillance, scoring, or coercive analytics cues.
