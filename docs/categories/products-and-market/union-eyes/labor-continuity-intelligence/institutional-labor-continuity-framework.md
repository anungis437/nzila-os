# Institutional Labor Continuity Framework

## Purpose
Union Eyes labor continuity intelligence exists to preserve institutional continuity, reduce stewardship fragmentation, and support governance-readable operations.

This framework defines the canonical philosophy for institutional labor continuity intelligence across Union Eyes, Console, and executive operational surfaces.

## Canonical Position
The subsystem is institutional infrastructure, not a chatbot product.

It provides:
- Stewardship continuity support
- Governance-safe intelligence
- Operational continuity interpretation
- Institutional memory preservation
- Labor operational cognition support
- Onboarding continuity support
- Committee continuity support
- Grievance continuity support
- Executive labor continuity support

It does not provide labor authority.

## Core Definitions
### Stewardship continuity
The institution can transfer active representation work between stewards and committees without losing context, lineage, or decision rationale.

### Governance-safe intelligence
All intelligence outputs are bounded, attributed, reviewable, and explicitly non-authoritative.

### Operational continuity interpretation
Interpretive support that explains context, precedent relationships, and operational options while preserving human governance authority.

### Institutional memory preservation
Operational facts, transitions, and prior decisions remain legible across role change, committee turnover, and executive transition.

### Labor operational cognition
A governed synthesis layer that surfaces what matters now for continuity, risk containment, and stewardship cadence.

### Onboarding continuity
New operators inherit context quickly through continuity summaries, lineage views, and role-specific orientation artifacts.

### Committee continuity
Committee work remains coherent through handoff summaries, prior motion context, and governance review traces.

### Grievance continuity
Casework continuity across transitions through chronology, precedent references, and steward handoff records.

### Executive labor continuity
Executive briefings prioritize continuity posture, institutional stability risk, and governance-safe interpretation.

## Human Authority Guardrail
The subsystem never replaces human governance.

Required behavior:
- Assist continuity and stewardship
- Preserve institutional memory
- Support onboarding and governance readability
- Support institutional cadence

Forbidden behavior:
- Make labor decisions
- Replace interpretation authority
- Autonomously govern
- Produce authoritative legal rulings

## Explicit Rejections
This framework rejects:
- Autonomous labor governance
- Surveillance labor analytics
- AI replacement framing
- Automated labor authority

## Operating Principles
- Calm: output is concise, low-drama, and governance-readable.
- Bounded: recommendations are framed as support, not direction.
- Traceable: interpretation includes source and rationale context.
- Escalatable: human escalation paths are always visible.
- Institutional: language centers continuity, stewardship, and legitimacy.

## Cross-App Applicability
This framework governs:
- Union Eyes casework, grievance, onboarding, and governance surfaces
- Console executive continuity review and cadence surfaces
- Executive operational briefing projections and governance summaries

## Governance Evidence
Compliance evidence should be captured by:
- Terminology validation in source and docs
- UX pattern validation (non-chatbot-primary)
- Escalation pathway presence checks
- Anti-surveillance term checks
