# Executive Labor Continuity Briefings

## Objective
Support executive labor governance continuity with governance-safe interpretation and stabilization posture visibility.

## Required Briefing Content
- Operational continuity posture
- Stewardship continuity posture
- Governance continuity posture
- Committee continuity status
- Organizational continuity risk
- Institutional stabilization posture

## Briefing Composition Model
### Section 1: Posture
Current continuity status and trajectory.

### Section 2: Signals requiring human review
Governance-sensitive signals requiring executive or committee attention.

### Section 3: Continuity risk concentration
Where continuity loss is concentrated and why it matters.

### Section 4: Escalation routing
Named escalation pathways and owners.

## Interpretation Constraints
Executives must receive continuity-safe labor interpretation.

Not allowed:
- AI-generated labor authority
- Autonomous governance determinations
- Unattributed claims with no review path

## Output Tone Contract
- Institutional
- Calm
- Bounded
- Reviewable
- Legitimacy-preserving

## Cross-App Use
Executive briefing posture should be coherent in:
- Union Eyes executive operating intelligence surface
- Console executive weekly and governance review surfaces
- Governance review and continuity cadence systems
