# Cross-App Continuity Intelligence Consistency

## Objective
Ensure labor continuity intelligence behaves coherently across Union Eyes, Console, and executive governance surfaces.

## Consistency Axes
- Operational language consistency
- Continuity interpretation consistency
- Governance posture consistency
- Stewardship posture consistency
- Escalation posture consistency

## Required Validation Set
### Language consistency
Terms should center:
- institutional continuity intelligence
- stewardship continuity
- governance-safe interpretation

Terms to avoid:
- chatbot-first prompts
- assistant anthropomorphism
- authoritative automation cues

### Interpretation consistency
All apps should frame interpretation as:
- bounded
- attributed
- reviewable
- non-authoritative

### Governance posture consistency
All apps should reinforce:
- human governance authority
- institutional cadence
- legitimacy-preserving review

### Stewardship posture consistency
All apps should support:
- handoff continuity
- onboarding continuity
- operational memory continuity

### Escalation posture consistency
All critical continuity surfaces must include explicit escalation pathways.

## Cross-App Implementation Targets
- Union Eyes: grievance, onboarding, governance, continuity intelligence, executive briefing surfaces
- Console: weekly review and executive governance summary surfaces
- Executive operational surfaces: posture, review signals, and continuity risk routing

## Audit Checklist
- Check copy and metadata for prohibited AI framing
- Check continuity-safe language presence in live surfaces
- Check escalation language presence
- Check anti-surveillance commitments
