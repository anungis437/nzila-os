# Grievance Continuity Intelligence

## Objective
Support grievance operational continuity by preserving case context, precedent lineage, and stewardship transitions without automating grievance authority.

## Capability Scope
The subsystem supports:
- Contextual continuity
- Precedent continuity
- Steward continuity
- Transition continuity
- Operational history continuity
- Governance-safe interpretation

It does not issue grievance rulings.

## Required Implementations
### Grievance continuity summaries
Each case view should include:
- Current procedural posture
- Open obligations
- Known deadlines
- Escalation requirements

### Operational lineage views
Lineage should reveal:
- Prior related cases
- Similar facts and outcomes
- Relevant policy and agreement references
- Changes in case interpretation context over time

### Steward handoff surfaces
Handoff views should include:
- Prior steward notes
- Pending owner actions
- Risk-sensitive gaps
- Next recommended review checkpoint

### Institutional memory restoration panels
Restoration panels should recover:
- Historical context not captured in current task notes
- Prior committee/governance touchpoints
- Operational reasoning traces

## Governance-Safe Interpretation Rules
- Interpretation remains advisory.
- Legal or labor authority remains human-owned.
- Escalation to steward lead or committee remains explicit.

## Anti-Authority Guardrails
The grievance continuity subsystem must not:
- Auto-determine outcome
- Auto-close grievance authority questions
- Present interpretation as final or binding

## UX Contract
The grievance surface should prioritize:
- Continuity context first
- Lineage and evidence second
- Suggested bounded next steps third
- Escalation options always visible
