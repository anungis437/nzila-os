# Full Procurement & Sales Motion Refactor

> Rebuild procurement and sales motion around institutional operational maturity.

## 1. Motion Statement

Sales must increasingly feel **governance-native** — not software-selling.

## 2. Refactor Targets

- sales narratives
- pilot structures
- procurement walkthroughs
- governance walkthroughs
- onboarding walkthroughs
- operational proving narratives
- executive briefing narratives

## 3. Procurement-Safe Motion

- Anti-surveillance posture stated up-front.
- Bounded interpretation posture stated up-front.
- Continuity-safe modernization commitment stated up-front.
- Forbidden framings (AI replacement, autonomous decision-making, copilot, chatbot, AI-first) absent.
- Reviewer-of-record discoverable in every interpretive demo.

## 4. Federation-Safe Motion

- Multi-org governance preserved without commoditizing local autonomy.
- Federation-tier procurement templates available.
- Cross-org continuity primitives demonstrated.

## 5. National-Union Motion

- Sovereign-grade trust posture.
- Sovereign-grade anti-surveillance disclosures.
- Multi-jurisdiction governance accommodation.

## 6. Public-Sector-Safe Motion

- Procurement-grade trust posture.
- Bounded interpretation posture.
- Continuity-safe modernization commitment.
- Audit-ready documentation per tier.

## 7. Governance-Body-Safe Motion

- Bounded interpretation only — never autonomous adjudication.
- Reviewer-of-record always named.
- Escalation paths always discoverable.

## 8. Pilot Structure Discipline

- Pilots are continuity-safe by default; rollback paths preserved.
- Pilot cadence is sustainable; never engagement-optimized.
- Pilot success criteria measure operational stabilization, not feature usage.

## 9. Sales Narrative Discipline

Sales narratives must:

- center continuity, governance, and resilience
- avoid productivity-optimization framing
- avoid automation-maximalist framing
- preserve operator authority context throughout
