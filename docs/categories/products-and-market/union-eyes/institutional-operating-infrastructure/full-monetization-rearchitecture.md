# Full Monetization Rearchitecture

> UE monetizes institutional resilience — not AI utility.

## 1. Monetization Statement

Union Eyes' commercial architecture monetizes **institutional operational maturity**: continuity, governance, executive cognition, cadence, stewardship, memory, federation, resilience.

It does **not** monetize:

- AI feature counts
- assistant interactions
- automation hours
- productivity scores
- user-seat optimization

## 2. Tier Architecture

### 2.1 Institutional Continuity Core

- Audience: smaller institutional locals; entry-level continuity needs
- Includes: continuity substrate, bounded-interpretation surfaces, basic governance, basic cadence
- Posture: governance-safe entry tier; not "freemium AI"

### 2.2 Governance & Continuity Operations

- Audience: established locals and mid-size institutional bodies
- Includes: full governance infrastructure, full cadence, stewardship continuity, institutional memory, executive bounded interpretation
- Posture: operational-stabilization tier

### 2.3 Institutional Operating Infrastructure

- Audience: federations, large institutional bodies, modernization programs
- Includes: federation governance, operational proving, full procurement-grade trust posture, full executive cognition
- Posture: institutional-infrastructure tier

### 2.4 Institutional Sovereignty Layer

- Audience: national unions, sovereign-grade institutional bodies, public-sector federations
- Includes: multi-org federation, sovereign procurement posture, sovereign anti-surveillance disclosures, governance-body-safe operations
- Posture: sovereignty-grade tier

## 3. Monetization Targets

Each tier monetizes maturity along these axes:

- governance maturity
- operational continuity
- executive cognition
- operational proving
- institutional memory
- stewardship continuity
- cadence infrastructure
- federation governance
- institutional resilience

## 4. Pricing Architecture (Doctrinal)

- Pricing is **institutional-tier-priced**, not per-seat-optimized.
- Base institutional fee per tier; bounded scaling by institutional size, not by AI usage.
- Federation tier supports multi-org accounting without per-seat penalty.
- Sovereignty tier supports negotiated procurement-grade contracts.

## 6. Procurement Packaging

- Procurement-safe contract templates per tier
- Anti-surveillance disclosure included by default
- Continuity-safe modernization commitment included by default
- Bounded interpretation posture included by default

## 7. Federation & National-Union Packaging

- Federation tier supports cross-org governance without compromising local autonomy
- National-union packaging supports sovereign-grade procurement, multi-jurisdiction governance, and anti-surveillance sovereignty disclosures

## 8. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- per-AI-call pricing
- per-message pricing
- engagement-rate pricing
- "AI minutes" pricing
- automation-hour pricing
