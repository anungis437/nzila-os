# Full Module Architecture Review

> Doctrinal review of every UE module through institutional-infrastructure lens.

## 1. Review Lens

For every module, classify as:

- **Core Infrastructure** — foundational operating substrate
- **Governance Infrastructure** — bounded interpretation, escalation, reviewer-of-record
- **Operational Infrastructure** — cadence, review, continuity surfaces
- **Cognition Infrastructure** — bounded interpretation surfaces
- **Onboarding Infrastructure** — operator / steward continuity
- **Cadence Infrastructure** — review rhythm
- **Deprecated Workflow Utility** — flagged for retirement
- **Merge Candidate** — fold into larger infrastructure layer
- **Hidden Infrastructure Layer** — necessary substrate not surfaced as a "feature"
- **Monetizable Infrastructure Layer** — packaged into a tier

## 3. Hidden Infrastructure Discoveries

These layers exist as substrate but should remain **non-surfaced as features**:

- continuity audit trail
- bounded-interpretation envelope
- escalation registry
- reviewer-of-record resolution
- cadence sustainability throttling
- restoration-summary substrate

## 4. Monetizable Infrastructure Discoveries

These layers anchor commercial tiers (see [Full Monetization Rearchitecture](full-monetization-rearchitecture.md)):

- Continuity Infrastructure
- Governance Infrastructure
- Cadence Infrastructure
- Stewardship Continuity
- Institutional Memory
- Federation Governance

## 5. Deprecation Candidates

- standalone "AI assistant" surfaces (collapsed into bounded-interpretation surfaces)
- engagement-optimized cadence surfaces (collapsed into sustainable cadence)
- standalone "automation" framings (folded into operational continuity)
