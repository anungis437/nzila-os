# Full Navigation & IA Rearchitecture

> Rebuild UE information architecture around institutional operational cognition.

## 1. IA Statement

Navigation must increasingly feel **institutionally inevitable** — flows, not features.

## 2. IA Convergence Targets

Converge — into coherent institutional operational flows:

- Work
- Priority
- Outcomes
- Intelligence (rebadged as Operational Cognition)
- Governance
- Continuity
- Onboarding
- Cadence
- Stewardship
- Executive
- Operational Reviews

## 3. Canonical Top-Level Groupings

- **Continuity** — the substrate; always present; never gated below entry tier.
- **Governance** — bounded interpretation, escalation, reviewer-of-record; tiered.
- **Operational Cognition** — bounded interpretation surfaces; tiered.
- **Cadence** — sustainable review rhythm; tiered.
- **Stewardship** — steward continuity; tiered.
- **Executive** — bounded executive interpretation; tiered.
- **Onboarding** — operator / steward / executive paced flows; always present.
- **Operational Reviews** — review cadence + escalation; tiered.

## 4. Canonical IA Discipline

- Governance grouping over feature grouping.
- Continuity is always at the top of the navigation tree.
- Cognition surfaces always sit under their governance context.
- Escalation paths discoverable from every interpretive surface.
- No engagement-optimization patterns in navigation.
- No surveillance-adjacent grouping (no "monitoring", no "tracking").

## 5. Implementation Discipline

Sidebar, route group, and navigation-component refactors are doctrinally specified here and rolled into dedicated follow-on PRs to preserve CI stability.

## 6. Refactor Targets

- sidebars
- routing groups
- operational grouping
- executive grouping
- governance grouping
- continuity grouping
- onboarding grouping
