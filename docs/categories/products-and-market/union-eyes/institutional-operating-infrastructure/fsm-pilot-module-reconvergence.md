# FSM & Pilot Module Reconvergence

> Re-evaluate legacy pilot and FSM modules through doctrine convergence.

## 1. Reconvergence Statement

This phase reduces **feature fragmentation** by folding legacy pilot, experimental, and AI-centric surfaces into infrastructure layers — or deprecating them where they no longer fit the institutional-infrastructure category.

## 2. Reconvergence Decision Matrix

| Legacy Surface | Decision | Rationale |
|----------------|----------|-----------|
| FSM (field service management) | Merge → Operational Infrastructure | Folds into operational-continuity primitives; surfaces continuity-safe field cadence rather than dispatch optimization. |
| Pilot — workflow automation | Convert → Operational Continuity Primitive | Reframed as bounded continuity automation under explicit human authority. |
| Pilot — AI assistant | Hidden Substrate | Folded into bounded-interpretation surfaces; never re-surfaced as standalone "assistant". |
| Pilot — recommendation engine | Hidden Substrate | Folded into bounded-interpretation surfaces. |
| Pilot — autonomous routing | Deprecate | Incompatible with continuity-safe + bounded-interpretation posture. |
| Experimental ops layers | Merge → Cadence Infrastructure | Cadence sustainability replaces engagement experiments. |
| AI-centric legacy surfaces | Hidden Substrate | Substrate retained; surfaces removed or rebadged. |
| Operational automation systems | Convert → Continuity Primitive | Bounded automation under reviewer-of-record. |

## 3. Discipline

- No legacy surface is removed without doctrinal specification.
- Deprecations are staged across follow-on PRs to preserve CI and customer continuity.
- Hidden-substrate decisions never re-surface as marketing features.

## 4. Outcomes

- reduced surface count
- reduced feature fragmentation
- increased substrate coherence
- increased category clarity
