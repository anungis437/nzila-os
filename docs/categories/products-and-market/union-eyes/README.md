## What UnionEyes Does

UnionEyes supports the full representation flow:

- **Intake** — Members submit workplace issues in plain language.
- **Work** — Stewards and LROs review, convert, assign, and advance official casework.
- **Priorities** — Representatives see what needs action next.
- **Intelligence** — Leadership and advanced roles review patterns, risk, and strategy.
- **Outcomes** — Members and representatives can review progress, resolutions, and follow-through.

## Key Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Intake** | A member-submitted issue awaiting review |
| **Case** | Official casework managed by a steward, LRO, or officer |
| **Inbox** | Member landing area for updates, requests, and intake-related activity |
| **Priorities** | Steward and officer landing area for work that needs action |
| **Work** | The active casework surface for steward-led representation |
| **Outcomes** | The results and follow-through view for members and reps |
