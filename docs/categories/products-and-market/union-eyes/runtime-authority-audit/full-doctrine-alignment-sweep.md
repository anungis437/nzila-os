# Full Doctrine Alignment Sweep

Validates that the runtime surfaces and language carry Union Eyes doctrine
(continuity, governance-of-record, stewardship, sovereignty) rather than
generic SaaS framing ("AI assistant", "workflow", "dashboard" as identity).

This Wave 2 sweep is **diagnostic**, not corrective: it inventories
doctrine-misaligned surfaces and queues them for Wave 4 copy normalisation.
No copy strings were rewritten in this revision.

## Doctrine pillars

1. **Continuity** — institutional memory that survives staff turnover.
2. **Governance of record** — every motion, decision, vote, and grievance is
   immutable and provable.
3. **Stewardship** — front-line representation, not "users".
4. **Sovereignty** — member data and cognition stay under organizational
   control.
