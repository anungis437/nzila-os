# Constitutional Semantic Dictionary

Date: 2026-05-25
Scope: Union Eyes constitutional vocabulary for continuity-aware platform governance.

## Continuity

Definition: The system capacity to preserve institutional function and public trust across leadership, operational, or environmental disruption.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use when describing durability of service, mission, and governance legitimacy.
- Prefer institutional continuity or continuity-aware in doctrinal and procurement language.

## Governance Continuity

Definition: Governance operation that maintains accountable decision rights, policy traceability, and constitutional oversight during change.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for policy, board, oversight, and constitutional control surfaces.
- Avoid framing as generic admin management.

## Institutional Intelligence

Definition: Longitudinal evidence and insight layer that informs continuity decisions without detaching from operational reality.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for cross-period observability, trend interpretation, and constitutional decision support.
- Do not reduce to dashboard analytics language.

## Survivability

Definition: Ability to maintain mission-critical service and stewardship capability through stress, transition, and constrained-resource periods.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for transition resilience and leadership continuity outcomes.
- Pair with continuity planning and operational inheritance where relevant.

## Continuity-aware Operations

Definition: Operational execution model in which workflows, priorities, and evidence chains are explicitly aligned to continuity outcomes.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for frontline workbench, case operations, and commitment execution framing.
- Link to evidence, outcomes, and reconstruction burden reduction.

## Governance-safe AI

Definition: AI capability constrained by governance controls, explainability, and auditability to protect institutional legitimacy.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use where AI is bounded by policy, trust, and transparency obligations.
- Avoid autonomous optimization language disconnected from governance controls.

## Operational Inheritance

Definition: Transferability of process knowledge, obligations, and supporting evidence between roles without reconstruction failure.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for onboarding survivability, handoff quality, and continuity transfer design.
- Tie to role transitions and leadership change resilience.

## Reconstruction Burden

Definition: Cost of reassembling context, evidence, and intent when continuity systems fail or stewardship transitions are weak.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use to quantify continuity debt and hidden operational drag.
- Position reduction of reconstruction burden as a platform value outcome.

## Stewardship Concentration

Definition: Risk condition where critical institutional knowledge or control is held by too few actors, increasing continuity fragility.

Canonical usage guidance:
- Use for governance risk review and concentration mitigation planning.
- Pair with operational inheritance and evidence-chain visibility.
