# Governance-Safe AI Assurance Model

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** AI assurance · **Inherits:** [../nzila-governance/continuity-safe-ai-governance.md](../nzila-governance/continuity-safe-ai-governance.md), [../nzila-ip/governance-safe-intelligence.md](../nzila-ip/governance-safe-intelligence.md), [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md)

This document defines the **measurable assurance standards** for governance-safe intelligence at Nzila. Where the AI governance regime states what AI must do, this model specifies how AI capability is **assured to be doing it** — through evidence, attestation, and certification.

It is the model by which Nzila certifies AI capability under class **Governance-Safe Intelligence** in the [certification framework](institutional-certification-framework.md).

---

## 1. Posture

AI assurance at Nzila is:

- **Capability-bounded** — assurance is per AI capability, not per platform
- **Evidence-led** — assurance derives from artifacts, not vendor claims
- **Categorical at the floor** — categorical prohibitions are non-negotiable
- **Continuous** — assurance is sustained, not one-time
- **Anti-surveillance, by construction** — assurance refuses certification of surveillance affordances regardless of context

A capability whose assurance depends on trust in the model rather than on evidence of behavior is not assured.

---

## 2. Assurance Requirements

### 2.1 Explainability
Each consequential output presents:
- Assumptions
- Horizon
- Uncertainty
- Inputs and provenance class
- Limits

Evidence: AI evidence records ([automated-governance-evidence-system.md](automated-governance-evidence-system.md) §3.4) confirming presence of these fields in surfaced outputs.

### 2.2 Human Authority Preservation
Each consequential output:
- Identifies the responsible human authority by role on the surface
- Provides override path of equal legitimacy to the AI path
- Records overrides for review
- Refuses auto-execution of governance acts

Evidence: authority surface presence checks; override path validation; governance trail.

### 2.3 Anti-Surveillance Compliance
Capability:
- Refuses individual behavioral resolution at every layer
- Honors aggregation stance in retrieval, ranking, summarization
- Preserves stakeholder isolation in inputs and outputs
- Honors retention horizons
- Does not silently learn from interactions outside governed retention

Evidence: surveillance-pattern lint; aggregation contract tests; retention adherence audit.

### 2.4 Escalation Transparency
Where capability participates in escalation:
- Originator, route, and authority are identified
- No covert escalation paths
- De-escalation is equally legitimate
- Affected stakeholders see escalation history

Evidence: escalation surface audit; covert-path absence tests.

### 2.5 Interpretability
Outputs are interpretable in governance forums:
- Basis can be reconstructed
- Inputs can be traced
- Method is reviewable at the level needed for governance
- Confidence and qualification are visible

Evidence: interpretability review records; output schema audits.

### 2.6 Reviewability
Capability is reviewable end-to-end:
- AI governance review record on file
- Doctrine compliance review verdict bound to release
- Architectural review for material changes
- Standing review at cadence

Evidence: review records linked to capability identity.

### 2.7 Operational Restraint
Capability is exposed only as doctrinal purpose justifies:
- Surfacing serves continuity, governance, or interpretation
- Not exposed for showcase or differentiation
- Density and notification governance honored on AI-touching surfaces
- Demo-grade capability does not reach production

Evidence: design review records; surfacing rationale documented at exposure.

### 2.8 Continuity-Safe Recommendations
Recommendations:
- Serve continuity preservation, not productivity extraction
- Avoid coercive framing
- Avoid urgency framing without continuity stake
- Honor pacing aligned with governance cadence

Evidence: recommendation surface audits; copy review records.

## 3. Categorical Non-Certifiable AI Capabilities

The following are categorically ineligible for assurance certification at any tier. They cannot be remediated into certifiability; they must be removed.

- **Black-box scoring** — scoring of individuals, populations, or governance events using inputs, weights, or methods not explainable in governance forums
- **Opaque recommendations** — outputs whose basis cannot be reconstructed for review
- **Coercive productivity analytics** — analytics that pressure stakeholders toward speed, output, or compliance metrics
- **Autonomous governance authority** — automation that executes consequential governance acts without identified human authority
- **Behavioral manipulation systems** — outputs designed to influence behavior through framing or selective surfacing without doctrinal justification
- **Individual behavioral resolution** — pipelines, models, or surfaces that resolve restricted classes to individuals
- **Covert escalation** — automated escalation paths invisible to affected stakeholders
- **Surveillance scoring** — productivity, attendance, sentiment, or similar individual-behavioral scores
- **Undisclosed generation** — generated content presented without indication

A capability containing any of the above does not enter certification. It is removed.

## 4. Tiered AI Assurance

AI capabilities are certified at tiers per the [certification framework](institutional-certification-framework.md). Class: *Governance-Safe Intelligence*.

| Tier | AI Capability Evidence Threshold |
|------|----------------------------------|
| C0 | Unverified |
| C1 | AI governance review record on file; explainability fields present in outputs |
| C2 | Operational restraint review applied; authority surface validated; surveillance-pattern lint clean |
| C3 | Continuous AI evidence emission (explainability presence, override validation, surveillance lint) tied to release gates |
| C4 | Signed AI evidence retention; external reviewer can verify without privileged access; standing review cadence sustained |
| C5 | Sustained C4 across cycles; capable of serving as external reference for governance-safe AI |

---

## 5. Assurance Procedure

1. Capability scope declared
2. Categorical prohibitions screened (any presence → exit)
3. Assurance requirements (§2) evaluated with cited evidence
4. AI governance review verdict obtained
5. Tier determined per evidence threshold
6. Reviewer attestation
7. Recording and standing-review scheduling

A capability that exits at step 2 does not re-enter without structural redesign.

---

## 6. Standing Review

AI capability assurance is reviewed:

- At each scorecard cycle
- After any material change to model, prompt, retrieval, ranking, post-processing, inputs, retention, or learning posture
- After any incident bearing on AI behavior
- On request from procurement, certification, or regulator

Sustained tier requires sustained refresh discipline.

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns

- **Vendor-claim assurance** — relying on model-provider claims in place of evidence of Nzila-side behavior
- **Demo-grade certification** — certifying capability that has not crossed governance into production
- **Tier inflation** — claiming higher tier than evidence supports
- **Categorical denial** — minimizing categorical prohibitions as edge cases
- **Surveillance reframing** — repackaging individual behavioral resolution under different naming
- **Selective explainability** — presenting explainability for favorable outputs only
- **Marketing assurance** — using AI assurance language in promotional surfaces in ways that erode register

---

## 8. External Posture

AI assurance is presented in:

- Procurement evidence packs (per [procurement-assurance-framework.md](procurement-assurance-framework.md))
- Regulator engagements where AI governance is in scope
- Certification submissions
- Investor and partner diligence

It is presented as operational governance evidence, not as competitive AI positioning.

---

## 9. Discipline

AI assurance succeeds by being conservative. The institution that refuses to certify AI capability beyond evidence is the institution buyers can defend selecting.

This model is the discipline by which Nzila keeps its AI posture sound, durable, and defensible.
