# Assurance Readiness Review

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** Standing readiness · **Inherits:** all assurance documents in this directory

This document is the **standing readiness review** for the assurance and certification layer. It assesses how completely Nzila's governance has been translated into continuously verifiable assurance infrastructure: where certification and evidence are mature, where they are partial, where they are absent, and what the trajectory looks like toward external attestation.

It is the assurance-side mirror of the [governance readiness review](../nzila-governance/doctrine-operationalization-readiness-review.md) and the [IP formalization readiness review](../nzila-ip/ip-formalization-readiness-review.md).

It exists to ensure Nzila reads its own assurance posture honestly — without the optimistic drift that has historically eroded vendor governance claims at the moment of external scrutiny.

---

## 1. Posture

This review:

- Is **honest** — partial maturity is named partial; absence is named absent
- Is **standing** — refreshed at the cadence specified in §11
- Is **evidence-based** — assertions are anchored in instrument citations and pipeline status
- Is **forward-looking** — gaps are paired with remediation paths and target tiers
- Is **anti-surveillance-safe** — the review itself honors aggregation stance

Optimistic readiness reviews are the leading indicator of assurance failure.

## 14. Verdict

The assurance layer is **operational in instrument and partial in binding maturity**.

- Specification: complete across all twelve assurance instruments
- Per-product binding: progressing; uneven across products and surfaces
- External reviewability: achievable today at C2 across primary classes; C3+ as continuous evidence and signing bind
- External attestation readiness: progressing; signing infrastructure and external reviewer access are the principal next milestones

Nzila has crossed the threshold from governed systems into **continuously attestable institutional governance infrastructure** — at C2 today, with credible trajectory toward C3 across primary classes and selective C4 ambition where institutional engagements warrant.

The institution that can read its own assurance posture honestly is the institution that can sustain external trust over decades. This review is the discipline by which Nzila does so.

## 15. Refresh Cadence

This review is refreshed:

- At each scorecard cycle (per quarter)
- After any material expansion of the assurance layer
- After any incident bearing on assurance posture
- Before any certification body engagement
- On request from board, stewards, or institutional counterparties

Refreshes record changes since the last review and re-state the verdict.
