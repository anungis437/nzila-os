# Institutional Trust Scoring Model

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** Trust maturity scoring · **Inherits:** [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md), [../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md](../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md)

This document defines a **governance-safe trust maturity scoring framework**: a stewardship instrument that reads, in interpretive form, the trust posture of Nzila systems across institutional dimensions.

The model exists to support stewards, reviewers, and external counterparties in reading trust maturity at a glance. It is **interpretive**, not coercive. It is structurally protected from misuse.

A trust scoring model that drifts into ranking, productivity scoring, or behavioral resolution would itself be a doctrine violation. This model is built to refuse that drift.

---

## 1. Posture

The model:

- **Reads** trust posture, does not assign trust performance
- **Aggregates only** — never resolves to individuals
- **Surfaces** maturity for stewardship, not for ranking
- **Is interpretive** — scores are reading aids, not authority
- **Is honest** — partial maturity is named partial
- **Is non-coercive** — outputs cannot be used for compensation, ranking, performance evaluation, or pressure
- **Is anti-surveillance-safe** — by construction

The model is written in stewardship register, not vendor-evaluation register.

---

## 2. Scoring Dimensions

Trust is read across nine dimensions. Each produces a band: *Strong*, *Established*, *Forming*, *Concern*.

### 2.1 Governance Legitimacy
Are governance acts human-authoritative, reviewable, recorded, and consequence-bearing?

### 2.2 Continuity Resilience
Does the system preserve continuity through change? Are continuity surfaces honored, posture readings honest, isolation enforced?

### 2.3 Operational Calmness
Do surfaces and operational rhythms meet calmness floors? Is restraint a sustained property, not a moment?

### 2.4 Explainability
Are AI, analytic, and recommendation outputs explainable in governance forums? Are assumptions and uncertainty visible?

### 2.5 Deployment Legitimacy
Are releases manifest-governed, paced, reversible, and stakeholder-visible? Is environment isolation preserved?

### 2.6 Modernization Safety
Is modernization paced to absorption? Reversible? Sequenced? Stakeholder trust observed?

### 2.7 Anti-Surveillance Compliance
Does the system refuse individual behavioral resolution at every layer? Is aggregation stance structural?

### 2.8 Executive Cognitive Safety
Do executive surfaces meet density, pacing, and calmness thresholds? Is overload avoided?

### 2.9 Institutional Readiness
Is the system operating in real institutions under governance, with stakeholder trust observed and trust pacing honored?

---

## 3. Banding Definitions

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Strong | Sustained evidence at certification class C3+ for the dimension's anchor class |
| Established | Sustained evidence at C2 with a credible trajectory to C3 |
| Forming | Reviews and partial evidence in place; certification class at C1; trajectory unclear |
| Concern | Drift indicators present, doctrine violations on file, or evidence absence at the expected level |

Bands are read from the [evidence pipeline](governance-evidence-pipeline-architecture.md), the [scorecard](../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md), and standing certification status. They are not assigned by impression.

---

## 4. Reading Discipline

A trust read consists of:

- Subject and scope
- Banding per dimension with cited evidence
- Trajectory note per dimension (improving, stable, drifting)
- Standing review reference
- Open concerns and remediation paths
- Window covered

A trust read with bands but no cited evidence is not a read; it is impression.

---

## 5. Cadence

Trust reads are produced:

- At each scorecard cycle
- On material change to subject (release window, new pilot, new vertical)
- On request from stewards, board, or external counterparties
- After incident bearing on any dimension

Reads are versioned; prior reads remain accessible to support trajectory observation.

---

## 6. Use of Trust Reads

Trust reads feed:

- Stewardship deliberation
- Standing readiness review ([assurance-readiness-review.md](assurance-readiness-review.md))
- Procurement evidence packs (where the buyer requires aggregated trust posture)
- Investor and partner diligence
- Long-horizon institutional engagements
- Internal remediation prioritization

Trust reads do **not** feed:

- Individual performance evaluations
- Compensation
- Ranking of products, teams, institutions, or vendors
- Marketing surfaces
- Competitive collateral

The scoring model is structurally protected from these uses by doctrine, by reviewer discipline, and by refusal of any tooling that would surface reads in coercive form.

---

## 7. Categorical Refusals

The model categorically refuses:

- **Behavioral ranking** of individuals, populations, or institutions
- **Productivity scoring** of any kind
- **Institutional coercion** — using trust reads to pressure institutions toward Nzila preferences
- **Composite trust scoring** — collapsing dimensions into a single number for marketing or comparative use
- **Public ranking** of customer institutions
- **Adversarial reads** — producing trust reads of competitors or third parties for Nzila benefit

These refusals are non-negotiable. Their breach makes the scoring model itself a doctrine violation.

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns in the Model Itself

- **Composite collapse** — combining dimension bands into a single overall score for convenience
- **Aspirational banding** — *Strong* claimed where evidence supports *Established* or worse
- **Cosmetic remediation** — closing concerns without addressing underlying cause
- **Cadence laxity** — extending cadence to defer hard reads
- **Evidence absence** — banding without cited evidence
- **Marketing weaponization** — using bands as competitive surfaces
- **Surveillance drift** — model expansion toward individual resolution to "improve fidelity"

---

## 9. External Posture

Trust reads are presentable in:

- Procurement evidence packs (with full dimensional banding and cited evidence)
- Standing readiness review
- Investor and partner diligence in institutional posture
- Certification submissions where aggregated trust posture is in scope

They are not presentable as marketing or competitive material.

---

## 10. Discipline

The model's authority depends entirely on its restraint. A scoring model that becomes a tool of pressure becomes the most damaging form of doctrine violation possible: doctrine inverted into surveillance under stewardship language.

This document is the discipline that prevents that inversion.

Used correctly, the model is the instrument by which Nzila reads its own institutional trust posture honestly, calmly, and at stewardship cadence.
