# Continuous Doctrine Compliance Observability

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** Continuous observability · **Inherits:** [automated-governance-evidence-system.md](automated-governance-evidence-system.md), [../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md](../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md)

This document defines how Nzila makes **doctrine compliance continuously observable** — so that drift, violations, destabilization, overload, contamination, and degradation are detected as they emerge rather than discovered in retrospect.

The premise: **doctrine drift is a slow signal**. Without continuous observability, drift is detected only at incident time, by which point institutional standing has already been damaged.

This layer is the early-warning system for governance integrity.

---

## 1. Posture

The observability layer:

- **Watches signals, not individuals** — observability honors aggregation stance
- **Surfaces leading indicators** — drift signals precede violations
- **Stays calm** — observability does not generate alert chaos
- **Attributes to systems, not people** — findings target structures
- **Feeds the scorecard** — observability outputs flow into [../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md](../nzila-governance/doctrine-governance-scorecard.md)
- **Refuses surveillance affordances** — anti-surveillance applies to observability itself

Observability that resolves to individuals would itself be a doctrine violation.

## 2. Observability Domains

### 2.1 Doctrine Drift
Indicators:
- Off-doctrine vocabulary appearing in code, docs, or surfaces
- Composite-score components introduced without review
- Authority framing absent on consequential surfaces
- Aggregation-stance type tagging coverage decreasing
- Repeat waivers on the same doctrinal concern

### 2.2 Governance Violations
Indicators:
- Compliance review verdicts trending from PASS to CONDITIONAL or worse
- Architectural review escalations rising
- AI governance review records missing for new AI-touching capability
- Deployment manifests missing required fields
- Pilot scope drift detected at runtime

### 2.3 UX Destabilization
Indicators:
- Density-budget breaches per surface class
- Layout instability under live updates
- Motion or notification rates above ceilings
- Urgency framing without continuity stake
- Composite-score widget detection on executive surfaces

### 2.4 Continuity-Breaking Changes
Indicators:
- Cross-isolation traversal attempts
- Retention-policy breaches
- Portability surface absence on data classes that require it
- Continuity posture trajectory degradation
- Stakeholder isolation contract test regressions

### 2.5 Operational Overload
Indicators:
- Concurrent-change ceilings exceeded for an institution
- Recovery interval violations between substantive changes
- Sustained release velocity above absorption capacity
- Stakeholder communication backlog
- Rollback rate elevation

### 2.6 Executive Cognitive Burden
Indicators:
- Tile count or density exceeding executive thresholds
- Notification arrivals on executive surfaces above ceilings
- Refresh cadence drifting toward data-cadence rather than governance-cadence
- Reading time to posture exceeding doctrinal floor
- Click-through acknowledgment rates suggesting alert fatigue

### 2.7 Pilot Contamination
Indicators:
- Pilot capability invoked from non-pilot routes
- Pilot data appearing in production analytics
- Pilot scope extended without governance record
- Pilot exit overdue without governance review
- Demo-mode artifacts touching production state

### 2.8 AI Explainability Degradation
Indicators:
- Recommendation outputs missing assumption/horizon/uncertainty
- Authority surface absence on AI-touching surfaces
- Generation indication absent on generated content
- Override path latency or friction increasing
- Surveillance-pattern lint regressions

## 3. Indicator Categories

Observability indicators are organized into health categories surfaced at the scorecard:

- **Governance-health indicators** — review velocity, verdict trends, waiver concentration
- **Continuity-health indicators** — isolation integrity, retention adherence, posture trajectory
- **Operational calmness indicators** — density, motion, notification, urgency framing
- **Doctrine-alignment indicators** — vocabulary, composite-score absence, authority framing presence
- **Modernization safety indicators** — pacing, concurrency, reversibility, stakeholder visibility

Each category produces a directional reading: *Strengthening*, *Stable*, *Drifting*, *Breach*.

---

## 4. Signal Sources

Indicators are derived from:

- CI policy lint outcomes ([automated-governance-evidence-system.md](automated-governance-evidence-system.md))
- E2E doctrine assertions
- Runtime evidence (sampled, anti-surveillance-safe)
- Deployment manifests and release governance records
- Doctrine compliance review records
- Architectural review records
- AI governance review records
- Operational telemetry from environments

No indicator is sourced from individual behavioral monitoring.

---

## 5. Cadence and Surfacing

Indicators are evaluated:

- **Continuously** — at evidence emission
- **Per release** — rolled into release scorecards
- **Per quarter** — surfaced at the standing scorecard
- **On-demand** — when drift is suspected

Surfacing is calm. Indicator dashboards (where they exist for stewards) honor the same calmness, density, and authority-framing standards that govern any other surface.

---

## 6. Drift Response

Indicator drift triggers:

1. **Surface to scorecard** — entry recorded with cited evidence
2. **Remediation path identification** — owner and proposed correction
3. **Prioritization** — based on doctrinal severity, not commercial pressure
4. **Cadence tightening** — re-observation at higher frequency until trajectory reverses
5. **Escalation** — to architectural review or doctrine governance forum where structural

Remediation closure requires evidence that the drift indicator has reversed, not merely that the remediation has been merged.

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns

- **Alert chaos** — observability that itself violates calmness standards
- **Individual attribution** — surfacing indicators against named individuals
- **Vanity dashboards** — observability surfaces designed for impression rather than stewardship
- **Indicator hoarding** — keeping unfavorable indicators private to protect appearance
- **Drift normalization** — treating sustained drift as the new baseline without correction
- **Surveillance creep** — observability that drifts toward behavioral resolution to "improve coverage"

---

## 8. External Posture

Observability outputs are presentable in procurement, regulator, and certification engagements as evidence of:

- Continuous governance discipline
- Honest acknowledgment of drift and remediation
- Anti-surveillance posture sustained even in observability itself
- Stewardship-grade institutional operating mode

They are not used as competitive surfaces.

---

## 9. Discipline

Observability succeeds by being trusted: by stewards, by reviewers, by external auditors. Trust depends on honesty. Honesty depends on the refusal to suppress unfavorable indicators.

Continuous observability is the institutional commitment that doctrine remains a living governance condition, not a stated past intention.
