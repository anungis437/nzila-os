## SECTION 3: THE PLATFORM

**Heading:**
> Five modules. One integrated platform.

---

**Module 1: ABR Classification Intelligence**
> Applies structured pattern detection to inbound complaints and historical case records — identifying probable ABR signals even in cases not explicitly coded at intake. Every output has a documented evidence basis and requires human reviewer confirmation.

---

**Module 2: Pattern Dashboard**
> Aggregates case data across your institution to surface what individual case review can never reveal: where signals cluster by department, how trends move over time, and what your "dark matter" score is — the estimated proportion of protected grounds harm not currently captured in your records.

---

**Module 3: Investigations Readiness Score**
> Evaluates every active investigation against ABR-specific documentation standards. Surfaces gaps before files close — not after a tribunal reopens them. Tribunal-loss prevention.

---

**Module 4: Remediation Tracking**
> Converts case closures into structured remediation records with owners, deadlines, evidence requirements, and completion verification. Institutional memory that survives leadership transitions.

---

**Module 5: Equity Intelligence Reporting Suite**
> Board-ready, bilingual (EN/FR) equity accountability reports, generated on demand. Consistent, reproducible, audit-ready. Not whatever the equity officer had time to build.
