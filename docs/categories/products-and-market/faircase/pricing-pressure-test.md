# FAIRCASE Pricing Pressure Test

## Scenario 1: 25-seat Union

- Typical profile: regional or sector union operations team.
- Fit tier: Foundation with limited services.
- Recommended price band:
  - Base: $18,000-$28,000 annual.
  - Optional onboarding/services: $3,000-$8,000 one-time.
- Why:
  - Needs strong grievance governance with moderate user count.

## Scenario 2: 500-Employee Company

- Typical profile: HR + legal + compliance stakeholders.
- Fit tier: Growth.
- Recommended price band:
  - Base: $36,000-$60,000 annual.
  - Optional onboarding/services: $8,000-$20,000 one-time.
- Why:
  - Multi-team process and executive reporting requirements.

## Scenario 3: 3-Campus University

- Typical profile: ombuds, HR, legal, student conduct offices.
- Fit tier: Growth to Enterprise.
- Recommended price band:
  - Base: $65,000-$110,000 annual.
  - Optional onboarding/services: $20,000-$45,000 one-time.
- Why:
  - Cross-campus governance and confidentiality complexity.

## Scenario 4: Municipality

- Typical profile: HR, oversight, legal, and department operations.
- Fit tier: Enterprise public-sector package.
- Recommended price band:
  - Base: $85,000-$140,000 annual.
  - Optional onboarding/services: $25,000-$60,000 one-time.
- Why:
  - Procurement overhead and multi-department rollout needs.

## Scenario 5: Hospital Network

- Typical profile: multi-facility people and risk operations.
- Fit tier: Enterprise.
- Recommended price band:
  - Base: $120,000-$220,000 annual.
  - Optional onboarding/services: $35,000-$80,000 one-time.
- Why:
  - High trust sensitivity, cross-site governance complexity.

## Tier Recommendation Summary

- Foundation: smaller unions and single-unit teams.
- Growth: mid-market HR and multi-office institutions.
- Enterprise: public sector, universities, healthcare networks, and multi-department buyers.

## Commercial Guardrails

- No discounting without scope discipline.
- Tie concessions to term, prepay, or expansion commitments.
- Keep diagnostic and pilot as value proof, not free consulting.
