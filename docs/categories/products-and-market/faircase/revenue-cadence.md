# FAIRCASE Revenue Operating Cadence

## Weekly Rhythm

## Monday: Pipeline Review

- Review funnel by stage and ICP.
- Confirm top opportunities and blockers.
- Assign owner actions for each active deal.

## Tuesday: Outbound Day

- Execute outbound targets (75 touches, 15 follow-ups).
- Launch new messaging tests.
- Update reply and objection tracker.

## Wednesday: Demo Day

- Run scheduled demos and diagnostics.
- Capture buyer objections and urgency signals.
- Advance qualified accounts to proposal path.

## Thursday: Proposal + Partner Day

- Build and send proposals.
- Run partner co-sell and referral calls.
- Move legal/procurement workstreams forward.

## Friday: Metrics + Improvements

- Review conversion metrics and stage velocity.
- Compare planned vs actual activity.
- Lock next-week improvements and owner commitments.

## Operating KPIs

- Leads created.
- Meetings booked.
- Demo conversion rate.
- Proposal rate.
- Pilot rate.
- Close rate.
- Sales cycle days.
- Source attribution quality.
