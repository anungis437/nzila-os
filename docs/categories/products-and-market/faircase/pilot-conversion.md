# FAIRCASE Pilot Conversion System

## Pilot Success Metrics

- Faster resolution time.
- Cleaner documentation quality.
- Reduced missed SLAs.
- Improved reporting speed and confidence.
- Manager satisfaction.
- Improved risk visibility.

## Conversion Timeline

## Day 1: Onboarding

- Confirm sponsor, operators, and governance reviewers.
- Baseline KPI snapshot.
- Define conversion criteria and annual decision date.

## Day 14: Checkpoint

- Review early adoption and workflow usage.
- Identify blockers and adjust enablement.
- Validate procurement/security thread owner.

## Day 30: Proof Review

- Present KPI movement vs baseline.
- Share evidence of process consistency and reporting improvements.
- Confirm economic buyer alignment.

## Day 45: Expansion Talk

- Define annual scope options (department, region, enterprise).
- Align commercial options and contracting path.
- Start legal and procurement draft loop.

## Day 60: Annual Close

- Present pilot success package.
- Confirm final pricing and support model.
- Execute annual agreement and rollout plan.

## Pilot Exit Criteria for Annual Conversion

- At least 3 agreed KPI improvements observed.
- Sponsor confirms operational fit.
- Procurement path is defined and active.
- Executive buyer has reviewed annual proposal.
