# FAIRCASE Proposal Template Suite

## Common Sections (All Proposal Types)

- Executive summary
- Scope and objectives
- Implementation timeline
- Commercials and terms
- Support model and SLA
- Security and privacy summary
- Success metrics and governance cadence

---

## 1) Pilot Proposal Template

### Scope

- One department or operating unit.
- Complaint intake, assignment, SLA, evidence, and reporting workflows.

### Timeline

- 8 weeks total.
- Weekly sponsor checkpoint.

### Pricing

- Fixed pilot fee + optional setup services.
- Clearly state any pilot-to-annual credit terms.

### Support

- Weekly enablement sessions.
- Named implementation lead.

### Security Summary

- Access controls, audit trail posture, evidence integrity process.

### Success Metrics

- Resolution cycle-time delta.
- SLA adherence delta.
- Reporting prep time reduction.
- Sponsor confidence score.

---

## 2) Annual SaaS Proposal Template

### Scope

- Annual subscription by tier (Foundation/Growth/Enterprise).
- User, module, and environment entitlements.

### Timeline

- 30-60 day onboarding depending on complexity.

### Pricing

- Annual subscription.
- Optional multi-year and prepay terms.

### Support

- SLA by tier, ticketing model, escalation path.

### Security Summary

- Procurement-ready controls summary and documentation pack.

### Success Metrics

- Adoption and workflow completion.
- SLA performance.
- Reporting velocity and quality.

---

## 3) Enterprise Rollout Proposal Template

### Scope

- Multi-department rollout with phased onboarding.
- Integration and policy mapping.
- Executive governance reporting framework.

### Timeline

- Wave-based rollout (for example 3 waves over 6-9 months).

### Pricing

- Enterprise master agreement + wave allocation schedule.

### Support

- Program governance cadence.
- Executive sponsor reviews and risk register.

### Security Summary

- Data handling boundaries, role governance, audit and evidence readiness.

### Success Metrics

- Department-level adoption.
- Cross-department SLA performance.
- Governance and trust indicators.

---

## 4) Public Sector Proposal Template

### Scope

- Public accountability complaint workflows.
- Procurement and compliance aligned implementation plan.

### Timeline

- Procurement-aware timeline with milestone gates.

### Pricing

- Annual or multi-year structure aligned to public purchasing constraints.

### Support

- Public-sector implementation governance model.

### Security Summary

- Privacy, retention, auditability, and accessibility commitments.

### Success Metrics

- Reduced unresolved backlog.
- Improved reporting to oversight stakeholders.
- Better SLA and process consistency across departments.
