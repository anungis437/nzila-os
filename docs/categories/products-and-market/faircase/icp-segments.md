# FAIRCASE ICP Segments

## ICP Priority Ranking

1. HR leaders at 200-5000 employee organizations
2. Unions and associations with grievance volume
3. Universities and colleges with misconduct and ombuds workflows
4. Municipal and public institutions
5. Healthcare networks and hospitals

## ICP #1: HR Leaders (200-5000 employees)

- Pains:
  - Case handling is inconsistent across HRBPs and legal teams.
  - Investigations are slow and hard to audit.
  - Board and executive reporting is manual and politically risky.
- Buying triggers:
  - Escalating complaint backlog.
  - New compliance pressure or board scrutiny.
  - Leadership change with mandate to improve trust and accountability.
- Budget owner: CHRO with legal/compliance co-sponsor.
- Objections:
  - "We already have HRIS and ticketing systems."
  - "Adoption will be too hard for managers."
- Urgency level: High.
- Ideal offer: Paid diagnostic followed by 8-week pilot in one business unit.
- Sales cycle estimate: 70-110 days.

## ICP #2: Unions and Associations

- Pains:
  - Grievances and representation records are fragmented.
  - Evidence quality varies by steward and region.
  - Leadership visibility is delayed and incomplete.
- Buying triggers:
  - Arbitration losses due to weak documentation.
  - National office pressure to standardize handling.
  - New intake volume from workplace shifts.
- Budget owner: Executive Director / General Secretary with labour relations lead.
- Objections:
  - "We use spreadsheets and shared drives today."
  - "Field reps are too busy to learn another system."
- Urgency level: High.
- Ideal offer: Pilot with 1 federation/regional unit and 2-3 local teams.
- Sales cycle estimate: 60-120 days.

## ICP #3: Universities and Colleges

- Pains:
  - Misconduct, ombuds, and policy workflows are siloed.
  - Investigations need strict confidentiality and defensibility.
  - Reporting to boards and regulators is labor intensive.
- Buying triggers:
  - Policy reform cycle.
  - Incident cluster exposing process gaps.
  - Ombuds office resource constraints.
- Budget owner: VP People / General Counsel / Risk & Compliance office.
- Objections:
  - "Procurement and governance approvals are slow."
  - "We need assurance on privacy and data residency."
- Urgency level: Medium-High.
- Ideal offer: Diagnostic plus phased pilot for one office first (ombuds or HR).
- Sales cycle estimate: 90-150 days.

## ICP #4: Municipal and Public Institutions

- Pains:
  - Public trust risk from unresolved complaints.
  - Audit trails and policy evidence are inconsistent.
  - Multi-department handoffs create SLA misses.
- Buying triggers:
  - External audit findings.
  - FOI pressure and public scrutiny.
  - Council or oversight body directive.
- Budget owner: CAO/City Manager office with HR and legal.
- Objections:
  - "Public procurement cycles take too long."
  - "Need stronger security and accessibility assurances."
- Urgency level: Medium-High.
- Ideal offer: Public-sector pilot with procurement-ready scope and controls.
- Sales cycle estimate: 120-210 days.

## ICP #5: Healthcare Networks

- Pains:
  - Complaint and incident workflows cross multiple facilities.
  - Documentation quality and timeliness vary by site.
  - Leadership lacks clean risk visibility.
- Buying triggers:
  - Accreditation or compliance cycles.
  - Sentinel events requiring systemic process correction.
  - Labour relations pressure and staff trust concerns.
- Budget owner: VP People/HR, Compliance, and Risk leadership.
- Objections:
  - "Clinical systems already consume change bandwidth."
  - "Security and privacy review will be heavy."
- Urgency level: Medium.
- Ideal offer: 8-week pilot in 1-2 facilities with expansion playbook.
- Sales cycle estimate: 110-180 days.
