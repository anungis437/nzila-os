## What FairCase Is Not Doing in This Pilot

This is not an external investigation. FairCase does not make findings of fact, does not determine whether discrimination occurred, and does not produce legal determinations of any kind.

What FairCase produces is a **classification intelligence view** — a structured analysis of what your existing case data captures, what signals are present in that data, and where your investigation documentation has gaps relative to evidence-grade standards.

Every classification output is a **recommendation for human review**, not an automated determination. Your designated reviewer makes all final classification decisions. FairCase provides the analytical framework and evidence basis. Your people make the calls.

## Privacy and Data Security

Your case data is among the most sensitive information your institution holds. FairCase's handling reflects that.

**What we protect:**

- All data is processed and stored in Canada (Microsoft Azure Canada Central)
- Complainant identities are stored in an identity vault with differential access controls — they are never exposed to the classification AI
- Cases are analyzed using anonymized case records; identity is accessible only to authorized designated reviewers
- All data transfers use encrypted channels (TLS 1.3)
- Your data is never used to train FairCase models for other clients
- Full data deletion upon pilot completion if you do not proceed to annual subscription

**What you control:**

- You designate which team members can access which data views
- You control which cases enter the classification review
- You review and override any classification recommendation
- You receive a complete audit log of every access event in your dataset

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What data do we need to provide?**
Your existing HR case records, investigation files, grievance logs, or equity office case notes from the past 2–3 years. We work with what you have — you do not need to have a structured system. We have handled everything from Excel logs to SharePoint folders to structured HRIS exports.

**How do we transfer data securely?**
We use an encrypted secure file transfer protocol (SFTP) with keys provided to your IT team. We can also work with your IT team's preferred secure transfer method. We do not accept USB, unencrypted email attachments, or public cloud sharing links.

**What if our data is messy or incomplete?**
That is normal, and it is part of what we diagnose. Incomplete data is itself a finding — it tells us where your classification infrastructure has the most gaps.

**Who sees our data?**
Only your designated reviewer (from your team) and the FairCase implementation lead. Access is logged. We do not share your data with any other client, partner, or government body without your explicit written consent.

**What happens if we find something concerning in the findings?**
That is your institution's decision, not ours. FairCase produces a classification intelligence view. What you do with those findings — further investigation, remediation planning, legal review — is your decision. We can provide guidance on what the next steps typically look like, but we do not make that call for you.

**Can we keep the findings confidential?**
Yes. The findings report is produced for internal use. It is not shared, published, or disclosed to any external party by FairCase without your explicit written consent. You control the disclosure of your findings entirely.

**What does the annual platform look like after the pilot?**
The annual platform is the full FairCase Equity Intelligence Platform — ongoing classification intelligence on new cases, the live pattern dashboard, the investigations workflow for all new and active cases, and the equity reporting suite. Pricing starts at $24,000/year (Foundation) and scales with organizational size and module scope.
