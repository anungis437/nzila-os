# FAIRCASE Competitor Positioning

## Category

Justice and equity governance platform for institutional accountability.

## Alternatives Evaluated by Buyers

- Generic LMS and DEI course libraries
- Horizontal HR case management tools
- Generic legal research platforms
- Spreadsheet-based internal governance workflows

## FAIRCASE Differentiation

- Governance-first incident and remediation lifecycle
- Role-filtered executive and board reporting exports
- Evidence integrity controls with seal/verify workflows
- Justice and equity domain workflows instead of generic templates

## Why FAIRCASE Wins

- Faster path from issue intake to accountable remediation
- Better governance posture for audit and board review
- Higher trust from traceable and reproducible reporting artifacts
- Designed to support institutional anti-racism outcomes at scale

## Competitive Message

"FAIRCASE is where equity policy becomes governed operations."
