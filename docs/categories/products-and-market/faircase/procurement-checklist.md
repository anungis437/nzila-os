# FAIRCASE Procurement Checklist

## Commercial Readiness

- Proposal scope and pricing summary.
- MSA and order form draft.
- Pilot-to-annual conversion terms.

## Security and Privacy

- Security one-pager.
- Data handling and retention summary.
- Access control and audit posture summary.

## Technical Readiness

- Implementation scope and onboarding plan.
- Integration boundaries and assumptions.
- Support model and escalation contacts.

## Governance and Compliance

- Success metrics and reporting cadence.
- Executive sponsor and operating owner identified.
- Review and sign-off timeline confirmed.

## Procurement Process Controls

- Required legal and procurement approvers listed.
- Decision milestones and target dates documented.
- Contract blockers and owners tracked weekly.
