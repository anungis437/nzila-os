# FAIRCASE Pilot Brochure (8 Weeks)

## Pilot Objective

Prove that FAIRCASE improves case workflow quality, accountability, and leadership reporting confidence in a live operating environment.

## What Is Included

- Intake and case workflow configuration.
- Assignment and SLA governance setup.
- Evidence and reporting workflows.
- Weekly operating checkpoint.

## Pilot Timeline

- Weeks 1-2: setup and baseline.
- Weeks 3-4: active workflow execution.
- Weeks 5-6: optimization and governance review.
- Weeks 7-8: impact summary and annual recommendation.

## Target Outcomes

- Faster case resolution.
- Fewer missed workflow SLAs.
- Cleaner case documentation.
- Better executive reporting quality.

## Success Review

Pilot concludes with KPI scorecard, implementation findings, and annual rollout options.
