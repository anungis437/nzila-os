# Live Operational Adoption Hardening

> Elevate live operational adoption maturity.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The ecosystem must increasingly feel **operationally adoptable** — by stewards, governance operators, executives, and pilot operators — under realistic field conditions.

## 2. Adoption Targets

Harden:

- onboarding readiness
- steward readiness
- executive readiness
- governance review readiness
- rollout readiness
- operational cadence readiness
- pilot readiness

## 3. Adoption Refinements

### 3.1 Operator Onboarding

- Calm, paced onboarding — no overload at first contact.
- Continuity cues on every onboarding step.
- Bounded scope statements on every interpretive surface introduced during onboarding.
- Escalation path discoverable from step one.

### 3.2 Steward Continuity

- Steward surfaces preserve continuity context across sessions.
- Steward review cadence is sustainable (not engagement-optimized).
- Stewards always have a visible reviewer-of-record reference.

### 3.3 Governance Walkthroughs

- Governance surfaces present clearly bounded interpretation.
- Escalation paths are explicit and named.
- Review cadence is calm and predictable.

### 3.4 Executive Briefings

- Executive surfaces present interpretive support, not authoritative judgment.
- Briefings are bounded, calm, and continuity-oriented.

### 3.5 Pilot Operations

- Pilot surfaces preserve continuity-safe rollback paths.
- Pilot cadence load is bounded.
- Pilot summaries preserve human authority context.
