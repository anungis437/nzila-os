# Institutional Field-Pressure Hardening

> Prepare the ecosystem for real institutional pressure.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The system must increasingly feel **resilient under institutional pressure** — governance ambiguity, executive confusion, cadence fatigue, operational fragmentation, continuity breakdown, institutional skepticism, and procurement scrutiny.

## 2. Field-Pressure Targets

Harden against:

- governance ambiguity
- onboarding overload
- executive confusion
- cadence fatigue
- operational fragmentation
- continuity breakdown
- institutional skepticism
- procurement scrutiny

## 3. Pressure Rehearsal Protocols

### 3.1 Governance Ambiguity Rehearsal

When a governance surface is asked to resolve an ambiguous case:

- present bounded interpretation only
- name the escalation path explicitly
- preserve human reviewer-of-record authority
- avoid implicit adjudication

### 3.2 Onboarding Overload Rehearsal

When onboarding load increases:

- pace surfaces; never collapse into dense walkthroughs
- preserve continuity-safe defaults
- expose discoverable escalation

### 3.3 Executive Confusion Rehearsal

When executives encounter ambiguous interpretive output:

- surfaces must present bounded scope explicitly
- continuity cues must remain visible
- the human reviewer of record must be named

### 3.4 Cadence Fatigue Rehearsal

When cadence load grows:

- no escalation in alert frequency
- no engagement-optimization mechanics
- cadence must remain calm and sustainable

### 3.5 Operational Fragmentation Rehearsal

When operations span multiple apps:

- governance, cognition, and continuity vocabularies remain identical
- bounded interpretation cues remain identical
- escalation paths remain consistent

### 3.6 Continuity Breakdown Rehearsal

When continuity is at risk:

- restoration summaries are calm and bounded
- rollback flows preserve operator authority
- restoration UX reaffirms continuity, not blame

### 3.7 Institutional Skepticism Response

When asked "is this AI making decisions?":

- canonical answer: "Bounded interpretive support only; final authority remains with accountable human operators."
- always reference the cognition doctrine
- always name the escalation path

### 3.8 Procurement Scrutiny Response

When procurement asks about anti-surveillance, autonomous decisions, or workforce impact:

- canonical procurement positioning applies
- forbidden framings (AI replacement, autonomous intelligence, copilot, AI-first) are rejected
- continuity-safe modernization posture is presented
