# Cross-App UX Embodiment Finalization

> Finalize ecosystem-wide institutional embodiment.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The ecosystem must increasingly feel **institutionally embodied** — not cross-product assembled.

UX embodiment is the difference between **a portfolio of apps** and **one piece of operating infrastructure**.

## 2. Convergence Targets

Converge:

- operational rhythm
- spacing systems
- review posture
- stabilization posture
- operational summaries
- cadence embodiment
- executive posture
- escalation posture
- onboarding posture

Across UE, Console, ExecutiveOS, UE Ops, FairCase, CFO systems.

## 3. Canonical Embodiment Patterns

### 3.1 Rhythm

- Section spacing converged across surfaces.
- No anxiogenic transitions; cadence prefers calm reveal over animated emphasis.

### 3.2 Posture

- Governance grouping over feature grouping.
- Continuity-restoring tone in all stabilization surfaces.
- Bounded interpretation cues on every interpretive surface.

### 3.3 Operational Grouping

- Review surfaces grouped under continuity, not engagement.
- Escalation surfaces always co-located with the human reviewer of record.

### 3.4 Operational Calmness

- No popovers on cognition surfaces.
- No celebratory toasts on stabilization surfaces.
- No urgency-inflation on cadence surfaces.

## 4. Implementation Discipline

- Refactor layouts, summaries, transitions, cadence surfaces, review flows in place.
- Preserve component contracts to keep imports and tests stable.
- Defer larger layout reorganizations to dedicated UX PRs.
