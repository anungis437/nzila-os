# Canonical Operational Architecture

> **Status:** Canonical convergence · **Layer:** Architecture · **Inherits:** [README.md](README.md)

## 1. Objective

Define the canonical operating architecture for the Nzila ecosystem — one shape every app inherits.

## 2. Operational hierarchy

| Tier | Responsibility | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Doctrine | Defines what may exist | Cross-product |
| Governance | Decides under doctrine | Cross-product |
| Operations | Enacts under governance | Per product, governance-bound |
| Continuity | Stabilizes operations | Per system |
| Stabilization | Slows or pauses change | Per system |
| Review | Records institutional decisions | Cross-product |
| Executive | Sets pace and priority | Cross-product |

## 3. Governance hierarchy

Governance authority always flows: **doctrine → governance forum → operating product**. A product never authorizes its own governance.

## 4. Review hierarchy

Reviews always cite doctrine. The decision ledger is append-only across all apps. Supersession is the only correction mechanism.

## 5. Continuity hierarchy

Continuity is system-scoped. No app resolves continuity to a person. Stabilization guidance is advisory; humans hold authority.

## 6. Rollout hierarchy

Rollout pacing is constrained by stabilization signals. Cadence is preserved; acceleration is refused.

## 7. Executive hierarchy

Executives consume bounded summaries. Reading load is capped per surface. Composite scoring is refused.

## 8. Operational interaction hierarchy

Per surface: at most three actions per card; at most one primary action per screen; at most one decision per session.

## 9. Required principles

- Calmness over completeness.
- Sparseness over density.
- Coherence over uniformity.
- Continuity over novelty.
- Legitimacy over reactivity.

## 10. Discipline

The architecture succeeds when an operator who knows one Nzila product can reason about another within minutes and never feels they have entered a different institution.
