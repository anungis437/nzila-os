## 9. Unresolved fragmentation risks

- **Adoption.** The canonical contract ships; sustained adoption across all apps remains pending.
- **Operator validation.** Cross-app validation rounds not yet executed.
- **Per-app divergent role keys.** Some apps still maintain local role enums; promotion or removal is pending.
- **Sidebar harmonization.** Apps with deep historical sidebars (Console) require staged refactors to consume `buildCanonicalSidebar()` end-to-end.
- **Cadence enforcement.** Several legacy refresh intervals remain hard-coded; migration to `cadenceFor()` is incremental.

## 10. Discipline

`forming` is not a failure. Promotion requires evidence — operator findings, in-app adoption, and cross-app journey validation — not self-assessment.
