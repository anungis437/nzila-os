# Executive Cognitive Consistency

> **Status:** Canonical convergence · **Layer:** Executive cognition · **Inherits:** [docs/nzila-governance-experience/executive-governance-experience.md](../nzila-governance-experience/executive-governance-experience.md)

## 1. Objective

Standardize executive operational cognition across every Nzila product so leaders never experience operational fragmentation, conceptual drift, posture inconsistency, or governance inconsistency.

## 2. Canonical executive standards

| Aspect | Standard |
|---|---|
| Information density | ≤ 6 cards per executive screen |
| Review pacing | One decision per session |
| Continuity interpretation | Banded reading + one institutional sentence |
| Governance interpretation | Banded reading + cited doctrine |
| Rollout interpretation | Banded pacing + advisory |
| Legitimacy review | Release × environment × verdict, text-first |
| Refused | Composite scores, real-time refresh, animation, person-resolving content |

## 3. Cross-app executive continuity

The executive experience in Console must reuse the same posture vocabulary as Control Plane and UE. Modernization pacing, continuity briefing, and posture cards must read identically.

## 4. Bounded reading

A single executive surface MUST be readable inside two minutes without orientation. Surfaces that exceed the bound are split, not paginated.

## 5. Required outputs

An `executiveSurfaceContract(surfaceId)` helper ships in [`@nzila/operational-convergence`](../../packages/operational-convergence) describing the bounded reading expectations per surface.

## 6. Discipline

Executive cognition succeeds when leadership ends a session with one clear decision, recorded under doctrine, with the same vocabulary they would use in any other Nzila product.
