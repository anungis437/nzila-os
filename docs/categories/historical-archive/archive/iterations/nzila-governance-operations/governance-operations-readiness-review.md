## 9. Unresolved operational governance risks

- **Adoption.** The operations layer is built; it is not yet the default operational surface. Until it is, operators may continue to default to lower-governance dashboards.
- **Embedding cadence.** Live review panels are designed but not yet embedded in every host app. Drift risk grows the longer the embeddings lag.
- **External attestation surface.** Procurement-grade external attestation surface is not yet released; it depends on signing maturity in the runtime layer.
- **Interpretation library.** The interpretation helpers ship as primitives; the institution-level reading library is still being authored.

## 10. Discipline

A `forming` banding is not a failure. Claiming `established` or `strong` without the evidence to back it is. The operations layer matures by surface, by product, and by audience — never by self-assessment alone.
