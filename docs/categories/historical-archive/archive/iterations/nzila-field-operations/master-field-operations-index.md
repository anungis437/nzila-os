# Master Field Operations Index

**Status:** Active
**Effective:** 2026-05-09
**Authority root:** Nzila Ventures institutional field operations layer.

This index is the canonical entry point to the institutional field
operations doctrine. The field operations layer sits above the rollout
governance layer and operationalizes it into living institutional
practice — operator cadence, governance review rhythm, stabilization
operations, executive briefings, and operational legitimacy auditing.

The field operations layer is **stabilization-first**. It optimizes
for institutional calmness, continuity-safe pacing, and governance
readability. It does not optimize for rollout speed, adoption
pressure, or operational intensity.

## Operating principles

1. **Stabilization is the goal**, not throughput.
2. **Cadence is interpretive**, not bureaucratic.
3. **Operators are reviewed, not surveilled.**
4. **Every workflow names its authority.**
5. **Field operations are deterministic projections of recorded
   attestations** — there is no hidden operational state.
