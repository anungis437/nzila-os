# Institutional Field Operations Framework

**Status:** Active
**Effective:** 2026-05-09
**Authority:** [master-field-operations-index.md](./master-field-operations-index.md)

This document defines the canonical institutional operations model
for Nzila Ventures.

---

## 1. Posture

Field operations are **stabilization-first**. Every operational
decision is interpreted against:

- continuity-safe pacing
- governance readability
- institutional sustainability

The framework is deliberately non-aggressive. It does not encode
throughput targets, adoption velocity, or competitive cadence.

---

## 2. Cadence model

Cadence is layered:

| Layer                 | Default rhythm           | Authority                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Operational cadence   | daily light / weekly deep| operator-cadence-system.md                |
| Governance cadence    | weekly                   | governance-review-cadence.md              |
| Rollout cadence       | per-promotion            | nzila-rollout-governance corpus           |
| Stabilization cadence | windowed (per tier)      | stabilization-operations-system.md        |
| Onboarding cadence    | phased, paced            | onboarding-governance-operations.md       |
| Executive cadence     | bi-weekly briefing       | executive-briefing-rhythm.md              |
| Environment cadence   | per-tier lifecycle       | environment-lifecycle-governance.md       |

Cadences may be slowed for institutional stabilization. They may
**not** be accelerated without a recorded exception attestation.

---

## 3. Continuity-safe operational pacing

No two cadence-bearing operations on the same tier may overlap their
stabilization windows. An operator may not initiate a new field
operation against a tier whose continuity window is open.

This rule is enforced in the rollout governance UI today and is the
behavioral substrate of every field operations workflow defined in
this corpus.

---

## 4. Executive operational rhythm

The executive rhythm is a **sparse summary** of governance posture.
It is not an analytics surface. Its job is to support strategic
cognition, not operational micromanagement.

See [executive-briefing-rhythm.md](./executive-briefing-rhythm.md).

---

## 5. Environment lifecycle rhythm

Each tier moves through:

1. provisioned → 2. attested → 3. promoted → 4. stabilizing →
5. observed → 6. (optionally) retired or restored.

See [environment-lifecycle-governance.md](./environment-lifecycle-governance.md).

---

## 6. Long-term institutional stability

This framework is optimized for:

- low operator burnout
- low governance ritualism
- low review queue debt
- high continuity assurance
- high institutional readability

It is **not** optimized for:

- maximum throughput
- maximum coverage
- maximum visibility

These are deliberate non-goals.
