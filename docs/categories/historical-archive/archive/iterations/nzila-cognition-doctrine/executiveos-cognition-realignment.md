# ExecutiveOS Cognition Realignment

> Canonical realignment of executive intelligence surfaces toward **Executive Operational Cognition Infrastructure**.

## 1. Convergence Statement

ExecutiveOS surfaces are **executive operational cognition infrastructure** — bounded interpretive briefings designed to preserve executive cadence, surface continuity-relevant context, and support executive judgment.

ExecutiveOS must **never** behave as:

- AI CEO software
- autonomous executive intelligence
- strategic AI authority
- autonomous decision agents

## 2. Refactor Targets

- Executive insights → bounded executive interpretive briefings.
- Decision systems → bounded decision-support cognition with explicit human authority of record.
- Recommendations → bounded operational suggestions, always presented with confidence bounds and human approval gate.
- Forecasting systems → continuity-safe trend interpretation; not authoritative predictions.
- Executive intelligence digests → executive cognition digests with bounded interpretation and explicit final-authority statement.
- Operational interpretation → calm, cadence-preserving operational reading.
- Executive summaries → continuity briefings.

## 3. Required Surface Behavior

Every ExecutiveOS surface must:

- include the canonical phrasing: **"Interpretive support only; final authority remains with accountable human operators."**
- expose the human reviewer of record on any decision-affecting output
- avoid autonomous executive framing
- avoid AI-CEO framing
- avoid optimization-maximalist framing
- preserve a calm, infrastructural tone

## 4. Approval Gate Requirement

Any "autopilot" or "agent" semantics in ExecutiveOS surfaces must:

- expose the bounded scope explicitly
- present a human approval gate before any execution-tracked action
- include explicit confidence bounds
- never auto-execute decisions on behalf of accountable operators
