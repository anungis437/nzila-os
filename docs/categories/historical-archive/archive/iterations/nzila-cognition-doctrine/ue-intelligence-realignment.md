# UE Intelligence Realignment

> Canonical realignment of Union Eyes intelligence systems toward **Institutional Labor Continuity Intelligence**.

## 1. Convergence Statement

UE intelligence surfaces are **institutional labor continuity infrastructure**. They preserve labor-relations memory, support stewardship cadence, and provide bounded operational interpretation. They are **not** AI assistants, copilots, or autonomous labor governance systems.

This realignment ratifies and extends the prior labor-continuity refactor under the global cognition doctrine.

## 2. Refactor Targets (Status)

- ✅ AI assistant framing — removed across UE onboarding wizards.
- ✅ Copilot flows — replaced with continuity guidance.
- ✅ Precedent matching — reframed as bounded continuity precedent surfacing with human review of record.
- ✅ Semantic search — reframed as institutional memory retrieval.
- ✅ Grievance intelligence — reframed with continuity disclaimer and explicit non-authoritative escalation note.
- ✅ Steward guidance — converged to continuity guidance + human authority preservation.
- ✅ Operational summaries — reframed as bounded interpretive briefings.
- ✅ Onboarding summaries — reframed as continuity orientation.
- ✅ Governance review intelligence — reframed as institutional labor continuity intelligence.

## 3. Convergence Target

Every UE intelligence surface must read as **Institutional Labor Continuity Intelligence** — calm, infrastructural, governance-safe, and continuity-oriented.

## 4. Required Implementation Posture

UE surfaces must:

- expose the human reviewer of record on every interpretive output
- include the canonical bounded-interpretation disclaimer
- avoid AI personality framing
- avoid "ask anything" framing
- avoid steward scoring, productivity ranking, or behavioral interpretation
