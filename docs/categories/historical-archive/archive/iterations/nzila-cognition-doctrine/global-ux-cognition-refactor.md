# Global UX Cognition Refactor

> Removes consumer AI behavior patterns repo-wide; emphasizes institutional, governance-native UX posture.

## 1. Convergence Statement

UX must increasingly feel **institutional and governance-native**. It must not feel like consumer AI.

## 2. Required Removals

Remove repo-wide:

- chatbot primacy
- assistant anthropomorphism
- AI personality framing
- "ask anything" framing
- recommendation dominance
- optimization framing
- AI companion semantics

## 3. Required Additions

Emphasize repo-wide:

- continuity summaries
- operational context
- governance readability
- bounded interpretation
- institutional lineage
- operational cadence
- stabilization posture
- escalation pathways
- human reviewer of record

## 4. UX Tone Canon

The canonical tone is:

- calm
- infrastructural
- continuity-oriented
- legitimacy-preserving
- non-anthropomorphic
- non-optimizing
- non-coercive

## 5. Canonical Disclaimers

When an interpretive surface is rendered, include one of:

- **"Interpretive support only; final authority remains with accountable human operators."**
- **"Bounded institutional interpretation; final authority remains with accountable [domain] operators."**

## 6. Authority

This refactor derives from the [Institutional Operational Cognition Doctrine](institutional-operational-cognition-doctrine.md).
