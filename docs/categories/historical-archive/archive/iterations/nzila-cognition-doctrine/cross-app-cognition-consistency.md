# Cross-App Cognition Consistency

> Cognition must behave coherently across the entire ecosystem.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The ecosystem must increasingly feel like **one cognition philosophy** — not a collection of AI-enabled enterprise applications.

## 2. Validated Postures (Across Apps)

Cognition posture, governance posture, continuity posture, escalation posture, operational interpretation posture, and stabilization interpretation posture must be consistent across:

- UE (Union Eyes)
- Console (ExecutiveOS)
- ExecutiveOS dashboard surfaces
- FairCase
- CFO
- platform packages

## 3. Required Cross-App Invariants

Every intelligence surface, regardless of app, must:

- present interpretation as bounded
- expose an explicit human escalation path
- declare final authority remains with accountable human operators
- avoid AI-personality, autonomous-agent, surveillance, or optimization framing
- prefer continuity, cadence, and stabilization framing

## 4. Cross-App Tone Canon

- **UE** → governance-safe institutional labor continuity intelligence.
- **ExecutiveOS / Console** → executive operational cognition infrastructure.
- **FairCase** → institutional equity continuity infrastructure.
- **CFO** → governance-safe financial operational cognition.
- **Platform packages** → cognition substrate.
- **Knowledge / memory** → institutional memory infrastructure.

All converge to the same doctrine: **governance-safe institutional operational cognition infrastructure**.

## 5. Validation

Cross-app consistency is validator-enforced via `node tooling/scripts/validate-institutional-cognition-convergence.mjs` and supplemented by `node tooling/scripts/validate-labor-continuity-governance.mjs` for the UE labor surface.
