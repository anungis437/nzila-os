# CFO Cognition Realignment

> Canonical realignment of CFO intelligence surfaces toward **governance-safe financial operational cognition**.

## 1. Convergence Statement

CFO surfaces are **bounded financial operational cognition** — they interpret, contextualize, stabilize, and support cadence. They do **not** simulate autonomous financial leadership.

## 2. Refactor Targets

- Treasury intelligence → bounded treasury continuity interpretation.
- Collections intelligence → bounded collections continuity support.
- Forecasting intelligence → bounded operational forecasting; not authoritative.
- Financial recommendations → bounded financial suggestions with explicit human approval of record.
- Operational finance guidance → calm, cadence-preserving financial cognition.
- Advisory AI → **operational financial cognition** (renamed in surface).
- AI Insights → **financial operational interpretations** (renamed in surface).

## 3. Required Surface Behavior

Every CFO intelligence surface must:

- include the canonical phrasing: **"Bounded institutional interpretation; final authority remains with accountable financial operators."**
- expose the human reviewer of record on any decision-affecting output
- avoid AI-CFO framing
- avoid autonomous financial-leadership framing
- avoid optimization-maximalist framing

## 4. Document Intelligence Specifics

`apps/cfo/lib/document-intelligence.ts` is internal extraction infrastructure. It must:

- present extracted fields with confidence bounds
- never present extractions as authoritative without human review
- log all extraction decisions for audit

## 5. Authority
