# Procurement & Governance Positioning Refactor

> Canonical procurement positioning for Nzila OS.

## 1. Convergence Statement

Nzila OS is positioned for procurement as **governance-safe institutional cognition infrastructure**.

## 2. Required Positioning

Position Nzila as:

- continuity-safe
- governance-safe
- operationally assistive
- anti-surveillance
- legitimacy-preserving
- institutionally stabilizing
- cognition-safe
- procurement-safe
- difficult to commoditize
- difficult to replicate coherently
- category-defining

## 3. Explicitly Avoid

Procurement collateral, decks, RFP responses, partner positioning, and external messaging must avoid:

- AI replacement framing
- autonomous intelligence framing
- optimization AI framing
- workforce AI framing
- copilot framing
- chatbot framing
- "AI-first" framing

## 4. Required Procurement Disclosures

Every procurement-facing intelligence claim must include:

- the bounded-interpretation posture
- the explicit anti-surveillance posture
- the human-authority-preserving posture
- the institutional continuity posture

## 5. Authority

This positioning derives from the [Institutional Operational Cognition Doctrine](institutional-operational-cognition-doctrine.md).
