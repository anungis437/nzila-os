# Doctrine-Compatible Product Expansion Framework

> **Status:** Canonical governance · **Layer:** Cross-product expansion governance · **Inherits:** [cross-vertical-doctrine-mapping.md](../nzila-ip/cross-vertical-doctrine-mapping.md), [doctrine-vs-implementation-boundary.md](../nzila-ip/doctrine-vs-implementation-boundary.md), [institutional-modernization-methodology.md](../nzila-ip/institutional-modernization-methodology.md)

This document specifies how new Nzila products — verticals, suites, modules, or institutional offerings — **inherit, express, and remain accountable to doctrine**.

It exists because growth is the most common drift vector: a new vertical introduced without doctrinal grounding silently relocates Nzila's center of gravity and erodes cross-product portability.

A new product is not an opportunity to deviate from doctrine. It is an opportunity to demonstrate doctrine's reach.

---

## 1. Posture

Every new Nzila product:

- **Inherits invariants** — the foundational doctrine layer is identical across products
- **Expresses adaptation** — vertical context is expressed in adaptation layers, never by redefining doctrine
- **Adopts the ontology** — continuity, governance, role, signal, and posture vocabulary is consistent
- **Meets cross-product UX expectations** — calmness, density, role-centering, restraint
- **Honors operational legitimacy** — pilot discipline, deployment governance, modernization pacing
- **Earns its place** — by serving an institutional category compatible with doctrine

A vertical that requires doctrinal exception is not a Nzila vertical.

---

## 2. Doctrine Inheritance

A new product inherits, by construction:

### 2.1 Continuity Ontology
Continuity posture is multi-dimensional; signals carry aggregation stance; surfaces honor exposure mapping. (See [continuity-ontology.md](../nzila-ip/continuity-ontology.md), [continuity-signals-taxonomy.md](../nzila-ip/continuity-signals-taxonomy.md).)

### 2.2 Governance-Safe Intelligence
Outputs are interpretive, explainable, authority-preserving. AI exposure follows [continuity-safe-ai-governance.md](continuity-safe-ai-governance.md).

### 2.3 Role-Centered Architecture
Surfaces center on role-bearer continuity, not module inventory. First encounter exposes operational center-of-gravity. (See [role-centered-continuity-architecture.md](../nzila-ip/role-centered-continuity-architecture.md).)

### 2.4 Operational Legitimacy
Pilot discipline, deployment governance, stakeholder visibility, reversibility, modernization pacing. (See [operational-legitimacy-framework.md](../nzila-ip/operational-legitimacy-framework.md), [continuity-safe-deployment-governance.md](continuity-safe-deployment-governance.md).)

### 2.5 Anti-Surveillance Posture
No individual behavioral resolution, no covert escalation, no productivity scoring.

### 2.6 Calmness Contract
Density budgets, motion restraint, layout stability, notification governance. (See [institutional-design-governance.md](institutional-design-governance.md).)

### 2.7 Doctrine Vocabulary
Doctrinal terms are used consistently; product-specific synonyms do not displace doctrinal vocabulary.

These inheritances are not adoption choices. They are conditions for the product to be a Nzila product.

---

## 3. Modernization Compliance

A new product modernizes its vertical under doctrinal pacing:

- Continuity-safe sequencing
- Pilot discipline as a structural property
- Reversibility as a release property
- Stakeholder trust preserved across change
- Concurrent change kept within absorption ceilings

A vertical whose competitive landscape pressures faster pacing must adapt — without violating doctrine. Doctrine bounds product strategy; product strategy does not bound doctrine.

---

## 4. UX Expectations

Every Nzila product satisfies the cross-product UX expectations:

- Role-centered first encounter
- Calmness floors per surface class
- Density budgets per surface class
- Multi-dimensional posture rendering
- Authority framing on consequential surfaces
- Rationale availability for surfaced signals
- Stable doctrinal vocabulary
- Restrained dashboards
- Pilot scope visible where applicable
- Cross-isolation honored where applicable

A surface that meets product needs but violates these expectations is restructured before launch.

---

## 5. Legitimacy Expectations

Every product launches with:

- Identified executive role-bearers and governance forums
- Documented stakeholder populations and isolation requirements
- A pilot plan honoring pilot discipline
- A deployment plan honoring deployment governance
- A retention and aggregation policy honoring memory governance
- A doctrine compliance review verdict on launch surfaces
- An architectural review verdict on platform integration

Launches that omit any of these are deferred.

---

## 6. Cross-Product Consistency

Cross-product consistency is verified by:

- Shared platform packages encoding invariants used by every product
- Cross-product contract tests
- Periodic doctrinal vocabulary audit
- Periodic UX consistency audit
- Periodic narrative consistency audit (under [investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md](investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md))

A product that diverges in any of these dimensions is reconverged, not exempted.

---

## 7. Application to Existing Products

This framework applies to existing Nzila products as continuing accountability:

- **Union Eyes** — labor and representation; doctrine-aligned with role-centered representation surfaces, aggregation stance, executive cognitive governance, anti-surveillance posture
- **FairCase** — case and matter governance; doctrine-aligned with cross-isolation, authority preservation, interpretive intelligence
- **ExecutiveOS** — executive continuity reading; doctrine-aligned with executive cognitive governance, dashboard restraint, governance-cadence pacing
- **Veridian** — clinical and care; doctrine-aligned with stakeholder isolation (clinical vs. workforce), authority preservation, anti-surveillance posture
- **Future products** — doctrine-aligned by inheritance; reviewed at architectural and compliance layers

Each existing product is periodically re-reviewed against doctrine; drift is corrected.

---

## 8. New Vertical Introduction Procedure

Introducing a new vertical follows:

1. **Doctrinal compatibility assessment** — does the vertical sit within the institutional categories doctrine serves?
2. **Adaptation mapping** — which doctrine layers are adapted and how
3. **Architectural review** — platform integration, shared package usage, isolation boundaries
4. **Surface design under design governance**
5. **AI governance review** for any AI capability
6. **Deployment governance plan**
7. **Doctrine compliance review** on launch surfaces
8. **Narrative alignment review** for external materials
9. **Pilot exposure under pilot discipline**
10. **Post-launch doctrine audit** within a doctrinally specified horizon

Verticals that fail doctrinal compatibility (step 1) are not introduced.

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns

- **Vertical-driven doctrine drift** — modifying doctrine to accommodate a vertical
- **Per-product UX dialects** — visual or interaction patterns that diverge from cross-product calmness, density, and restraint standards
- **Vertical surveillance affordances** — vertical-specific behavioral resolution (e.g., "this vertical needs scoring")
- **Per-product AI exposure** — AI exposure outside the AI governance regime "because the vertical demands it"
- **Naming dialects** — per-vertical synonyms for doctrinal vocabulary
- **Calendar-driven vertical launch** — launching to capture market timing without doctrinal compatibility
- **Marketing-driven category leak** — describing a vertical in incumbent-category language

---

## 10. Discipline

Expansion is where doctrine is tested by ambition. The institution that can grow without drifting is the institution that endures.

This framework is the test. Verticals that pass it strengthen Nzila's institutional standing. Verticals that would fail it are not pursued.
