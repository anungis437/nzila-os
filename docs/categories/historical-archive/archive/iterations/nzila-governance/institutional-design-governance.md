# Institutional Design Governance

> **Status:** Canonical governance · **Layer:** Experience governance · **Inherits:** [executable-experience-governance.md](../nzila-ip/executable-experience-governance.md), [executive-cognitive-governance.md](../nzila-ip/executive-cognitive-governance.md), [role-centered-continuity-architecture.md](../nzila-ip/role-centered-continuity-architecture.md)

This document codifies the **UX and experience governance standards** that operationalize doctrine into the design layer. It is the contract every Nzila surface — across every product, vertical, and stakeholder population — must satisfy.

Where the [doctrine compliance review framework](doctrine-compliance-review-framework.md) defines the *procedure* for reviewing surfaces, this document defines the *standards* the surfaces must meet.

---

## 1. Governing Posture

The Nzila experience posture is:

- **Calm by construction**, not by aesthetic
- **Continuity-safe**, not productivity-coercive
- **Role-centered**, not module-centered
- **Interpretive**, not assertive
- **Restrained**, not maximalist
- **Invisible where appropriate**, never opaque where consequence exists

A Nzila surface should leave a role-bearer feeling steadier, not faster.

---

## 2. Continuity-Safe UX Principles

### 2.1 First Encounter Is Centered, Not Comprehensive
The first surface a role-bearer meets exposes their **operational center-of-gravity** — the small set of continuity exposures that define their role. It does not show capability inventory, navigation tree, or product surface area.

### 2.2 Density Is Budgeted
Every surface has an explicit **density budget** measured in:

- Distinct interactive regions
- Distinct information classes
- Concurrent state indicators
- Concurrent affordances

Budgets vary by surface class (executive, governance, role-bearer, operator, public). They are not ornamental targets — they are review-enforced standards.

### 2.3 Hierarchy Is Doctrinal
Visual hierarchy reflects continuity hierarchy: posture and stake first, signal and evidence second, action third. Hierarchy never amplifies novelty over significance.

### 2.4 Motion Is Quiet
Motion expresses state transitions and confirms acts; it does not entertain, decorate, or signal vitality. Default motion is conservative; non-default motion requires design review.

### 2.5 Layout Is Stable
Layouts do not shift under live updates. Async arrivals occupy reserved zones. Stability is an institutional trust signal.

### 2.6 Stable Vocabulary
Doctrinal vocabulary is consistent across surfaces, products, and verticals. Synonym proliferation is a governance defect.

---

## 3. Calmness Standards

Calmness is measurable. The following are the floor standards for any doctrine-touching surface.

| Standard | Floor |
|----------|-------|
| Distinct color hues per surface | ≤ small bounded set defined per surface class |
| Concurrent state indicators per primary view | bounded; further indicators require disclosure |
| Real-time-style feeds on executive surfaces | none |
| Notification arrivals per unit time per role-bearer | bounded by notification governance |
| Animation looping | none on doctrine-touching surfaces |
| Color-only meaning | prohibited; meaning carries through type and form |
| Urgency framing without continuity stake | prohibited |

Calmness floors are tightened — never loosened — for executive, governance, and reading surfaces.

---

## 4. Executive Cognitive Load Thresholds

Executive surfaces are bound by additional thresholds (see also [executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md](executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md)):

- Reading time to surface posture: short, bounded, doctrinally specified
- Action paths from posture to governance act: short, predictable
- Refresh cadence: governance-aligned, not data-aligned
- Dismissal/acknowledgment: meaningful, not click-through
- Page count to comprehensive posture: doctrinally minimal

Exceeding any threshold is a governance defect, not a cosmetic miss.

---

## 5. Density Rules

### 5.1 Default Posture Is Less
Less, with greater interpretive density, is the default. Maximal-density layouts are off-doctrine for governance-touching surfaces.

### 5.2 Disclosure Is Mandatory for Depth
Depth is reachable through purposeful disclosure, never through default exposure.

### 5.3 No Aggregation of Independent Concerns
Distinct continuity concerns are not co-located onto a single composite widget. Co-location is conditional on doctrinal coherence.

### 5.4 No Score Reduction
Multi-dimensional postures do not collapse into a single score for display convenience. (See [continuity-metrics-philosophy.md](../nzila-ip/continuity-metrics-philosophy.md).)

---

## 6. Navigation Standards

- Navigation is **role-canonical**: structured around the role's continuity center-of-gravity, not around product modules
- Top-level navigation is bounded; depth is reached by purposeful descent
- Navigation labels use doctrinal vocabulary
- Cross-product navigation respects isolation; surfaces from another stakeholder population are not casually adjacent
- Search is purposeful; recent-activity surfacing respects aggregation stance

---

## 7. Anti-Chaos Rules

The following are categorically off-doctrine and produce a CONDITIONAL or VIOLATION verdict at compliance review:

- Dashboard overload — concurrent multi-widget grids on executive surfaces
- Real-time activity feeds visible to executives
- Color-saturated alert walls
- Notification badges without doctrinal cause
- Unbounded list views as primary surfaces
- Score collapsing of multi-dimensional posture
- Unbounded "personalization" affordances that fragment the doctrinal surface

---

## 8. Progressive Sophistication Standards

- Day-one surfaces show role-centered essentials
- Depth, configuration, and adjacent capability are reachable but not promoted
- New capability rolls out behind doctrine compliance review and pacing governance
- Capability density grows with absorption, not with release calendar

Surfaces that promote inventory (capability lists, "what's new" panels, achievement metrics) are off-doctrine.

---

## 9. Invisible Orchestration Principles

The mechanism behind a surface — pipelines, models, retrieval, adapters, scoring chains — is **not surfaced** to role-bearers or executives unless it is essential to interpretation.

When mechanism *is* essential (rationale, assumption, source, uncertainty), it is presented in the interpretive register, not in the engineering register.

Engineering register on doctrine-touching surfaces is off-doctrine.

---

## 10. Dashboard Restraint Principles

Dashboards exist to support **interpretation and governance acts**, not to demonstrate system breadth.

A dashboard is doctrine-aligned only if:

- Each tile expresses a doctrinally coherent continuity concern
- Tile inclusion has a stated governance purpose
- Tile density respects the surface's density budget
- Tiles do not compete for executive attention through motion, color, or urgency framing
- The dashboard does not present a composite continuity score

Speculative tiles, vanity tiles, and capability-showcase tiles are removed at review.

---

## 11. Prohibited Patterns

The following patterns are categorically prohibited on Nzila surfaces. Their presence in a submission produces a DOCTRINE VIOLATION verdict.

- **Dashboard overload** — exceeding density budget on executive or governance surfaces
- **AI hype UX** — surfacing AI capability for its own sake; "AI-powered" framing without doctrinal purpose
- **Orchestration exposure** — presenting pipelines, agents, models, or chains as a surface concept
- **Alert chaos** — unbounded alert walls or notification streams
- **Productivity coercion** — framing that pressures role-bearers toward speed, output, or compliance metrics
- **Surveillance framing** — surfacing individuals against behavioral metrics; ranking populations
- **Notification density** — arrival rates exceeding doctrinal ceilings
- **Complexity for its own sake** — interactions whose purpose is to demonstrate capability
- **Composite continuity scoring** — single-number reduction of multi-dimensional posture
- **Demo theater** — surfaces designed to impress rather than to support continuity

---

## 12. Design Review Bindings

Every doctrine-touching surface enters design review. Design review applies this document and produces a finding linked back to the [doctrine compliance review framework](doctrine-compliance-review-framework.md).

Design review may not approve a surface that:

- Exceeds density budget
- Violates a prohibited pattern
- Departs from role-canonical navigation without doctrinal rationale
- Reduces multi-dimensional posture to a single score
- Presents orchestration as a surface concept

A design reviewer is empowered to block. Their authority is the doctrine, not their seniority.

---

## 13. Discipline of This Layer

Design governance is not aesthetic governance. It is the front line where doctrine becomes felt by role-bearers and executives. Drift here is the most damaging because it is the most visible — and the most easily rationalized as taste.

Calmness is a contract. Restraint is a discipline. Both are enforced.
