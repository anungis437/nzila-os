# Master Governance Index

> **Status:** Canonical governance · **Layer:** Index · **Inherits:** all governance documents in this directory and the doctrine corpus in [../nzila-ip/](../nzila-ip/)

This index is the **single navigational map** of Nzila's operational governance layer. It enumerates each governance instrument, its purpose, its doctrinal basis, and its enforcement points.

It is the document a reviewer, auditor, regulator, procurement officer, or new team member opens first to understand how Nzila governs itself.

## 2. Governance Instruments

### 2.1 Traceability
- **[doctrine-to-product-mapping.md](doctrine-to-product-mapping.md)** — explicit map from doctrine principle to implementation expression, affected products, UX surfaces, enforcement mechanisms, and E2E linkage. The traceability spine.

### 2.2 Review Methodology
- **[doctrine-compliance-review-framework.md](doctrine-compliance-review-framework.md)** — standardized review procedure, dimensions, and verdicts for any feature, AI integration, dashboard, workflow, routing change, or modernization.
- **[architectural-doctrine-review-system.md](architectural-doctrine-review-system.md)** — architecture-level review tier for platform, cross-product, and modernization decisions.

### 2.3 Domain Governance — Experience
- **[institutional-design-governance.md](institutional-design-governance.md)** — UX standards: calmness, density, hierarchy, navigation, anti-chaos rules, dashboard restraint, prohibited patterns.
- **[executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md](executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md)** — measurable thresholds for executive surfaces: density, pacing, calmness, readability, alerting, escalation visibility.

### 2.4 Domain Governance — AI
- **[continuity-safe-ai-governance.md](continuity-safe-ai-governance.md)** — AI exposure regime: explainability minimums, human oversight, prohibited automation, anti-surveillance requirements, review procedure.

### 2.5 Domain Governance — Deployment
- **[continuity-safe-deployment-governance.md](continuity-safe-deployment-governance.md)** — release governance: rollout discipline, pilot discipline, deployment legitimacy, environment isolation, modernization pacing, rollback legitimacy.

### 2.6 Domain Governance — Narrative
- **[investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md](investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md)** — external narrative discipline: approved/prohibited language, category framing, anti-surveillance positioning, drift prevention.

### 2.7 Domain Governance — Expansion
- **[doctrine-compatible-product-expansion-framework.md](doctrine-compatible-product-expansion-framework.md)** — how new products inherit doctrine; cross-product UX, legitimacy, vocabulary expectations.

### 2.8 Executable Enforcement
- **[executable-doctrine-enforcement.md](executable-doctrine-enforcement.md)** — enforcement layers: code review, CI policy, E2E assertions, routing, visibility, feature exposure, deployment gates.

### 2.9 Measurement and Trend
- **[doctrine-governance-scorecard.md](doctrine-governance-scorecard.md)** — qualitative four-band scorecard across ten dimensions; cadence, outputs, and protections.

### 2.10 Readiness
- **[doctrine-operationalization-readiness-review.md](doctrine-operationalization-readiness-review.md)** — coverage assessment of the operational governance layer; gaps, weaknesses, maturity.
