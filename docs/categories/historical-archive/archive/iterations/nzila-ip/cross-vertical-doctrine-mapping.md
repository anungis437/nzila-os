# Cross-Vertical Doctrine Mapping

> **Status:** Canonical · **Layer:** Strategic doctrine · **Inherits:** All foundational and architectural doctrine

This document maps the **applicability** of Nzila doctrine across institutional verticals. It establishes which doctrine layers are **invariant** (binding everywhere), which are **adaptable** (specific operational expressions vary), and how vertical-specific implementations remain in conformance with the broader doctrine corpus.

This mapping is what enables Nzila to operate as a **continuity doctrine for institutions** rather than a specialist vendor of vertical software.

## 2. Vertical Coverage

The following verticals are addressed by the current doctrine and product portfolio. Each entry identifies the **center-of-gravity** of the vertical and the dominant continuity concerns to which doctrine is applied.

### 2.1 Labor / Union
- **Center-of-gravity:** representation of working populations across employer, regulatory, and political contexts
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** representational legitimacy, member relationship continuity, leadership transition under elected governance, institutional memory across multi-decade campaigns
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** stakeholder isolation, governance-safe intelligence, continuity-safe visibility

### 2.2 Governance (Public Sector, Federations)
- **Center-of-gravity:** legitimate exercise of mandated authority on behalf of constituents
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** democratic legitimacy, mandate continuity, succession across electoral cycles, multi-stakeholder accountability
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** governance-preserving transformation, executive cognitive governance, institutional memory governance

### 2.3 Legal / FairCase
- **Center-of-gravity:** dignified, structured advocacy and case lifecycle stewardship
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** matter continuity across personnel turnover, evidentiary memory integrity, governance-safe interpretation of sensitive material
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** memory integrity, aggregation stance discipline, anti-surveillance posture

### 2.4 ExecutiveOS
- **Center-of-gravity:** leadership-level interpretive surfaces across the institution
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** strategic calmness, leadership transition, institutional reading at executive density
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** executive cognitive governance, scenario intelligence, continuity metrics philosophy

### 2.5 HealthTech (Veridian and adjacent)
- **Center-of-gravity:** care-relationship continuity across providers, populations, and regulatory contexts
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** care continuity across episodes, professional memory continuity, institutional accountability
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** governance-safe intelligence, continuity-safe deployment, anti-surveillance discipline (clinical and workforce)

### 2.6 Education / NACP
- **Center-of-gravity:** continuity of learning, credentialing, and institutional educational mission
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** cohort continuity, credential integrity, educator continuity
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** institutional memory governance, role-centered architecture, modernization methodology

### 2.7 Federations and Multi-Member Bodies
- **Center-of-gravity:** alignment across member institutions while preserving each member's autonomy
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** federation-level coherence, member-institution continuity, governance across asymmetric authority
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** stakeholder isolation, cross-vertical doctrine portability, continuity field activation

### 2.8 Public-Sector Operating Bodies
- **Center-of-gravity:** mandated public service delivery under accountability constraints
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** mission continuity across political cycles, regulatory standing, public trust
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** governance-preserving transformation, operational legitimacy, executive cognitive governance

### 2.9 Associations and Professional Bodies
- **Center-of-gravity:** member continuity, standards stewardship, profession-level memory
- **Dominant continuity concerns:** standards continuity, member trust, intergenerational credibility
- **Doctrinal emphasis:** modernization methodology, memory governance, field activation

### 2.10 Adjacent Domains (Trade, Mobility, Agrimo, ABR, Cora, etc.)
- Each adjacent domain is mapped to the same invariant doctrine. Adaptation occurs only at the role-definition, signal-instance, surface-expression, and field-choreography layers.

## 3. Invariant Doctrine Layers

The following layers are **identical** across all verticals:

- The Continuity Ontology
- The Governance-Safe Intelligence stance
- The Institutional Risk Philosophy
- The Modernization Methodology
- The Executive Cognitive Governance discipline
- The Continuity Metrics Philosophy
- The aggregation-stance prohibitions
- The anti-surveillance commitments
- The human-authority statement

A vertical implementation that requires relaxation of any of these is doctrinally rejected. The vertical is not the right fit, or the implementation is wrong.

---

## 4. Adaptable Operational Layers

The following layers **adapt** in their concrete expression while preserving their structural integrity:

- Role definitions (the roles that exist vary by vertical; their architectural treatment does not)
- Signal instances (specific signals vary; the taxonomy structure does not)
- Surface expressions (concrete UIs vary; their doctrinal properties do not)
- Field choreography (events vary; activation discipline does not)
- Memory substrates (specific records vary; governance discipline does not)
- Scenario domains (continuity scenarios vary; framework hygiene does not)

Adaptation is bounded by invariants. Where adaptation appears to require violation of an invariant, the invariant wins.

---

## 5. Industry-Specific Implementation Expressions

Each vertical produces a **vertical implementation manual** (held in product/engineering documentation, not in this IP corpus) that records:

- Role canon for the vertical
- Signal canon for the vertical
- Surface canon for the vertical
- Field canon for the vertical
- Memory canon for the vertical
- Vertical-specific governance interfaces (regulatory, political, federation)

Vertical manuals **inherit from doctrine**; they do not redefine it.

---

## 6. Cross-Vertical Reuse

Cross-vertical reuse is high precisely because invariants are strong:

- A continuity reading practice developed for one vertical transfers to others with adaptation only at the signal-instance layer
- A scenario discipline developed for one vertical transfers structurally
- A memory governance discipline developed for one vertical transfers with substrate adaptation
- An executive surface designed under doctrine transfers with role redefinition

This produces compounding leverage: doctrine work in one vertical strengthens all verticals.

---

## 7. Cross-Vertical Conflict Resolution

Where vertical-specific pressures appear to conflict with doctrine:

1. Distinguish vertical adaptation from doctrinal violation
2. If adaptation, document and proceed
3. If violation, escalate to doctrine review
4. If review confirms violation, decline the requirement
5. Update the vertical implementation manual to reflect the boundary

Doctrinal coherence is non-negotiable. Vertical specificity must always remain inside it.
