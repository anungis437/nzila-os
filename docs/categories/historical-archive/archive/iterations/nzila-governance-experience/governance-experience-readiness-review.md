## 9. Unresolved embodiment risks

- **Adoption.** The experience layer is built; it must become the default operational surface to mature beyond `forming`.
- **Operator validation.** No operator validation rounds executed yet; the `forming` bandings cannot promote without findings.
- **Cross-product reach.** Embodied surfaces ship in Control Plane and Console; embedding in additional products and pilot administration remains pending.
- **Evidence experience completeness.** The evidence review journey ships as primitives + a placeholder; the bound auditor experience requires further work.
- **Design tokens propagation.** Tokens are exported as semantic identifiers; the Tailwind theme mapping has not yet been hardened across every host app.

## 10. Discipline

A `forming` banding is not a failure. Promotion requires evidence — operator findings, cross-product embedding, and design system adoption — not self-assessment.
