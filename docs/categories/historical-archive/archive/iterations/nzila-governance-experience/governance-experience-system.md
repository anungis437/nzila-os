# Governance Experience System

> **Status:** Canonical governance experience · **Layer:** Architecture · **Inherits:** [governance-operations-dashboard-system.md](../nzila-governance-operations/governance-operations-dashboard-system.md)

## 1. Objective

Define the canonical governance experience architecture: the journeys, pacing, interpretation language, and operational embodiment of governance for human operators.

## 2. Operational journeys

| Journey | Audience |
|---|---|
| Governance posture review | Governance officers, executives |
| Continuity posture review | Continuity reviewers, executives |
| Attestation review | Auditors, governance officers |
| Deployment legitimacy review | Deployment reviewers, platform leadership |
| Stabilization review | Continuity reviewers, platform operators |
| Executive governance briefing | Executive leadership |
| Pilot governance review | Pilot operators, governance forum |

## 3. Journey pacing

- Journeys are designed for **slow** consumption. Default reading time per surface is at least 90 seconds.
- Each surface presents one decision at a time.
- Refresh cadence is governed by the operations layer (60s / 5min). Journeys never auto-advance.

## 4. Interpretation UX

- Every band, verdict, and signal is paired with a single sentence in institutional voice.
- Language never escalates on repeat.
- Words like "critical", "urgent", "emergency" are reserved for verified blocking events.

## 5. Required principles

- Calmness over completeness.
- Sparseness over density.
- Readability over capability.
- Cognitive sustainability over visibility maximization.
- Institutional legitimacy over operational reactivity.

## 6. Discipline

A governance experience succeeds when a non-engineer can complete a review without orientation and feels well-served. If a journey requires training, it is too dense. If a journey induces anxiety, it is too loud.
