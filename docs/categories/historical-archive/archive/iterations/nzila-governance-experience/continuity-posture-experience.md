# Continuity Posture Experience

> **Status:** Canonical governance experience · **Layer:** Continuity · **Inherits:** [continuity-posture-review-system.md](../nzila-governance-operations/continuity-posture-review-system.md)

## 1. Objective

Transform continuity posture systems into a living operational interface — interpretive, never scoring-oriented, always system-scoped.

## 2. Required implementation

- Posture summaries — one card per continuity dimension.
- Continuity narratives — short institutional sentences.
- Stabilization interpretation — advisory text per banding.
- Onboarding continuity panels — system-scoped pathway integrity.
- Governance friction review panels — banded friction across acts.
- Modernization pacing views — banded pacing across the rollout corridor.

## 3. Posture

Continuity posture must remain **interpretive**:

- No numeric continuity scores.
- No team rankings.
- No person-resolving content.
- No accelerationist guidance.

## 4. Stabilization guidance

Each banding maps to a calm advisory phrase (provided by `@nzila/continuity-review`):

- `stable` → maintain cadence.
- `warming` → extend cadence by one cycle.
- `concerning` → reduce rollout density.
- `destabilizing` → pause non-essential changes.

## 5. Discipline

A continuity posture experience succeeds when reviewers leave with a calm understanding of institutional continuity. If reviewers leave resolved to push harder, the experience has failed.
