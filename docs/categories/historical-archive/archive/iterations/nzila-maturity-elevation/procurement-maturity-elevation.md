# Procurement Maturity Elevation

> Finalize procurement-grade maturity posture.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The ecosystem must increasingly feel **procurement-native** — governance-safe, anti-surveillance, continuity-preserving, and operationally sustainable.

## 2. Procurement Posture Targets

- Trust posture
- Governance posture
- Cognition posture
- Anti-surveillance posture
- Operational sustainability posture
- Continuity-safe modernization posture

## 3. Required Procurement Disclosures

Every procurement-facing intelligence claim must include:

- bounded-interpretation posture
- explicit anti-surveillance posture
- human-authority-preserving posture
- institutional continuity posture
- operational sustainability posture

## 4. Forbidden Procurement Framings

Procurement collateral, decks, RFPs, partner positioning, and external messaging must avoid:

- AI replacement framing
- autonomous intelligence framing
- optimization AI framing
- workforce AI framing
- copilot framing
- chatbot framing
- "AI-first" framing

## 5. Procurement Surfaces Posture

- Trust center surfaces, executive summaries, governance summaries, and onboarding summaries must reflect the canonical procurement positioning.
