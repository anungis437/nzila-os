# Cross-App UX Maturity Convergence

> Canonical UX convergence canon across UE, Console, ExecutiveOS, UE Ops, CFO, and FairCase.

## 1. Convergence Statement

The ecosystem must feel **singular and institutionally coherent**. UX, navigation, cadence, governance grouping, and operational grammar must converge across apps.

## 2. Canonical UX Postures

- **Tone**: calm, infrastructural, continuity-oriented.
- **Layout**: governance grouping over feature grouping.
- **Navigation rhythm**: predictable across UE, Console, ExecutiveOS, CFO, UE Ops, FairCase.
- **Operational grammar**: bounded interpretation; human reviewer of record; explicit escalation cues.
- **Spacing & transitions**: stable, non-anxiogenic; avoid attention-capture mechanics.

## 3. Required Convergence Targets

- Navigation rhythm
- Operational IA
- Governance grouping
- Review flows
- Rollout flows
- Onboarding flows
- Cognition surfaces
- Escalation flows
- Stabilization summaries

## 4. Required Cross-App Patterns

### 4.1 Disclaimer Pattern

Every interpretive surface must include one of the canonical disclaimers:

- "Interpretive support only; final authority remains with accountable human operators."
- "Bounded institutional interpretation; final authority remains with accountable [domain] operators."

### 4.2 Escalation Pattern

Every interpretive surface must surface a discoverable escalation path or human reviewer of record.

### 4.3 Continuity Pattern

Every interpretive surface should reference continuity (continuity-safe, continuity briefing, continuity inquiry, continuity interpretation, etc.).
