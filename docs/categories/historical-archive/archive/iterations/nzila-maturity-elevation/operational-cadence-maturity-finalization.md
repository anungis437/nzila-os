# Operational Cadence Maturity Finalization

> Cadence must remain **sustainable and calming**.

## 1. Convergence Statement

Operational cadence must be readable, sustainable, and calm. Cadence is not engagement; it is institutional rhythm.

## 2. Cadence Targets

- Review cadence
- Onboarding cadence
- Rollout cadence
- Stabilization cadence
- Executive cadence
- Governance review cadence

## 3. Cadence Maturity Invariants

- **Operational sustainability**: cadence load on operators is bounded and predictable.
- **Cadence fatigue prevention**: surfaces avoid noisy alerting, attention capture, or repeated low-signal notifications.
- **Cadence readability**: each cadence surface is legible at a glance.
- **Cadence calmness**: no anxiogenic UX patterns.
- **Cadence governance coherence**: cadence supports — does not displace — governance review.

## 4. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- engagement-optimization cadence
- attention-capture cadence
- pop-up notification surfacing on cognition surfaces
- repeated unactioned notifications
- cadence that reads as authoritative judgment rather than interpretive support
