# Product Expansion Strategy

## Principle

Expand depth before breadth.

## Sequence

1. Convert Tier 1 pilots into repeatable paid implementations
2. Lift Tier 2 products using shared platform capabilities proven in Tier 1
3. Only re-open Tier 4 candidates with named demand and funded pipeline

## Gating Conditions for Expansion

- Repeatable onboarding process
- Support burden within target envelope
- Verified buyer trust package completeness
- Positive conversion trend in pilot cohorts
