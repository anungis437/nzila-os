# Three-Year Growth Narrative

## Year 1: Conversion Discipline

- Focus on converting Tier 1 pilots into repeatable paid deployments.
- Establish implementation excellence and support reliability baselines.

## Year 2: Strategic Expansion

- Expand Tier 2 products where platform leverage shortens delivery cycles.
- Increase cross-sell from existing flagship customer base.

## Year 3: Scale and Selective New Bets

- Scale proven products with operational consistency.
- Re-open frozen or incubated bets only if evidence-backed demand exists.
