# Risk Register and Mitigations

## Risk 1: Pilot Conversion Risk

- Description: pilots do not convert at expected rate
- Mitigation: stricter qualification, clearer success criteria, tighter implementation playbooks

## Risk 2: Support Burden Growth

- Description: support load scales faster than product maturity
- Mitigation: enforce support tiers, increase automation, instrument churn-risk signals

## Risk 3: Portfolio Dilution

- Description: too many products absorb capital and operator attention
- Mitigation: freeze/archive/merge policy and explicit execution model gates

## Risk 4: Trust and Procurement Delays

- Description: missing buyer documentation slows enterprise deals
- Mitigation: maintain trust package and questionnaire starter pack as controlled artifacts

## Risk 5: Platform Dependency Concentration

- Description: shared platform incident impacts multiple products
- Mitigation: incident response discipline, DR readiness, and staged release controls
