# Portfolio Overview

Nzila OS is a governed multi-product software platform serving labour, commerce, agriculture, and operations across emerging markets.

## Flagship Products (Tier 1 — Sell Now)

### Union Eyes

**Labour Representation & Case Management**

Purpose: End-to-end case management for unions, labour boards, and worker advocacy organizations. Manages grievances, arbitrations, collective bargaining workflows, and compliance tracking.

- Status: Pilot with active contracting
- Deployment: Azure Container Apps (Canada Central)
- Primary buyer: Executive Director / Chief People Officer
- Gross margin: 82%
- Proof level: Pilot-proof with evidence packs

### FairCase

**Justice & Equity Governance**

Purpose: Enterprise compliance and justice workflow automation for organizations managing equity, accommodation, and fairness obligations.

- Status: Pilot with active contracting
- Primary buyer: Chief People Officer
- Gross margin: 80%
- Proof level: Pilot-proof with evidence packs

### Flow

**SMB Operations & Commerce Automation**

Purpose: Operational backbone for small and mid-size businesses — invoicing, scheduling, client management, and workflow automation.

- Status: Pilot with active contracting
- Primary buyer: Operations Lead
- Gross margin: 79%
- Proof level: Demo-ready
