## What the Pilot Delivers

A fully operational deployment of Union Eyes — configured for your collective agreement structure, member count, and steward network — live within 14 days of contract signature.

### Included in the 90-Day Pilot

**Track A — Grievance Management & Case Intelligence**

- Grievance intake form configured to your CA step structure
- Automated deadline tracking with rep notifications
- Case file management with document upload and version history
- Arbitration-ready evidence bundle export (one click)
- AI-assisted pattern detection across your grievance history
- Reporting dashboard: cycle times, resolution rates, rep workload

**Track B — Member Communications + Governance**
*(Optional add-on — may run concurrently with Track A or in month 2)*

- Member communication hub: announcements, targeted messages, push notifications
- Governance module: motion tracking, meeting minutes, voting records
- Elections administration: nomination management, secure balloting, result certification

### Pilot Configuration

- Up to 5,000 members
- Up to 20 rep/steward user accounts
- 2 executive/administrator accounts
- Dedicated Canada Central Azure tenant (no shared infrastructure with other organizations)
- Bilingual (English/French) — all member-facing communications

## Why CUPE Specifically

Union Eyes was built in direct partnership with CUPE locals. The vocabulary, the workflow, the reporting cadences — all were shaped by CUPE reps describing what they actually needed. This is not a generic case management tool with "union" labels. It knows what a Step 3 is, what a ULP filing looks like, and what "failure to bargain in good faith" means as a case classification.

CUPE locals currently on the pilot program include healthcare, municipal services, and post-secondary units across Ontario and British Columbia.
