## Outreach Formula

Every first message should answer these three things in under 100 words:

1. What the platform is (one sentence, no jargon)
2. Why it is relevant to them specifically (one phrase)
3. What we are asking for (a 20-minute conversation, no pressure)

**Template A — Cold (no mutual connection):**

> We built a governed workflow and case management platform purpose-built for labour unions and member organizations — grievance tracking, evidence management, rep coordination, and officer-level analytics in one place.
>
> Given [ORGANIZATION]'s work in [SECTOR / REGION], I thought you might be the right person to have a short conversation with. We're looking for 1–2 design partner organizations for a controlled pilot starting this summer.
>
> Would a 20-minute call make sense? Happy to send a one-pager first.

**Template B — Warm (via intro or known contact):**

> [MUTUAL CONTACT] suggested I reach out. We've built a governance and case management platform specifically for unions — [ORGANIZATION]'s context around [SPECIFIC CONTEXT] seemed like a strong match.
>
> We're offering a limited design partner pilot to a small number of organizations. Worth a 20-minute call?

## Stage Definitions

| Code | Stage | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| `0-RESEARCH` | Research | Identified; contact not yet found or message not yet drafted |
| `1-READY` | Ready to send | Contact found, message drafted, not sent |
| `2-SENT` | Outreach sent | First message sent; awaiting response |
| `3-REPLIED` | Response received | Prospect responded (positive, neutral, or negative) |
| `4-CALL-SCHEDULED` | Call scheduled | Readiness briefing booked |
| `5-PROPOSAL` | Proposal sent | Pilot tier proposal shared |
| `6-PILOT-ACTIVE` | Pilot active | Signed agreement, environment provisioned |
| `7-CONVERTED` | SaaS customer | Pilot completed, annual agreement signed |
| `8-DECLINED` | Declined | Passed — note reason; re-engage in 6+ months |
| `9-PAUSED` | Paused | Interested but timing not right — follow up date set |

## Warm Lead Sources to Activate

Before cold outreach, identify warm paths through:

- [ ] LinkedIn 1st-degree connections who work in labour / unions
- [ ] Former colleagues at target organizations or their peer orgs
- [ ] Legal contacts — labour lawyers often advise target orgs
- [ ] Academia — labour relations faculty at York, Queen's, Osgoode
- [ ] Conference circuit — check Canadian Industrial Relations Association (CIRA) attendee lists
- [ ] Existing CUPE pilot references — use CUPE relationship as a reference for all other CUPE locals

---

## Disqualification Criteria

Do not pursue if any of the following apply:

- Organization is primarily US-based and unwilling to operate in Canadian cloud
- Active IT vendor relationship with an existing grievance system and no budget cycle in the next 12 months
- Less than 50 active members (too small for meaningful pilot KPIs)
- No executive sponsorship available (no champion identified after 2 conversations)
