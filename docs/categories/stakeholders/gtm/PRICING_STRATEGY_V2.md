## Pricing Philosophy

**Good / Better / Best framing.** Not a menu of complexity. The middle offer closes most deals.

For every buyer conversation, the goal is to have them choose between options — not evaluate a price in isolation. Pricing psychology: anchor high, make the middle look safe, make the low option look limited.

| Psychological Frame | Union Eyes | Flow |
|--------------------|-----------|------|
| **Good** (low risk, low commitment) | Discovery Sprint $7K–15K | Workflow Sprint $5K–10K |
| **Better** (recommended, most popular) | Controlled Pilot $20K–40K | Team Launch $12K–25K |
| **Best** (full value, full commitment) | Production Pilot $50K–100K+ | Enterprise Ops Plan Year 1 |

**Always present three options.** Always call the middle one the most common choice.

## Discount Policy and Anti-Negotiation Rules

### Standing Discounts

| Scenario | Discount |
|----------|---------|
| Design partner (first 3 orgs signed) | 20% off Year 1 SaaS only |
| Non-profit / registered charity | Up to 15% off SaaS |
| Multi-year commitment at signing | Per multi-year schedule above |

Maximum total discount in Year 1: **25%**. No stacking beyond this ceiling.

### Anti-Discount Rules

These rules are non-negotiable and must be internalized before every negotiation:

1. **Never discount the pilot fee.** It covers delivery cost. If it doesn't cover delivery cost, the scope is wrong.
2. **Price cuts are the last lever.** Before discounting, try: scope reduction, phased delivery, payment flexibility.
3. **"It's too expensive" usually means "I don't see the value yet."** Respond by restating ROI, not by lowering the number.
4. **If a prospect insists on a discount without a design-partner qualification, the answer is no.** Offer scope reduction instead.
5. **Monthly billing is available at +15% premium.** It is not a discount; it is a convenience fee.
6. **Do not reveal the top of the pricing band first.** Lead with the middle of the range. High anchoring invites negotiation downward from the ceiling.

### Negotiation Response Scripts

**"Your price is too high."**
> "Let me understand what's driving that — is it the upfront investment, or uncertainty about the annual value? If it's the upfront, we have a phased option. If it's the annual value, let me walk through what the typical ROI looks like for an organization your size."

**"Can we do a free trial?"**
> "We don't run free trials — the pilot is the mechanism for that. The reason pilots are paid is that they produce a documented outcome report, real case data, and a governance proof pack. That takes real delivery work. The Discovery Sprint at [price] gets you the confidence you need without a production commitment."

**"We need X% off."**
> "I want to make this work. Let me check if you qualify for our design partner rate — we give the first three organizations in the program 20% off Year 1 as a reference partnership. That requires a brief case study commitment at the end, if results are positive. Does that work?"
