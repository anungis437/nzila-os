# OCI Stabilization Framework™

**Status:** Canonical doctrine for Product 3 of the OCI product ladder — the Diagnostic & Stabilization Program™.

**Audience:** Internal stewards of the method, certified facilitators, sponsoring institutions evaluating Product 3, governance bodies receiving a stabilization engagement.

**Convention:** "Product 3" refers to the Diagnostic & Stabilization Program. "The framework" refers to the Stabilization Framework. "The institution" refers to any organisation engaging Product 3.

---

## 1. Purpose of the framework

The Stabilization Framework defines the canonical shape of Product 3 engagements. It exists so that every facilitated stabilization engagement is delivered against a stable, reviewable spec — not a consulting improvisation.

The framework holds a single commitment: an institution that has had a continuity exposure recognised and mapped (Products 1 and 2) must be offered a disciplined way to act on that recognition without displacing the stewards who carry it.

The framework is not a transformation programme. It is not a restructuring methodology. It is not change management. It is the disciplined practice of reducing institutional fragility while preserving the dignity, recognition, and continuity standing of every steward already in place.

---

## 2. Position relative to Products 1 and 2

Product 1 (Institutional Recognition) opens the recognition relationship. Product 2 (Governance Entropy Workbook™) produces the institution's continuity reading.

Product 3 begins only when:

- Product 2 has been completed and ratified by the institution's governance body, and
- The institution has acknowledged at least one continuity exposure it intends to act on, and
- The Stabilization Readiness Thresholds (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md)) are met or the institution has accepted a recognition-only extension instead.

Where readiness is not yet present, Product 3 is deferred. The institution is offered a Product 2 deepening or a recognition-only continuation. Honest non-engagement is a successful method outcome.

---

## 3. Scope boundaries

Product 3 acts within explicit boundaries. The framework does not:

- Adjudicate personnel matters, performance, or compensation,
- Resolve labour disputes or grievances,
- Replace legal, regulatory, or auditor counsel,
- Restructure governance bodies,
- Recommend dismissals, role eliminations, or organisational redesign,
- Produce ranked comparisons between stewards or between institutions,
- Continue once institutional consent is withdrawn.

The framework acts only on continuity exposures the institution has explicitly named and explicitly accepted as in scope.

## 4. The framework's structural elements

The framework is composed of seven structural elements. Each is documented in its own canonical reference.

1. **Intervention Model** — methodology, sequencing, facilitator role, reversibility, deferral. See [OCI_INTERVENTION_MODEL.md](OCI_INTERVENTION_MODEL.md).
2. **Principles** — binding doctrinal principles enforced across the engagement. See [OCI_STABILIZATION_PRINCIPLES.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_PRINCIPLES.md).
3. **Lifecycle** — five-phase delivery lifecycle (Recognition → Mapping → Stabilization → Operationalization → Intelligence). See [OCI_STABILIZATION_LIFECYCLE.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_LIFECYCLE.md).
4. **Severity Model** — five ordinal severity bands across six structural dimensions; governs escalation gravity, facilitator response, and engine signal severity assignment. See [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md).
5. **Continuity Debt** — signature OCI executive vocabulary for accrued institutional fragility and its categories. See [OCI_CONTINUITY_DEBT.md](OCI_CONTINUITY_DEBT.md).
6. **Readiness Thresholds** — five readiness conditions that gate engagement entry; defines deferral discipline. See [OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md).
7. **Intervention Ethics** — binding ethical frame for the intervention layer. See [OCI_INTERVENTION_ETHICS.md](OCI_INTERVENTION_ETHICS.md).

Together these seven elements are the framework. A reader who reads each in order will hold the full doctrine surface of Product 3.

---

## 5. Engagement modes

The framework supports three engagement modes. The mode is set at engagement open and may only be narrowed during delivery, never broadened without a new ratification.

- **Entry Stabilization** — short, narrowly scoped engagement focused on a single continuity exposure the institution has named.
- **Enterprise Stabilization Programme** — full-lifecycle engagement across multiple continuity exposures, governance survivability, and stewardship redistribution.
- **Stewardship Retainer** — ongoing continuity advisory cadence following a completed engagement.

Commercial terms for each mode are documented separately in [OCI_STABILIZATION_COMMERCIALIZATION.md](OCI_STABILIZATION_COMMERCIALIZATION.md). The commercial document is downstream of the framework, not the reverse.

---

## 6. The framework's stance

The framework holds an explicit institutional stance:

- **Recognition precedes intervention.** No stabilization move is offered before the institution has been recognised on its own terms.
- **Reduction precedes addition.** The framework reduces institutional fragility before it adds any new governance structure.
- **Stewards are preserved.** The framework does not act against the standing of any steward already in place.
- **Deferral is a method outcome.** An engagement that ends with honest non-engagement is a successful application of the framework.
- **Reversibility is a precondition.** Every stabilization move retains an explicit reversal path until the institution ratifies it.

These commitments are not aspirational. They are enforceable. A facilitator who breaches them is no longer practising Product 3 and is referred to recertification review under the OCI Method™.

---

## 7. The framework's relation to the OCI Method™

The framework is a downstream specialisation of the OCI Method™. The Method's five phases (see [OCI_METHOD.md](../../OCI_METHOD.md) §2) remain canonical. The framework expresses the Method's third phase (Stabilization) and adjacent phases (Operationalization, Intelligence) as a delivered product.

Where the framework and the Method diverge in language, the Method governs vocabulary and the framework governs delivery shape.
