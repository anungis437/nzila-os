# OCI Stabilization Commercialization

**Status:** Canonical doctrine. Names the commercial posture of Product 3, the entry- and enterprise-tier engagement ranges, the Product 4 retainer transition, and the Readiness-gated discipline that binds every commercial conversation. Binding for the certification body, facilitators authorised to scope engagements, and internal stewards of the framework's commercial surface.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators authorised to scope engagements, the certification body, executive sponsors evaluating engagement cost, governance bodies reviewing the framework's commercial posture, internal stewards of Product 4 transition.

---

## 1. Purpose

OCI Stabilization Commercialization defines the framework's commercial posture so that engagement pricing, scope, and retainer transitions remain receivable as institutional doctrine — not as sales surfaces — and so that no commercial conversation precedes a Readiness reading.

The framework's commercial posture is bounded by two non-negotiable principles:

1. **Readiness precedes pricing.** No price is offered, indicated, or implied before the Readiness reading is complete (`OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md`).
2. **Decline is honoured.** The institution may decline at any commercial surface — entry, expansion, or retainer — without consequence to its standing with the framework.

---

## 2. The two engagement tiers

Product 3 is offered in two engagement tiers. The tier is determined by the scope of stabilization the institution recognises in itself, not by the institution's budget.

### 2.1 Entry-tier engagement

**Range:** CAD 15,000 to CAD 25,000 per engagement.

**Scope:** One or two playbooks (`playbooks/README.md`), bounded to a single stabilization domain (most often `LEADERSHIP_TRANSITION_STABILIZATION.md` or `CONTINUITY_CAPTURE_SPRINT.md`).

**Duration:** Typically six to twelve weeks from the close of onboarding (`OCI_ONBOARDING_STABILIZATION.md`).

**Appropriate when:** The institution's continuity debt is concentrated in a single domain, Readiness is sufficient across all five conditions, and the institution wishes to test the framework before committing to broader work.

### 2.2 Enterprise-tier engagement

**Range:** CAD 35,000 to CAD 125,000 per engagement.

**Scope:** Three to all seven playbooks, spanning multiple stabilization domains, with cross-domain sequencing read continuously.

**Duration:** Typically four to nine months from the close of onboarding.

**Appropriate when:** The institution's continuity debt spans multiple domains, the institution has internal capacity to carry parallel stabilization moves without exhausting its stewards, and the governance body has explicitly ratified the broader scope.

A facilitator who scopes an enterprise-tier engagement against insufficient internal capacity is referred to recertification review.

---

## 3. Scope discipline

The tier band is the institution's protection, not the framework's revenue ceiling. The discipline is:

- A tier is never inflated for revenue reasons.
- A tier is never compressed for the institution's price comfort if the scope warrants the higher tier; in that case the scope is reduced to fit the lower tier, not the price increased to fit the scope.
- Scope changes mid-engagement require a written addendum to the engagement agreement and a re-read of Readiness against the expanded scope.
- An entry-tier engagement may not be silently expanded into an enterprise-tier engagement; expansion requires a new scoping conversation, a new Readiness reading, and a new agreement.

---

## 4. Product 4 retainer transition

Product 4 (continued accompaniment) is offered only at the close of a Product 3 engagement and only when Readiness conditions remain sufficient at the transition point.

**Range:** CAD 2,500 to CAD 8,500 per month, for an agreed term of six to twenty-four months.

**Scope:** Recognition-tier accompaniment for the institution's own carrying of the work it has internalised. Product 4 is not a continuation of stabilization moves.

**Annual recurring revenue posture:** Product 4 is the framework's primary annual recurring revenue surface. It is structurally bounded so that the framework's annual recurring revenue cannot depend on any single institution remaining engaged. No facilitator's recertification standing is tied to retainer retention.

**Decline is honoured at transition.** An institution that declines Product 4 at the close of Product 3 receives the same engagement closure record, the same continuity-debt retirement receipts, and the same downstream options (Product 5 contribution under `OCI_STABILIZATION_EVOLUTION.md` §6) as an institution that accepts.

---

## 5. Readiness-gated pricing

Every commercial surface is Readiness-gated:

- **No price is named before Readiness is read.** Recognition conversations and workbook prerequisite verification (Stages One and Two of onboarding) carry their own fixed fee structures named separately; no Product 3 price is named at those stages.
- **No tier is offered against insufficient Readiness.** Where Readiness is insufficient on any condition, the institution is offered the alternative continuations named in `OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md` §2 — never a discounted Product 3 engagement.
- **No retainer is offered against insufficient transition Readiness.** Where Readiness has shifted during Product 3, Product 4 is deferred until the conditions return to sufficient band.

A facilitator who indicates a price before Readiness has been read is referred to recertification review.

---

## 6. What is not commercialised

The following are structurally excluded from the framework's commercial surface:

- **Individual institution case material.** No institution's continuity work is named in marketing material without that institution's written consent and right of withdrawal (`OCI_STABILIZATION_EVOLUTION.md` §3).
- **Comparative institution rankings.** The framework does not produce or sell rankings of institutions.
- **Predictive models trained on a single institution.** Product 5 carries only field-level patterns above the k-anonymity floor (`OCI_STABILIZATION_TRACKING.md`).
- **Performance assessments of stewards or members.** No person-level material is commercialised under any product.
- **Urgency-priced engagements.** No tier is offered with a deadline-driven uplift; urgency framing is forbidden in scoping conversations (`OCI_ONBOARDING_STABILIZATION.md` §4).

---

## 7. Facilitator compensation discipline

Facilitator compensation is structured so that no facilitator has a financial incentive to:

- Advance an engagement against insufficient Readiness.
- Inflate a tier beyond the scope the institution recognises.
- Retain a retainer against the institution's interest in closure.
- Solicit Product 5 pattern contribution from an institution that has declined it.

The certification body publishes the compensation discipline annually. A facilitator who is found to have advanced any of the above is referred to recertification review and to the certification body's commercial conduct review.

---

## 8. Withdrawal posture

The institution may withdraw from any commercial surface at any time:

- **Before the engagement agreement is signed:** no cost; the institution receives its onboarding closure note.
- **During Product 3:** prorated to the stabilization moves completed; the institution receives all evidence and ratification receipts to date.
- **At the Product 4 transition:** no cost; the institution receives its engagement closure record.
- **During Product 4:** terminable on thirty days' written notice; the institution receives its accompaniment record to date.

Withdrawal is not a commercial event the framework attempts to reverse. The institution is offered a closure conversation, not a retention conversation.
