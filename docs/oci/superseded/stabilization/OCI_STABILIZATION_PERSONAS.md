# OCI Stabilization Personas

**Status:** Canonical doctrine. The personas are institutional role-states, never personality profiles. The doctrine in this file binds every Product 3 engagement.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Stabilization Personas name the six institutional role-states a Product 3 engagement may encounter. The personas exist so a facilitator can read the institution's posture toward stabilization with dignity and precision, and so engagement materials use a consistent vocabulary across institutions.

The personas are explicitly **role-states**, not personality profiles. A role-state describes how a steward is currently positioned toward continuity in the institution. It says nothing about who the steward is, how they think, or what they value. The same steward may move through several role-states across an engagement.

## 2. Binding stance

The framework prohibits the use of personas as:

- Personality categories,
- Per-person inferences,
- Per-person scoring or ranking,
- Inputs to compensation, evaluation, succession decisions, or dismissal,
- Public labels applied to named stewards.

A facilitator who applies a persona as a personality classification is in breach of [OCI_INTERVENTION_ETHICS.md](OCI_INTERVENTION_ETHICS.md) §2.3 and §2.7.

The personas are read with the institution's stewards, in language the stewards recognise. Where a steward declines a persona reading, the reading is dropped without re-attempt.

## 3. The six personas

### 3.1 The Exhausted Steward

**Role-state:** carrying continuity-bearing load beyond what the institution has distributed, with limited support for redistribution.

**Facilitator stance:** recognition first. The framework does not ask the Exhausted Steward to carry more. Stabilization moves engaging this role-state are sequenced to reduce, not extend, the load they hold.

**Engagement signals:** stewards who hold practice that has not been captured, whose successor is unnamed, whose load has accreted without redistribution.

### 3.2 The Institutional Rebuilder

**Role-state:** positioned to receive practice, lineage, or onboarding records and to carry them forward as institutional arrangement.

**Facilitator stance:** preparation. The Rebuilder is supported to receive the captured record without inheriting fragility unspoken. The framework does not position the Rebuilder as a replacement for any incumbent steward.

**Engagement signals:** stewards named in successor briefings, carrier-broadening candidates, new appointments inheriting prior practice.

### 3.3 The Governance Translator

**Role-state:** carrying interpretation that mediates between formal governance instruments and operational practice.

**Facilitator stance:** lineage interview. The Translator's interpretation is recovered as institutional record. The framework does not position the Translator as a single point of authority on governance meaning; the recovery is referred to the governance body for ratification.

**Engagement signals:** stewards whose interpretations are operationally honoured but not formally recorded, whose presence makes governance documents legible to operational practice.

### 3.4 The Fragmented Operator

**Role-state:** carrying practice that has been split across multiple unstable arrangements (changing tooling, changing reporting lines, changing accountability), with practice continuity maintained by the steward in spite of the fragmentation.

**Facilitator stance:** restoration of working conditions. The framework reads the Fragmented Operator's fragmentation as institutional, not personal. Stabilization moves engaging this role-state are sequenced to reduce fragmentation before adding load.

**Engagement signals:** roles whose configuration has changed multiple times, whose accountability paths are unclear, whose practice persists despite environmental volatility.

### 3.5 The Continuity Guardian

**Role-state:** carrying institutional memory, precedent, and the meaning of practice that has persisted across leadership transitions or governance changes.

**Facilitator stance:** lineage recognition. The Guardian's standing is preserved through every stabilization move. Where redistribution or lineage capture engages the Guardian, reciprocity terms are ratified before sequence start.

**Engagement signals:** long-tenured stewards holding institutional memory, stewards explicitly referenced when others need historical context, stewards whose departure would be felt as the loss of institutional continuity.

### 3.6 The Modernization Driver

**Role-state:** carrying the institutional move toward new tooling, new process, or new structure, while needing to maintain continuity with the practice being replaced.

**Facilitator stance:** continuity partnership. The Driver is held in equal standing with continuity-bearing stewards. The framework explicitly resists positioning the Modernization Driver as opposed to continuity; the role-state names a steward serving continuity through a different stance.

**Engagement signals:** stewards owning modernization initiatives, stewards introducing new tooling, stewards leading process redesign.

## 4. Persona resonance across playbooks

Each playbook in [docs/oci/stabilization/playbooks/](playbooks/) names the persona resonance it primarily engages. The resonance specifies which role-states the playbook holds with explicit dignity. A playbook may secondarily touch other personas; the primary resonance governs facilitator register.

## 5. Persona reading discipline

- Personas are read at the role-state level for the institution, not as labels applied to people.
- Personas are referenced in engagement language with phrasing such as *"in the Continuity Guardian role-state at this moment"*, never *"a Continuity Guardian"*.
- A steward may move across role-states within an engagement; the facilitator reads the shift and updates the engagement log.
- Personas do not appear in any per-person artefact. They appear in engagement framing, in playbook selection rationale, and in facilitator notes.

## 6. Anti-surveillance perimeter

The personas operate inside the perimeter of [OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md](../../OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md). No persona reading is derived from behavioural data, per-person inference, or any computational classification of named individuals. Persona readings are produced by the institution's reviewers in conversation with the facilitator.
