# OCI Stabilization Playbooks

**Status:** Canonical index for the seven stabilization playbooks. Each playbook is a document, not executable code. Execution is facilitator-led under the OCI Method™ and gated by the Readiness Thresholds.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

The playbooks express, in canonical form, the seven recurring stabilization shapes a Product 3 engagement may take. Each playbook:

- Is offered only when its readiness band is met,
- Engages a defined severity band,
- Names the engines whose readings inform it,
- Lists the personas (institutional role-states) whose resonance it must respect,
- Documents its deferral conditions and success criteria,
- Names its reciprocity terms.

A playbook is never "deployed". It is offered, ratified, and run by a facilitator under explicit institutional consent.

## 2. The seven playbooks

| # | Playbook | Primary debt category | Severity bands engaged |
|---|----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | [Leadership Transition Stabilization](LEADERSHIP_TRANSITION_STABILIZATION.md) | Stewardship overload + Onboarding fragility | Elevated → Institutional Fragility |
| 2 | [Governance Lineage Recovery](GOVERNANCE_LINEAGE_RECOVERY.md) | Undocumented governance + Reconstruction burden | Moderate → Critical |
| 3 | [Continuity Capture Sprint](CONTINUITY_CAPTURE_SPRINT.md) | Undocumented governance + Onboarding fragility | Elevated → Critical |
| 4 | [Stewardship Redistribution](STEWARDSHIP_REDISTRIBUTION.md) | Stewardship overload | Elevated → Institutional Fragility |
| 5 | [Onboarding Survivability Recovery](ONBOARDING_SURVIVABILITY_RECOVERY.md) | Onboarding fragility | Moderate → Critical |
| 6 | [Modernization Continuity Remediation](MODERNIZATION_CONTINUITY_REMEDIATION.md) | Modernization without preservation | Moderate → Critical |
| 7 | [Operational Reconstruction Recovery](OPERATIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION_RECOVERY.md) | Reconstruction burden | Elevated → Institutional Fragility |

The seven are deliberately kept distinct. In particular, Playbook 3 (Continuity Capture) and Playbook 7 (Operational Reconstruction) address different phases of continuity failure: Capture preserves practice that is still carried; Reconstruction recovers practice that has lapsed.

## 3. Common template

Every playbook follows the same eleven-section template. Sections are uniform across playbooks so a facilitator who has read one playbook can read another in minutes.

1. **Purpose** — the institutional outcome the playbook serves.
2. **Preconditions** — what must be in place before the playbook is offered.
3. **Readiness band** — the [OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) conditions that must be at sufficiency.
4. **Severity band** — the [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md) bands the playbook engages.
5. **Intervention sequence** — the ordered moves the playbook contains, each with reversibility profile.
6. **Persona resonance** — the [OCI_STABILIZATION_PERSONAS.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_PERSONAS.md) role-states the playbook must respect.
7. **Engine inputs** — the workbook engines whose readings inform the playbook.
8. **Facilitator guidance** — practical guidance for running the sequence with dignity.
9. **Executive briefing format** — how the playbook's outcomes are returned to the governance body.
10. **Deferral conditions** — conditions under which the playbook is deferred rather than run.
11. **Success criteria + reciprocity terms** — what success looks like, and what the institution receives in reciprocity for the stewards' contribution.

## 4. Sequencing rules across playbooks

Playbooks may be combined within a single engagement. Sequencing across playbooks follows the [OCI_INTERVENTION_MODEL.md](../OCI_INTERVENTION_MODEL.md) §3 rules, with these additional cross-playbook rules:

1. **Lineage Recovery before Reconstruction Recovery.** If both are in scope, recover lineage that is still partially carried before reconstructing practice that has lapsed.
2. **Redistribution before Onboarding Survivability.** If both are in scope, broaden carriers before deepening onboarding for any one role.
3. **Modernization Continuity Remediation before any new modernization checkpoint.** If a modernization is in flight without preservation, remediate before adding another checkpoint.
4. **Leadership Transition Stabilization may precede or follow any other.** Transitions are time-bounded; the transition window governs sequencing.

A facilitator who departs from these sequencing rules records the departure with a stated reason. Repeated departures are a recertification matter.

---

## 5. Playbook ratification

Every playbook is ratified by the institution's governance body before it is run. Ratification covers:

- The playbook's purpose,
- Its severity band engagement,
- Its persona resonance,
- The named stewards it will engage,
- Its reciprocity terms.

Ratification is signed and dated. A playbook run without ratification is a principle breach.
