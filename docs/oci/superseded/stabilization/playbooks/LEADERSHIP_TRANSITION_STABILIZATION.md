# Playbook 1 — Leadership Transition Stabilization

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook. Run only under ratified engagement and met readiness.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, executive sponsors.

---

## 1. Purpose

Preserve institutional continuity through a leadership transition by capturing the outgoing leader's interpretation, lineage, and stewardship arrangements before departure, and by preparing the incoming leader to receive that record without inheriting fragility unspoken.

## 2. Preconditions

- A leadership transition is named, dated, and acknowledged at the governance-body level.
- The outgoing leader consents to participation.
- The incoming leader (or named successor candidate) is identified.
- The engagement is operating under a ratified Product 3 contract.

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **leadership alignment** (the outgoing and incoming, plus the governance body, are aligned on engagement scope) and **operational trust conditions** (decline carries no cost).

## 4. Severity band

Engaged when steward concentration, interpretation, or continuity-bearing standing reads at **Elevated** or above (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md)). Institutional Fragility readings require governance-body co-sponsorship.

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Lineage interview with the outgoing leader.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Captures interpretation, precedent, governance memory.
2. **Stewardship cartography refresh.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Updates the cartography to reflect the transition's effects.
3. **Successor briefing pack assembly.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. Pack is returned to the institution as the canonical successor record.
4. **Successor shadowing arrangement.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. A time-bounded shadowing period is offered, not imposed.
5. **Governance-body ratification of the transition record.** Reversibility: irreversible (once ratified, the record is institutional). Requires governance body sign-off.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily engages the **Continuity Guardian** and **Institutional Rebuilder** role-states (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_PERSONAS.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_PERSONAS.md)). The outgoing leader is consistently held in the Continuity Guardian register. The incoming leader is consistently held in the Institutional Rebuilder register. Personality language is not used.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `stewardshipCartography`
- `continuityLineageEngine`
- `governanceInterpretationMatrix`
- `onboardingFragilityAnalysis`
- `continuityDependencyGraph`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- Conduct the lineage interview in a register that recognises the outgoing leader's standing. The interview is recognition first, capture second.
- Do not press the outgoing leader for material they do not wish to surface. Deferral with reasons is preferred to extraction.
- Hold the successor briefing as the institution's property from session one. The facilitator does not retain a working copy beyond engagement close.
- Refuse any request to compare the outgoing and incoming leaders. This playbook is not a comparison instrument.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A two-page brief returned to the governance body:
- Page 1: continuity record summary, ratified record location, transition readiness reading.
- Page 2: deferred items with re-open conditions, reciprocity terms honoured, residual band readings.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- The outgoing leader declines participation. Playbook is deferred; a Product 1 continuation is offered.
- The transition date is unstable. Playbook is deferred until the transition window stabilises.
- The successor is not identified. Playbook runs in lineage-capture mode only; successor sections are deferred.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a governance-ratified transition record.
- The successor (when present) has been briefed on the record.
- No steward exits the playbook with diminished standing.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- The outgoing leader receives a named record of their continuity contribution.
- The institution names the successor briefing as Product 3 deliverable.
- The governance body retains the canonical record; the facilitator severs the working copy per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
