# Playbook 2 — Governance Lineage Recovery

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

Recover the governance lineage of an institution — the interpretations, precedents, and policies-in-practice that operate as institutional fact but are not recorded in a governance-receivable form — and codify the recovered lineage as a ratified institutional record.

## 2. Preconditions

- The Product 2 governance entropy reading has surfaced lineage gaps the institution has acknowledged.
- The governance body sponsors the recovery and commits to ratifying its outputs.
- At least one current steward of the relevant governance memory consents to participation.

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **governance openness**.

## 4. Severity band

Engaged across **Moderate**, **Elevated**, **Critical**, and **Institutional Fragility** in the governance dimension (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md)). The recovery may run partially at Moderate; full recovery is typically engaged at Elevated or above.

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Lineage scoping with the governance body.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Names the interpretations and precedents in scope.
2. **Interpretation interviews with current stewards of the governance memory.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Captures intent, application history, and known boundary cases.
3. **Interpretation matrix codification.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The matrix is drafted, reviewed, and revised with the institution.
4. **Precedent reconstruction workshops.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. Where precedents have faded, the workshop reconstructs the most defensible reading with the institution.
5. **Governance-body ratification.** Reversibility: irreversible. The ratified matrix and reconstructed precedents become the institutional record.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily **Governance Translator** and **Continuity Guardian**. The stewards holding undocumented interpretation are recognised as the carriers of governance memory the institution depends on. No steward is positioned as having "withheld" information.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `governanceInterpretationMatrix`
- `continuityLineageEngine`
- `governanceEntropyEngine`
- `reconstructionBurdenAnalyzer`
- `precedentContinuityMapper`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- Frame the work as recovery of institutional record, not audit of past practice.
- Capture the steward's intent in their language first; codify second.
- Refuse to retrofit blame onto historical decisions. The matrix records what was, not what should have been.
- Where stewards disagree on the reading of a precedent, record the disagreement in the matrix and refer it to the governance body for ratification.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A three-section brief returned to the governance body:
- Section 1: ratified matrix summary with counts of recovered, reconstructed, and unresolved entries.
- Section 2: residual lineage gaps with re-open conditions.
- Section 3: reciprocity terms and the named contributors recognised in the matrix.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- The governance body declines to commit to ratification. Playbook is deferred to a Product 2 deepening.
- The stewards of the governance memory decline participation. Playbook is deferred; an alternate recovery path through documented secondary sources is offered, with explicit weakness statement.
- The scope cannot be bounded without exceeding engagement capacity. Playbook is run in a bounded subset with explicit scope record.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a ratified governance interpretation matrix.
- The matrix is governance-receivable in form.
- Residual gaps are documented with re-open conditions.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- Stewards whose memory contributed to the matrix are named as contributors.
- The institution receives the canonical record; the facilitator's working copy is severed per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
- Reciprocity moves the institution offers (e.g., recognition, succession standing) are recorded in the engagement log.
