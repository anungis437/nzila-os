# OCI Stabilization Evolution

**Status:** Canonical doctrine. Governs how Product 3 deliverables, learnings, and institutional patterns feed forward into Product 4 (continued accompaniment) and Product 5 (institutional intelligence), without ever feeding individual institutional data into commercial intelligence products.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies considering downstream products, internal stewards of Product 4 and Product 5 design, the executive sponsor reading the framework's downstream posture.

---

## 1. Purpose

OCI Stabilization Evolution defines the disciplined relationship between Product 3 (the stabilization engagement) and the downstream surfaces the framework offers: Product 4 (continued accompaniment retainers) and Product 5 (institutional intelligence shared across the field).

It exists to make explicit what evolves, what does not evolve, and what is structurally prohibited from evolving, so the institution entering a Product 3 engagement can read with confidence the downstream consequences of doing so.

---

## 2. What evolves from Product 3

Three surfaces evolve from a closed Product 3 engagement, each governed separately.

### 2.1 The institution itself

The institution that completes a Product 3 engagement carries forward: the playbook practice it has internalised, the stabilization actions whose evidence it owns (see `OCI_ACTION_SYSTEM.md`), the governance ratifications it has recorded, the lineage entries it has restored, and the continuity debt it has retired. These are the institution's property in full. The framework retains no claim on them.

### 2.2 The framework's pattern knowledge

The framework carries forward anonymised, aggregated patterns: which playbooks were applied in which institutional role-states (`OCI_STABILIZATION_PERSONAS.md`), which sequencing rules held, which Readiness conditions correlated with which stabilization trajectories, which categories of continuity debt were most receivable to retirement.

These patterns inform: facilitator recertification curricula, doctrine refinement, future doctrine documents, and the design of Product 5 intelligence offerings. They never include institution-identifying material and never include person-identifying material.

### 2.3 The facilitator's practice

The facilitator who closed the engagement carries forward their practice maturity, which is reflected in their recertification record and in their assignment eligibility for future engagements. The institution does not own the facilitator's practice; the facilitator does not own the institution's continuity work.

---

## 3. What does not evolve

The following do not evolve from a Product 3 engagement under any circumstance:

- Individual institutional case material into commercial intelligence offerings.
- Institution-identifying data into Product 5 datasets.
- Person-level data of any kind into any downstream surface.
- Comparative ranking of institutions against each other.
- Predictive models trained on a single institution's continuity work.
- Marketing material that names an institution without that institution's written consent and right of withdrawal.

A facilitator or downstream product designer who causes any of the above is referred to recertification review and, where applicable, removed from Product 5 design participation.

---

## 4. Product 4 — continued accompaniment

Product 4 is offered to institutions that have closed a Product 3 engagement and wish to retain access to facilitator accompaniment for a defined period (typically six to twenty-four months). It is not a continuation of stabilization; stabilization closes at the end of Product 3. Product 4 is recognition-tier accompaniment to support the institution's own carrying of the work it has internalised.

Product 4 is gated by the same Readiness conditions as Product 3, re-read at the point of transition. An institution whose Readiness has shifted during Product 3 may not be offered Product 4 until the conditions return to sufficient band.

The annual recurring revenue posture of Product 4 is named in `OCI_STABILIZATION_COMMERCIALIZATION.md`. Product 4 is structurally bounded so that no institution is dependent on the framework for its own continuity.

---

## 5. Product 5 — institutional intelligence

Product 5 is the framework's institutional intelligence surface. It is offered to the field — governance bodies, sector associations, public-interest researchers — as anonymised, aggregated pattern intelligence drawn from many institutions.

Product 5 is bound by the following structural commitments:

- **k-anonymity** at the field level (k=5 default, never below k=3 — same posture as `OCI_STABILIZATION_TRACKING.md`).
- **No institution-identifying material.** Aggregations are reported only where the cohort size protects identifiability.
- **No person-level material of any kind.** Product 5 does not contain person-level inputs and cannot derive them.
- **Institutional consent to inclusion.** Any institution may withdraw its anonymised pattern contribution from Product 5 at any time, without justification, and the contribution is purged within thirty days.
- **No predictive scoring.** Product 5 reports patterns; it does not score institutions, predict institutional behaviour, or rank institutions.

Product 5's design is governed separately and is not a commitment of Product 3. The Product 3 engagement closes without any obligation on the institution to contribute to Product 5.

---

## 6. The pattern contribution surface

If — and only if — the institution explicitly consents at engagement closure, anonymised patterns from its Product 3 engagement may contribute to Product 5. The consent surface is:

1. Distinct from the engagement closure note.
2. Time-limited (twelve months by default, renewable on explicit re-consent).
3. Revocable at any point, with the contribution purged within thirty days of revocation.
4. Scoped to enumerated pattern categories (the institution sees and approves the categories its contribution may be aggregated into).

An institution that declines contribution receives the same engagement closure and the same Product 4 eligibility as an institution that consents. No commercial or accompaniment posture is contingent on contribution.

---

## 7. Doctrine evolution

The framework's doctrine evolves in response to closed-engagement learning. The evolution surface is:

- Quarterly doctrine review by the certification body.
- Public publication of doctrine changes with a 90-day comment window for the field.
- Versioned doctrine documents in this repository.
- No retroactive application of doctrine changes to engagements already underway without the institution's explicit consent.

Doctrine refinement that materially changes a binding gate (Readiness, ethics, tracking) requires a 180-day comment window and ratification by the certification body before taking effect.
