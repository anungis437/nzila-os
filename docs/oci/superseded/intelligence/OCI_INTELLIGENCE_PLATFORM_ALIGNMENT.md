# OCI Intelligence — Platform Alignment

**Artifact type:** Doctrine
**Module:** OCI Intelligence Network
**Doctrine version:** 1.0.0
**Posture:** platform-coherent · reviewer-led · refusal-first

---

Product 5 must remain fully aligned with the Nzila OS platform services that
already carry continuity infrastructure. The intelligence layer reinforces
those services rather than displacing them.

| Platform layer                             | How Product 5 reinforces it                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Continuity-aware operations                | Sector baselines and trajectory readings give operations a longitudinal frame for what they already feel locally. |
| Governance-safe modernization              | Entropy drift readings reveal whether modernization is preserving or eroding governance continuity over time.     |
| Onboarding survivability                   | Survivability trajectories make it visible whether onboarding survivability is strengthening, holding, or weakening. |
| Continuity runtime systems                 | Runtime stabilisation persistence (Part 4) confirms whether the runtime layer is actually holding continuity.     |
| Institutional resilience planning          | The resilience trajectory engine produces a reviewer-readable institutional resilience band over time.            |

## Alignment posture

- The intelligence layer is **read-only** to the platform. It never writes back
  into institutional governance records.
- The intelligence layer is **opt-in** at the institution and per-scope level.
- The intelligence layer is **anonymised** by construction; it never reaches
  for institution-identifying data.
- The intelligence layer is **reviewer-led**: it presents readings, never
  remediations.

## Operating boundary

The intelligence layer does not:

- automate governance authority,
- author institutional decisions,
- expose institutions to other institutions,
- rank institutions,
- profile workers,
- or compose continuity readings outside an institution's explicit grant.
