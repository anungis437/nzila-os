# OCI Procurement Positioning

DOCTRINE_VERSION: 1.0.0

> **Posture.** OCI™ is the **human continuity layer** beneath operational resilience and governance continuity systems. It is **not** a substitute for ISO 22301, ISO 37000, COBIT, ISO 31000, or any certified BCMS. It does not certify compliance. It does not rank institutions. It does not generate risk scores.

## What OCI is

A standards-traceable, confidence-aware, statistically contextualised, auditably reproducible, and operationally defensible methodology for characterising **institutional continuity** through the lens of the people who carry it.

## What OCI is NOT

- A certification regime.
- A risk-management framework.
- An audit replacement.
- An AI inference system about people.
- A ranking or scoring service.

## Procurement-grade positioning sentence

> *"OCI complements our existing BCMS and governance frameworks by surfacing the human continuity layer that those frameworks implicitly assume is documented. Every OCI reading carries a confidence envelope, a caution state, and a reproducibility hash, and every cross-reference to ISO or COBIT is documented as `complements` — never as equivalent-to."*

## Defensibility pillars

1. **Standards traceability** — see [`OCI_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`](OCI_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md) and the per-standard crosswalk documents.
2. **Confidence awareness** — every output carries a Universal Confidence Envelope. See [`../methodology/OCI_METHOD_WHITEPAPER_v1.md`](../methodology/OCI_METHOD_WHITEPAPER_v1.md) §7 and the Confidence Architecture extension.
3. **Statistical contextualisation** — HHI and Gini outputs contextualise (never replace) OCI interpretation. See the HHI/Gini appendices in the whitepaper.
4. **Auditable reproducibility** — Entropy Audit Packets™ are content-addressed; two reviewers replaying the same observations produce identical hashes.
5. **Operational defensibility** — Doctrine governance, audit gates, reviewer variance modelling, evidence sufficiency engine, decay schedule.

## Anti-claims (procurement-safe)

- We do **not** claim ISO equivalence.
- We do **not** claim certification replacement.
- We do **not** claim risk scoring.
- We do **not** claim behavioural inference.
- We do **not** claim institutional ranking.
