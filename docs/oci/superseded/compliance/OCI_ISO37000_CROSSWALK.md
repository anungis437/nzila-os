# OCI ↔ ISO 37000:2021 (Governance of organisations) Crosswalk

DOCTRINE_VERSION: 1.0.0  
RELATIONSHIP CLASS: **gap-coverage** for the human continuity layer of governance (NEVER equivalent-to)

> **Hard rule.** OCI™ does NOT certify governance compliance, does NOT replace governance audit frameworks, and does NOT supersede ISO 37000. OCI provides a **governance entropy** lens that contextualises whether the organisational governance system has the human continuity fabric to sustain its stated structures over time.

## What OCI does NOT cover (explicit `OUT_OF_SCOPE`)

- Board composition guidance
- Strategy formation
- Executive compensation
- Value-creation models
- Stakeholder enumeration
- Performance evaluation
