# OCI Executive Email Sequence

**ARTIFACT TYPE:** Institutional Doctrine — Pre-Engagement Correspondence
**DOCTRINE_VERSION:** 1.0.0
**STATUS:** Canonical
**INTENDED USE:** Five-message sequence from facilitator (or sponsoring partner) to a prospective executive sponsor, delivered over six to eight weeks. Used after the sponsor has been introduced; not for cold acquisition.
**PARENT FRAMEWORK:** [OCI Institutional Activation](./OCI_INSTITUTIONAL_ACTIVATION.md)

> These messages exist to give an executive who has been
> introduced to the OCI work the time and the written
> reference they need to consider it on their own terms.
> They are not marketing emails. They are correspondence.

---

## Sequence overview

| # | Purpose | Sent | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First written reference after introduction | Week 1 | ~150 words |
| 2 | A single question to consider | Week 2 | ~120 words |
| 3 | A short reading | Week 4 | ~180 words |
| 4 | The institutional offer | Week 6 | ~200 words |
| 5 | The off-ramp | Week 8 (only if no reply) | ~100 words |

The cadence is generous. If the executive replies at any stage,
the sequence pauses; the conversation continues at their pace
through their preferred channel.

If the executive does not reply after message 5, the sequence
closes. No further messages are sent unless the executive
initiates.

---

## Message 1 — First written reference after introduction

**Subject:** A short reference, following our introduction

**Body:**

> [Salutation],
>
> Following our introduction earlier this week, I wanted to send
> a short written reference so you have something on the page
> when you have a quiet ten minutes.
>
> The work I described is called Operational Continuity
> Intelligence — OCI for short. The institutional question it
> takes seriously is the one most institutions notice late:
> how much of the organisation lives in a small number of
> people's calendars, memories, and judgement, and what happens
> when those people are unavailable.
>
> I am attaching one page. If there is interest in a longer
> conversation, I will send a fuller reference next.
>
> No reply needed. I will be in touch in a fortnight.
>
> With respect,
>
> [Facilitator name]

**Attachment:** A single printable page summarising the OCI
question, the four pilot phases by name only, and a single
sentence on the anti-surveillance and AI boundaries.

**What this message is not:**

- not a sales email,
- not a pitch deck,
- not a calendar invitation,
- not a request for a meeting.

---

## Message 2 — A single question to consider

**Subject:** One question, if it is useful

**Body:**

> [Salutation],
>
> Following on from the brief note I sent earlier this month,
> I wanted to offer a single question — not to answer, simply
> to hold.
>
> *If three of the people you most rely on for institutional
> memory were unavailable for the next quarter, which
> conversations in your institution would slow down first?*
>
> Most leaders find the answer surfaces within a few minutes,
> often with a name attached. The pattern that emerges is
> the entry point for the kind of work I described.
>
> I will write again in a fortnight with a short reading.
>
> With respect,
>
> [Facilitator name]

---

## Message 3 — A short reading

**Subject:** A short reading, on the institutional question

**Body:**

> [Salutation],
>
> Following on from earlier correspondence, here is the short
> reading I mentioned.
>
> It is two pages. It describes how institutions typically come
> to recognise the continuity load they have been carrying, the
> shape of the recognition, and the four phases of the pilot
> we use to address it. There is one page on what the work does
> not do — it is not a surveillance engagement, and it does not
> operate on autonomous-AI decisioning. That page matters as
> much as the rest.
>
> Read at your pace. There is no decision in front of you yet.
> If you want to talk it through with a colleague before
> deciding whether to talk to me, that is the right order.
>
> I will write once more, in a fortnight, with a brief account
> of how an engagement begins. After that, the next move is
> yours.
>
> With respect,
>
> [Facilitator name]

**Attachment:** A condensed version of the
[OCI Pilot Framework™](./OCI_PILOT_FRAMEWORK.md) and the
[OCI Board Overview](./OCI_BOARD_OVERVIEW.md), produced as a
single short reading.

---

## Message 4 — The institutional offer

**Subject:** How an engagement begins

**Body:**

> [Salutation],
>
> This is the last of the four short messages I planned to send.
> If the reading I sent has settled with you, here is how an
> engagement typically begins.
>
> The first step is a ninety-minute activation conversation
> with you, the colleague you would name as continuity
> steward, and the person who handles governance liaison for
> your institution. It is a working session, not a
> presentation. We use it to confirm whether the engagement is
> the right shape for the institution and, if it is, to agree
> the scope in writing.
>
> Until the scope is signed, the engagement has not begun. No
> staff are introduced to the work. No system access is
> arranged. No procurement is initiated. Nothing is
> communicated externally.
>
> If you would like to schedule the activation conversation, or
> if you would like a longer reading first, please reply to
> this note. If you would prefer to set the work aside, that
> is honourable too; I will close the sequence here and you
> will not hear from me unless you write.
>
> With respect,
>
> [Facilitator name]

---

## Message 5 — The off-ramp (sent only if no reply)

**Subject:** Closing the sequence

**Body:**

> [Salutation],
>
> A brief note to close the short sequence I have been sending.
>
> I have not heard back, which is a clear enough signal that
> the work is not the right shape for your institution at this
> time. No further notes will follow.
>
> If circumstances change at any point — a leadership
> transition, a governance event, a continuity moment that
> reframes the question — I would be glad to hear from you.
> Until then, my best wishes for your institution's work.
>
> With respect,
>
> [Facilitator name]

## Conduct rules

These rules govern the entire sequence and any related
correspondence.

- **No automation.** Each message is sent by a person, to a
  named individual, with the time and the day chosen by that
  person.
- **No tracking.** No read receipts. No link tracking. No
  pixels. The institution's reading habits are not measured.
- **No CRM enrichment from the correspondence.** Reply or
  non-reply is recorded as state (engaged / not engaged); no
  inference about the executive's intent is recorded.
- **No further messages after message 5 without a reply.** The
  sequence has an end. Silence is honoured.
- **No urgency manufacture.** No "limited capacity," no
  "before the end of the quarter," no "before our next cohort."
  The doctrine does not have cohorts in the marketing sense.
- **No multi-recipient sends.** Each message is one-to-one,
  composed for the named recipient.

---

## What is never in these messages

- No pricing.
- No customer logos.
- No metrics or social-proof claims.
- No reference to "transformation," "optimisation,"
  "productivity," or "automation."
- No screenshots.
- No video.
- No marketing collateral.

The correspondence is text and, where useful, short readings.
Nothing more.
