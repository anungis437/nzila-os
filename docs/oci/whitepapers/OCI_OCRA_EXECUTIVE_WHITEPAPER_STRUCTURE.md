# OCI/OCRA Executive Whitepaper Structure

## The Continuity Gap: Why Institutional Knowledge Breaks Down

**Artifact type:** Executive whitepaper architecture
**Audience:** Leadership, governance stakeholders, modernization sponsors, operations decision-makers, procurement sponsors
**Purpose:** Category understanding and continuity problem recognition

---

## Core Thesis

Organizations increasingly modernize operations without preserving operational survivability.

This creates:

- continuity debt,
- onboarding fragility,
- governance inconsistency,
- dependency concentration,
- institutional memory degradation.

OCI/OCRA help organizations understand and stabilize continuity safely over time.
