## Canonical Definitions

- OCI: Organizational Continuity Infrastructure
- OCRA: Organizational Continuity Risk Analysis
- Governance Entropy System (GES): Governance continuity fragility interpretation framework
- Continuity debt: Accumulated undocumented operational dependency
- Reconstruction burden: Difficulty rebuilding operational continuity from memory
- Stewardship concentration: Critical continuity knowledge concentrated among too few individuals

---

## Binding Method Boundaries

OCI/OCRA do not:

- monitor productivity,
- rank individuals,
- infer behavioral compliance,
- provide workforce surveillance,
- replace formal external audits,
- claim ISO/COBIT certification equivalence,
- function as deterministic ranking systems for institutions.

OCI/OCRA are:

- evidence-sensitive,
- confidence-aware,
- reviewer-led,
- governance-safe,
- non-surveillance continuity infrastructure.
