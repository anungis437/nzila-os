## Dual-Entry GTM Doctrine

### Path A: OCRA-first

Entry motivation: "Help us understand continuity fragility."

Flow:

```text
OCI
-> OCRA
-> continuity roadmap
-> operational continuity activation
-> governance continuity
-> longitudinal continuity
```

### Path B: Operations-first

Entry motivation: "Help us stabilize operational workflows."

Flow:

```text
Operations Core
-> continuity pain visibility
-> OCI activation
-> OCRA intelligence
-> governance continuity
-> longitudinal continuity
```

### Convergence rule

Both paths must converge into the same continuity-aware operational infrastructure.
No ontology split into separate "ops product" and "OCI product" narratives.

## Shared Non-Negotiables Across Both Whitepapers

### Proof architecture requirements

Every major section must define:

- required operational proof artifacts,
- required visual/diagram assets,
- objection handling statements,
- procurement boundary statements where relevant.

### Anti-surveillance boundary

Union Eyes does not:

- monitor productivity,
- rank workers,
- infer behavior,
- run workforce surveillance.

AI is assistive, reviewer-led, and governance-safe.

### Standards boundary

OCI/OCRA complement ISO/COBIT governance and continuity frameworks.
They do not claim certification equivalence and do not replace formal audits.
