# Cover email — external independent review request

> **Template.** Personalise before sending. Send to a single addressee.

**To:** Richard Sharpe &lt;richard.sharpe@ontario.ca&gt;
**Subject:** Request for independent public-sector judgment on an
institutional-continuity assessment architecture

Dear Richard Sharpe,

I'm writing to ask for something narrow and specific: your **independent
judgment** — not your endorsement — on the shape of a public-sector
assessment we are preparing for a small number of Crown corporation and
municipal engagements.

We have built the architecture and the internal tests. What we are missing,
and what we cannot supply for ourselves, is a senior public-administration
reviewer's answer to a small number of questions we cannot answer without
overclaiming.

Enclosed:

1. **Independent Review Brief** (5–7 pages) — the problem we are addressing,
   the category hypothesis, the architecture in one diagram, one worked
   finding rendered end-to-end, what is implemented, what remains unverified,
   and the five decisions we are asking you to help with.
2. **Reviewer Response Form** (2 pages) — the five decisions themselves,
   with space for your written verdict on each.
3. **Evidence Index** — the deeper materials (implementation status matrix,
   validation binder, obligation taxonomy, source-instrument architecture,
   security & data-handling brief, procurement readiness assessment, test
   suites, standards crosswalks) available on request. **We are not asking
   you to read these.** They exist if you want to test any part of the brief.

What is **out of scope** for this pass:

- **Legal verification** of instruments — the source-instrument catalogue is
  seeded as `UNVERIFIED` and no clause is asserted; verifying instruments
  requires qualified counsel and is not something we would ask you to do.
- **Formal security attestation** — a full third-party security review is
  planned separately.
- **Commercial terms** — liability, IP, service levels, and exit terms are
  drafted separately with a procurement specialist.

If any of the five decisions properly belongs to counsel, a security auditor,
a procurement authority, or a methodology specialist, please flag it. A
short attribution of *who* should sign off on *what* is more valuable to us
than a single overall verdict.

If it would be easier, I'm happy to walk the brief with you for 45 minutes
before you commit anything to writing.

Thank you for considering this. There is no expectation of endorsement, and
no obligation created by reading — the only thing we are asking for is your
honest read of whether this architecture, as designed and as honestly
qualified, is a defensible foundation for public-sector use.

With respect,
Aubert Nungisa
Founder, Product, Technology & Commercialization
Nzila Ventures
