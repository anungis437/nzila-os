# OCI Observatory™ Foundations

**Artifact type:** Doctrine
**Module:** OCI Intelligence Network
**Doctrine version:** 1.0.0
**Posture:** public-legitimacy infrastructure · sector-only · anonymous

---

The OCI Observatory™ is the future public continuity legitimacy surface of
OCI. Product 5 establishes the foundations; the observatory itself is a
downstream artefact composed from network-level readings.

The observatory exists so that institutional continuity can be discussed
publicly **without** exposing or ranking any individual institution.

## What the observatory will eventually support

- Annual continuity reports composed from anonymised sector envelopes
- Sector continuity studies (one envelope per recognised sector)
- Institutional survivability publications written at sector level
- Stewardship burden research, sector-only
- Continuity evolution reports composed from longitudinal sector trajectories

## What the observatory will not become

- An institutional leaderboard
- A governance prestige index
- An exposure surface for continuity weaknesses of named institutions
- A workforce performance dashboard
- A telemetry surface or operational analytics product

## Doctrinal floors carried forward

Every observatory output must continue to satisfy:

1. The k-anonymity floor of `K_ANONYMITY_FLOOR = 5`.
2. Refusal-first semantics — readings that cannot be composed remain refusals.
3. Reviewer-led interpretation — observatory text describes; it does not conclude.
4. No ranking payloads — forbidden-key scan applies to every published artefact.

## Composition source

The observatory composes its outputs from sector baseline envelopes, sector
archetype readings, cross-institution pattern readings, and reviewer-authored
sector continuity profiles. It never composes from per-institution records.
