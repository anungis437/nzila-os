# Continuity Intervention Ethics

**Status:** Canonical, binding ethical frame for every act inside a Product 3 engagement. Cross-referenced from [OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md](../OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md), [OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md](../OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md), and [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies receiving an engagement, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Intervention Ethics document is the binding ethical frame for the intervention layer of the OCI Stabilization Framework™. It governs how a facilitator may act, what a facilitator may not do regardless of authorisation, and what conditions terminate an engagement immediately.

A facilitator who breaches any clause of this document is no longer practising Product 3. The breach is grounds for engagement pause, recertification review, and — where the breach is structural — withdrawal of certification.

This document is not advisory. It is the ethical perimeter of the framework.

---

## 2. The ten ethical commitments

### 2.1 Intervention restraint

The facilitator does not act where deferral is the institution's better path. A facilitator who can defer, defers. A facilitator who must act, acts only with explicit consent and only within the ratified scope.

### 2.2 Continuity dignity

Every steward present at engagement open carries continuity standing the engagement preserves. No act of the engagement diminishes that standing. Where redistribution is offered, the originating steward's recognition is maintained or enhanced — never reduced.

### 2.3 Stewardship sensitivity

The facilitator does not surface a steward's load, gap, or fragility to leadership without the steward's prior consent and prior briefing on what will be surfaced. The engagement does not move ahead of the steward's awareness of what is being said about their practice.

### 2.4 Operational non-harm

The engagement does not introduce operational instability into the institution. Stabilization moves that would interrupt operational continuity are sequenced behind moves that preserve it. Where instability is unavoidable, the institution names and accepts the instability before the move is taken.

### 2.5 Consent revocability

Institutional consent and steward-level consent are revocable at any point. Withdrawal is honoured immediately, recorded in the engagement log, and closes any move in flight that depended on the withdrawn consent. No clause of an engagement contract overrides this.

### 2.6 Exit conditions

The facilitator names the conditions under which the engagement must close at engagement open. The conditions include: consent withdrawal, readiness loss that cannot be re-established, principle breach, ethical breach, governance-body decline to continue, and the institution's request to close.

An engagement that has hit an exit condition closes at the next session boundary regardless of work in progress. Work in progress is documented, returned to the institution, and severed from the facilitator's working files per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).

### 2.7 Refusal of extractive use

The facilitator declines requests to use engagement findings to extract value from stewards — including but not limited to: using findings to set compensation, using findings to set ranking, using findings to support dismissal, using findings as input to any per-person evaluation.

The decline is recorded in the engagement log. The decline does not require justification beyond reference to this clause.

### 2.8 Refusal of governance policing

The facilitator declines requests to use the framework as an instrument of governance enforcement against stewards or constituent groups. The framework reads continuity; it does not police compliance, does not adjudicate disputes, and does not produce evidence for adjudicatory processes.

### 2.9 Refusal of continuity coercion

The facilitator declines requests to use Continuity Debt™ vocabulary or severity-band readings as instruments of pressure against stewards to extract additional load, extend tenure beyond intent, or accept conditions they would otherwise decline.

The vocabulary is institutional. Using it against a person is a breach of this clause.

### 2.10 Refusal of theatrical engagement

The facilitator declines to produce engagement theatre — visible activity that is not paying down Continuity Debt. The framework is read for what it reduces, not for the appearance of activity it produces. An engagement that has no reductive moves available is closed honestly, not extended for appearance.

## 3. Boundary with the Anti-Surveillance Position

This document operates inside the perimeter of the OCI Anti-Surveillance Position. Where this document and the Anti-Surveillance Position diverge, the Anti-Surveillance Position governs.

In particular: no clause of this document authorises per-person behavioural data collection, per-person inference, per-person scoring, or per-person ranking. The framework's continuity readings are institutional and remain institutional regardless of any other clause.

---

## 4. Boundary with the AI Boundary

Any computational support inside an engagement is reviewer-led under [OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md](../OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md). The facilitator never substitutes engine output for institutional interpretation. The engagement's interpretations are produced by the institution's reviewers; the engines surface structural readings the reviewers ratify or decline.

---

## 5. Boundary with the Data Handling commitments

The engagement's data retention, severance, and return obligations are governed by [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md). On engagement close, the facilitator severs the working copy of all engagement materials. The institution holds the canonical record.

---

## 6. Recertification on breach

A breach of any clause in this document is a recertification matter. The recertification review process is documented in [OCI_FACILITATOR_CERTIFICATION_RUBRIC.md](../OCI_FACILITATOR_CERTIFICATION_RUBRIC.md). A breach finding may result in:

- Required remediation training,
- Suspension of facilitator authorisation pending review,
- Withdrawal of certification.

A facilitator under suspension does not deliver Product 3.
