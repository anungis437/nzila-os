# OCI Stabilization Readiness Thresholds

**Status:** Canonical doctrine. Binding gate for every Product 3 engagement entry and for every stabilization move within an engagement.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies considering a Product 3 engagement, executive sponsors, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Readiness Thresholds define the institutional preconditions under which a Product 3 engagement — or any stabilization move within it — may proceed. They exist so the framework can decline to engage when engagement would not serve the institution, and so that decline is recognised as a successful method outcome.

The thresholds are not a maturity assessment. They are not a scoring of the institution. They are five structural conditions, each of which must be at a sufficient band before a Product 3 engagement (or a specific move) is offered.

---

## 2. Honest non-engagement as method outcome

The framework treats honest non-engagement as a successful application of the method. An institution that does not meet readiness is offered:

- A Product 2 deepening (extending the Governance Entropy Workbook™ work),
- A recognition-only continuation under Product 1,
- An invitation to return when readiness has shifted.

The institution is not pushed into Product 3. A facilitator who advances a Product 3 engagement against unmet readiness is no longer practising the framework and is referred to recertification review.

---

## 3. The five readiness conditions

Readiness is read across five conditions. Each carries an explicit sufficiency band and an explicit "not yet ready" disposition.

### 3.1 Leadership alignment

**Sufficiency:** The institution's executive sponsor and at least one governance-body representative are aligned on entering Product 3, with no active executive transition that would supersede their authority during the engagement.

**Not yet ready disposition:** Defer Product 3. Offer a Product 1 recognition continuation. Reopen the readiness reading after the leadership transition has stabilised.

### 3.2 Stewardship visibility

**Sufficiency:** Product 2 has produced a stewardship cartography the institution has reviewed and accepted as accurate. The institution can name the stewards it intends to support through the engagement.

**Not yet ready disposition:** Defer Product 3. Offer a Product 2 deepening focused on cartography completion.

### 3.3 Continuity acknowledgement

**Sufficiency:** The institution has acknowledged at least one continuity exposure it intends to act on. Acknowledgement is institutional (governance-body level), not merely facilitator-asserted.

**Not yet ready disposition:** Defer Product 3. Offer a Product 1 or Product 2 cadence focused on recognition and reading. Do not present acknowledgement as a prerequisite the institution has failed.

### 3.4 Governance openness

**Sufficiency:** The institution's governance body is willing to receive the engagement's artefacts and to ratify or decline stabilization moves on the cadence the Lifecycle requires.

**Not yet ready disposition:** Defer Product 3. Offer a Stewardship Retainer in observer mode without stabilization moves until governance openness is established.

### 3.5 Operational trust conditions

**Sufficiency:** Stewards in the institution can decline an engagement move without consequence to their standing. The institution can attest, in writing, that decline carries no institutional cost to the declining steward.

**Not yet ready disposition:** Defer Product 3. The framework does not run inside an institution where decline carries cost to stewards. Offer a recognition-only continuation under Product 1.

---

## 4. Readiness reading rules

### 4.1 All-or-none for engagement entry

Engagement entry requires all five readiness conditions to be at sufficiency. A single condition below sufficiency defers engagement entry.

### 4.2 Per-move for moves within an engagement

Stabilization moves within an engagement require readiness for the move's dimension to be at sufficiency. A move whose dimension is below sufficiency is deferred, not abandoned. Deferral with re-open conditions is recorded in the engagement log.

### 4.3 Readiness reverification at phase boundaries

Readiness is re-read at every Lifecycle phase boundary. A condition that was at sufficiency at engagement open may drift; the facilitator pauses the engagement to surface the drift and offer a re-ratification or a partial close.

### 4.4 Readiness is read with the institution, not for the institution

The facilitator does not unilaterally pronounce readiness. Readiness is read in conversation with the engagement sponsor and the governance body. The institution holds the final word on its own readiness.

---

## 5. Readiness as a band, not a verdict

Each condition is read on a band — sufficient, partial, insufficient — not as pass/fail. A partial reading is a candidate for explicit support before engagement open. A partial reading is never silently elevated to sufficient.

The band reading is held in the engagement log and is part of the engagement's institutional record.
