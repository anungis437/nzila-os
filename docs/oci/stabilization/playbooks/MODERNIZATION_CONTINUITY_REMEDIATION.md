# Playbook 6 — Modernization Continuity Remediation

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

Remediate continuity exposure introduced by an in-flight or recently completed modernization that substituted tooling or process without preserving the institutional interpretation embedded in what it replaced. The playbook recovers the interpretation before further substitution proceeds.

## 2. Preconditions

- A specific modernization is named, scoped, and acknowledged at the governance-body level.
- The stewards of the practice the modernization replaced consent to participation.
- The modernization owner agrees to the remediation cadence and to pausing further substitution until remediation completes (or to an explicit decision to continue without preservation).

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **leadership alignment** between the modernization owner and the continuity-bearing stewards.

## 4. Severity band

Engaged across **Moderate**, **Elevated**, and **Critical** in the modernization-without-preservation dimension (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md)).

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Modernization scope and substitution audit.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Identifies what was substituted and what continuity it carried.
2. **Pre-substitution practice interviews.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Stewards of the replaced practice describe the interpretation embedded in it.
3. **Continuity preservation record draft.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The draft captures the institutional interpretation in a tool-independent form.
4. **Post-substitution continuity confirmation.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The institution confirms whether the new tooling honours the preserved interpretation.
5. **Governance-body ratification.** Reversibility: irreversible. The preservation record becomes the institutional artefact.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily the **Modernization Driver** and the **Continuity Guardian**. The playbook explicitly resists positioning these as opposed role-states. Both are recognised as serving institutional continuity through different stances.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `continuitySafeModernization`
- `modernizationAlignmentEngine`
- `governanceModernizationReview`
- `governanceInterpretationMatrix`
- `continuityLineageEngine`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- The remediation is not a critique of the modernization. It is a recovery of the interpretation the substitution did not capture.
- Hold the modernization owner and the continuity stewards in equal standing throughout. Neither is positioned as the cause of the gap.
- Where the institution decides to continue without preservation, record the decision as an explicit governance act with a re-open condition.
- Refuse to use the remediation as input to attribution of fault.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A two-page brief returned to the governance body:
- Page 1: substitution audit, preservation record summary, continuity confirmation status.
- Page 2: residual modernization-without-preservation readings, governance decisions recorded, reciprocity terms honoured.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- The modernization owner declines to pause further substitution and the institution declines to record the decision. Playbook is deferred.
- The stewards of the replaced practice are no longer present in the institution. Playbook is run in best-effort reconstruction mode with explicit weakness statement.
- The modernization is too in-flight to pause without operational instability. Playbook is rescheduled to a stabilization window.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a ratified continuity preservation record.
- Continuity confirmation has been recorded for the substituted areas.
- Stewards of the replaced practice exit with recognised standing.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- Stewards whose interpretation contributed to the preservation record are named as contributors.
- The modernization owner's standing is preserved; the remediation is not positioned as their failure.
- The record is returned to the institution; the facilitator's working copy is severed per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
