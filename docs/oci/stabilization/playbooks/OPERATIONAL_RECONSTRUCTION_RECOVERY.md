# Playbook 7 — Operational Reconstruction Recovery

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

Recover institutional practice that has lapsed — practice that was previously carried but is no longer actively held by any current steward. The playbook reconstructs the most defensible reading of the lapsed practice with the institution and ratifies it as the institutional record going forward.

## 2. Preconditions

- Product 2 reconstruction-burden reading surfaces the lapse and the institution acknowledges it.
- At least one secondary source (former steward, archival record, adjacent steward) is available for the reconstruction.
- The governance body sponsors the reconstruction and commits to ratifying its outputs.

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **governance openness** (the reconstruction's output will become governance-receivable).

## 4. Severity band

Engaged at **Elevated** or above on the reconstruction-burden dimension. Institutional Fragility readings require governance-body co-sponsorship and a longer cadence.

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Reconstruction scoping with the governance body.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Names the lapsed practice in scope and the secondary sources available.
2. **Secondary source interviews and archival review.** Reversibility: fully reversible. Captures the strongest available reading.
3. **Reconstruction workshop with adjacent stewards.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. Adjacent stewards review the reconstruction for plausibility and institutional fit.
4. **Reconstruction record draft.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The draft explicitly distinguishes recovered material, reconstructed material, and unresolved gaps.
5. **Governance-body ratification.** Reversibility: irreversible. The ratified record becomes the institutional artefact for the practice going forward.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily the **Institutional Rebuilder** and the **Continuity Guardian**. The playbook explicitly resists positioning the reconstruction as a substitute for the original practice. The record is the institution's best current reading, not a claim to have recovered the original.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `reconstructionBurdenAnalyzer`
- `precedentContinuityMapper`
- `continuityLineageEngine`
- `governanceInterpretationMatrix`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- The reconstruction is an act of institutional honesty. Mark recovered material, reconstructed material, and unresolved gaps clearly throughout.
- Refuse to inflate confidence in reconstructed material. A reconstructed reading is a reading, not a recovery.
- Where adjacent stewards disagree on the reconstruction, record the disagreement in the record and refer it to the governance body for ratification.
- Refuse to use the reconstruction as a basis for attributing fault to anyone whose tenure preceded the lapse.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A three-section brief returned to the governance body:
- Section 1: ratified record summary with counts of recovered, reconstructed, and unresolved entries.
- Section 2: residual reconstruction burden with re-open conditions.
- Section 3: reciprocity terms and the named contributors recognised in the record.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- No secondary source is available. Playbook is deferred; an explicit governance act records the lapse as unrecoverable under current conditions.
- Adjacent stewards decline participation. Playbook is run in archival-only mode with explicit weakness statement.
- The governance body declines to commit to ratification. Playbook is deferred to a Product 2 deepening.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a ratified reconstruction record.
- The record distinguishes recovered, reconstructed, and unresolved material.
- Unresolved gaps are documented with re-open conditions.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- Contributors to the reconstruction are named in the record.
- The record is returned to the institution; the facilitator's working copy is severed per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
