# Playbook 5 — Onboarding Survivability Recovery

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

Recover institutional onboarding survivability for a role whose succession would currently be unsupported. The playbook produces a ratified onboarding record a successor steward could absorb without depending on the current steward's continuous presence.

## 2. Preconditions

- Product 2 onboarding-fragility reading surfaces a role at risk.
- The current steward of the role consents to participation.
- The institution names whether the recovery is preparing for a known transition or pre-empting a possible one.

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **stewardship visibility**.

## 4. Severity band

Engaged across **Moderate**, **Elevated**, and **Critical** in the onboarding-fragility dimension (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md)).

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Onboarding scoping with the current steward.** Reversibility: fully reversible. The steward defines what they will surface and what they defer.
2. **Role practice walk-through.** Reversibility: fully reversible. The steward walks the role in their language, with the facilitator capturing.
3. **Onboarding playbook draft.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The draft is reviewed and revised with the steward.
4. **Shadowing arrangement (when a successor candidate is named).** Reversibility: reversible with cost. A time-bounded shadowing period is offered, not imposed.
5. **Governance-body ratification of the onboarding record.** Reversibility: irreversible. The record becomes the institutional artefact for the role.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily the **Exhausted Steward** and the **Institutional Rebuilder**. The playbook explicitly resists positioning the recovery as evidence of the current steward's replaceability. The steward is recognised as carrying the role beyond what the institution has supported.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `onboardingFragilityAnalysis`
- `stewardshipCartography`
- `continuityDependencyGraph`
- `continuityLineageEngine`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- The onboarding record is the steward's voice. The facilitator records; the institution receives only what the steward releases.
- Where the role has informal practice the steward does not wish to surface, that decline is honoured and recorded as a remaining deferral.
- Refuse to use the onboarding record as input to compensation, ranking, or evaluation.
- Where a successor candidate is named, the shadowing arrangement is offered to both parties; either may decline.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A one-page brief returned to the governance body:
- Onboarding record scope.
- Shadowing status (when applicable).
- Remaining deferrals with re-open conditions.
- Reciprocity terms honoured.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- The current steward declines participation. Playbook is deferred.
- The role is currently in active transition turbulence. Playbook is deferred until conditions stabilise.
- The institution cannot commit to ratifying the onboarding record. Playbook is deferred to a Product 2 deepening.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a ratified onboarding record for the role.
- A successor candidate (when present) has shadowed the role.
- The current steward exits with recognised standing.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- The current steward is named as the originating carrier in the onboarding record.
- The institution names the onboarding record as institutional arrangement, not as the steward's replacement plan.
- The record is returned to the institution; the facilitator's working copy is severed per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
