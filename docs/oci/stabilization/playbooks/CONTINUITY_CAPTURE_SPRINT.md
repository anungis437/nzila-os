# Playbook 3 — Continuity Capture Sprint

**Status:** Canonical Product 3 playbook.

**Audience:** Certified facilitators, governance bodies, internal stewards of the method.

---

## 1. Purpose

Capture, in a time-bounded sprint, the practice and interpretation a single steward (or a small cluster of stewards) carries that the institution would not survive losing without prior capture. The sprint converts undocumented practice into a governance-receivable record while the steward is present.

## 2. Preconditions

- A specific concentration of continuity-bearing practice has been identified through Product 2 cartography.
- The carrying steward(s) consent to the sprint and to the time commitment it requires.
- A successor or carrier-broadening candidate is named (the sprint produces a record someone receives).

## 3. Readiness band

All five [Readiness Thresholds](../OCI_STABILIZATION_READINESS.md) at sufficient. Particular attention to **stewardship visibility** and **operational trust conditions**.

## 4. Severity band

Engaged at **Elevated** or above on the steward-concentration dimension (see [OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md](../OCI_STABILIZATION_SEVERITY_MODEL.md)). Sprints at Critical or Institutional Fragility require an explicit governance-body decision to engage.

## 5. Intervention sequence

1. **Sprint scoping with the steward.** Reversibility: fully reversible. The steward defines what they will surface and what they defer.
2. **Practice walk-throughs.** Reversibility: fully reversible. The steward walks the practice in their language, with the facilitator capturing in writing.
3. **Written practice draft.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The draft is the steward's property until they release it to the institution.
4. **Practice review with the carrier-broadening candidate.** Reversibility: reversible with cost. The candidate reads the draft, raises questions, and reviews with the steward.
5. **Ratified practice record return to the institution.** Reversibility: irreversible. The record becomes the institutional artefact for the captured practice.

## 6. Persona resonance

Primarily the **Exhausted Steward** and the **Institutional Rebuilder**. The sprint is run with explicit dignity for the steward whose load it surfaces. Capture does not become extraction.

## 7. Engine inputs

- `stewardshipCartography`
- `continuityDependencyGraph`
- `continuityLineageEngine`
- `onboardingFragilityAnalysis`

## 8. Facilitator guidance

- The sprint cadence is steward-led. The facilitator follows the steward's pace; the institution does not set a deadline that overrides the steward's capacity.
- The written practice is the steward's voice. The facilitator records; the institution receives only what the steward releases.
- Where the steward declines to surface a part of their practice, that decline is honoured and recorded as a remaining deferral.
- Refuse to use the capture as input to evaluation. The captured practice is institutional record, not steward performance evidence.

## 9. Executive briefing format

A one-page brief returned to the governance body:
- Captured practice scope.
- Carrier broadening status.
- Remaining deferrals with re-open conditions.
- Reciprocity terms honoured.

## 10. Deferral conditions

- The steward declines the sprint. Playbook is deferred; the alternative is a longer-cadence stewardship recognition under Product 1.
- No carrier-broadening candidate is named. The sprint may run in capture-only mode with an explicit downstream broadening plan.
- The steward is already at overload. The sprint is deferred until redistribution or rest has occurred.

## 11. Success criteria + reciprocity terms

**Success criteria:**
- The institution holds a ratified practice record.
- A second carrier has reviewed the record.
- The steward exits the sprint with recognised standing.

**Reciprocity terms:**
- The steward is named as the originating carrier in the institutional record.
- The sprint's cadence respected the steward's capacity.
- The captured record is returned to the institution; the facilitator's working copy is severed per [OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md](../../OCI_DATA_HANDLING.md).
