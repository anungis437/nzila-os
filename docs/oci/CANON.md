## What is OCI? What is OCRA? Which comes first?

**OCI** = **Organizational Continuity Intelligence**. One expansion, everywhere. It is the method
and the brand: a facilitator-led practice that helps an institution see and protect the knowledge
it depends on, without scoring the people who hold it.

**OCRA** = **Organizational Continuity Recognition Assessment**. OCRA is **Product 1** — the
Recognition-phase instrument inside OCI. Neither "comes first" in time; OCRA is a layer inside OCI,
the way the Governance Entropy Workbook is P2 inside OCI. Commercially, an institution buys "an
OCI Assessment," which is built on the OCRA instrument.

## The one clock

The pilot **is** these five phases — there is no second clock. "Embedding," used in delivery
conversations, is not a sixth phase; it is the plain-language name for the Phase 4 decision point:
whether the institution embeds Infrastructure (P4) and later joins Intelligence (P5), or operates
its stabilisation plan independently. Same five rows, one name for the exit decision.

| # | Phase | Product |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognition | P1 — OCRA |
| 2 | Mapping | P2 — Governance Entropy Workbook™ |
| 3 | Stabilization | P3 — OCI Diagnostic & Stabilization |
| 4 | Infrastructure ("Embedding" decision) | P4 — OCI Runtime |
| 5 | Intelligence (not sold — see below) | P5 — OCI Intelligence Network |

No "from." No bare range. Every range has a named scope driver (bargaining units / sites in
scope). Self-serve tiers cannot claim a stabilised, ratified, or embedded outcome — only a
facilitated Assessment or later tier can.

## What do I pay, for how many weeks, and what do I hold if I fire you in week 6?

You pay $18,000 (base; scope drives it up to $45,000). The engagement runs ~12 weeks across 9
sessions. Week 6 falls inside Mapping (weeks 4–8), before Stabilization begins. If you end it
then, you hold **the adopted continuity map plus the deferred items list, if either has been
adopted by that point — or nothing, if neither has.** Either way, the facilitator writes a short
closure note recording the date and the state at exit; that costs you nothing further and is not
conditional on how far the engagement got. See [`OCI_METHOD.md` §4](./OCI_METHOD.md#4-method-artefacts).

## What will you not look at?

**Default is refuse.** Out of scope, refused without exception: bargaining strategy, active
grievance detail, staff performance, legal-privileged material, internal political strategy. In
scope, offered by the institution: governance lineage, role structure, succession, operational
memory. See [`OCI_METHOD.md` §6.3](./OCI_METHOD.md#63-information-boundary-labour-engagements)
for the full four-category table.

## Do you score my people?

No. Every instrument reads the institution, never a named person. See
[`OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md` — Instrument disposition](./OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md#instrument-disposition).

## Is this AI?

**AI assists; the facilitator judges.** Every AI-assisted draft is read and endorsed by a named
human reviewer before it informs anything; AI never scores, decides, or ranks. If AI is
unavailable, the engagement continues — engines compute deterministically, the facilitator drafts
manually. See [`OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md`](./OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md), the only canonical AI document.

## When do you walk away?

Three conditions, written down, none of them a failed sale:

1. **No named executive sponsor** — no single accountable leader can ratify a continuity plan.
2. **Active adversarial process** — litigation, contested labour action, or governance crisis where
   the work would read as taking a side.
3. **Consent cannot be sustained** — the institution withdraws or cannot commit steward time before
   Recognition closes.

See [`OCI_METHOD.md` §6.4](./OCI_METHOD.md#64-kill-criteria-when-an-institution-is-not-assessable).
