## What is OCI? What is OCRA? Which comes first?

**OCI** = **Organizational Continuity Intelligence**. One expansion, everywhere. It is the method
and the brand: a facilitator-led practice that helps an institution see and protect the knowledge
it depends on, without scoring the people who hold it.

**OCRA** = **Organizational Continuity Recognition Assessment**. OCRA is **Product 1** — the
Recognition-phase instrument inside OCI. Neither "comes first" in time; OCRA is a layer inside OCI,
the way the Governance Entropy Workbook is P2 inside OCI. Commercially, an institution buys "an
OCI Assessment," which is built on the OCRA instrument.

## The one clock

Five phases, one product layer each:

| # | Phase | Product |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognition | P1 — OCRA |
| 2 | Mapping | P2 — Governance Entropy Workbook™ |
| 3 | Stabilization | P3 — OCI Diagnostic & Stabilization |
| 4 | Infrastructure | P4 — OCI Runtime |
| 5 | Intelligence (not sold — see below) | P5 — OCI Intelligence Network |

The pilot delivery view collapses this to four delivery phases (Recognition, Mapping,
Stabilization, Embedding); "Embedding" is the decision point onto Phases 4–5. One clock, two views.

## The one price table

| Tier | Price (CAD) | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Free Readiness Check | Free | Self-serve, instant |
| Leadership Briefing Report | $1,200 | Self-serve, PDF |
| Full Diagnostic & Action Plan | $6,500 | Facilitated, one workshop; 100% credits toward the Assessment within 90 days |
| **OCI Assessment** (OCRA, P1) | **$18,000 base, $45,000 ceiling** | Facilitated, ~12 weeks, 9 sessions. Scope driver: bargaining units / sites in scope |
| Platform Activation (P4, OCI Runtime) | **$40,000/yr base, $140,000/yr ceiling** | Annual, post-Assessment. Same scope driver |
| Longitudinal Continuity Support | Bespoke, scoped in writing | Federations / national unions |

No "from." No bare range. Every range has a named scope driver (bargaining units / sites in
scope). Self-serve tiers cannot claim a stabilised, ratified, or embedded outcome — only a
facilitated Assessment or later tier can.

## What do I pay, for how many weeks, and what do I hold if I fire you in week 6?

You pay $18,000 (base; scope drives it up to $45,000). The engagement runs ~12 weeks across 9
sessions. If you end it in week 6, you keep every artefact drafted and adopted to that point,
plus a one-page **closure minute** the facilitator writes recording what was stabilised, what was
deferred, and the date. See [`OCI_METHOD.md` §4](./OCI_METHOD.md#4-method-artefacts).

## What will you not look at?

Bargaining strategy, active grievance detail, staff performance, legal-privileged material, and
internal political strategy. Default is refuse. See
[`OCI_METHOD.md` §6.3](./OCI_METHOD.md#63-information-boundary-labour-engagements).

## Do you score my people?

No. Every instrument reads the institution, never a named person. See
[`OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md` — Instrument disposition](./OCI_ANTI_SURVEILLANCE_POSITION.md#instrument-disposition).

## Is this AI?

No. AI drafts under a named human reviewer; it never scores, decides, or ranks. If AI is
unavailable, the engagement continues — engines compute deterministically, the facilitator drafts
manually. See [`OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md`](./OCI_AI_BOUNDARY.md), the only canonical AI document.

## When do you walk away?

Three conditions, written down, none of them a failed sale: no named executive sponsor; an active
adversarial process (litigation, contested labour action, governance crisis) where the work would
read as taking a side; or consent that cannot be sustained through Recognition. See
[`OCI_METHOD.md` §6.4](./OCI_METHOD.md#64-kill-criteria-when-an-institution-is-not-assessable).

answering a question required opening a government-readiness binder, this pass has failed —
nothing above requires it.
