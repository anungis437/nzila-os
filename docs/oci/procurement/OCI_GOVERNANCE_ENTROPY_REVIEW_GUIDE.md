# OCI Governance Entropy Review Guide

DOCTRINE_VERSION: 1.0.0

A reviewer's working guide to producing defensible Governance Entropy™ readings.

## The five entropy ordinals

| Ordinal | Posture | Typical observations |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — **Coherent** | Governance roles, succession, and decision rights are documented and observed in practice. | Documentary + Governance + Procedural evidence converging. |
| 2 — **Recognised** | Roles and accountabilities are recognised by the institution but documentation is partial. | Mixed Documentary/Verbal evidence with no contradictions. |
| 3 — **Patterned** | Governance behaviour exhibits patterns but those patterns are not codified. | Operational + Historical evidence; documentation thin. |
| 4 — **Institutional** | Governance asymmetry is visible at institutional scale — patterns vary by sub-unit. | CrossFunctional reviewers reporting divergent posture. |
| 5 — **Systemic** | Governance entropy is systemic; continuity-bearing roles operate without recognition. | Strong evidence of role-without-recognition across reviewers; high reconstruction burden if any carrier exits. |

## Review workflow (calm and reproducible)

1. **Record observations using the Observable Evidence Taxonomy™.** Choose `evidenceType`, `evidenceStrength`, `evidenceSource`, `evidenceConfidence`, `reviewerConfidence` for every observation.
2. **Run the Evidence Sufficiency Engine™.** Capture the sufficiency verdict, confidence, and any contradictions.
3. **Build the Entropy Audit Packet™.** The packet's `reproducibilityHash` is the citation key.
4. **Wrap the entropy reading in a Universal Confidence Envelope™.** Provide sample, completeness, stability, age.
5. **Consult the Reviewer Consistency Layer™ output if a panel ran.** If `calibrationConfidence ∈ { LOW, INSUFFICIENT }`, schedule reviewer-led re-examination.

## Hard rules

- Governance Entropy MUST fail cautiously, NOT infer aggressively.
- Verbal evidence is never sufficient on its own.
- Contradictions between strong observations downgrade a sufficient verdict to partial.
- No reading is final without a confidence envelope.
- The methodology never overrides a reviewer's classification — it constrains the methodological drift around it.
