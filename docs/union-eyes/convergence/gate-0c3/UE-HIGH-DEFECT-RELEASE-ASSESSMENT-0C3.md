## What this is

An independent, versioned assessment of the 71 HIGH defects that Gates 0B.1 to 0B.6 recorded before a release disposition was required. Each defect keeps its historical record, its severity and its hash. The register adds, per defect, who can reach it, what it can do, whether it is active, what containment the sources show, what is proven and what is inference, a proposed disposition with its reason, and whether runtime qualification is still needed.

A proposed disposition is a recommendation. It is not an accepted release determination, it changes no severity and it grants no release. No defect has an executed proof on file (0 of 71), so every assessment rests on reading the source.

## Rubric

- `RB_XT`: It reaches or changes another organization data and no executed proof of containment is on file.
- `RB_MF`: It discloses sensitive records to every authenticated member of the organization.
- `RB_FALSE`: It returns or records a false outcome, attestation or success for work that did not happen or cannot be verified.
- `RB_CRED`: It exposes or accepts credential or authentication state in a forgeable or client-visible form.
- `RB_SHARED`: It lets a tenant role write or delete data that every organization reads.
- `RB_ANYMUT`: It lets a caller below the owning authority change another person record or a protected field.
- `CB_AUD`: The over-disclosure is confined to the organization at a restricted floor; whether it is acceptable depends on an institutional audience decision that is outstanding.
- `CB_PLAT`: The floor is a platform role title over cross-organization data; it blocks unless the title is shown to be held only by platform staff under an adopted mandate.
- `CB_INERT`: The route is inert or misnamed; it blocks release only while it is reachable or advertised as a working capability.
- `CB_DEP`: The impact depends on a deployment or scheduler fact that the repository cannot establish.
- `CB_INT`: The integrity defect is limited to role-restricted callers of the same organization; it blocks while those callers can reach the route.
- `RR`: The frozen record does not carry enough evidence to place the defect; the missing evidence is named.
