## Design note

Product design decisions can be settled by the product specification review. Institutional authority decisions name who may issue, approve, decide or receive something. Nothing in this repository settles an institutional authority decision, so each stays outstanding and the behaviour it governs stays inactive.

## Authority states

An outstanding institutional authority decision does not stop engineering. It stops production or institutional activation of the authority the decision governs.

- `ENGINEERING_IMPLEMENTATION`: PERMITTED. Drafts, queues, previews, read models, refusal paths and positive paths may be built.
- `SYNTHETIC_AUTHORIZED_PATH_TESTING`: PERMITTED. A positive path may be demonstrated with test-issued authority in a synthetic organization that is marked synthetic and holds no real member, money or privileged record.
- `PRODUCTION_INSTITUTIONAL_ACTIVATION`: PROHIBITED until a duly authorized body adopts the decision. No real organization receives the authority.
- `EXTERNAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_ADOPTION`: REQUIRED. The decision is settled outside this repository and is recorded here only as outstanding.

Synthetic authority: Test-issued authority exists only inside a test fixture. It is never copied into, derived from or promoted to a real organization.

Prohibited without institutional adoption:

- `REAL_FINANCIAL_MANDATES`: Issuing a real financial mandate. FINANCIAL_MANDATES_NOT_ISSUED stays the answer.
- `INSTITUTIONAL_OFFICES`: Establishing, naming or filling an institutional office, including a steward office.
- `REAL_PAYMENTS`: Authorizing or executing a real payment, payment rail or export to an external accounting system.
- `PRIVILEGED_DISCLOSURE`: Disclosing privileged, counsel-controlled or third-party restricted material to anyone.
- `COUNSEL_EXECUTION`: Activating counsel execution or transmitting anything to counsel. UE-CONV-G-003 stays an external gate.

Product acceptance of this baseline activates none of these.
