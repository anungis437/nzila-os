## How to read the labels

- `HISTORICALLY_EVIDENCED`: The identifier is individually evidenced by a recovered historical record. Only the identifier and the recorded title or status are historical; the specification is new.
- `PROPOSED_NEW_BASELINE`: The specification is a new design in this proposed baseline. Every contract carries this label.
- `IMPLEMENTED_SOURCE`: The cited source files show some implementation. A cited file is not verification, conformance was not checked, and no run exists.
- `REQUIRES_INSTITUTIONAL_ADOPTION`: The behaviour depends on an institutional authority decision that is outstanding. It stays inactive until a duly authorized body adopts it.

The labels are never merged. A contract can carry several, and each is derived from a separate field the validator re-checks.

## Authorization bases used

- `ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP_NAVIGATION` (navigation): A live membership in the active organization. A navigation entry is only an entry point: every resource behind it is decided individually, and an entry is not shown when its destination would resolve to an unavailable state.
- `CASE_OWNER_OR_ASSIGNEE` (case): A live owner or assignee relationship to this one case, held by a principal whose live membership carries a representation capacity (steward, chief steward or officer). The relationship is a fact about the case; the capacity is eligibility only. Closing, disclosing to the member and exporting sealed evidence are additionally limited to the primary handler.
- `CASE_PRIMARY_HANDLER_ONLY` (case): The owner or assignee relationship, limited to the primary handler. A collaborator grant can never carry these actions.
- `CASE_SUPERVISORY_MANDATE` (case): A live supervisory mandate whose unit, territory or resource scope covers this case and whose powers name the action. Seniority, role title and platform status are not inputs. Privileged matters are outside every mandate except assignment. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `CASE_TRIAGE_DESIGNATION` (case): A live, expiring, revocable triage designation for the cases scope, granted by an organization administrator to an eligible staff member for at most 90 days. It exposes the unassigned queue metadata only and grants no kernel action on any case. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `DIRECTORY_SCOPE_BY_CASE_RELATIONSHIP` (member): The member directory policy: a steward sees self and the members of cases assigned to them; supervisory and officer scopes widen the directory without sensitive attributes only through the stated policy; only platform administrators cross organizations.
- `DOCUMENT_CASE_RELATIONSHIP_AND_CLASS` (document): A kernel decision on the linked case together with the document classification. A privileged classification needs an explicit independent privileged-access grant; a role never reaches it.
- `ROLE_ELIGIBILITY_ONLY` (role): A role, capacity, title or seniority makes a person eligible to hold an authority. It never is the authority. A denial contract names what a role never grants.
- `SELF_SCOPED` (self): The signed-in principal’s own preferences and session. No other principal’s resource is touched.
- `THREAD_PARTICIPANT` (message): The member of the conversation, or its assignee when that membership carries an assignee capacity. Participation follows the conversation row; no mandate and no delegation confers it.
- `THREAD_TRIAGE_DESIGNATION` (message): A live triage designation for the messages scope. It exposes the unassigned conversation queue only and is not a read of any conversation. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `WORKAID_REFERRAL_WITH_CONSENT` (workaid): The case relationship that permits a referral, plus a live member disclosure consent whose categories, purpose, recipient and expiry cover what moves. A union-authored record can never open counsel material. Consent wording is an institutional instrument that this baseline does not write. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
