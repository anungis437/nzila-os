## Authority states

An outstanding institutional authority decision does not stop engineering. It stops production or institutional activation of the authority the decision governs.

- `ENGINEERING_IMPLEMENTATION`: PERMITTED. Drafts, queues, previews, read models, refusal paths and positive paths may be built.
- `SYNTHETIC_AUTHORIZED_PATH_TESTING`: PERMITTED. A positive path may be demonstrated with test-issued authority in a synthetic organization that is marked synthetic and holds no real member, money or privileged record.
- `PRODUCTION_INSTITUTIONAL_ACTIVATION`: PROHIBITED until a duly authorized body adopts the decision. No real organization receives the authority.
- `EXTERNAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_ADOPTION`: REQUIRED. The decision is settled outside this repository and is recorded here only as outstanding.

Synthetic authority: Test-issued authority exists only inside a test fixture. It is never copied into, derived from or promoted to a real organization.

Prohibited without institutional adoption:

- `REAL_FINANCIAL_MANDATES`: Issuing a real financial mandate. FINANCIAL_MANDATES_NOT_ISSUED stays the answer.
- `INSTITUTIONAL_OFFICES`: Establishing, naming or filling an institutional office, including a steward office.
- `REAL_PAYMENTS`: Authorizing or executing a real payment, payment rail or export to an external accounting system.
- `PRIVILEGED_DISCLOSURE`: Disclosing privileged, counsel-controlled or third-party restricted material to anyone.
- `COUNSEL_EXECUTION`: Activating counsel execution or transmitting anything to counsel. UE-CONV-G-003 stays an external gate.

Product acceptance of this baseline activates none of these.

## How to read the labels

- `HISTORICALLY_EVIDENCED`: The identifier is individually evidenced by a recovered historical record. Only the identifier and the recorded title or status are historical; the specification is new.
- `PROPOSED_NEW_BASELINE`: The specification is a new design in this proposed baseline. Every contract carries this label.
- `IMPLEMENTED_SOURCE`: The cited source files show some implementation. A cited file is not verification, conformance was not checked, and no run exists.
- `REQUIRES_INSTITUTIONAL_ADOPTION`: The behaviour depends on an institutional authority decision that is outstanding. It stays inactive until a duly authorized body adopts it.

The labels are never merged. A contract can carry several, and each is derived from a separate field the validator re-checks.

## Separation of duties

Viewing, preparing, approving, executing, reconciling and auditing stay separate powers. Whether one principal may hold two of them is decided by a separation rule evaluated against the actual transaction, account, amount, mandate, approval and principal. No rule is assumed for consecutive steps.

Stance: `CONFIGURABLE_NOT_UNIVERSAL`. Separation is evaluated against: transaction, account, amount, mandate, approval, principal. The three-person issuance design (SEP-SW-07) is evidence of an implemented software rule. It is not proof that an institution adopted it.

## Reports and exports

A report, an aggregate and an export are each authorized on their own. A view duty over the records behind a report does not carry them.

- `DISCLOSED_FIELDS`: Each class of field is granted: member-identifying, contact, banking and amount fields are separate grants.
- `AGGREGATION`: An aggregate over a population is a separate grant from the rows beneath it. A small population can identify an individual, so a minimum-population rule and suppression are decided per report.
- `SCOPE`: The accounts, periods, locals and bargaining units the report covers are named by the mandate.
- `SENSITIVITY`: Each report carries a sensitivity class. Member personal data, audit-restricted data and privileged data are never in a general report.
- `EXPORT`: An export is a separate duty-checked action with its own record naming requester, scope, fields, destination and time. An external accounting target needs DEC-IA-29.
- `INFERENCE`: Two permitted reports must not combine to disclose what neither may disclose. Comparison and differencing risk is reviewed per report.

No union finance report engine exists in source. Related decisions: DEC-IA-16, DEC-IA-23, DEC-IA-27, DEC-IA-29.

## Authorization bases used

- `ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP_NAVIGATION` (navigation): A live membership in the active organization. A navigation entry is only an entry point: every resource behind it is decided individually, and an entry is not shown when its destination would resolve to an unavailable state.
- `AUDITOR_APPOINTMENT_DUTY` (finance): The auditor capacity, which is earned only by a recorded appointment and never by role, together with a live mandate naming the audit duty and the period. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_ADMINISTRATION` (finance): Issuance, acceptance, suspension and revocation of a financial mandate under a live issuance rule. The implemented software rule SEP-SW-07 requires the issuer, the holder and the approver to be three different people, and SEP-SW-08 stops the issuer and approver activating the mandate; that is evidence of a software rule and not proof that an institution adopted it. The holder activates; an expired term is not activated. No mandate is issued from a request body or derived from a role title. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_APPROVE` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the approve duty, within its monetary limit and currency. The implemented software rules SEP-SW-01 and SEP-SW-02 refuse an approver who prepared the same transaction or who later executes it. An adverse party is refused. Institutional adoption of the separation is outstanding. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_AUDIT` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the audit duty. The implemented software rule SEP-SW-04 refuses an auditor who took part in the same transaction. Institutional adoption is outstanding. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_EXECUTE` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the execute duty. Execution is the money movement itself and happens only in the route that moves the money. The implemented software rules SEP-SW-02 and SEP-SW-03 refuse an executor who prepared or approved the same transaction, and a reconciler who took part in it. Institutional adoption is outstanding. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_PREPARE` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the prepare duty. The implemented software rule SEP-SW-01 stops a preparer approving the same transaction; whether an institution adopts that separation is outstanding. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_RECONCILE` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the reconcile duty. The implemented software rule SEP-SW-03 refuses a reconciler who took part in the same transaction. Institutional adoption is outstanding. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_MANDATE_VIEW` (finance): A live financial mandate that names the account and the view duty, held by a principal with a financial capacity. A member may always view their own record. A role title, including the Secretary-Treasurer title, is not a mandate. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `FINANCIAL_SUBJECT_OWN_RECORD` (finance): The member viewing their own financial record, and nothing else’s.
- `PLATFORM_BOUNDARY_NO_AUTHORITY` (finance): Nzila platform billing is not union finance. Platform staff status and SaaS entitlement confer no authority over union financial records.
- `ROLE_ELIGIBILITY_ONLY` (role): A role, capacity, title or seniority makes a person eligible to hold an authority. It never is the authority. A denial contract names what a role never grants.
- `SELF_SCOPED` (self): The signed-in principal’s own preferences and session. No other principal’s resource is touched.
- `TRUSTEE_APPOINTMENT_DUTY` (finance): The trustee capacity, which is earned only by a recorded appointment and never by role, together with a live mandate naming the duty. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
- `WORKAID_REFERRAL_WITH_CONSENT` (workaid): The case relationship that permits a referral, plus a live member disclosure consent whose categories, purpose, recipient and expiry cover what moves. A union-authored record can never open counsel material. Consent wording is an institutional instrument that this baseline does not write. It needs an institutional instrument that is outstanding.
