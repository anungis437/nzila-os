# Pilot Overview — UnionEyes (CUPE)

> What the pilot includes, how it works now, and what happens next.

## What Is the Pilot?

The pilot is a time-limited trial of UnionEyes with a smaller group of CUPE
members, stewards, officers, and admins. The goal is to validate the current
workflow in production-like use: member intake, steward casework, and outcome
visibility.

## What's Included

| Feature area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Member-facing stream for updates, requests, and intake activity |
| **Priorities** | Rep-facing action view for what needs attention next |
| **Work** | Steward and LRO casework surface |
| **Outcomes** | Resolution and follow-through view |
| **Pilot Program** | Officer-facing pilot health, milestones, and progress tracking |
| **Onboarding and support** | Guided setup, checklisting, and pilot support materials |

## What's Not Included (Yet)

The pilot intentionally limits scope so the core workflow can be validated
cleanly. Broader modules and extended platform capabilities may be enabled
after the pilot depending on results and operating needs.

## How It Works

1. **Admin sets up** — Configure structure, invite users, and verify role routing
2. **Members submit intakes** — Workplace issues enter the system through intake, not direct case creation
3. **Stewards and officers manage work** — Reps review, convert, assign, and advance official casework
4. **Users follow progress** — Members use Inbox and Outcomes; reps use Priorities and Work
5. **Officers monitor pilot health** — Pilot metrics and readiness are reviewed in `/dashboard/pilot`

## Pilot Boundaries

- **Users:** The agreed pilot cohort only
- **Duration:** Time-limited per rollout agreement
- **Data:** Isolated within the organization's scope
- **Support:** Via the [Admin Runbook](../pilot/cupe/CUPE_PILOT_ADMIN_RUNBOOK.md) and [Support SOP](../pilot/cupe/CUPE_PILOT_SUPPORT_SOP.md)

## Success Criteria

The pilot is successful when real users consistently move work through the
product's core path and the platform shows healthy operational signals.

Typical indicators include:

- Sustained active usage across the pilot window
- Repeated intake and follow-up activity
- Evidence that reps are advancing work inside UnionEyes
- Sufficient pilot-health signals to support a go or no-go decision

## What Happens After the Pilot?

If the pilot is healthy, the organization can broaden rollout and enable more
of the platform. If not, the admin team and platform team review adoption,
friction, and feedback before the next decision point.
