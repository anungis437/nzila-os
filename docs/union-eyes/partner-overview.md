# Union Eyes — Partner Overview

> A plain-language explanation for partners, lawyers, and stakeholders.
> No technical knowledge required.

## What Is Union Eyes?

Union Eyes is a case management system built for unions. It replaces
phone calls, emails, paper forms, and spreadsheets with a single
structured record for every workplace issue — from first report
through resolution.

## What Does It Do?

**For members:**
- Report a workplace issue through a simple online form
- Track the progress of their issue from start to finish
- Add notes or updates as things develop

**For stewards (union representatives):**
- See all cases assigned to them in one place
- Track deadlines and follow-up dates
- Keep a complete history of everything that happened on each case

**For administrators:**
- Monitor how the system is being used
- See which cases are overdue or need attention
- Generate reports for leadership

## How Is Data Protected?

- Each union organization's data is completely separate from others
- Users can only see cases they're involved in
- All access is controlled by role (member, steward, admin)
- The system keeps a complete audit trail of who did what and when
- For full security details, see the [Security Policy](../../SECURITY.md)

## What Union Eyes Does NOT Do

- **No surveillance** — The system does not monitor employees, track location, or watch activity. Members decide when and what to submit.
- **No automated decisions** — No case is opened, escalated, closed, or prioritized by AI without a human choosing to do so. Every status change is a person's decision.
- **No override of union processes** — Union Eyes does not replace collective agreements, grievance procedures, or the role of a steward. It records what happens; the union runs the process.
- **No employer access** — Employers cannot see cases, member data, or system activity. The system is for the union only.
- **No data selling or sharing** — Member data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for purposes outside the union's own operations.

## What Is the Pilot?

The pilot is a trial run with a small group of users. It includes the core
features (creating and tracking cases) but not the more advanced tools like
analytics or bargaining support. The goal is to confirm the system works
well for real workplace issues before rolling it out more widely.

## What Does This Mean for the Union?

- **Documentation clarity** — Every case has a structured record: what happened, when it happened, who was involved, and what was done about it. No ambiguity.
- **Reduced reliance on memory** — Stewards and members don't have to reconstruct events weeks later from memory or scattered emails. The timeline is already there.
- **Defensibility** — If a case goes to arbitration, the documented history stands on its own. Timestamped, uneditable, complete.
- **Accountability** — Stewards and admins can see what needs attention at a glance — nothing falls through the cracks.

## Who Built This?

Union Eyes is part of the Nzila platform, built specifically for union
operations. It's designed by people who understand how unions work — not
a generic helpdesk repurposed for labour relations.
