# UnionEyes — Partner Overview

> A plain-language explanation for partners, lawyers, and stakeholders.
> No technical knowledge required.

## What Is UnionEyes?

UnionEyes is a union operations system built around the actual representation
workflow. It replaces fragmented email, phone, paper, and spreadsheet handling
with a structured record from first member intake through steward work and
final outcome.

## What Does It Do?

**For members:**

- Submit a workplace issue through a guided intake
- Follow requests, updates, and progress in one place
- Review outcomes and next steps without relying on scattered communications

**For stewards and LROs:**

- Review incoming intakes
- Convert appropriate intakes into official casework
- Assign, advance, document, and resolve work inside a structured workflow

**For officers and administrators:**

- Monitor pilot and operational health
- See where work is aging or blocked
- Export defensible records and leadership summaries

## Why the Intake-to-Outcome Model Matters

UnionEyes deliberately separates:

- **Member intake** — the initial issue report
- **Official casework** — the steward or LRO-managed representation process
- **Outcomes** — the result, resolution, or follow-through

That separation makes the workflow clearer, more auditable, and closer to how
representation work actually happens.

## How Is Data Protected?

- Each organization's data is isolated from every other organization
- Access is role-based and scoped to the work a user is authorized to see
- The system records auditable activity for intake, review, conversion, work, and outcomes
- For full security details, see the [Security Policy](../../SECURITY.md)

## What UnionEyes Does Not Do

- **No surveillance** — It does not monitor workers, devices, or movement. Members decide what to submit.
- **No automated representation decisions** — The platform does not independently open, convert, escalate, or resolve work. Human representatives make those decisions.
- **No override of union process** — UnionEyes supports union workflows; it does not replace representation judgment, collective agreements, or steward authority.
- **No employer access** — Employers do not get access to union records through the platform.
- **No data selling or advertising use** — Member data is not sold or reused outside the union's own operations.

## What Is the Pilot?

The pilot is a trial deployment with a limited user group. It validates the
core workflow in practice: intake capture, representative work management,
and outcome visibility.

## What Does This Mean for the Union?

- **Clear intake handling** — A member issue enters once and can be reviewed consistently.
- **Better operational memory** — Representatives do not have to reconstruct history from inboxes and recollection.
- **Defensible records** — Audit history, notes, uploads, and outcomes stay tied to the work.
- **Better oversight** — Officers can see whether work is moving and where intervention is needed.

## Who Built This?

UnionEyes is part of the Nzila platform, designed specifically for union
operations and representation workflows.
