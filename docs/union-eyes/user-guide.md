# User Guide — Union Eyes

> Complete guide for members and stewards using Union Eyes.

## 1. Logging In

Navigate to your Union Eyes URL and sign in with your email address. First-time
users will see a 4-step onboarding wizard that explains the Dashboard, how to
create a case, how to add updates, and where to find help.

## 2. The Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the **Pilot Dashboard**. It has three sections:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **My Cases** | Count of your active cases with a direct link |
| **Create Case** | Green card — one click to start a new case |
| **Recent Activity** | Latest updates across your cases |

The Dashboard also displays **Signals** — real-time alerts for items that
need attention (overdue cases, SLA risks, required actions).

## 3. Creating a Case

1. Click **Create Case** on the Dashboard
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — Short description of the issue
   - **Category** — Select from the list (e.g., Safety, Scheduling, Discrimination)
   - **Priority** — Low, Medium, High, or Urgent (defaults to Medium)
   - **Description** — Full details of what happened
   - **Date** — When it happened (cannot be a future date)
3. Click **Submit**
4. You'll receive a case number (e.g., `GRV-2025-0001`)

## 4. Viewing Your Cases

Click **My Cases** on the Dashboard to see all your cases. Each case shows:

- Case number and title
- Current status badge
- Priority level
- Date created

### Status Badges

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟡 Submitted | Waiting for acknowledgement |
| 🔵 Under Review | Being reviewed by a steward |
| 🟣 Assigned | A steward is handling it |
| 🟠 Investigation | Active investigation underway |
| ⚪ Pending Documentation | More information needed from you |
| 🟢 Resolved | Decision made |
| 🔴 Rejected | Case not actionable |
| ⚫ Closed | Complete |

## 5. Case Detail & Timeline

Click on any case to open the detail view. The **Timeline** shows every event
in chronological order — notes, status changes, assignments, and system events.

## 6. Adding Updates

On the case detail page, type your note in the Timeline section and click
**Add Update**. Updates are visible to you and your assigned steward.

## 7. Feedback

After creating your first case, a small feedback widget appears in the
bottom-right corner. It's completely optional — use it to share what's
working or what could be better.

## 8. Getting Help

- **In-app**: Click the **?** icon in the navigation
- **Your steward**: Contact your workplace steward directly
- **Admin support**: Reach out to your union administrator

## Terminology

| UI Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Case | A tracked workplace issue |
| Timeline | Chronological log of events on a case |
| Dashboard | Your home screen |
| Update | A note or status change on a case |
| Signal | An alert for something that needs attention |
