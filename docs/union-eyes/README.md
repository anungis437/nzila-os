## What Union Eyes Does

Union Eyes lets you:

- **Create cases** — Report workplace issues in plain language
- **Track progress** — See where your case sits at every step
- **Collaborate** — Stewards and members work together via the Timeline
- **Stay informed** — Dashboard shows what needs attention right now

## Key Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Case** | A tracked workplace issue — the primary unit of work |
| **Timeline** | The chronological log of everything that happens on a case |
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen — shows your cases, signals, and quick actions |
| **Steward** | A union representative who handles cases on behalf of members |
| **Pilot** | A time-limited trial deployment with a smaller group of users |

For the full glossary, see [Glossary](../index/glossary.md).
