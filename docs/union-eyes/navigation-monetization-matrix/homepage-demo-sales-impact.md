# Homepage, Demo, and Sales Impact

> The downstream consequences of the navigation/monetization architecture for the homepage, the demo motion, and the sales motion.

## 1. Homepage Impact

### Old Hero (Rejected)

> "AI-Powered Union Operations"

This framing is rejected as commoditizing, surveillance-adjacent, and procurement-skeptical.

### New Hero (Adopted)

> **"Continuity-Safe Institutional Labor Operations"**

or equivalently:

> **"Operational Infrastructure for Institutional Labor Continuity"**

The homepage must communicate the institutional category from the first hero impression. AI framing, copilot framing, and assistant framing are forbidden above the fold.

### Required Hero Properties

- category-defining headline (institutional, not utility)
- continuity-safe modernization theme
- governance posture above the fold
- procurement-readable subhead
- no engagement bait CTAs
- no productivity metrics in marketing chrome

## 2. Demo Impact

The demo must **no longer** feel like:

- feature exploration
- AI tooling demonstration
- workflow demos

The demo now becomes **operational continuity storytelling**. Demo arcs include:

- steward continuity
- governance continuity
- onboarding continuity
- institutional memory continuity
- operational stabilization
- modernization without disruption

Demo storytelling reinforces the navigation tiers — every demo step illustrates an operational maturity surface, never a feature comparison.

## 3. Sales Impact

Sales motion shifts from:

> software utility selling

toward:

> institutional operational risk reduction.

This dramatically improves:

- procurement posture
- executive trust
- federation trust
- modernization safety perception

Sales conversations are no longer "here are the features you get" — they are "here is the operational maturity tier appropriate to your institution, and here is the procurement posture that protects you."

## 4. Final Enterprise Result

The navigation itself communicates **institutional maturity**. The UX becomes:

- calmer
- deeper
- more operationally coherent
- more procurement-safe
- more institutionally defensible
- much harder to commoditize

Competitors can copy dashboards, AI, workflows, and summaries. They cannot copy:

> a coherent institutional operational philosophy embodied consistently across navigation, gating, cognition, cadence, governance, continuity, onboarding, and monetization simultaneously.

That coherence is now **the UE moat**.
