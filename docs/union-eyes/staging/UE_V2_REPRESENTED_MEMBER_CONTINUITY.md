# Represented member continuity

Source/native policy inspection exposed a concrete conversion break: the new case correctly records its staff converter as `created_by`, but the original member's identity was not retained. The loader derived only the converter's creator relationship, and the detail endpoint treated the internal-file access result as the only alternative to authorship. Generic related-case links and supplied names/emails cannot establish party identity.

Use the existing `grievant_id` user-identity field: a member intake derives it from the authenticated principal, conversion retains it (or the valid originating creator for an older intake), and the loader derives the existing `represented_member` relationship. The relationship prefilters include that stored party. The actual converter remains creator and audit/event actor. No assignment, mandate, role hierarchy or generic related-case linkage is promoted into authority. No schema change, historical backfill or business-data rewrite is performed.

A non-internal party must still obtain the kernel's `case:read` allow. The member detail response whitelists submission/progress fields, permits public case documents while excluding confidential working documents and strategy, and removes other actors' free-form event notes. The primary representative comes from live kernel relationships; the internal collaborator roster is withheld. Staff assignment and internal access remain separate governed actions; members use their own/shared document repository for documents they may access. The member's creator/party status cannot bypass recusal, classification or active-membership policy.

Local physical tests use the real resource loader and case kernel: the originating member gets member-visible read, but not internal read or assignment; unrelated and foreign identities are denied. Route tests bind intake identity from authentication, retain converter authorship and represented identity, and verify the member response omits staff notes and internal fields. Real deployed intake/conversion/member and steward checks remain required; local tests do not claim staging acceptance.
