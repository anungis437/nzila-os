# Full Operational Rhythm & Cadence Embodiment

> Doctrinal authorization to ensure the runtime embodies **sustainable institutional cadence** — review pacing, notification pacing, escalation pacing, governance reminders, continuity pacing, onboarding pacing.

## Objective

The runtime must increasingly feel **sustainable under institutional load**. Cadence is not a feature; it is the operational pulse of the institution.

## Required Targets

Refine across `apps/union-eyes/`:

- review pacing (weekly review, monthly review, quarterly review)
- notification pacing (no notification spam; cadence-bounded)
- escalation pacing (governance-safe, reviewer-of-record gated)
- operational summaries (daily / weekly / monthly cadence)
- governance reminders (continuity-safe, not nagging)
- continuity pacing (succession, transition, handoff)
- onboarding pacing (institutional commitment, not engagement gamification)

## Required Implementation

Actually refactor:

- notification systems (consolidated digests, not interruptions)
- summary systems (cadence-aligned, not real-time-everything)
- cadence surfaces (`/cadence`, `/weekly-review`, etc.)
- reminder posture (institutional, not gamified)
- escalation posture (governance-safe, reviewer-of-record)

## Canonical Cadence Posture

- **daily**: operational coherence (work, priority, outcomes)
- **weekly**: governance review (continuity, governance, executive)
- **monthly**: institutional cadence (cadence, stewardship, memory)
- **quarterly**: maturity progression (assurance, sovereignty, federation)

## Anti-Patterns to Eliminate

- **notification spam** (every event becomes a notification)
- **engagement gamification** (streaks, badges, "you're on fire")
- **real-time-everything** (interrupting cadence with always-on dashboards)
- **escalation as default** (every priority becomes a P0 escalation)
- **reminder fatigue** (the runtime nagging the user)

## Scope Discipline

This document authorizes one PR (`refactor/ue-cadence-embodiment`) covering notification, summary, and reminder systems.
