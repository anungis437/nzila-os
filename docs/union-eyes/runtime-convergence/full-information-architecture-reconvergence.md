# Full Information Architecture Reconvergence

> Doctrinal authorization to rebuild runtime IA around **institutional operational cognition** rather than around historically accumulated route groups.

## Objective

The runtime must increasingly feel **institutionally embodied** — not historically accumulated. IA is the topology of operational cognition: route groups, page hierarchy, breadcrumbs, and runtime transitions must each express maturity, continuity, governance, and stewardship.

## Required Targets

Converge into coherent operational maturity flows:

- Work
- Priority
- Outcomes
- Intelligence (→ cognition)
- Governance
- Continuity
- Executive
- Cadence
- Onboarding
- Operations
- Stewardship
- Memory
- Assurance

## Required Implementation

Actually refactor:

- `app/[locale]/(group)/...` route-group topology
- page hierarchy (parent/child relationships)
- operational transitions between sibling pages
- breadcrumbs (operational, not technical)
- operational summaries on landing pages
- runtime flows (executive, governance, continuity, onboarding)
- onboarding flows (maturity-gated, not feature-gated)

## Anti-Patterns to Eliminate

- **dashboard-centric** IA (where every surface is a dashboard with KPIs at the top)
- **wizards-as-taxonomy** (where multi-step flows live as top-level routes)
- **flat route lists** without operational grouping
- **duplicate landing pages** for the same operational surface
- **historically appended** routes that fragment the cognition surface

## Required Posture

IA must be **legible to executives** in under sixty seconds. A reviewer-of-record, an executive, or a procurement officer must be able to read the topology and immediately understand: where institutional memory lives, where cadence is enforced, where governance is observed, where continuity is preserved.

## Scope Discipline

This document authorizes a single PR (`refactor/ue-information-architecture-reconvergence`) that re-organizes route groups and consolidates duplicate landing surfaces. Symbol renames, package renames, and FSM merges are separate PRs.
