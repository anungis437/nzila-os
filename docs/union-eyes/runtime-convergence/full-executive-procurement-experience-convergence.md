# Full Executive & Procurement Experience Convergence

> Doctrinal authorization to ensure executive and procurement journeys fully embody the doctrine — operational stabilization, not software acquisition.

## Objective

Executives must increasingly feel **operational stabilization**, not software acquisition. Procurement must feel calm, governance-readable, continuity-readable, and audit-readable from first touch through pilot.

## Required Targets

Converge:

- executive walkthroughs
- governance walkthroughs
- trust-center flows
- procurement flows
- pilot walkthroughs
- onboarding walkthroughs
- operational proof flows

## Required Implementation

Actually refine across `apps/union-eyes/`:

- operational pacing (no speed-feature framing; institutional cadence)
- proof readability (every claim is anchored to evidence and reviewer-of-record)
- governance readability (controls and oversight visible without drilling)
- continuity readability (continuity posture visible at every step)
- audit readability (audit-of-record present in trust center, not buried)
- procurement calmness (no salesy urgency, no AI hype, no growth-marketing patterns)
- institutional legitimacy signaling (category statements, not feature lists)

## Canonical Executive Posture

The executive walkthrough must read as a **governance-safe operational stabilization briefing**, not as a product tour. The procurement walkthrough must read as a **continuity-safe institutional commitment**, not as a SaaS evaluation.

## Anti-Patterns to Eliminate

- **growth marketing patterns** in trust center (e.g., social proof reels, customer logos as trust signals)
- **AI-as-trust-signal** anywhere in executive flows
- **feature-as-proof** anywhere in procurement flows
- **urgency framings** ("limited spots", "starting at", "save N%")
- **dashboard-as-onboarding** (showing KPI dashboards before establishing the operational story)

## Scope Discipline

This document authorizes one PR (`refactor/ue-executive-procurement-experience-convergence`) covering executive/procurement-facing surfaces only.
