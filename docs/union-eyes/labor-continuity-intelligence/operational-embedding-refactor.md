# Operational Embedding Refactor

## Objective
Embed labor continuity intelligence directly into daily operations so it is infrastructural and workflow-native, not a separate AI tool.

## Embedding Targets
- Onboarding
- Grievance review
- Governance review
- Committee operations
- Steward transitions
- Executive briefings
- Continuity reviews
- Operational cadence systems

## Embedding Requirements
### Workflow-native continuity intelligence
Continuity guidance appears in the same pane where decisions and handoffs occur.

### Continuity summaries inside reviews
Review workflows show continuity posture, unresolved risks, and escalation requirements.

### Onboarding continuity surfaces
Onboarding includes memory transfer summaries and role-safe guidance.

### Grievance continuity panels
Grievance workspace shows lineage and context restoration rather than chat-first behavior.

### Governance continuity summaries
Governance pages provide continuity framing and review requirements.

### Executive continuity briefings
Executive summaries prioritize continuity posture and institution stabilization signals.

## Refactor Actions
- Reframe CBA intelligence surface as labor continuity operations.
- Replace assistant-centric onboarding copy with continuity-support language.
- Add governance-safe notes and escalation cues in grievance intelligence surfaces.
- Align executive copy around continuity posture and human review cadence.
- Align Console weekly review framing with continuity-safe interpretation language.
