# Onboarding Continuity Intelligence

## Objective
Reduce institutional memory loss during onboarding and transition events.

## Supported Roles
- New stewards
- New reviewers
- New governance operators
- Committee transitions
- Leadership transitions

## Required Implementations
### Continuity onboarding summaries
Provide role-based summaries of:
- Active responsibilities
- Current continuity risks
- Open governance dependencies
- Escalation and support channels

### Operational history guides
Provide compressed history of:
- Recent decisions
- Current casework and governance posture
- Prior transition events and outcomes

### Governance continuity orientation
Present governance-safe behavior expectations:
- Boundaries of interpretation support
- Human authority preservation
- Escalation checkpoints

### Institutional memory onboarding panels
Expose institutional memory artifacts:
- Precedent map
- Policy and agreement references
- Steward/committee continuity notes

## Onboarding Design Rules
- Calm language over urgency theater
- Continuity readiness over feature walkthroughs
- Operational stewardship over assistant anthropomorphism

## Success Metrics
- Faster time-to-operational-readiness for new operators
- Reduced context drop during transitions
- Increased escalation correctness
- Lower repeat clarification burden on senior stewards
