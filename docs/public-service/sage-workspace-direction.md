# SAGE Workspace Direction

SAGE = Service Assurance, Governance, and Evidence.

## Positioning

SAGE is the future workspace direction for organizations that later need a repeatable operating layer for service assurance, governance evidence, continuity risk tracking, and modernization readiness.

SAGE is not currently the public-service front-door offer.

SAGE is not presented here as a launched platform claim.

## What SAGE may support later

- repeatable continuity intelligence workflows
- longitudinal evidence and control tracking
- governance and accountability reporting layers
- review-oriented risk and readiness operations

## Human boundary

SAGE is not designed to replace public-service judgment, legal review, HR decision-making, equity offices, internal audit, or program accountability.

Its intended role is to organize evidence, trace obligations, surface uncertainty, and support human-reviewed decisions.
