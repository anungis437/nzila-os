# SAGE Workspace — One-Page Summary

*Service Assurance & Governance Evidence — the future workspace architecture behind CIVIC and CLEAR.
This is a credibility companion to the canonical architecture, not product copy. Nothing in SAGE is
available or launched today.*

## What SAGE is

SAGE — **Service Assurance & Governance Evidence** — is the future workspace architecture behind CIVIC
and CLEAR. It describes how public institutions could organize service continuity, governance
evidence, review routing, decision context, and assurance-ready outputs in a controlled,
human-reviewed evidence workspace.

SAGE is future-facing only. Nothing in SAGE is available or launched today; this document describes a
direction, not a system.

## Relationship to CIVIC and CLEAR

The three layers are deliberately simple:

- **CIVIC** opens the conversation — the public-service continuity front door.
- **CLEAR** defines the evidence discipline — the repeatable, human-reviewed method.
- **SAGE** describes the future workspace architecture that could make CLEAR repeatable at
  institutional scale.

SAGE inherits CLEAR's boundaries. It does not expand CLEAR into automated decisions or compliance
certification.

## Why SAGE matters

Public institutions often have evidence, but it is fragmented — spread across systems, inboxes, people,
records, audit files, HR files, service systems, policy documents, and operational workarounds. During
workforce transition and modernization, the evidence needed to explain decisions, preserve service
logic, and maintain accountability becomes difficult to find, trust, and review.

CLEAR provides the discipline to turn that fragmented evidence into decision-grade material. SAGE
describes how an institution could apply that discipline repeatably — with provenance and uncertainty
preserved — while systems of record and human accountability stay exactly where they are.

## What SAGE would organize

SAGE describes future workspace capability groups, not current features. They would help organize:

- institutional questions
- evidence source registers
- CLEAR brief workspaces
- proposed risk-tier and review-routing records (human-confirmed)
- accountable-function maps
- decision-context logs
- continuity memory maps
- implementation traceability views
- modernization readiness views
- human-review risk registers
- assurance package exports
- follow-up evidence trackers

Risk-tiering and review routing would always be recorded as *proposed* and confirmed by accountable
humans — SAGE would never assign them automatically.

## Canadian public-sector fit

SAGE would support evidence organization for Canadian public-sector obligations, including:

- service and digital management
- information and data governance
- privacy and ATIP
- records and retention
- accessibility
- Official Languages
- GBA Plus
- automated-decision review
- auditability
- values and ethics
- departmental accountability

For these areas, SAGE would help organize evidence, provenance, review records, uncertainty, and
next evidence needed. It would not certify compliance or replace institutional review processes.
Government of Canada instruments would serve as a recognizable
federal baseline; other public-sector contexts would map to their own applicable requirements.

## Human-review boundary

SAGE would preserve the review path: evidence, provenance, confidence, routing, human-review records,
uncertainty, and next evidence needed. It keeps the material a leader needs to review, explain, and
preserve through change.

SAGE would not produce the conclusion. Accountable humans and institutional authorities would remain
responsible for interpreting evidence, applying law and policy, consulting required functions, and
making decisions through their own authorities.

## What SAGE does not replace

- SAGE is not a current product.
- SAGE is not a procurement offer.
- SAGE is not a compliance engine.
- SAGE is not an automated decision system.
- SAGE is not a system of record.
- SAGE is not a scoring dashboard.
- SAGE does not make findings of racism, discrimination, wrongdoing, misconduct, or legal breach.
- SAGE does not score, rank, or rate people, groups, programs, or institutions.
- SAGE does not replace departmental systems, records authorities, privacy/ATIP, legal, HR, equity,
  accessibility, Official Languages, audit, program authority, or executive accountability.

SAGE is a future workspace architecture that would make CLEAR repeatable at institutional scale while
keeping human review authoritative and departmental authority intact. That restraint is the point.
