# Workforce Transition and Institutional Memory

## Trigger conditions

Early retirements, attrition, restructuring, and role transitions can increase continuity risk when institutional knowledge remains tacit.

## What can break

- tacit process knowledge leaves with individuals
- decision rationale becomes hard to reconstruct
- ownership drifts across teams without clear accountability
- onboarding quality declines under speed pressure
- service continuity depends on informal handoffs

## Why this matters

If knowledge transfer is weak, modernization and service redesign can lock in hidden fragility instead of improving resilience.

## CIVIC relevance

CIVIC focuses on preserving continuity and traceability so institutions can manage transition while maintaining accountability and trust.
