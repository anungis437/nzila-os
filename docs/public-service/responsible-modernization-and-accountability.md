# Responsible Modernization and Accountability

## Modernization pressure

Public institutions face pressure to digitize, automate, and deploy AI-enabled workflows while preserving service quality, recourse, and institutional legitimacy.

## Operational risk

Modernization can outpace governance controls if evidence, ownership, and accountability are not traceable through implementation.

## Control expectations

- explicit evidence trails
- clear ownership and approval paths
- auditable change rationale
- human review at consequential decision points
- recourse and transparency mechanisms

## Human boundary

No autonomous legal, HR, equity, or employment decisions are in scope.

No automated discrimination findings or legal advice are in scope.

## CIVIC and CLEAR linkage

CIVIC provides the public-service continuity framing.

CLEAR provides the evidence discipline for organizing modernization signals into human-reviewable briefs.
