## 1. Purpose

Decide whether CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE is ready for controlled external review, and under what constraints.

This memo does not authorize public launch, demo, procurement, pilot, or product positioning. It decides
one narrow question: whether a single trusted external reviewer may react to the CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE framing
under strict feedback-only conditions.

## 2. Decision posture

- Not public launch.
- Not demo.
- Not procurement.
- Not pilot.
- Not product.
- Controlled external review only.

## 3. Evidence base

This decision rests on the completed internal build-out, all on main:

- Three fictional proof runs (Crown corporation, regulator, tribunal/ombuds office) with retrospectives and
  calibrations.
- Manual Operating Readiness Summary (GREEN internal / AMBER real-institution / RED external-public-product).
- Manual Operating Pack v0.1 (usability dry run passed; v0.1 closure note recorded).
- Internal doctrine map (OCI/OCRA → CIVIC → CLEAR → SAGE → Manual Operating Pack), including the corporate
  continuity exception.
- Proof Run 0.4 (health-system) remains deferred; no `004-*` folder exists.

## 4. Question to answer

Are we ready to let one trusted external reviewer react to the CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE framing under strict
feedback-only conditions?

## 5. Gate criteria (GREEN → AMBER)

The gate to controlled external review requires all of the following:

1. External reviewer is trusted / warm / bounded.
2. Review scope is feedback-only.
3. No real institution data is used.
4. No SAGE availability is implied.
5. No procurement/pilot/pricing language is included.
6. OCI/OCRA is not used as the public-service label.
7. CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE hierarchy is explained cleanly.
8. Reviewer receives only approved materials.
9. Feedback is captured through the validation protocol.
10. Any follow-up artifact requires separate review.

## 6. What this gate does not authorize

- No public route, page, or launch.
- No demo, pilot, or proof-of-concept for a real institution.
- No procurement, pricing, or sales conversation.
- No SAGE product or availability claim.
- No CLEAR certification or validation claim.
- No real-institution outreach or targeting.
- No use of OCI/OCRA as the public-service label.
- No new proof run, Proof Run 0.4, or `004-*` folder.

## 7. Boundary posture (unchanged)

- GREEN for internal fictional dry runs.
- AMBER for real-institution use.
- RED for external/public/software/product use.

Controlled external review is a bounded, feedback-only step inside AMBER — it does not move the stack toward
public or product use. SAGE remains future-facing and non-productized; CLEAR remains evidence-organizing;
CIVIC remains the public-service umbrella, not a procurement offer.

## 8. Recommendation

- YES — for controlled external review, provided all ten gate criteria are met.
- NO — for public launch, demo, pilot, procurement, or product positioning.

This is the clean AMBER gate.

## 9. Decision

Decision: Approve, in principle, a single controlled, feedback-only external review of the CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE
framing, conditional on satisfying all ten gate criteria before any material is shared.

Rationale: The internal build-out is coherent and boundaries have held across every proof run and
calibration. A bounded reaction from one trusted reviewer is a low-risk way to test the framing without
implying availability, positioning, or procurement.

Status: Internal decision recorded. External review not yet initiated. Owner confirmation required before
sharing any material.

## 10. Next recommended action

Confirm the trusted reviewer and assemble the approved feedback-only material set against the ten gate
criteria. Do not begin outreach, and do not create any public, demo, pilot, procurement, or product artifact
until the gate is satisfied and separately confirmed.
