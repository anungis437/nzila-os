## 1. Institutional question

How can the institution preserve complaint-pathway continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility
implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability without influencing complaint intake,
investigation decisions, protected disclosures, adjudicative or quasi-adjudicative processes, findings,
reasons, recommendations, remedies, or individual case outcomes?

## 2. Institution type and adaptation row

Institution type: Tribunal / ombuds office. Adaptation posture: independence-bearing complaint intake,
investigation, protected-disclosure handling, adjudication, evidence assessment, finding/recommendation
development, remedy, and case-outcome functions are excluded; only public complaint-pathway continuity,
public-guidance traceability, accessibility implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability are in
scope.

## 3. Scope and exclusions

**In scope:** complaint-pathway continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility implementation
evidence, and modernization reviewability, using public and (where authorized) administrative sources.

**Excluded:** complaint intake decisions, investigation decisions, evidence assessment, protected
disclosures, adjudicative or quasi-adjudicative processes, findings, reasons, recommendations, remedies,
individual case outcomes, case files, witness/complainant/respondent records, privileged legal advice, and
personal information.

## 4. Accountable functions

Executive sponsor, public guidance owner, intake/service owner, modernization lead, digital/IM lead,
privacy/access/records, legal, internal audit/risk, and accessibility/Official Languages/EDI. These
functions own review and any decision; CLEAR only organizes evidence for them.

## 5. Evidence source register summary

Evidence is drawn from public sources (mandate statement, complaint process guide, guidance inventory,
accessibility plan, service standards, modernization strategy, annual report, outreach materials) plus two
`[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` internal placeholders that were not accessed. See
[evidence-source-register.md](evidence-source-register.md). Overall source quality is public and
administrative; provenance is official; confidence ranges from Insufficient (unaccessed placeholders) to
High (published mandate).

## 6. CLEAR pillar findings

- **Continuity** — The public complaint process guide and service standards exist, but pathway steps and
  superseded-version archiving are inconsistent across public guidance. Moderate.
- **Legitimacy** — The public mandate and outreach materials anchor legitimacy; consistency between
  outreach and current guidance is not always explicit. Moderate.
- **Evidence** — Public-guidance traceability is partial: some guidance pages lack version history, so the
  evidence thread from mandate to published complaint pathway breaks in places. Low–Moderate.
- **Accountability** — Accountable functions are identifiable and the annual report discloses some
  continuity context; ownership of guidance-update history is not clearly assigned. Moderate.
- **Readiness** — The modernization strategy signals intent but does not clearly define reviewability gates
  before a change to the public complaint pathway becomes the default. Low–Moderate.

## 7. Evidence confidence

Per the calibrated confidence levels (Insufficient / Low / Low–Moderate / Moderate / High): most
public-source findings are Moderate; guidance-traceability and modernization-readiness findings are
Low–Moderate; the two `[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` placeholders are Insufficient (not accessed). No confidence level
converts any finding into a decision.

## 8. Risk tier

Level 3 — Elevated adjudication-adjacent boundary question, applied for this fictional dry run. In a real
engagement, risk tiering would require accountable human confirmation.

## 9. Human-review path

Public guidance owner and intake/service owner review complaint-pathway continuity and traceability
findings; modernization lead reviews reviewability; legal and privacy/access/records confirm boundary
adherence and that nothing approaches complaint intake, investigations, protected disclosures, adjudication,
findings, reasons, recommendations, remedies, or case outcomes; the executive sponsor confirms before
anything is treated as decision-grade.

## 10. Key gaps and uncertainty

- Version history and archiving for superseded public complaint-pathway guidance is inconsistent.
- Consistency between outreach materials and current guidance is not always explicit.
- Reviewability gates for modernization of the public pathway are undefined.
- The two `[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` sources were not accessed; their content is unknown.

## 11. Next evidence needed

Public guidance version-history inventory; a mapping from outreach materials to current guidance; a
documented modernization reviewability gate for the public complaint pathway — all obtainable from public or
authorized administrative sources without touching any excluded function.

## 12. Boundary check

No prohibited conclusions; no influence on or assessment of complaint intake, investigations, evidence
assessment, protected disclosures, adjudication, findings, reasons, recommendations, remedies, or case
outcomes; no independence-bearing function touched; no product/procurement language; source-system authority
preserved; personal information minimized; SAGE not described as launched, available, or productized.

## 13. Recommended decision posture

Proceed only as an internal evidence-organization exercise. Do not create public copy, product claims,
procurement language, institution-specific outreach, legal validation, procedural-fairness validation,
assurance opinion, compliance certification, complaint-resolution material, or adjudicative support material
from this proof run.
