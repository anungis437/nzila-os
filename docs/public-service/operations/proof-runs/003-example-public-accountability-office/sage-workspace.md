## 1. Workspace metadata

Fictional label: Example Public Accountability Office. Question reference: Proof Run 0.3. Operator: internal
proof-run reviewer (fictional). Dates: 2026-07-11.

## 2. Institution adaptation profile

Institution type: Tribunal / ombuds office. Best framing: public complaint-pathway continuity and
implementation traceability only. First safe wedge: complaint-pathway continuity + public-guidance
traceability + accessibility implementation evidence + modernization reviewability. Language to avoid: any
suggestion of influencing or assessing complaint intake, investigations, protected disclosures, adjudication,
findings, reasons, recommendations, remedies, or individual case outcomes.

## 3. Institutional question

How can the institution preserve complaint-pathway continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility
implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability without influencing any independence-bearing
complaint, investigation, protected-disclosure, adjudicative, finding, reason, recommendation, remedy, or
case-outcome function?

## 4. Red lines and exclusions

No influence on complaint intake, investigation decisions, evidence assessment, protected disclosures,
witness/complainant/respondent/whistleblower treatment, adjudicative or quasi-adjudicative processes,
findings, reasons, recommendations, remedies, or individual case outcomes. No legal conclusion, fairness
determination, misconduct/wrongdoing/breach finding, audit opinion, compliance certification,
scoring/ranking, direct data-access assumption, source-system replacement, or SAGE availability/product
claim.

## 5. Accountable-function map

Owns review/decision: executive sponsor, public guidance owner, intake/service owner, modernization lead,
digital/IM lead, privacy/access/records, legal, internal audit/risk, accessibility/Official Languages/EDI.
On the exclusion list: complaint intake, investigation, protected-disclosure handling, adjudication or
quasi-adjudication, evidence assessment, finding/recommendation development, remedy recommendation, and case
outcome determination.
