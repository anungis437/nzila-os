## 2. Institution adaptation profile

Institution type: Regulator. Best framing: policy-continuity and implementation traceability only. First
safe wedge: regulatory-policy continuity + public-guidance traceability + accessibility implementation
evidence + modernization reviewability. Language to avoid: any suggestion of influencing or assessing
investigations, enforcement, inspections, licensing, compliance, penalties, adjudication, or
regulated-entity outcomes.

## 3. Institutional question

How can the institution preserve regulatory-policy continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility
implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability without influencing any independence-bearing
regulatory function or regulated-entity outcome?

## 4. Red lines and exclusions

No influence on investigations, enforcement priorities, inspections, licensing decisions, compliance
determinations, penalties/sanctions, adjudicative or quasi-adjudicative processes, or regulated-entity
outcomes. No legal conclusion, audit opinion, compliance certification, scoring/ranking, direct
data-access assumption, source-system replacement, or SAGE availability/product claim.

## 5. Accountable-function map

Owns review/decision: executive sponsor, regulatory policy owner, public guidance owner, service
modernization lead, digital/IM lead, privacy/access/records, legal, internal audit/risk, accessibility/
Official Languages/EDI. On the exclusion list: enforcement, inspection, licensing, investigation, penalty/
sanction, adjudicative/quasi-adjudicative, and enforcement-linked complaint handling.
