## 1. Institutional question

How can the institution preserve regulatory-policy continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility
implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability without influencing investigations, enforcement
priorities, inspection decisions, licensing decisions, compliance determinations, penalties, adjudicative
processes, or regulated-entity outcomes?

## 2. Institution type and adaptation row

Institution type: Regulator. Adaptation posture: independence-bearing enforcement, inspection, licensing,
investigation, and adjudicative functions are excluded; only public policy-continuity, public-guidance
traceability, accessibility implementation evidence, and modernization reviewability are in scope.

## 3. Scope and exclusions

**In scope:** regulatory-policy continuity, public-guidance traceability, accessibility implementation
evidence, and modernization reviewability, using public and (where authorized) administrative sources.

**Excluded:** investigations, enforcement priorities, inspections, licensing decisions, compliance
determinations, penalties/sanctions, adjudicative or quasi-adjudicative processes, regulated-entity
outcomes, complaint files tied to enforcement, privileged legal advice, and personal information.

## 4. Accountable functions

Executive sponsor, regulatory policy owner, public guidance owner, service modernization lead, digital/IM
lead, privacy/access/records, legal, internal audit/risk, and accessibility/Official Languages/EDI. These
functions own review and any decision; CLEAR only organizes evidence for them.

## 5. Evidence source register summary

Evidence is drawn from public sources (mandate statement, regulatory policy, guidance inventory,
accessibility plan, service standards, digital strategy, annual report, consultation summary) plus two
`[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` internal placeholders that were not accessed. See
[evidence-source-register.md](evidence-source-register.md). Overall source quality is public and
administrative; provenance is official; confidence ranges from Insufficient (unaccessed placeholders) to
High (published mandate).

## 6. CLEAR pillar findings

- **Continuity** — Public regulatory policy and service standards exist, but superseded-policy archiving
  and version history are inconsistent across public guidance. Moderate.
- **Legitimacy** — The public mandate and consultation record anchor legitimacy; traceability from
  consultation outcomes to policy is not always explicit. Moderate.
- **Evidence** — Public-guidance traceability is partial: some guidance pages lack version history, so
  the evidence thread from authority to published guidance breaks in places. Low–Moderate.
- **Accountability** — Accountable functions are identifiable and the annual report discloses some
  continuity context; ownership of guidance-update history is not clearly assigned. Moderate.
- **Readiness** — The digital strategy signals modernization intent but does not clearly define
  reviewability gates before a change becomes the default. Low–Moderate.

## 7. Evidence confidence

Per the calibrated confidence levels (Insufficient / Low / Low–Moderate / Moderate / High): most
public-source findings are Moderate; guidance-traceability and modernization-readiness findings are
Low–Moderate; the two `[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` placeholders are Insufficient (not accessed). No confidence
level converts any finding into a decision.

## 8. Risk tier

Level 3 — Elevated regulatory-boundary question, applied for this fictional dry run. In a real engagement,
risk tiering would require accountable human confirmation.

## 9. Human-review path

Regulatory policy owner and public guidance owner review continuity and traceability findings; digital/IM
lead reviews modernization readiness; legal and privacy/access/records confirm boundary adherence; the
executive sponsor confirms nothing approaches an excluded function before anything is treated as
decision-grade.

## 10. Key gaps and uncertainty

- Version history and archiving for superseded public guidance is inconsistent.
- Traceability from consultation outcomes to policy is not always explicit.
- Reviewability gates for modernization are undefined.
- The two `[AUTHORIZED-ONLY]` sources were not accessed; their content is unknown.

## 11. Next evidence needed

Public guidance version-history inventory; a mapping from consultation outcomes to policy changes; a
documented modernization reviewability gate — all obtainable from public or authorized administrative
sources without touching any excluded function.

## 12. Boundary check

No prohibited conclusions; no influence on or assessment of investigations, enforcement, inspections,
licensing, compliance, penalties, adjudication, or regulated-entity outcomes; no independence-bearing
function touched; no product/procurement language; source-system authority preserved; personal information
minimized; SAGE not described as launched, available, or productized.

## 13. Recommended decision posture

Proceed only as an internal evidence-organization exercise. Do not create public copy, product claims,
procurement language, institution-specific outreach, regulatory assurance, compliance certification, or
enforcement-related conclusions from this proof run.
