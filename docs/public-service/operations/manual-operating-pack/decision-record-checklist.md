# Decision-Record Checklist

> **Internal operating template. Not public copy. Not for external use unless separately reviewed and approved.**

## 1. Purpose

Ensure every feedback-integration decision record stays controlled and never drifts into public copy,
product, or validation language.

## 2. Required decision fields

- Decision summary
- Feedback source
- Signal classification (or "not applicable — internal test")
- Affected layer
- Alternatives considered
- Decision
- Boundaries checked
- Follow-up

## 3. Allowed affected layers

- Internal operations only
- Kit calibration
- Proof-run documentation
- Retrospective documentation
- No change

## 4. Public-copy drift check

Confirm the decision does not create, imply, or authorize public copy. A dry run's default outcome is
"no public copy change."

## 5. Productization drift check

Confirm the decision does not imply SAGE availability, launch, productization, procurement readiness,
pricing, pilot, or demo.

## 6. Boundary drift check

Confirm the decision does not create a prohibited conclusion, scoring/ranking, source-system replacement,
direct data-access assumption, or a domain-specific violation (regulator, tribunal/ombuds, or deferred
health/PHI).

## 7. Calibration decision check

If the decision recommends a calibration, confirm it is low-risk, reusable, and routed to a small
calibration PR — not a public/product change.

## 9. No-go outcomes

The following affected layers are prohibited unless separately reviewed and approved:

- Public copy
- Product
- SAGE UI
- Pricing
- Procurement
- Pilot/demo
- External assurance
- Real-institution positioning

## 10. Completion criteria

- Affected layer is one of the allowed layers.
- Public-copy, productization, and boundary drift checks all pass.
- Any calibration is routed to a separate low-risk PR.
- The record remains internal only.
