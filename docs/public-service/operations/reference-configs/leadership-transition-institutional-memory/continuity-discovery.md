# Continuity discovery questions — Leadership Transition

Use in CIVIC conversations and CLEAR intake. Answers become evidence sources only
when authorized and registered — not free-text “AI memory.”

## A. Mandate and services

1. What services must not degrade during the transition window?
2. Which obligations are statutory vs policy vs informal practice?
3. Where is ownership currently ambiguous?

## B. Decision history

4. For the last material leadership or program choice: **why was option B chosen?**
5. What changed between the prior choice and today?
6. What remains unresolved, and who owns resolution?

## C. Knowledge location

7. Which knowledge is documented, which is tacit, which is person-dependent?
8. Which systems hold authoritative records (and who can grant access)?
9. What must the incoming executive learn in the first 30 / 90 days?

## D. Restrictions

10. What classes of material must the incoming executive **not** see by default?
11. Who can grant `authorized_only` / `sensitive` / `excluded` access?
12. Are any matters under legal hold, complaint, or privilege?

## E. Accountability

13. Who will sign decision records as the named human reviewer?
14. How will board oversight see progress without over-disclosure?
