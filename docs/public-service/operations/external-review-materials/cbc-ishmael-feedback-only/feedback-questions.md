# Feedback Questions

> **Controlled feedback-only reviewer material. Not public copy. Not a product, demo, pilot, procurement, pricing, or availability claim. Do not share unless owner-approved.**

Twelve questions, grouped. React to as many or as few as are useful.

## 1. Framing clarity

1. Does CIVIC sound like a useful way to frame public-institution change and continuity?
2. Do the five CIVIC words hold together, or does one feel forced?

## 2. Change-management usefulness

3. Would this framing help a change-management conversation, or add vocabulary without adding clarity?
4. What question would a change-management leader ask first?

## 3. Institutional fit

5. Does this framing fit how a public institution actually talks about continuity and implementation?
6. Where would it feel foreign or off-key inside a public institution?

## 4. Boundary clarity

7. Does CLEAR sound like a method, or does it sound too much like a certification?
8. Does SAGE read clearly as a future/manual workspace concept rather than available software?
9. Are the boundaries (no product, no procurement, no assessment) clear enough on a first read?

## 5. Language risks

10. Which phrases would raise concerns in a public institution?
11. What would you remove before showing this to a broader audience?

## 6. Next-step discipline

12. If this framing were useful, what would be a responsible, low-pressure next step to test it further —
    without turning it into a pitch?
