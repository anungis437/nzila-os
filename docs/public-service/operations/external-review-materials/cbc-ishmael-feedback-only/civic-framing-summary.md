# CIVIC Framing Summary

> **Controlled feedback-only reviewer material. Not public copy. Not a product, demo, pilot, procurement, pricing, or availability claim. Do not share unless owner-approved.**

CIVIC = Continuity, Implementation, Visibility, Integrity, Capacity

## 1. What CIVIC is

CIVIC is a public-service framing for helping institutions preserve continuity, clarify implementation
evidence, improve visibility into change work, maintain integrity of decision support, and build operational
capacity.

- **Continuity** — keeping institutional memory and mandated service delivery intact through change.
- **Implementation** — making the evidence of implementation work legible and reviewable.
- **Visibility** — improving how leaders and reviewers see change work in progress.
- **Integrity** — keeping decision support honest, traceable, and free of unsupported conclusions.
- **Capacity** — building durable operational capability rather than one-off outputs.

## 2. Why it matters

Public institutions carry continuity, accountability, and implementation obligations that outlast any single
project or team. CIVIC is a way to talk about that work in language a change-management audience can use.

## 3. What CIVIC helps organize

- continuity of institutional memory during change
- evidence of how implementation actually happened
- visibility into where change work stands
- integrity and traceability of decision support
- capacity that remains after a change effort ends

## 4. What CIVIC does not do

- does not make decisions
- does not score institutions
- does not certify readiness
- does not replace institutional judgment
- does not create a procurement offer
