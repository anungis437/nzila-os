# SAGE Concept Summary

> **Controlled feedback-only reviewer material. Not public copy. Not a product, demo, pilot, procurement, pricing, or availability claim. Do not share unless owner-approved.**

SAGE = Service Assurance & Governance Evidence

> **SAGE is not available software. It is not a launched platform, product, demo, pilot, procurement offer,
> or implementation proposal.**

SAGE is a future/manual workspace pattern for organizing evidence, continuity, governance traceability,
implementation questions, and review-ready institutional memory.

## 1. What SAGE means

SAGE describes a way of organizing service-assurance and governance evidence so it is review-ready. Today it
is a manual pattern and a future concept — a way of thinking about the workspace, not a system you can log
into.

## 2. Why the workspace pattern matters

Change and continuity work generates evidence that is often scattered. A consistent workspace pattern makes
that evidence easier to assemble, review, and hand off — before any question of software exists.

## 3. How SAGE relates to CIVIC and CLEAR

- CIVIC is the public-service framing (why the work matters).
- CLEAR is the evidence-organizing method (how the questions are organized).
- SAGE is the future/manual workspace pattern (where the organized evidence would live).

## 4. What SAGE does not do

- does not claim availability
- does not replace systems of record
- does not automate decisions
- does not score, certify, or validate
- does not create a product or procurement offer
