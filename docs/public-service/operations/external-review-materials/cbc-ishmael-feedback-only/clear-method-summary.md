# CLEAR Method Summary

> **Controlled feedback-only reviewer material. Not public copy. Not a product, demo, pilot, procurement, pricing, or availability claim. Do not share unless owner-approved.**

CLEAR = Continuity, Legitimacy, Evidence, Accountability, Readiness

## 1. What CLEAR is

CLEAR is an evidence-organizing method for making institutional change, service continuity, implementation
work, and accountability questions easier to review.

- **Continuity** — organizing what must persist through change.
- **Legitimacy** — keeping the framing of change grounded in mandate and authority.
- **Evidence** — assembling the record of what happened so it can be reviewed.
- **Accountability** — making it clear who is responsible and how decisions can be traced.
- **Readiness** — surfacing what is prepared and what is not, without overstating either.

## 2. What CLEAR organizes

- continuity requirements through a change
- the legitimacy basis for the work
- the evidence trail behind implementation
- accountability and traceability of decisions
- an honest readiness picture

## 3. Why it may help change-management conversations

Change-management work often stalls on unclear evidence and unclear accountability. CLEAR is a shared way to
organize those questions so a reviewer can follow them, without pretending to answer them automatically.

## 4. What CLEAR does not do

- does not certify
- does not validate legal, regulatory, procedural, clinical, or compliance conclusions
- does not score or rank
- does not automate decisions
- does not replace accountable institutional review
