> This defines how to run CLEAR and SAGE internally before software exists. It does not mean SAGE is
> launched, available, or productized.

## 1. Purpose

To make CLEAR usable immediately as a manual method, and SAGE operable as a manual internal workspace,
without creating a public product, route, UI, demo, pricing, pilot, procurement offer, or availability
claim.

## 2. Operating posture

- **CLEAR** is the method.
- **SAGE** is the future workspace architecture.
- **The Alpha Operating Kit** is the manual internal workspace used to run the method before software
  exists.

## 3. What exists today

- The CLEAR method (canonical) and its one-page summary.
- The SAGE architecture (canonical, future-facing) and its one-page summary.
- The Public-Institution Adaptation Framework.
- This manual operating kit of internal templates.

## 4. What does not exist today

- Any SAGE software, route, UI, or product.
- Any procurement-ready service, pricing, pilot, or demo.
- Any automated evidence system, scoring, or data integration.

## 5. Stage 1 workflow

1. Define institution type.
2. Select adaptation row.
3. Record institution-specific red lines.
4. Define institutional question.
5. Identify accountable functions.
6. Register evidence sources.
7. Map evidence to CLEAR pillars.
8. Apply confidence rubric.
9. Record gaps and uncertainty.
10. Draft CLEAR brief.
11. Build SAGE workspace summary.
12. Review boundaries.
13. Prepare assurance package.
14. Decide integration path.

## 6. Roles

- **Operator** — runs the kit end to end for one question.
- **Accountable function(s)** — the institution's own owners (program, service, CIO, privacy/ATIP,
  legal, audit, equity/accessibility/Official Languages, HR, executive sponsor) who review and decide.
- **Human reviewer** — the person who confirms the workspace before any package is shared.

The operator never decides; accountable humans and the institution own every conclusion.

## 8. Evidence handling rules

- Reference and link first; copy only when necessary and controlled.
- Preserve source-system authority; the kit never becomes the system of record.
- Minimize personal information; do not collect protected-domain material unless explicitly authorized
  and necessary.
- State confidence and uncertainty; never hide gaps.

## 9. Institution adaptation rules

- Select the institution type from the adaptation framework *before* framing.
- Record institution-specific red lines *before* evidence review.
- Avoid independence-bearing functions (newsroom/editorial, adjudication, enforcement, clinical care,
  student assessment/child protection, electoral administration).
- Apply the institution-specific boundary language.

## 10. Output package

The kit produces a human-reviewed assurance package (via the assurance-package checklist), containing
the institutional question, scope/exclusions, evidence source register, CLEAR findings, confidence
levels, risk tier, accountable functions, human-review record, and uncertainty/next evidence needed.

## 11. Quality gate

The kit is usable only when the operator can answer yes to all of the following:

- Have we selected the correct institution type?
- Have we recorded institution-specific red lines before evidence review?
- Have we avoided independence-bearing functions?
- Have we avoided direct data access or extraction assumptions?
- Have we preserved source-system authority?
- Have we stated confidence and uncertainty?
- Have we avoided prohibited conclusions?
- Have we avoided product, procurement, pricing, pilot, demo, or SOW language?
- Have we kept SAGE internal, manual, and non-public?

## 12. What not to do

- Do not describe SAGE as launched, available, or productized.
- Do not create a public route or UI.
- Do not create product copy.
- Do not create procurement, pricing, pilot, demo, or SOW language.
- Do not imply direct data access or system integration.
- Do not weaken human-review, non-scoring, non-automation, or source-system boundaries.

## 13. Relationship to CIVIC, CLEAR, SAGE, and the adaptation framework

- **CIVIC** opens the conversation; the kit never changes the front door.
- **CLEAR** is the method the kit runs by hand.
- **SAGE** is the future architecture the kit imitates manually — without claiming it exists as
  software.
- **The adaptation framework** supplies the institution type, red lines, and role mapping the kit
  applies. This kit is how those rules get exercised in practice before any software is built.
