## 1. Institution adaptation profile

- **Institution (fictional):** Example Public Broadcaster
- **Institution type:** Public broadcaster / public media institution

## 2. Institutional question

How can the institution preserve accessibility implementation traceability, institutional memory, and
digital modernization reviewability without touching editorial, journalistic, creative, programming,
newsroom, source-protection, or ombudsman material?

## 6. CLEAR pillar findings (illustrative)

- **Continuity** — institutional memory for accessibility commitments appears concentrated in a small
  number of roles (illustrative; confidence: low without owner confirmation).
- **Legitimacy** — accessibility and inclusion commitments are publicly stated; traceability into
  operations is the open question.
- **Evidence** — public commitments exist; operating evidence of implementation is fragmented across
  public documents.
- **Accountability** — accountable functions are identifiable; ownership of accessibility traceability
  through modernization is the gap.
- **Readiness** — digital modernization is active; the question is whether accessibility evidence
  survives the change and remains reviewable.

## 7. Evidence confidence and gaps

- Confidence: **Low–Moderate**, based on public sources only.
- Gap: no authorized owner confirmation yet; no operating-layer evidence beyond public documents.
- Next evidence: authorized owner interview; traceability of one accessibility commitment from strategy
  to operation to review.
