## 4. Three participant task sheets

### Daphne lens - fictional operational coordinator

1. Why does this matter exist, and what are you expected to do?
2. What evidence is missing and what would you request next?
3. Where do accounts disagree? How would you handle an unresolved exception?
4. Which information belongs to another source system rather than this brief?
5. Find the previous decision and explain what changed.
6. What must change for another institution's terminology, roles and approval route?
7. What onboarding, administrator help and ongoing support would you need?
8. Complete the same decision/evidence task using a conventional packet; describe
   whether reconciliation or entry work increased. Do not assert savings.

### Richard lens - fictional institutional governance reviewer

1. Who holds authority now, and where does it come from?
2. Was authority valid for the original commitment? What changed afterward?
3. Distinguish identity, assigned role, application permission and delegated
   institutional authority using this matter; which supports the spending decision?
4. Which source is authoritative for each claim, and where is interpretation?
5. What remains missing, disputed or time-sensitive?
6. Can you preserve the earlier legitimate decision without granting new authority?
7. What would you need to establish institutional boundaries and provenance?
8. What next decision belongs to whom? What would invalidate this model?

### Tom lens - fictional accountable executive

Read only the single brief in section 5 for the first five minutes.

1. What were we trying to achieve and who was accountable?
2. What was authorized, committed and received?
3. What evidence exists and what is missing?
4. What happened relative to expectation; what changed?
5. What is the material variance and what cannot be causally concluded?
6. What competing explanations and risks remain?
7. What institutional decision is needed now and who can make it?
8. What was understandable from this brief alone? What required reconstructing
   the entire record? That need is an executive-usability observation, not blame.

All participants complete the common unprompted questions and core T01-T10 task
mapping from the [plan](CIVIC_CONTROLLED_HUMAN_VALIDATION_PLAN.md). No one is

## 7. Facilitator-only rubric and evidence-quality thresholds

First ask eight questions, in the participant's own words: why am I here,
what am I responsible for, what is missing, what can I trust, who has authority,
what changed, what decision is required, what happens next?
Observe before teaching. Outcome 2 = independently correct with evidence;
1 = correct after neutral prompt; 0 = wrong, abandoned or substantive explanation.
Proposed individual comprehension indicator: at least six of eight answers correct
independently within the five-minute task window. It is not a lens pass percentage.

Record authority/evidence correctness, actionable next decision, configuration
distinctions, support ownership, burden/time/counts and confidence before feedback.
Confidence is not correctness. Architecture coaching is a product finding.
Critical authority/privacy errors are not outweighed by favorable opinion.

Per lens with at most two sessions:

- CLEAR_POSITIVE_SIGNAL: both relevant sessions supply comparable independently
  correct core tasks, evidence references and no unresolved material concern;
  observed behavior and opinion are distinguished. Directional, not validation at
  population scale or automatic PASS.
- MIXED_SIGNAL: contradictory observable results, prompts required or partial
  usefulness, without a blocking pattern. Preserve divergence; do not average.
- MATERIAL_CONCERN: an unsafe-authority error or repeated incorrect source/
  decision interpretation, excessive coaching, net burden increase, required
  custom fork or unusable executive brief materially undermines the lens.
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE: fewer than two relevant usable sessions, missing consent,
  non-comparable tasks, withdrawal, incomplete evidence or only stated opinion.

Missing/inadequate evidence cannot become positive by a one-of-two majority.
No lens classification is populated now.

Material findings carry one or more D_GATE/R_GATE/T_GATE; severity BLOCKING,
MATERIAL, MODERATE, MINOR or POSITIVE_SIGNAL; basis OBSERVED_BEHAVIOR,
PARTICIPANT_OPINION or FACILITATOR_INTERPRETATION. Use separate records for bases,
source references, counter-evidence and limitations; do not launder opinion into
observed proof. Any actual outcome is the custodian's later evidence judgment.

## 8. Burden worksheet

Use equivalent conventional/CIVIC static packets for the same E01-E12 task.
Counterbalance order within each lens and log learning effects. At equal or
better correctness, record active seconds, manual entries, duplicate entries,
external lookups (simulated only), reconciliation steps, clarification requests,
time to next action, navigation/backtracking and facilitator interventions.

Reconciliation saving = conventional reconciliation time minus CIVIC time.
New entry overhead = CIVIC entry time minus conventional entry time.
Net burden change = CIVIC total active time minus conventional total time.
Report individual comparisons, not pooled averages hiding failure.
Positive burden evidence requires comparable tasks, positive reconciliation
saving exceeding positive entry overhead, no unnecessary duplicates and no
increase in total active task time. If no fair comparison is possible, use
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE for burden; participant preference alone is not reduction.
Static walkthroughs cannot prove sustained live operational savings.

## 9. Facilitator guide and answer key

Use about 60 minutes: consent/context 0-5, unprompted comprehension 5-10,
tasks/evidence 10-30, configuration/burden 30-40, lens/support 40-50, reflection
50-60. No more than ten minutes stuck on a core task; mark unsuccessful, ask
permission to move on. Executive starts with brief only. Record every neutral
prompt and substantive intervention. Do not teach intended answers or sell.

Answer key: E01/E08 explain need/target; current E03 governs next authority;
coordinator cannot spend, identity/membership/AI output is not a delegation.
E04 remains valid historically under E02. E05 is receipt, not accepted outcome.
E06 missing; E09/E10 conflict without resolution. E07 is 14 vs E08 10,
four-day target gap/six-day baseline improvement, no causal attribution.
Extension total CAD 55,000 requires executive review; no automated approval.
Next step depends on role: collect evidence, assess authority or decide remediation/
conditional acceptance/escalation. Kernel invariants cannot be configured away.

If participant cannot proceed without explanation, record failure before help.
Do not patch materials between individual sessions; preserve comparability and
propose remediation after synthesis. Stop for withdrawal, privacy concern, real
data, access beyond scope, missing consent, inadequate scenario, expiry/revocation
or any request to reopen runtime. Use CIVIC_HUMAN_VALIDATION_STOP_REQUIRED.

## 10. Session closeout questions

- In your own words, what does this help you do? When would you reject it?
- What was confusing or required extra work? Point to the step.
- Which source did you trust, and what would change your view?
- What would you do if missing evidence never arrives?
- What needs institutional configuration versus a protected rule?
- Who provides onboarding, training, administrator/incident support and policy review?
- Could you identify the next decision without rebuilding the whole matter?
- What remained unanswered? What might make your interpretation wrong?
- Is any note inaccurate, or would you like to stop/withdraw?

No real institutional cases, solicitation, purchase interest or promised product
commitment. Close by explaining next findings review, not promising a launch.
