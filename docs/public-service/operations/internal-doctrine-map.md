## 1. Purpose

This internal doctrine map preserves the lineage between Nzila's original OCI/OCRA institutional-readiness
doctrine and the current CIVIC / CLEAR / SAGE public-service operating model. It is intended to prevent
naming drift, public-copy confusion, and premature product claims.

## 2. Doctrine map

```

  ↓
```

## 4. What carries forward from OCI/OCRA

- continuity before transformation
- evidence before conclusion
- readiness before implementation
- human confirmation before institutional use
- boundaries before packaging
- institutional memory as operational infrastructure
- no unsupported findings
- no scoring or ranking
- no automated decisions
- no public/product claim without separate review

## 5. What changes in the public-service framing

OCI/OCRA remains internal lineage. CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE translates the same readiness discipline into
public-service language focused on continuity, implementation visibility, governance evidence,
accountability, and capacity.

The public-service stack should not expose internal doctrine names unless there is a specific reviewed
reason to do so.

## 6. Public-facing naming rule

Do not describe CIVIC/CLEAR/SAGE publicly as "OCI/OCRA for government."

Preferred internal-safe phrasing:

> CIVIC adapts Nzila's institutional continuity and readiness work into a public-service context. CLEAR
> provides the evidence method. SAGE remains the future/manual workspace pattern for organizing continuity,
> implementation, and governance evidence.

Forbidden public-facing phrasing:

- OCI/OCRA for government
- OCRA-powered government platform
- SAGE platform available now
- CLEAR certification
- CIVIC procurement offer
- public-sector scoring engine
- institutional readiness score
- automated governance assessment
- compliance certification
- legal/regulatory/procedural/clinical validation

## 7. Corporate continuity exception

OCI/OCRA may remain public where it is clearly part of Nzila's corporate continuity or organizational
continuity product line.

The restriction in this doctrine map is narrower: do not use OCI/OCRA as the public framing for CIVIC,
CLEAR, SAGE, or public-service outreach unless separately reviewed.

For the public-service stack, lead with CIVIC, CLEAR, and carefully bounded SAGE language. Treat OCI/OCRA as
internal lineage for that stack, not as the public-sector label.

## 8. Operating boundary rule

The doctrine map does not change the current operating-readiness verdict.

- GREEN for internal fictional dry runs.
- AMBER for real-institution use.
- RED for external/public/software/product use.

SAGE remains future-facing and non-productized. CLEAR remains evidence-organizing, not conclusion-drawing.
CIVIC remains the public-service umbrella, not a procurement offer.

## 9. Current status

The proof-run and Manual Operating Pack loop is closed at v0.1. Proof Run 0.4 remains deferred. The current
priority is doctrine clarity, internal packaging discipline, and controlled review before any
external-facing use.

Decision: Preserve OCI/OCRA as internal lineage and lead public-service work with CIVIC, CLEAR, and
carefully bounded SAGE language.

Rationale: This keeps the original institutional-readiness doctrine intact while avoiding public-copy
confusion, acronym overload, product drift, or unsupported claims.
