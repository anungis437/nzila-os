# Richard Forwardable Intro

I wanted to introduce you to Aubert and Michel, who are developing a public-service continuity initiative called CIVIC by Nzila.

CIVIC focuses on a timely institutional challenge: how public organizations preserve service continuity, accountability, institutional memory, equity implementation, and responsible modernization capacity while experiencing workforce transition, restructuring, and digital change.

Their current framing is not a product pitch. It is a public-service continuity and evidence concept built around the CLEAR Method: Continuity, Legitimacy, Evidence, Accountability, and Readiness.

They are looking for feedback from federal and provincial public-service leaders on whether this framing is useful, where it would fit institutionally, and what kind of evidence brief would be valuable to executives managing workforce and modernization pressure.
