# CLEAR Method Note

CLEAR = Continuity, Legitimacy, Evidence, Accountability, and Readiness.

## Positioning

CLEAR is the evidence discipline behind CIVIC.

CLEAR is not presented here as a product to buy, a booking flow, or a first-touch paid assessment.

## Evidence sources CLEAR can organize

- HR and workforce transition signals
- service operations and delivery indicators
- audit and control findings
- complaints and recourse data
- equity implementation records
- modernization program evidence
- institutional knowledge documentation

## Confidence handling

CLEAR emphasizes explicit confidence language and uncertainty reporting so human reviewers can see what is strong, weak, or missing.

## Potential future outputs (later stage)

- executive public-service continuity brief
- service continuity risk map
- workforce knowledge-loss exposure map
- accountability traceability map
- equity implementation traceability note
- modernization or AI readiness pre-check
- evidence confidence profile
- 30/60/90-day control plan

Potential verdict language in future briefs may include: Ready, Ready with controls, Not ready, and Insufficient evidence.
