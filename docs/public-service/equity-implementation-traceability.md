# Equity Implementation Traceability

## Why traceability matters

Equity commitments are only durable when they remain operationally traceable through ownership, controls, evidence, and review pathways.

## Required framing

Anti-racism / ABR is an optional visibility and accountability lens for implementation traceability.

It is not an automated racism detector.

## Explicit boundaries

- no automated racism detection
- no scoring of people or communities
- no autonomous adjudication outcomes
- no substitution for human accountability functions

## What is in scope

Human-reviewed pattern visibility can help identify where commitments may be under-implemented, weakly evidenced, or inconsistently operationalized.

## CIVIC linkage

CIVIC uses this lens as part of continuity and accountability intelligence in public-service contexts.
