# Conversation Notes Template

> Internal governance material. Copy this template for every Richard / institution / advisor
> conversation. It captures reactions as structured evidence for the feedback intake protocol.

> **Sensitive-material reminder.** Do not record confidential, personal, privileged, journalistic,
> clinical, case-specific, enforcement, adjudicative, child/youth, student, health, Indigenous
> protocol-sensitive, or source-protected material unless explicitly authorized and necessary. Capture
> the *reaction to the framing*, not protected institutional content.

## 2. Institution / role context

*Briefly: what kind of institution, what the person's role touches, why the conversation happened.*

## 3. What we sent or described

*Which materials or framing they actually saw (e.g., email + one-page brief). Keep to the first-send
discipline unless a second-touch was authorized.*

## 4. Their first reaction

*The unfiltered opening reaction, in their words where possible.*

## 5. What resonated

*What landed and mattered to them.*

## 6. What confused them

*What was misunderstood, unclear, or needed re-explaining.*

## 7. Objections or risks raised

*Pushback, disagreement, or reputational/political/institutional risks they flagged.*

## 8. Language they used

*Exact words and phrases they used — evidence about framing and institution-specific language.*

## 9. Institution-type fit

*Which adaptation-framework institution type applies; any fit or no-fit signal.*

## 10. Boundary sensitivity

*Any concern about scope, independence, or overreach; any institution-specific red line touched.*

## 11. Evidence or proof requested

*What evidence or concrete proof point, if any, they asked to see.*

## 12. Referral or next-contact signal

*Anyone they suggested, or an opening for a next conversation.*

## 13. Follow-up promised

*What we committed to send or do — kept within first-send / second-touch discipline.*

## 14. Signal classification

*Feedback categories and signal quality level (per the intake protocol), and the rubric score if
computed.*

## 15. Recommended integration path

*The chosen path from the intake-protocol ladder (no action → parking lot → copy clarification → ...).*

## 16. Do-not-change notes

*Anything that must NOT be changed in response — boundaries to hold, over-reactions to avoid, SAGE not
to be surfaced early, no procurement/pilot/demo/pricing language.*
