## 4. Aubert / Michel internal reminder

- This is a feedback conversation, not a sales call — say so in the first minute.
- Lead with the problem, not an offer. Do not introduce commercial terms, engagements, demos, or trials.
- CIVIC in one line: a public-service continuity framing that helps leaders see where institutional
  memory, evidence, accountability, and modernization readiness may be weakening — before change
  becomes irreversible.
- CLEAR in one line: the evidence discipline behind CIVIC — organizing fragmented signals into
  decision-grade material for human review, with explicit confidence and uncertainty.
- On equity / anti-racism: frame it strictly as a visibility and traceability lens. It renders no
  judgments and produces no automated findings. Human review stays authoritative.
- Close by asking for candid feedback and, if welcome, targeted introductions — then offer to
  request a briefing tailored to their context.
