# Five-Slide Intro Deck — Outline

*A markdown outline for a short, feedback-oriented intro. Not a product deck, not a sales
deck. No pricing, no demo, no pilot ask. Build the visual deck later from this outline.*

---

## Slide 1 — The public-service continuity problem

**Message:** Continuity is under pressure, and the risk is quieter than turnover.

- Public institutions must modernize, adopt AI responsibly, preserve trust, and manage
  workforce transition — simultaneously.
- Experienced staff are retiring or moving on; tacit knowledge leaves with them.
- Evidence for accountability and equity implementation is often fragmented across systems.
- The deeper risk: undocumented knowledge and unclear ownership get embedded into
  redesigned services.

**Speaker note:** Open on the shared reality, not on us. Most public-service leaders
already feel this; the goal is to name it clearly and see if the framing resonates.

---

## Slide 2 — Why workforce transition + modernization changes the risk

**Message:** These pressures compound each other; they don't arrive in sequence.

- Workforce transition removes institutional memory at the same time modernization
  redesigns services.
- Automation can lock in undocumented assumptions before they are reviewed.
- Accountability and equity commitments must stay traceable through the change.
- When continuity, evidence, and ownership weaken together, trust is what erodes.

**Speaker note:** Emphasize the "at the same time" dynamic. The point is not that change
is bad — it's that change without traceability is where continuity risk hides.

---

## Slide 3 — CIVIC by Nzila

**Message:** A public-service continuity framing — a front door, not a product.

- CIVIC = Continuity, Implementation, Visibility, Integrity, and Capacity.
- Focused on the operating layer between policy commitment and institutional action.
- Introduced as a framing and briefing conversation, before any product or assessment.
- Purpose: help leaders see fragility early, while it is still correctable.

**Speaker note:** Be explicit that this is a framing we are testing, not something we are
selling. Invite disagreement — that is useful signal.

---

## Slide 4 — CLEAR Method and human-review principles

**Message:** Evidence discipline with human review as the boundary.

- CLEAR = Continuity, Legitimacy, Evidence, Accountability, and Readiness — the evidence
  discipline behind CIVIC.
- Organizes fragmented signals into decision-grade material for human review, with explicit
  confidence and uncertainty.
- Human review is authoritative; no autonomous legal, HR, equity, or employment decisions.
- No automated findings of discrimination or wrongdoing; privacy and minimization are in
  scope from the start.

**Speaker note:** This slide is the trust anchor. For a public-service audience, the
boundaries matter as much as the capability. Lead with what it will not do.

---

## Slide 5 — What feedback we are seeking

**Message:** We want to learn whether this is useful before it becomes anything more.

- Is the CIVIC framing useful in your context?
- Where would it fit institutionally — continuity/operations, modernization/responsible-AI,
  enterprise risk/internal audit, or equity implementation?
- What kind of evidence brief would actually be valuable to an executive?
- Who else would it be worth speaking with?

**Speaker note:** Close on the ask for feedback and targeted introductions. Offer to
**request a briefing** — a short, tailored continuity brief on their terms.
