# Executive Governance Experience

> **Status:** Canonical governance experience · **Layer:** Executive · **Inherits:** [executive-governance-review-workflows.md](../nzila-governance-operations/executive-governance-review-workflows.md)

## 1. Objective

Create executive-safe governance operational experiences. The audience is strategic leadership; the surface must preserve strategic cognition.

## 2. Required implementation

- Executive posture summaries — one banded card per product.
- Continuity briefings — short institutional narrative per cycle.
- Modernization pacing reviews — banded pacing across the rollout corridor.
- Deployment legitimacy reviews — release × environment × verdict.
- Governance confidence reviews — banded confidence across the last N legitimacy validations.
- Stabilization briefing panels — banded signals with advisory.

## 3. UX posture

- One screen, one truth per card.
- No engineering jargon.
- No orchestration internals.
- No telemetry density.
- No urgency framing for routine surfaces.
- Reading load per screen is bounded; the executive sets the pace.

## 4. Refused content

- Composite scoring of products.
- Real-time refresh.
- Animated indicators.
- Person-resolving content.

## 5. Discipline

An executive governance experience succeeds when leaders end the session with a clearer institutional picture and a single, deliberate decision. Sessions that produce ten micro-decisions are failures of the surface.
