# External Foundations Matrix

This matrix is a citation and review backlog, not a claim that the listed literature endorses IEBOK. Each relationship must be reviewed before it is represented as inherited, adapted, synthesized, extended, or original.

## Relationship Classification Rule

Only an evidence-backed review may classify a relationship as `inherited`, `adapted`, `synthesized`, `extended`, or `apparently original`. Until then, all entries are `unresolved` and cannot support originality or external-validation claims.
