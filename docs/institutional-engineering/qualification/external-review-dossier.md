# Wave 2B External Review Dossier

## Cover Memorandum

The Institutional Engineering Body of Knowledge Foundations are recovered working-draft manuscripts under internal qualification. Reviewers are asked to assess specific propositions, terminology, boundaries, and relationships to established fields. They are not asked to endorse a discipline, approve publication, adopt Nzila doctrine, or evaluate products.

## Scope and Non-Scope

Scope: IEBOK-0, IEBOK-I.1, IEBOK-I.2, IEBOK-I.3, their 1.1 editorial candidates, and Wave 2A/2B evidence artifacts.

Out of scope: Institutional Sciences ingestion; Nzila method reconciliation; OCI, OCRA, CIVIC, SAGE, or CLEAR; product implementation; IE-STD publication; mechanics scoring; and claims that the founding sport-federation case is a qualified reference implementation.

## Review Questions

1. Which claims are inherited, adapted, synthesized, extended, apparently original, or unresolved relative to cited adjacent fields?
2. Which definitions conflict with established disciplinary usage or need narrower scope?
3. Which propositions lack falsifiability, measurable constructs, boundary conditions, or evidence thresholds?
4. Which statements incorrectly imply normativity, validation, universality, technical completeness, or a reference implementation?
5. What precise revision, evidence, or research design would resolve each material finding?

## Conflict of Interest Declaration

Each reviewer must disclose employment, funding, authorship, consultancy, governance, product, or personal interests that could reasonably affect the review. A conflict does not automatically disqualify a reviewer; it must be recorded and dispositioned by the authorized review steward.

## Finding Severity and Disposition

`S0` blocks technical coherence or safety; `S1` blocks evidence, external review, or external claim; `S2` is material consistency; `S3` is editorial clarification; `S4` is presentation. Review findings enter a controlled disposition record: received, triaged, evidence checked, editorial decision proposed, authorized decision recorded, then applied only in a new candidate edition.

## Evidence, Publication, and Attribution

External evidence must identify source, scope, method, limitations, and any relevant conflict. Reviewer comments are review evidence, not automatic proof. Attribution requires reviewer consent and must not imply endorsement. No Foundation candidate may be described as published, approved, externally validated, standardized, or Nzila-adopted until separate authorized decisions establish those states.
