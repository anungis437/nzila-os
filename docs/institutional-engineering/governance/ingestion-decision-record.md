## Decision

Establish Institutional Engineering as an independent, emerging discipline in the repository and establish a logically separate governed corpus location, currently designated IEBOK. Establish Nzila as a proprietary implementation profile and contributor to selected parts of the discipline.

This decision does not approve, publish, standardize, adopt, implement, validate, or market any corpus assertion. It does not alter runtime behaviour, product positioning, contractual commitments, or existing Nzila doctrine.

## Controls

1. No implicit supersession: an existing Nzila artifact remains controlling until an explicit decision identifies it, its successor, and its impact.
2. Provenance is preserved: historical source status and current operational authority are recorded separately.
3. Corpus alignment, Nzila adoption, implementation, validation, and public claims are distinct states.
4. The Series Charter and Foundation manuscripts are ingested verbatim from the verified source archive and retain their source-declared `1.0 (Working Draft)` informative status.
