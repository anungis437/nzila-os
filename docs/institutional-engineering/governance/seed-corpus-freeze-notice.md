# Seed Corpus Freeze Notice

**Effective:** 2026-07-21  
**Scope:** Institutional Engineering ingestion reconciliation

The Seed Corpus is frozen against additions for the purpose of this reconciliation. This freeze does not prevent normal maintenance, remediation, or governance-required updates to existing Nzila materials. It prevents those changes from being silently represented as new Institutional Engineering corpus content.

New corpus candidates must be registered in the [Corpus inventory](IEBOK-CORPUS-INVENTORY.md), assigned a source and steward, and accepted through an explicit decision before entry.
