# Institutional Engineering Corpus

This directory is the governed repository location for the emerging, independent discipline of Institutional Engineering and its working corpus, currently designated IEBOK.

It is not a Nzila product, platform module, trademark assertion, or replacement for existing Nzila doctrine. Nzila may contribute to and implement selected parts of the discipline through its proprietary implementation profile and methods.

## Current State

Wave 1 established the governance boundary, inventory, provenance controls, vocabulary baseline, Nzila alignment, and canonical ingestion of the Series Charter and Foundations Volumes I-III. The manuscripts are imported from the verified `curl-link-hub (1).zip` source archive, recorded in the provenance register. They remain informative working drafts; source ingestion does not constitute Nzila adoption, implementation, validation, standardization, or a public claim.

## Governing Boundaries

- Existing Nzila doctrine remains operationally controlling unless explicitly superseded through its existing governance process.
- Existing identifiers and locations are preserved; no Seed Corpus material has been physically relocated.
- IEBOK working-draft content is informative until a governed standard is approved and Nzila records a separate adoption decision.
- Product, runtime, public-positioning, and contractual changes require their own decision records.
