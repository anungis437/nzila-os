# FAIRCASE Demo Script (20 Minutes)

## Demo Objective
Move buyer from problem recognition to concrete next step: paid diagnostic or pilot scoping call.

## Minute-by-Minute Flow

### Minute 1-3: Pain Framing
Talk track:
- "Most organizations do not fail because they lack policies. They fail because complaint and investigation execution is inconsistent, hard to audit, and too slow for leadership confidence."
- "FAIRCASE is built for fairness, accountability, and defensible resolution workflows."

Questions to anchor pain:
- "Where do cases currently break down: intake, assignment, SLA, evidence, or executive reporting?"
- "What happens when leadership asks for a board-ready summary in 48 hours?"

### Minute 4-8: Complaint Intake
Show:
- Structured intake capture.
- Role-based data handling.
- Priority and risk indicators.

Talk track:
- "Intake quality determines everything downstream. FAIRCASE standardizes it without forcing legal language on frontline users."

### Minute 9-12: Workflow + SLA + Assignments
Show:
- Triage and assignment logic.
- SLA timers and escalation visibility.
- Work ownership and handoff clarity.

Talk track:
- "Leaders get execution visibility. Managers get operational clarity. Investigators get defensible process continuity."

### Minute 13-16: Audit / Evidence / Analytics
Show:
- Evidence and timeline traceability.
- Export and integrity posture.
- Operational and executive analytics views.

Talk track:
- "When scrutiny arrives, FAIRCASE gives you evidence confidence, not reconstruction panic."

### Minute 17-19: ROI + Rollout
Show:
- ROI inputs and conservative scenario output.
- Pilot-to-annual pathway.

Talk track:
- "We start where risk is highest, prove measurable value in 8 weeks, then scale by department."

### Minute 20: Close Next Step
Close options:
- Paid diagnostic kickoff.
- Pilot design workshop.
- Security/procurement package review.

Close line:
- "Based on what you shared, the best next step is a diagnostic that quantifies your current cost of delay and defines a low-risk pilot scope."

## Persona Variants

### HR Buyer Script
- Emphasis: policy execution consistency, manager accountability, board-level confidence.
- Outcome framing: reduced cycle time and better leadership reporting.

### Union Buyer Script
- Emphasis: grievance defensibility, steward consistency, regional visibility.
- Outcome framing: fewer avoidable arbitration losses and cleaner case histories.

### University Buyer Script
- Emphasis: ombuds and misconduct workflow governance, confidentiality, review readiness.
- Outcome framing: stronger institutional trust posture and board reporting quality.

### Public Sector Buyer Script
- Emphasis: public trust, auditability, FOI/procurement readiness, cross-department SLAs.
- Outcome framing: reduced response risk and more defensible accountability operations.
