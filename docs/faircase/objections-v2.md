# FAIRCASE Objection War Room v2

## 1) "Too expensive"
- Rebuttal:
  - "That concern is valid. The real comparison is not subscription vs zero, it is structured operations vs hidden cost of slow, inconsistent case handling."
  - "We can start with a paid diagnostic or pilot to quantify ROI before annual commitment."
- Close move:
  - Offer phased rollout and ROI baseline to de-risk spend.

## 2) "We already use spreadsheets"
- Rebuttal:
  - "Spreadsheets can store data, but they cannot enforce role-based workflow accountability, SLA governance, or defensible audit trails at scale."
  - "FAIRCASE keeps operational discipline when case volume and scrutiny rise."
- Close move:
  - Run a workflow comparison against one real case stream.

## 3) "We have ServiceNow"
- Rebuttal:
  - "ServiceNow is strong for broad workflow orchestration. FAIRCASE is purpose-built for trust-sensitive complaint, investigation, and resolution governance."
  - "Many teams keep ServiceNow for IT/ops and use FAIRCASE where defensibility and fairness operations require specialized controls."
- Close move:
  - Position FAIRCASE as specialist layer, not rip-and-replace.

## 4) "We have HRIS tools"
- Rebuttal:
  - "HRIS platforms are system-of-record for HR data. They are rarely optimized for high-scrutiny complaint lifecycle execution and governance evidence."
  - "FAIRCASE complements HRIS by operationalizing case workflows and accountability."
- Close move:
  - Show role boundaries and integration options.

## 5) "Budget freeze"
- Rebuttal:
  - "When budgets tighten, governance and risk exposure costs become more visible."
  - "A small diagnostic can establish quantified risk and ROI to support internal funding decisions."
- Close move:
  - Offer diagnostic now, pilot start window aligned to budget cycle.

## 6) "Security review needed"
- Rebuttal:
  - "Expected for enterprise buyers. We have a procurement-ready security and controls package."
  - "We can run security review in parallel with pilot scope alignment to reduce cycle time."
- Close move:
  - Send security one-pager + procurement checklist within 24 hours.

## 7) "Procurement is slow"
- Rebuttal:
  - "Agreed, so we pull procurement forward early and define milestone gates upfront."
  - "Our public-sector and enterprise templates are structured for this reality."
- Close move:
  - Align legal, procurement, and sponsor threads in week 1.

## 8) "No urgency right now"
- Rebuttal:
  - "Understood. The best predictor of future urgency is unresolved operational risk today."
  - "If we can show where cycle-time and evidence risk are accumulating, urgency becomes objective."
- Close move:
  - Run a 2-week diagnostic and let data decide.

## 9) "Not enough volume"
- Rebuttal:
  - "Even moderate volume can create high risk when cases are sensitive and cross-functional."
  - "Value is not only volume reduction, it is consistency, defensibility, and leadership confidence."
- Close move:
  - Propose right-sized scope and pricing tier.

## 10) "Political sensitivity"
- Rebuttal:
  - "FAIRCASE is designed for trust-sensitive environments where fairness and accountability must be operational, not rhetorical."
  - "Role governance, auditable process, and defensible reporting reduce political volatility."
- Close move:
  - Start with one controlled unit and executive governance cadence.
