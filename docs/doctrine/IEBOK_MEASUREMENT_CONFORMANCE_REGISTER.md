# IEBOK Measurement Conformance Register

## Scope
Conformance bridge between Phase II Chapter I measurement constitution and existing scoring/maturity models.

| Model | Source | Construct definition | Scale/aggregation | Reliability/validity/calibration/uncertainty | Reproducibility | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Institutional Continuity Index (ICI) | `SCORING_MODELS.md` | Partial | Weighted composite 0–100 | Not established constitutionally | Not demonstrated | Transitional | Keep as decision-support instrument pending measurement program |

| ICA package (combined models) | `SCORING_MODELS.md` | Partial | Multi-model rollup | Not established | Not demonstrated | Illustrative only | Composite outputs are exploratory until validated |

## Migration Rule (Pre-Measurement-Constitution Models)
1. Legacy model outputs remain usable for exploratory diagnosis and decision support.
2. Legacy outputs are **not** admissible as constitutional empirical-law evidence until Ch. I conformance is demonstrated.
3. Any model promoted beyond transitional status must document: construct theory, indicator justification, scale type, aggregation defensibility, uncertainty attribution, reliability/validity evidence, and reproducibility protocol.
