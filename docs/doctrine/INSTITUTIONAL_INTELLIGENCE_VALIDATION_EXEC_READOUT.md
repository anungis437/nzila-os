## Validation Basis — Informal Cycle (v1)

This validation is based on accumulated discovery conversations rather than a formal structured interview cycle. That distinction is documented as a known limitation.

Sources:
1. CLC Convention interactions and executive discussions.
2. CUPE national and local discussions.
3. UNA discussions.
4. Brandon and CUPE 4373 demo preparation and discovery.
5. Union executive conversations across multiple provinces.
6. Early pilot discussions and continuity discovery sessions.

### What the signal consistently showed

1. Leaders recognize continuity risk without prompting. Test 0 analog: pass.
2. Leaders recognize institutional memory loss as an operational problem.
3. Leaders recognize governance fragmentation as a structural concern.
4. Leaders recognize leadership-transition fragility as a real and recurring pattern.
5. No existing framework in these conversations connected all four patterns.

### Documented Limitation

Validation based on executive discovery conversations, conference interactions, union leadership discussions, pilot discussions, and early market testing. Structured interview methodology with Test 0 through Test 6 scoring was not applied. Formal longitudinal validation remains future work.

### Decision Rationale

Nzila is not raising a Series A around an unproven category. The goal is intellectual leadership around a problem repeatedly observed in direct market contact. Informal validation is sufficient for v1 category publication. Formal structured validation (Cycle 1 kit) should be executed in parallel with or immediately following Artifact 001 publication.

## Core Category Under Test

### Category Name
Institutional Intelligence

### Definition
Institutional Intelligence is the governed capacity of institutions to remember, decide, adapt, and remain legitimate over time.

### Core Problem Statement
Organizations are becoming more digitally capable while becoming more institutionally fragile.

### Causal Model Candidate
Memory -> Continuity -> Governance -> Trust -> Institutional Intelligence -> Resilience
