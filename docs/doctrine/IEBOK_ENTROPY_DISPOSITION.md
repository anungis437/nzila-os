# IEBOK Entropy Disposition

## Constitutional Status (Single)
**Analogical model**

Institutional entropy is constitutionally treated as an analogical model for cumulative disorder/degradation tendencies in institutional coordination, traceability, and coherence.

## Prohibited Interpretations
- Not a thermodynamic quantity.
- Not an information-theoretic identity claim.
- Not a formal equation-bearing law in current constitutional state.

## Required Language Constraint
Any use of “institutional entropy” must avoid claims of established mathematical equivalence unless independently proven and constitutionally adopted.

## Distinctions
| Term | Distinct meaning relative to entropy |
|---|---|
| Degradation | Observed decline in capability/quality |
| Drift | Progressive divergence from intended governance/operation |
| Disorder | General loss of structural coherence |
| Fragmentation | Breakdown into disconnected records/processes/authority pathways |
| Fragility | Susceptibility to failure under stress/transition |
| Information loss | Missing or unrecoverable institutional record/context |
| Governance decay | Degradation specific to accountability/authority coherence |

## Interim Rule
Entropy language may be used descriptively for analysis/communication, but not as standalone constitutional empirical-law proof.
