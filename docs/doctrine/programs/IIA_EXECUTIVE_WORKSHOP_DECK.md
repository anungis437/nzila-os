## Slide 2: Session Outcome
- Establish evidence-based IIA baseline
- Identify top fragility exposures
- Align on 90-day priorities

## Slide 3: Why This Matters
- Institutional fragility is an operational and governance risk
- The cost appears during transition, audit pressure, and disruption
- This workshop quantifies where risk is concentrated

## Slide 4: The Model
- Institutional Fragility -> Institutional Intelligence -> Resilience
- Six assessment dimensions
- 0-4 scoring per dimension

## Slide 5: Scoring Rules
- Score evidence, not intent
- Score current operating reality, not policy aspiration
- Use concrete examples from last 12-24 months
