# IIA Post-Workshop Upsell Path

## Purpose
Convert a completed IIA workshop into a structured follow-on path without forcing a software-first pitch.

## Decision Tree
1. Complete IIA baseline
2. Identify top fragility dimensions
3. Route to the best next offer based on dominant risk pattern

## Route A: Executive Governance Hardening (General)
Trigger:
- Governance Maturity <= 2 or Accountability Architecture <= 2

Offer:
- 90-day governance architecture sprint
- Decision-rights mapping
- Escalation and explainability standards

## Route B: Continuity and Memory Hardening (General)
Trigger:
- Memory Integrity <= 2 or Continuity Capacity <= 2

Offer:
- Knowledge risk audit
- Transition protocol design
- Continuity drill setup

## Route C: Labour Operational Layer (Union Eyes)
Trigger:
- Labour institution with operational pain in grievances, steward workflows, case continuity, member service, officer transitions, bargaining memory

Offer:
- Union Eyes Risk Assessment / Workshop
- Scope a controlled Union Eyes pilot where fit is confirmed

Positioning script:
"The IIA identified institution-level fragility. The Union Eyes assessment addresses labour-specific operating workflows that drive those fragility patterns in day-to-day representation work."

## Route D: Reassessment and Observatory Contribution
Trigger:
- Client wants trend proof and external reference posture

Offer:
- 6-12 month reassessment
- anonymized benchmark contribution
- optional case-study quote program

## Commercial Timing
- Day 0: deliver readout
- Day 7: follow-up decision call
- Day 14: present follow-on scope and fee
- Day 21: close follow-on or schedule reassessment

## Packaging Rules
1. Never pitch Union Eyes as a replacement for IIA.
2. Always maintain IIA as the diagnostic umbrella.
3. Route by problem type, not by product preference.
4. Keep first follow-on scope fixed and outcome-bound.
