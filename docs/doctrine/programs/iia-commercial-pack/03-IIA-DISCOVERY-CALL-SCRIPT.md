# IIA Discovery Call Script (30 Minutes)

## Objective
Decide if there is fit for an IIA workshop pilot and route correctly:
- IIA umbrella diagnostic
- Union Eyes labour operational assessment (if labour-specific pain dominates)
- TrustCore governance route (if SMB governance/compliance pain dominates)
- Defer when no clear fit or readiness is missing

## Call Structure
1. Context and goal (3 min)
2. Problem discovery (10 min)
3. Fit and route decision (7 min)
4. Scope, price, and timeline (7 min)
5. Close and next step (3 min)

## 1) Opening Script (3 min)
"Thanks for taking the time. The goal today is simple: understand your continuity and governance pressure points, confirm whether an IIA workshop is the right first move, and if yes, agree a lightweight pilot scope."

## 2) Discovery Questions (10 min)
Use these in order and note verbatim signals.

### Executive fragility signals (IIA lead)
1. Where does leadership transition create the most disruption today?
2. If two key leaders left in the next 90 days, what would break first?
3. How easy is it to explain and defend major governance decisions from the last 2-3 years?
4. What governance or trust issue is currently consuming the most executive attention?

### Operational labour signals (UE lead)
1. Are grievances, cases, and steward workflows the dominant pain?
2. Is member service consistency breaking at local/officer boundaries?
3. Are officer transitions causing case continuity or bargaining memory loss?

## 3) Fit Decision Rule (7 min)
### Lead with IIA when pain is:
- leadership transition risk
- governance fragility
- institutional memory loss
- board/executive trust and accountability risk
- AI/governance readiness

### Lead with Union Eyes assessment when pain is:
- grievances and case operations
- steward/LRO workflows
- member service continuity
- local operations and officer-cycle handoffs

### Lead with TrustCore route when pain is:
- SMB governance discipline and accountability gaps
- policy and control inconsistency under growth pressure
- compliance-readiness and governance operating reliability issues

### Hybrid path
If both are present:
1. IIA first for executive baseline
2. Union Eyes assessment second for labour operating diagnosis

### Defer / not fit
Use defer when sponsorship, readiness, or problem definition is not sufficient for a credible engagement.

## 4) Scope and Pricing Script (7 min)
"We run this in three pilot tiers. Foundation is $2,500, facilitated is $5,000-$7,500, and Assessment Plus is $10,000-$15,000 with 90-day support. Most first engagements choose the facilitated tier."

Confirm:
- sponsor
- participants
- timing window
- decision process
- procurement constraints

## 5) Close Script (3 min)
"If useful, I will send a short proposal and draft SOW within 24 hours with fixed scope, fixed fee, and exact deliverables."

## Qualification Checklist
- Clear executive sponsor exists
- At least 3 leadership participants available
- Decision can be made within 2-3 weeks
- Buyer agrees to evidence-based scoring
- Reassessment openness within 6-12 months

## Route Control Gate
- Do not send proposal or SOW until Route Decision is completed.
- Do not send proposal or SOW until Qualification Matrix scoring is completed.
- If route is Defer, send readiness notes instead of a proposal.

## Red Flags
- Wants individual performance scoring (decline)
- Wants surveillance framing (decline)
- No executive participation (defer)
