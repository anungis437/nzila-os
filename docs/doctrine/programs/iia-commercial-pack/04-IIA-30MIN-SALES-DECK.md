# IIA 30-Minute Sales Deck (Talk Track + Slide Outline)

## Slide 1 (2 min) - Opening
- Institutional Intelligence Assessment (IIA)
- Executive diagnostic for institutional fragility and resilience

Talk track:
"This is not a software demo and not an audit. It is a practical diagnostic workshop to show where your institution is fragile and what to fix in 90 days."

## Slide 2 (2 min) - The Problem
- Institutions lose continuity through transitions
- Governance decisions become hard to explain
- Trust erosion accumulates as operational debt

## Slide 3 (2 min) - Why Existing Tools Miss It
- Compliance checks do not measure continuity quality
- Point tools optimize transactions, not institutional coherence
- Strategy documents do not create operating evidence

## Slide 4 (3 min) - The IIA Model
- Six dimensions:
  - memory
  - continuity
  - governance
  - trust
  - accountability
  - resilience
- Scored 0-4 each, composite 0-24

## Slide 5 (3 min) - What a Client Receives
- Composite score and maturity classification
- Dimension-by-dimension profile
- Fragility heatmap
- 90-day recommendations with owners

## Slide 6 (3 min) - Delivery Method
- Pre-work (2-3 days)
- Facilitated workshop (3-4 hours)
- Executive readout (within 5 business days)
