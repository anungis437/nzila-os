# Institutional Intelligence Assessment (IIA) Workshop

## One-Page Offer Sheet

### What this is
A sector-neutral executive diagnostic that measures institutional fragility and resilience across six dimensions:
1. Memory Integrity
2. Continuity Capacity
3. Governance Maturity
4. Trust Operations
5. Accountability Architecture
6. Institutional Resilience

### Who this is for
- Boards and executive teams
- Governance-intensive institutions
- Organizations preparing for transition, audit pressure, or AI governance change

### The business problem
Most institutions do not fail from intent. They fail from fragility:
- knowledge trapped in individuals
- inconsistent governance decisions
- transition disruption
- unresolved trust debt

### What you get
1. IIA composite score (0-24)
2. Dimension-level maturity profile
3. Fragility heatmap and risk narrative
4. 90-day recommendations with owners and timelines
