# IIA Workshop Pricing And Tiers

## Pilot Pricing Table
| Tier | Price | Best For | Scope | Deliverables |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Foundation Assessment | $2,500 | Lightweight baseline | Self-guided intake + synthesis | Composite score, dimension profile, short readout |
| Facilitated Workshop | $5,000-$7,500 | Standard executive diagnostic | Live 3-4 hour workshop | Score, heatmap, executive readout, 90-day recommendations |
| Assessment Plus | $10,000-$15,000 | Institutions needing momentum | Facilitated workshop + 90-day support | All workshop outputs + implementation checkpoints |

## Commercial Rules
1. Fixed-fee pilot pricing for first three case studies.
2. No custom scope before workshop baseline.
3. Any work beyond agreed tier requires change order.
4. Reassessment pricing can be discounted for baseline clients.
