# IIA Workshop Facilitator Guide

## Purpose
Run a 3-4 hour executive workshop that produces a validated IIA baseline, fragility map, and prioritized 90-day plan.

## Outcomes
1. Agreed six-dimension scores (0-4)
2. Composite score and maturity level
3. Top fragility risks by consequence
4. Named actions and owners for 90 days

## Participants
- Executive sponsor
- Governance lead
- Operations lead
- Functional stewards (policy, compliance, risk, HR, or equivalent)

## Materials
- IIA assessment source
- Scoring workbook template
- Heatmap template
- Readout template
- Timer and evidence capture notes

## Facilitation Principles
1. Evidence first, opinion second.
2. Disagreement is data; resolve by referencing facts and artifacts.
3. Capture assumptions explicitly when evidence is weak.
4. Keep focus on institutional systems, not individuals.

## Agenda (3.5 hours example)
1. Opening and scoring rules (20 min)
2. Dimension scoring rounds (120 min)
3. Composite and maturity calibration (20 min)
4. Fragility heatmap and consequence ranking (25 min)
5. 90-day recommendation design (20 min)
6. Commitments and close (5 min)

## Step-by-Step Playbook

### Step 1: Open the Session
- State objective: baseline and priorities, not blame.
- Confirm scoring rubric and evidence rule.
- Confirm decision protocol for score disputes.

### Step 2: Score Each Dimension
For each dimension:
1. Read the dimension definition
2. Review indicators and assessment questions
3. Ask for evidence from the last 12-24 months
4. Propose provisional score
5. Resolve disagreement
6. Record evidence and confidence level

Confidence levels:
- High: direct artifacts available
- Medium: partial evidence
- Low: mostly experiential evidence

### Step 3: Calibrate Composite
- Sum the six scores (max 24).
- Assign maturity level.
- Test for scoring distortion (one stakeholder dominating, optimistic inflation, weak evidence).

### Step 4: Build Fragility Map
- Rank lowest dimensions by consequence and urgency.
- Mark immediate vs structural issues.
- Select top three priorities.

### Step 5: Design 90-Day Plan
For each priority:
1. Action statement
2. Owner
3. Due date
4. First measurable proof point

### Step 6: Close with Commitments
- Confirm readout delivery date.
- Confirm owners and sponsors.
- Confirm reassessment date.

## Evidence Quality Checklist
Use this before finalizing scores:
1. At least one concrete artifact per dimension
2. At least one recent incident or transition example
3. Named accountable authority for each proposed action
4. No score justified only by policy existence

## Typical Failure Modes and Recovery
1. Failure mode: participants score aspirations.
   - Recovery: ask for last real example and supporting artifact.
2. Failure mode: session becomes role-defensive.
   - Recovery: restate institution-level frame and forward-looking objective.
3. Failure mode: all dimensions scored as 3.
   - Recovery: force comparison against rubric and request evidence variance.
4. Failure mode: recommendations too broad.
   - Recovery: rewrite as owner-based 90-day actions with deadlines.

## Post-Workshop Deliverables
Within 5 business days:
1. Executive readout
2. Heatmap visualization
3. 90-day recommendations report
4. Baseline benchmark record (anonymized fields)
