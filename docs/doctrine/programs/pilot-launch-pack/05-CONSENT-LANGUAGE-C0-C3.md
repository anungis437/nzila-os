# Consent Language - C0 to C3

Purpose: provide standard consent language for pilot agreements and intake conversations.

## C0 - Operational Delivery Only
Suggested language:
Your data will be used only to deliver your pilot outputs and contractual reporting. Your institution will not be included in benchmark datasets, cohort analysis, or case-study publication.

## C1 - Anonymous Benchmark Contribution
Suggested language:
Your institution authorizes use of de-identified assessment data for anonymized benchmark reporting. No identifiable institutional name or unique identifying details will be published.

## C2 - Anonymized Case Study Contribution
Suggested language:
Your institution authorizes use of anonymized narrative findings in case-study materials. Published case narratives will remove institution-identifying details and will be reviewed for de-identification prior to release.

## C3 - Attributed Quote or Named Case Publication
Suggested language:
Your institution authorizes attributed quote and or named case-study publication only for materials explicitly approved in writing. This consent is scoped to approved content and may be withdrawn for future publications.

## Mandatory Clarifications
1. Consent is scoped and recorded by class.
2. Consent classes are independent; C3 is never implied by C1 or C2.
3. Withdrawal rights are unconditional for future use.
4. Benchmark publication remains subject to cohort eligibility and anonymization controls.
