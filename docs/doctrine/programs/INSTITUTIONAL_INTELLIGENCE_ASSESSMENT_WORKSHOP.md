## Product Definition

**Product name:** Institutional Intelligence Assessment Workshop (IIA Workshop)

**Price band (pilot):** $2,500-$10,000

**Delivery window:** 5-10 business days from kickoff to executive readout

**Audience:** Executive leadership, governance leadership, senior operations stewards

**Primary outputs:**
1. IIA composite score (0-24)
2. Six-dimension maturity profile
3. Fragility map (heatmap + narrative)
4. Executive recommendations with 90-day action plan

## Engagement Model

### Phase 1: Pre-Work (Day 0 to Day 2)
1. Confirm sponsor, participants, and sector context
2. Gather evidence pack (policies, governance artifacts, transition docs)
3. Pre-score optional self-assessment

### Phase 2: Facilitated Session (Day 3 or Day 4)
1. 3-4 hour leadership workshop
2. Evidence-based scoring across six dimensions
3. Alignment on high-consequence fragility zones

### Phase 3: Synthesis and Readout (Day 5 to Day 10)
1. Score validation and calibration
2. Heatmap and fragility narrative
3. Executive readout and 90-day recommendations

## Scoring Backbone

The workshop uses the existing IIA scale:
- Six dimensions, each scored 0-4
- Composite score max = 24
- Maturity bands from Level 1 to Level 5

**Non-negotiable rule:** Score evidence, not intent.

## Success Criteria

A completed engagement is successful when all are true:
1. Leadership accepts the score as evidence-based
2. Top three fragility exposures are explicitly named
3. 90-day plan has named owners and dates
4. Reassessment date is scheduled

## Governance Constraints

1. Do not use this as employee surveillance or individual performance scoring.
2. Keep assessment at institutional system level.
3. Preserve evidence trail for every score.
4. Avoid cross-organization comparison without normalization and consent.
