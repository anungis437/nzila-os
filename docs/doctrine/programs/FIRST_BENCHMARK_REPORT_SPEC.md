## Report Title
Institutional Fragility Index - Founding Cohort

## Purpose
Establish the first public benchmark structure for Institutional Intelligence using founding-cohort data, while being explicit about sample-size limitations.

## Reporting Window
- Initial release target: 2027
- Data window: founding cohort pilot period through report cutoff date

## Audience
1. Executive teams
2. Boards and governance committees
3. Sector operators evaluating continuity and governance posture

---

## 1. Data Eligibility

Only include records that satisfy:
1. Benchmark eligibility rules in Observatory Data Collection Standard.
2. Complete minimum package in Observatory Schema.
3. Consent class C1 or higher.
4. Cohort-level publication threshold rules.

---

## 2. Report Sections

## Section 1: Method and Scope
1. What is measured.
2. What is not measured.
3. Inclusion/exclusion rules.
4. Consent and anonymization summary.

## Section 2: Founding Cohort Composition
1. Number of participating institutions (de-identified).
2. Sector composition.
3. Size-band composition.
4. Geography distribution (generalized).

## Section 3: Composite Fragility and Maturity Distribution
1. IIA composite score distribution summary.
2. Maturity level distribution.
3. High-risk concentration profile by dimension.

## Section 4: Sector Profiles (where thresholds permit)
1. Labour
2. Healthcare
3. Associations/non-profits
4. Municipal/public

For any sector below threshold, report as "insufficient cohort size for standalone publication" and aggregate appropriately.

## Section 5: Route and Remediation Insights
1. Route outcomes (IIA-first, UE-first, hybrid, TrustCore).
2. Dominant fragility patterns by route.
3. Early remediation signal patterns (where data exists).

## Section 6: Reassessment and Delta Signals
1. Count of reassessed institutions.
2. Composite score movement distribution.
3. Dimension-level improvement/regression patterns.

## Section 7: Founding Cohort Insights and Limits
1. What the data supports confidently.
2. What remains directional only.
3. Known blind spots and next-cycle priorities.

## Section 8: Appendix
1. Data dictionary snapshot.
2. Suppression and aggregation decisions.
3. Threshold and eligibility logs.

---

## 3. Core Metrics

1. Median and interquartile range of IIA composite.
2. Percent of institutions at each maturity level.
3. Mean score by dimension (with confidence flag).
4. Top three fragility dimensions by frequency.
5. Reassessment delta median (if reassessment n is sufficient).

---

## 4. Publication Rules

1. No institution identified unless C3 and explicit publication approval.
2. No cohort or cell published below threshold.
3. All charts must include confidence and sample-size annotations.
4. Findings must remain institutional-level, not individual-level.

---

## 5. Editorial Voice

1. Institutional and methodological tone, not marketing tone.
2. Verifiable claims only.
3. Explicit caveats for small sample conditions.
4. No causal claims beyond observed data support.

---

## 6. Release Readiness Checklist

1. Observatory quality checks passed.
2. Consent and publication eligibility checks passed.
3. Suppression logic reviewed and approved.
4. Methodology appendix complete.
5. Governance sign-off recorded.

---

## 7. Founding-Cohort Positioning Note

Use this language in the release:
"This founding cohort report establishes benchmark structure and methodological transparency. It is a baseline publication, not a final population representation."
