## Precision-Language Review
Flagged terms requiring evidentiary caution in doctrine corpus: "proportional to", "always", "necessarily", "increases with", "decreases with".

Disposition rule: where empirical evidence is insufficient, convert claim to **qualified hypothesis** or **principle**, not empirical/causal law.
