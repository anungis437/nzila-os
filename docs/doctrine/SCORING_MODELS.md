> Doctrine without measurement is philosophy.
> These scoring models convert doctrine frameworks into measurable, repeatable assessments.
> They provide: consulting leverage, procurement credibility, benchmarking authority,
> institutional transformation roadmaps, and category measurement standards.

---

# Scoring Approach

All scoring models follow the same structure:

1. **Observable Indicators** — measurable, non-subjective conditions
2. **Score Bands** — numeric ranges with clear interpretive labels
3. **Assessment Questions** — diagnostic questions to surface the indicators
4. **Action Guidance** — what each score band implies for institutional action

These models are designed to be administered as: workshops, procurement assessments, discovery diagnostics, or annual institutional reviews.

---

# Model 1 — Institutional Continuity Index (ICI)

**Purpose:** Measures overall organizational resilience across continuity, governance, memory, and sovereignty dimensions.

**Output:** Composite score 0–100 with band classification.

---

## ICI Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Governance Resilience | 25% | How well governance survives personnel and structural change |
| Operational Memory | 25% | How much institutional knowledge is preserved systematically |
| Continuity Survivability | 20% | How well operations continue through transitions |
| Sovereignty Posture | 15% | How much operational independence the institution maintains |
| Evidence Density | 15% | How much operational activity generates traceable evidence |

---

## ICI Score Bands

| Score | Band | Interpretation |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 85–100 | Continuity Intelligence | Institutionalized, resilient, and compounding |
| 70–84 | Continuity-Aware | Structured with identifiable fragility zones |
| 50–69 | Structured Governance | Inconsistent governance with coordination risk |
| 30–49 | Fragmented Documentation | Knowledge preserved but not integrated |
| 0–29 | Personality Dependent | High continuity risk; people-dependent operations |

---

## ICI Assessment Questions (Sample)

**Governance Resilience:**
- Can governance decisions be reconstructed without accessing the decision-maker?
- Are approval workflows documented and enforced systemically, not informally?
- Is escalation handling consistent across teams?

**Operational Memory:**
- How many critical processes exist only in individual memory?
- Can a new team member reach competency without extended mentorship?
- Where does institutional knowledge currently live?

**Continuity Survivability:**
- What would fail immediately if a key person left today?
- How long does operational recovery typically take after a leadership transition?
- How many processes have no documented backup owner?

**Sovereignty Posture:**
- Can operational data be exported without vendor assistance?
- Are audit records independently accessible?
- What would be lost if a primary vendor relationship ended?

**Evidence Density:**
- What percentage of governance decisions produce a traceable record?
- Can operational history be reconstructed from system records?
- Are approval chains documented or informal?

# Model 2 — Governance Fragility Score (GFS)

**Purpose:** Identifies specific governance fragility risks and quantifies exposure.

**Output:** Score 0–100 per fragility dimension, with composite fragility rating.

---

## GFS Fragility Dimensions

| Dimension | Observable Indicator |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Personality Dependency | % of processes dependent on specific individuals |
| Documentation Coverage | % of processes with documented procedures |
| Approval Formalization | % of approvals with traceable records |
| Shadow System Presence | Number of identified shadow systems (spreadsheets, inboxes, verbal approvals) |
| Governance Consistency | Variance in outcomes for equivalent governance decisions |
| Onboarding Duration | Time to operational competency for new team members |

---

## GFS Score Bands

| Score | Band | Risk Level |
|-------|------|-----------|
| 0–20 | Minimal Fragility | Low governance risk |
| 21–40 | Manageable Fragility | Identifiable risks with known mitigations |
| 41–60 | Moderate Fragility | Governance risk is operational, not hypothetical |
| 61–80 | High Fragility | Governance failure likely under transition pressure |
| 81–100 | Critical Fragility | Immediate governance risk requiring intervention |

---

## GFS Assessment Questions (Sample)

- What happens to operations if three key people leave simultaneously?
- How are approvals currently tracked?
- Where do shadow systems currently exist in your operations?
- How consistent are governance outcomes across different teams?
- How long does it take to onboard a new coordinator to full operational competency?

# Model 3 — Operational Memory Score (OMS)

**Purpose:** Measures how effectively the organization has institutionalized operational knowledge.

**Output:** Stage classification (1–5) + score 0–100.

---

## OMS Stage Model

| Stage | Name | Score Range | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Individual Memory | 0–20 | Knowledge trapped in people |
| 2 | Fragmented Documentation | 21–40 | Knowledge spread across disconnected tools |
| 3 | Structured Operations | 41–60 | Processes standardized but not integrated |
| 4 | Governed Continuity | 61–80 | Operational intelligence institutionalized |
| 5 | Continuity Intelligence | 81–100 | Organization resilient to personnel and structural change |

---

## OMS Assessment Questions (Sample)

- Where do staff go to find institutional knowledge?
- How much process knowledge currently exists only verbally?
- Can past operational decisions be reconstructed from records?
- How dependent is operational effectiveness on tenure?
- What institutional knowledge has been permanently lost in the last 2 years?

# Model 4 — Trust Debt Score (TDS)

**Purpose:** Quantifies accumulated institutional trust debt across governance, evidence, and operational dimensions.

**Output:** Score 0–100 (higher = more trust debt).

---

## TDS Indicator Dimensions

| Dimension | Observable Indicator |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Decision Traceability | % of significant decisions with documented rationale |
| Governance Consistency | Variance in governance outcomes for equivalent situations |
| Operational Ambiguity | Frequency of "nobody knows who owns this" situations |
| Audit Confidence | Confidence level in ability to pass audit without preparation |
| Record Integrity | Presence of gaps, inconsistencies, or disputes in operational records |
| AI Transparency | Degree to which AI-assisted decisions are explainable and auditable |

---

## TDS Score Bands

| Score | Band | Interpretation |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 0–20 | Low Trust Debt | Strong institutional trust posture |
| 21–40 | Manageable Trust Debt | Identifiable risks with known mitigations |
| 41–60 | Elevated Trust Debt | Trust erosion is detectable and growing |
| 61–80 | High Trust Debt | Institutional credibility under active pressure |
| 81–100 | Critical Trust Debt | Governance credibility at systemic risk |

---

## TDS Assessment Questions (Sample)

- When was the last time an internal governance decision was disputed due to lack of documentation?
- Can you currently pass an unexpected external audit with confidence?
- How often do operational disputes arise from record ambiguity?
- How explainable are AI-assisted decisions in your current systems?
- How many governance decisions in the last year have no documented rationale?

# Composite Assessment Package

These four models can be combined into a single institutional assessment engagement:

**Institutional Continuity Assessment (ICA)**

| Model | Assessment Time | Output |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| Institutional Continuity Index (ICI) | 60 min | Composite score + band |
| Governance Fragility Score (GFS) | 45 min | Fragility map |
| Operational Memory Score (OMS) | 30 min | Stage classification |
| Trust Debt Score (TDS) | 30 min | Trust debt exposure |
| **Full ICA** | **2.5–3 hours** | **Full institutional continuity profile** |

**ICA Output Deliverable:** A structured institutional continuity profile identifying current state, risk zones, priority intervention points, and doctrine-aligned transformation roadmap.

---

# Scoring Model Governance

- Scoring models may not make external claims without peer review of at least 3 institutional assessments.
- Score thresholds may be adjusted based on institutional feedback — changes require Standard doctrine review.
- Assessment questions are living — additions permitted without full review; removals require review.
- Composite scoring weightings in ICI require doctrine review to change.
