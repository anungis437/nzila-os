## Founder Capacity Model

This plan is founder-executed until the hiring gate is reached. The table below shows sustainable workload:

| Activity | Hours/Week | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Active pilots (3) | 6–8 hrs | Weekly check-ins + reporting |
| Sales pipeline (outreach + calls) | 5–8 hrs | Minimum 5 outreach, 1–2 calls |
| Proposal writing | 2–4 hrs | ~1 proposal/week at target pace |
| Delivery + product | 20–25 hrs | Platform ops, features, infra |
| Admin + reporting | 3–4 hrs | Command center, KPIs, customer success |
| **Total** | **36–50 hrs/week** | Sustainable; at 5 pilots, hire CS |

**Hiring triggers:**
- 3+ SaaS customers: hire Customer Success Manager
- $500K ARR: hire first Sales Development Rep (SDR) for outreach volume
- $1M ARR: hire Head of Sales to own pipeline; founder shifts to product strategy

## Revenue Risk Factors

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Long procurement cycles (national bodies) | Delays Year 1 ARR | Focus on local/provincial in Year 1; national = Year 2+ |
| Pilot conversion rate below 65% | ARR flat in H2 2026 | More outreach volume, tighter pilot KPI structure |
| Churn from early SaaS customers | ARR erosion | Pilot-to-paid conversion playbook, QBR cadence |
| Founder capacity hit by delivery | Sales stops | Hire CS at 3 customers; guard founder time for pipeline |
| Market awareness (no inbound) | Outreach-dependent only | 1 conference per quarter + content output to build inbound signals |
