## What Is Union Eyes?

Union Eyes is a governed SaaS platform built specifically for labour unions and member organizations.

It manages the complete representation lifecycle — grievance intake, case management, steward coordination, arbitration prep, and outcome reporting — with a built-in audit trail that is tamper-evident and arbitration-ready.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most unions manage representation work across spreadsheets, email threads, and institutional memory. The consequences are predictable:

- Grievances fall through the cracks between stewards
- Arbitration prep is a fire drill every time
- Leadership has no real-time view of case risk or load
- Members lose trust when follow-through is invisible

General CRM and case tools don't understand union workflow semantics or evidence requirements. No purpose-built platform for this work exists that is governed, secure, and AI-ready.

---

## Why Now

The cost of weak representation governance has never been higher — member expectations, regulatory scrutiny, and arbitration outcomes all depend on the quality of the underlying case record.

Union Eyes exists and is deployment-ready. No multi-year build. No custom IT project. A controlled pilot can begin in days.

## Security and Governance Posture

| Area | Position |
|------|---------|
| Identity | Platform authentication + optional Azure Entra SSO |
| Org isolation | Database-level row isolation; no cross-org data access |
| Audit trail | Hash-chained, tamper-evident event log on every case action |
| Evidence | SHA-256 sealed evidence packs, arbitration-ready export |
| Backup and recovery | Automated DB backup; documented disaster recovery runbooks |
| Role controls | Full RBAC — each user sees only what their role permits |
| Privacy | Canadian data residency; PIPEDA-aligned data handling |
