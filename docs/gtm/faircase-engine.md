# FAIRCASE GTM Engine

## Objective
Position FAIRCASE as a Tier-1 flagship commercial wedge alongside UnionEyes and Flow.

## ICP Segments
- Public institutions with equity and accountability mandates
- Education systems and school boards
- Enterprise HR/legal/compliance teams
- Labor and advocacy organizations

## Funnel Design
1. Awareness: thought leadership and webinar series on governed equity operations.
2. Qualification: discovery calls with policy and reporting gap analysis.
3. Pilot: 8-week implementation with KPI baselines and checkpoints.
4. Conversion: enterprise proposal tied to measurable pilot outcomes.
5. Expansion: multi-org rollout and integration upsell.

## Revenue Motions
- Direct enterprise sales for high-complexity buyers
- Partner-led channel for sector-specific rollout
- Procurement-ready packages for public-sector buying

## KPI Layer
- Pipeline value and weighted pipeline by segment
- Pilot-to-paid conversion rate
- Sales cycle days
- Renewal and expansion rate
- Cost of acquisition versus annual contract value

## Execution Cadence
- Weekly deal review and objection analysis
- Bi-weekly pilot performance review
- Monthly executive dashboard and board summary
- Quarterly packaging and messaging refresh

## Non-Negotiables
- Preserve governance and evidence integrity claims with validation
- Keep technical app IDs stable while rebranding market-facing surfaces
- Align portfolio truth source with flagship strategy
