## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Pilots are paid.** No free trials. Pilot investment signals seriousness and covers delivery cost.
2. **Price by org size, not feature gate.** The full platform is available to all paying customers; tiers reflect scale and complexity of delivery, not capability restriction.
3. **Services are line-itemed separately.** Custom integrations, migrations, and training do not come bundled into SaaS. This protects margin and makes the SaaS ARR number clean.
4. **No open-ended fixed-bid delivery.** Any data migration or integration engagement requires a scoping session and a capped time-and-materials estimate before commitment.

## Pilot Fees

| Tier | Duration | Price Range | Scope Driver |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Discovery | 30 days | $7,000–$15,000 | Demo env + workflow mapping + stakeholder sessions |
| Controlled | 60 days | $20,000–$40,000 | Real users + real cases + KPI reporting |

### Discovery Pilot Pricing Details

| Org Scale | Indicative Range | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Small union (< 500 members) | $7,000–$9,000 | 1–2 walkthroughs, minimal config |
| Mid-size (500–5,000 members) | $9,000–$12,000 | 3 stakeholder sessions, full workflow map |
| Large / federation (5,000+) | $12,000–$15,000 | Multiple departments, extended session set |

### Controlled Pilot Pricing Details

| Org Scale | Users | Indicative Range |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| Small union | 5–15 | $20,000–$28,000 |
| Mid-size union | 15–50 | $28,000–$36,000 |
| Large / federation | 50+ | $36,000–$40,000+ |

Upward adjustments apply for:

- Historical data import (adds $3,000–$8,000 depending on volume and source system)
- Integration with existing HR, document management, or CRM system (scoped separately)
- Extended training beyond standard admin + end-user sessions (billed as services)

### Production Pilot Pricing Details

Base range: $50,000–$75,000 for a single department of 20–80 users with standard data migration.

Adjustments:

- Each additional 50 users: +$8,000–$12,000
- Complex legacy migration (manual records, multiple source systems): +$10,000–$25,000
- Each custom integration: +$5,000–$15,000 (scoped separately)
- Dedicated delivery manager (full engagement): +$8,000–$15,000

## Ongoing SaaS — Annual Subscription

Billing cycle: Annual upfront. Monthly billing available at a 15% premium.

### By Org Size

| Segment | Member Count | Active Users (Reps + Admins) | Monthly Price | Annual Price |
|---------|-------------|------------------------------|--------------|--------------|
| Small union | < 500 | 1–10 | $2,000–$3,000 | $24,000–$36,000 |
| Mid-market union | 500–5,000 | 10–40 | $4,000–$8,000 | $48,000–$96,000 |
| Large union | 5,000–25,000 | 40–100 | $8,000–$12,000 | $96,000–$144,000 |
| Enterprise / federation | 25,000+ | 100+ | $12,000–$20,000+ | $144,000–$240,000+ |

*"Active users" = users who log in at least once per month. Member-portal users (read-only case status) are not counted.*

### What Is Included in SaaS

- Full platform access (all modules)
- Org-isolated cloud environment (Azure Canada Central)
- Automated backups and DR capability
- Security patches and platform upgrades (no version lock-in)
- Health monitoring and ops coverage
- Enhanced support tier (P0: 2h response; P1: 4h; P2: 1 business day)
- Monthly evidence packs
- Quarterly governance snapshots
- Access to new features as released

### What Is Not Included in SaaS

The following are always billed as services:

- New custom integrations
- Large-scale data migrations triggered by a system change
- On-site training or in-person delivery
- Custom report development
- Compliance audits or formal security reviews

## Discount Policy

| Scenario | Discount |
|----------|---------|
| Annual upfront payment (SaaS) | Standard pricing |
| Multi-year commitment (2+ years) | 10% per year |
| Design partner (first 3 orgs) | 20% off SaaS Year 1 only |
| Non-profit or federation (qualifying) | Up to 15% off SaaS |

No stacking. Maximum discount: 25% in Year 1, 10% thereafter.

No free pilots. No unlimited-scope pilots.

---

## Commercial Guardrails

These rules are non-negotiable and must be stated in every commercial conversation:

1. **Pilot is mandatory before multi-year SaaS.** No org skips pilot.
2. **No open-ended custom builds.** Every custom request is scoped, priced, and approved before work begins.
3. **Pilot investment does not apply as credit toward SaaS.** Pilot fees cover discovery and delivery cost.
4. **Data migration complexity must be assessed before pricing.** Never quote a migration flat-fee without a scoping call.
5. **SaaS pricing scales by user count, not feature set.** Attempting to negotiate by feature removal dilutes the product.
