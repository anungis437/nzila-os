# Pilot-to-Paid Conversion Playbook — NzilaOS

> **Authority:** `docs/gtm/GTM_MASTER_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`  
> **Owner:** Platform Owner  
> **Updated:** 2026-04-20  
> No pilot should drift. Every pilot has a defined conversion sequence baked in from Day 1.

---

## The Problem This Solves

Pilots fail to convert for one of three reasons:

1. KPIs weren't locked at kickoff, so there's no objective basis for a "yes"
2. No exec-level touchpoints during the pilot — the decision-maker drifts out of the process
3. The conversion ask comes at the end, as a surprise, instead of being built into the structure

This playbook eliminates all three.

---

## Pre-Pilot: Foundation Work

### Before provisioning, get three things in writing

**1. KPIs — locked, agreed, measurable**

Use the KPI set from `docs/union-eyes/pilot-kpis.md`. Select 4–6 KPIs that are relevant to the org's stated pain. Confirm with the executive sponsor which ones they care most about.

Standard KPI lock-in for a Controlled Pilot:

| KPI | Baseline (Day 0) | Target (Day 60) | Owner |
|----|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| Cases created and tracked | X (from intake interview) | 100% new cases in system | Pilot admin |
| SLA compliance rate | [baseline if known, else TBD] | ≥ 85% | Org team |
| Evidence pack export | 0 (not currently produced) | ≥ 1 per active case | Pilot admin |
| Avg response time (grievance filed → first response) | [baseline] | -20% improvement | Org team |
| Steward adoption | 0% | ≥ 70% active users | Org team |

**2. Decision timeline — agreed**

Ask: "If the pilot goes well, when do you expect to make a decision on an annual subscription?"

Get a month. Write it down. This is your conversion calendar.

**3. Success criteria — named**

Ask: "What would this pilot need to show you for you to feel comfortable recommending Union Eyes to your executive committee?"

Write down their answer verbatim. This becomes the final slide of the outcome report.

## Pilot Cadence — Controlled Pilot (60 days)

### Day 0 — Kickoff

**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Attendees:** Executive sponsor + pilot admin + founder  
**Agenda:**

- Environment walkthrough (5 min)
- Confirm KPIs, lock baselines (10 min)
- User onboarding plan (10 min)
- Reporting cadence agreement (5 min)
- Questions (5 min)
- Confirm: "We will send a renewal proposal in Week 6 — that gives us two weeks to finalize before the pilot ends."

**What you send within 24 hours of kickoff:**

- KPI baseline document (email or shared doc)
- Week 1 check-in calendar invite
- Platform support contact (<platform-support@nzila.com>)

---

### Week 2 — First Check-In

**Format:** 20-minute call or async email  
**Owner:** Founder  
**Agenda:**

- Usage check: how many cases entered? How many users active?
- Any friction or questions?
- One feature they haven't used yet → proactive demo

**Conversion signal watch:** Are they going deeper than expected? Entering more cases than projected? That's a positive expansion signal.

---

### Week 4 — Midpoint Review

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Attendees:** Executive sponsor + pilot admin + founder  
**Agenda:**

- KPI midpoint update (10 min) — compare to baseline
- Workflow questions or gaps (10 min)
- **Expansion ask (5 min):** "We're seeing good usage in this cohort. Would it be valuable to loop in [other department / additional stewards] in the second half of the pilot?"
- Preview: "We'll have a conversion proposal ready in Week 6."

**Key move at Week 4:**  
This is the right time to ask: "Based on what you've seen so far, is there anything standing between you and continuing after the pilot?"

If they name a concern here, you have two weeks to address it before the conversion proposal.

---

### Week 6 — Conversion Proposal

**Deliver:** Written conversion proposal — Annual Platform Plan  
**Include:**

- KPI progress (Week 1–6 data)
- Recommended SaaS tier (based on actual user count and org size)
- Annual pricing (from `docs/gtm/PRICING_STRATEGY_V2.md`)
- Design partner discount if applicable
- Multi-year option if conversation has suggested long-term commitment
- "If you'd like to continue on the current pilot environment while finalizing the agreement, we can accommodate that for up to 14 days post-pilot."

**Do not wait for Week 8.** Two weeks is not enough time for procurement or executive committee approval.

---

### Week 7 — Proposal Review Call

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Goal:** Address any objections to the proposal. Use `docs/gtm/BUYER_OBJECTION_BATTLECARDS.md`.

**Questions to ask:**

1. "Does the pricing work for your budget cycle?"
2. "Is there anything in the scope that isn't what you expected?"
3. "Who else needs to be part of the final approval?"

---

### Week 8 — Outcome Report + Close

**Deliver on the last day of the pilot (or earlier if conversion is already signed):**

**Outcome Report contents:**

1. KPIs: baseline vs. final vs. target — in a clean table
2. Cases processed during the pilot — volume, resolution rate, SLA compliance
3. Evidence packs generated — count, quality
4. User adoption rate
5. 2–3 workflow wins (specific stories, not generic claims)
6. Verbatim response to their success criteria (from Day 0 kickoff)
7. Recommendation section: "Based on these results, we recommend [SaaS tier] at [price]."

**If not yet signed:** Outcome report + renewal proposal = the close packet. Schedule a decision call for Day 5 post-pilot.

## Post-Pilot: Conversion Sequence

### If signed

1. Send congratulations + next steps email
2. Confirm SaaS environment provisioning (same environment, just renewed)
3. Invoice immediately
4. Book 30-day post-launch check-in
5. Ask: "Would you be open to being listed as a reference customer?" (see Reference Request sequence below)

### If undecided (most common)

**Day 1 after pilot end:**
> "Thank you for [X] weeks on the platform. I've attached the outcome report. I'd like to get 20 minutes this week to walk through it together and confirm where you are on the annual plan."

**Day 5:**
> "Following up — has there been a chance to review the outcome report? I want to make sure we address anything before the decision is made."

**Day 10:**
> "I want to make sure the outcome report has been seen by the right people internally. Is there a version I should prepare for your executive committee or board?"

**Day 15 (deadline nudge):**
> "We'll be deprovisioning the pilot environment at the end of this week unless we have a signed agreement in place. I want to make sure there's no gap in access for your team. What's the hold-up?"

**Day 21 (final decision):**
> "I'll keep the environment available through [date]. After that, I'll need to proceed with deprovisioning to free up capacity. If the timing isn't right this cycle, I'd like to book a conversation for [month] when your next budget window opens."

### If declined

- Log reason verbatim
- Ask: "Is there a specific reason that would help us improve the pilot for future organizations?"
- Confirm: "When would be the right time to revisit this — is it purely a budget cycle issue, or was there something about the product or outcome that didn't meet the threshold?"
- Set a 6-month reactivation calendar reminder

## Reference Request Sequence

Use within 30 days of a successful conversion.

**Message 1 (informal ask, Week 4 of live SaaS):**
> "We're starting to expand conversations with a few other organizations in the labour sector. Would you be comfortable being listed as a customer we're working with — not sharing outcomes, just that you're a customer? That type of reference matters a lot at this stage."

**Message 2 (formal case study, if they said yes):**
> "We'd love to document the outcomes from your pilot for our case study library — it helps future organizations understand what's possible. I'll send you a short brief and we can finalize together. Everything gets your sign-off before publication."

**Message 3 (peer reference ask, if strong relationship):**
> "Is there anyone in your network — another ED or exec at a similar organization — who might benefit from seeing what you've built with Union Eyes? A warm introduction from you would mean a lot more than a cold outreach from us."

## Discovery Sprint Conversion Path

The Discovery Sprint doesn't have a 60-day KPI structure. Its conversion path is simpler:

| Step | Timing | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| Sprint scoping call | Day 0 | Confirm deliverables and timeline |
| Midpoint check-in | Day 15 | Are they engaged with the demo environment? |
| Sprint outcome | Day 30 | Deliver workflow mapping report + go/no-go recommendation |
| Conversion proposal | Day 30 | Attach Controlled Pilot or Production Pilot scope |
| Follow-up | Day 35 | "Any questions on the proposal?" |
| Decision call | Day 40 | Book specifically to get a yes/no |

---

## Production Pilot Conversion Path

Same structure as Controlled Pilot but scaled:

| Milestone | Week |
|-----------|------|
| Kickoff + KPI lock | Week 1 |
| Midpoint review | Week 5 |
| Expansion conversation | Week 6 |
| Conversion proposal | Week 9 |
| Proposal review | Week 10 |
| Outcome report | Week 12 |
| Decision deadline | Week 14 |
