## Biggest Real-World Bottlenecks (Honest Assessment)

These are not solvable with documents. They require execution.

### 1. Founder Time Split

The biggest risk is the platform owner spending > 60% of time on product/engineering and < 25% on sales. The GTM system is built. Using it requires time in the calendar, not just files in the repo.

**Diagnosis signal:** Check Tab 0 of `rollout-command-center.md` — Founder Time Allocation table — every Monday.

### 2. Procurement Cycles in Large Orgs

National-body targets (CUPE, PSAC, Unifor) have 6–18 month procurement cycles. Pursuing them in Year 1 is capital-inefficient. Local unions and provincial associations (CUPE Local 79, ONA, OECTA) move faster and are easier to close without a formal RFP process.

**Implication:** Year 1 target mix should be 80% local/provincial, 20% national-body education.

### 3. Social Proof Gap

Until the first 2–3 case studies exist, every conversation is a "trust the founder" ask. This is manageable with the existing trust assets but will limit conversion speed at organizations with cautious procurement cultures.

**Fastest fix:** Close a pilot in the first 60 days. Prioritize a case study-friendly organization over a larger but slower-moving one.

### 4. Inbound = Zero Today

All pipeline is outbound. There is no content engine, no conference presence, no referral network generating inbound. This is normal at this stage but means every deal requires founder-initiated outreach.

**Fix timeline:** After first SaaS conversion, invest 2 hours/week in a content output strategy (one post per week on LinkedIn linking to Union Eyes positioning). Conference presence (CLC, CALM, labour law conferences) begins in Year 2.

### 5. No SDR / No Sales Support

The founder is the only seller. This creates a ceiling on outreach volume and a single point of failure. Until $500K ARR, this is the correct operating model — founder-led selling builds better product insight. But the ceiling is real.

**Implication:** At $500K ARR, the first SDR hire unblocks the pipeline constraint. Until then, 5–10 outreach messages/week is the realistic ceiling.
