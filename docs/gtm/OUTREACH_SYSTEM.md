# Outreach System — NzilaOS

> **Authority:** `docs/gtm/PIPELINE_ENGINE.md`  
> **Owner:** Platform Owner  
> **Updated:** 2026-04-20  
> Nine proven outreach templates. Use them. Do not improvise. Improvised outreach is unfocused and slow.

---

## Principles

- **Brevity wins.** Under 150 words in every cold email. Under 80 words in every LinkedIn DM.
- **Specificity over flattery.** "I saw your report on arbitration outcomes" beats "Your organization does great work."
- **One ask per message.** Either ask for a meeting, ask for a referral, or ask a question. Never multiple asks.
- **Name the pain.** Every message names a specific operational problem, not a feature set.
- **No attachments on first contact.** First email is to get a reply. The deck comes after.

## Template 1: Warm Intro Ask

*Use when you know someone who knows the decision-maker. Send to your shared contact, not the target.*

**Subject:** Intro request — [Target Name] / [Target Org]

---

Hi [Mutual Name],

Quick ask — I'm reaching out to [Target Name] at [Target Org] about a platform we built for union governance and representation operations. We help teams move from spreadsheet-based grievance tracking to a fully auditable, managed system.

I know you've worked with them / know them through [context]. Would you be comfortable making a brief intro if you think it's relevant?

Happy to send a short summary if that helps.

Thanks — [Your Name]

---

**Notes:** Keep it under 80 words. Make it easy for them to say yes or no. Don't oversell the product here.

## Template 2: Cold Executive Email

*Use for direct first-time contact. Decision-maker is Executive Director, Secretary-Treasurer, VP Operations, or General Counsel.*

**Subject:** Governance system for [Org Name]'s grievance operations

---

Hi [First Name],

I'm reaching out because I work with labour organizations on a specific problem: grievance cases tracked in spreadsheets, evidence assembled manually, and no leadership visibility into SLA compliance.

We built a managed platform — Union Eyes — that governs the full representation lifecycle. It's purpose-built for organizations like [Org Name] where evidence quality and audit trails aren't optional.

We've structured it as a paid pilot so you can validate before committing to anything long-term. Typically a 30-day scoped evaluation, or a 60-day live deployment with real cases.

Would 20 minutes make sense to see if this fits your operation?

[Your Name]  
[Title] · NzilaOS  
[Email] · [Phone]

---

**Notes:** "Purpose-built for organizations like [Org Name]" should reference something specific about them if possible. If you've read their public reports or know their caseload size, one sentence of specificity here will double response rates.

## Template 3: Conference Follow-Up

*Use within 24 hours of meeting or seeing them at a labour conference, legal conference, or sector event.*

**Subject:** Following up from [Conference Name]

---

Hi [First Name],

Good to [meet you / catch your panel] at [Conference Name] yesterday.

I'm following up briefly because your comments on [specific thing they said or a known topic from the conference] map closely to what we're solving at NzilaOS.

We built Union Eyes specifically for organizations that need governed representation workflows — not generic project management, but purpose-built for grievance, evidence, and audit accountability.

I'd like to show you what it does in 20 minutes. Are you available next week?

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** If you didn't talk to them directly, reference something public — a panel they were on, a session topic, an announcement. The goal is to signal that this isn't mass outreach.

## Template 4: LinkedIn DM

*First contact via LinkedIn. Keep it very short. The goal is a reply, not a sale.*

---

Hi [First Name] — I work with labour organizations on governed representation operations. We built a platform for grievance tracking and audit accountability that replaces spreadsheet workflows entirely.

Given your role at [Org], it might be worth a 15-minute call. Would that be useful?

---

**Notes:** Under 60 words. LinkedIn DMs are read on mobile. No long explanations. No attachments. If they reply, move to email immediately.

## Template 5: Referral Request

*Ask a satisfied or connected contact to refer you to a target org.*

**Subject:** Quick ask — referral to [Target Org]

---

Hi [Contact Name],

I'm trying to get in front of [Target Name or role] at [Target Org]. We built a governance platform for union operations (grievances, evidence management, audit trails) and I think there's a real fit.

Do you have any connection there you'd be comfortable activating? Even a quick LinkedIn message from you would open a door.

Happy to give you a full brief if helpful.

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** Be specific about who you're trying to reach, not just the organization. This makes it easier for them to say yes and easier for them to make the right intro.

## Template 6: Reactivation Email

*Use when a prospect went quiet after 1–3 positive interactions. Wait at least 2 weeks before sending.*

**Subject:** Re: [previous thread subject] / quick check-in

---

Hi [First Name],

I know you've had a lot on — just checking back in briefly.

The last time we spoke, you mentioned [specific thing they said — e.g., "you were dealing with a backlog of unresolved grievances going into arbitration season"]. I wanted to see if that's still a live problem or if the timing just isn't right.

No pressure — if the priority has shifted, just let me know and I'll follow up at a better point.

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** The reference to a specific thing they said is critical. Generic reactivation emails are ignored. Showing you listened is what reopens the conversation.

## Template 7: Pilot Urgency Email

*Use when a prospect has expressed interest but hasn't committed after 2+ weeks.*

**Subject:** Pilot slot availability — [Org Name]

---

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to be transparent with you — we're scoping pilot timelines for Q[X] and I have a limited number of provisioned environments I can stand up in that window.

If [Org Name] is still interested in starting in [month], I'd want to get the scope locked this week. The Discovery Sprint gets you in quickly — 30 days, no production commitment, full outcome report.

Should we get a 20-minute call this week to confirm scope?

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** This is real, not artificial scarcity. Pilot onboarding requires provisioning time. The framing should be genuine. Do not send this unless the constraint is real.

## Template 8: Post-Demo Follow-Up

*Send within 24 hours of a demo, if no response on next steps by the next morning.*

**Subject:** Following up — [Org Name] + Union Eyes

---

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for the time yesterday. To summarize where I think we landed:

- [Specific relevant workflow they described] = direct fit with how Union Eyes handles [specific module]
- [Any concern they raised] = [one sentence on how it's addressed]
- Recommended next step: [specific offer — e.g., "Discovery Sprint, 30 days, $X, starting in [month]"]

I'll put together a formal scope proposal today. Is there anything specific you want it to address before I send it?

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** This email shows you were listening and moves immediately toward a proposal. Fill in the blanks with real notes from the demo. This template is useless without real specifics.

## Template 9: Lost Deal Resurrection

*Use 3–6 months after a deal stalled or was explicitly declined. Tone is low-pressure and informational.*

**Subject:** Quick update — Union Eyes, [relevant to their context]

---

Hi [First Name],

We spoke earlier this year about governance tooling for [Org Name]. I know the timing wasn't right.

I wanted to share a quick update because it might be relevant now: [short, specific update — e.g., "We've added a full arbitration evidence packing module that generates court-ready dossiers automatically" OR "We recently completed a pilot with a provincial union of similar size and the outcome was strong"].

If the situation has changed at your end, I'd be glad to reconnect.

[Your Name]

---

**Notes:** The resurrection email works only if you have something new to say. "Just checking in" is a waste of their time. The specific update is what makes this credible.
