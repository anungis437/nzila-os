# CPA and Consultant Package Checklist

## Core Package
1. Corporate entity details
2. Fiscal year boundaries
3. Payroll by person
4. Contractor invoices
5. Project allocation percentages
6. Eligible vs excluded work split
7. Evidence index
8. Consultant agreement (if any)
9. Prior claims (if any)

## Submission Control
- Technical lead signs off on project narratives and evidence links.
- Finance signs off on allocation math and source records.
- Consultant validates language/alignment for T661 but does not author technical facts.

## CRA Readiness Notes
- Keep project-level uncertainty and experimental progression explicit.
- Maintain traceability from claim statements to evidence artifacts and time entries.
- Ensure exclusions are documented to reduce over-claim risk.
