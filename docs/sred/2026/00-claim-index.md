## CRA-Oriented Operating Principle
Work must be grouped into SR&ED projects with a clear technological uncertainty, systematic investigation, and supporting evidence. The claimant remains responsible for accuracy even when consultants are used.

- CRA "Get ready to claim": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/claim-sred-tax-incentive-what-claim/get-ready-claim.html

## Risk Controls
- Two-ledger rule:
  - SR&ED Candidate Work: uncertainty-driven experimental work only.
  - Non-SR&ED Commercial Work: productization, normal engineering, GTM, and operations.
- Exclusion rule: routine software build activities are explicitly tracked in non-eligible records.
- Time-code rule: all relevant time entries must use SR&ED and non-SR&ED codes.
