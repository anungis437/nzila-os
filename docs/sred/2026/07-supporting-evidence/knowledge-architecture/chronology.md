# Knowledge Architecture - Chronology

## Entry 1 - Early ontology and matrix construction
- Uncertainty: whether continuity/governance concepts could be represented in machine-readable relationships.
- Attempt: continuity ontology matrix and relationship modeling.
- Failure/constraint: ambiguous categories and anti-patterns emerged.
- Learning: ontology governance needed explicit anti-pattern identification.
- Evidence: apps/union-eyes/config/continuity-ontology-matrix.json, apps/union-eyes/reports/ontology-antipattern-inventory.md

## Entry 2 - Doctrine and standards structuring
- Uncertainty: whether doctrine and standards crosswalks could remain computable and internally consistent.
- Attempt: standards crosswalk and coefficient registry formalization.
- Failure/constraint: consistency risks appeared across category and coefficient definitions.
- Learning: architecture required explicit registries and governance checkpoints.
- Evidence: docs/oci/methodology/standards-crosswalk.yaml, docs/oci/methodology/coefficient-registry.yaml

## Entry 3 - Cross-sector reasoning preparation
- Uncertainty: whether architecture could support governance/continuity reasoning across contexts.
- Attempt: question architecture inventories and governance/procurement mappings.
- Failure/constraint: structure needed deeper inventory and trace controls.
- Learning: cross-sector reasoning depends on transparent, versioned architecture artifacts.
- Evidence: docs/oci/audit/QUESTION_ARCHITECTURE_INVENTORY.md, docs/oci/audit/QUESTION_ARCHITECTURE_GOVERNANCE.md
