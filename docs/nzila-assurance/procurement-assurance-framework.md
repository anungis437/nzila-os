# Procurement Assurance Framework

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** Procurement-grade evidence · **Inherits:** [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md), [automated-governance-evidence-system.md](automated-governance-evidence-system.md), [../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md](../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md)

This document translates Nzila doctrine and assurance evidence into the **language and shape that institutional procurement requires**: governance assurance, AI assurance, operational assurance, continuity assurance, deployment assurance, modernization risk assurance.

It exists because procurement officers, regulators, and institutional buyers need **operational trust evidence**, not marketing claims. This framework provides the bridge.

---

## 1. Posture

Procurement assurance at Nzila is:

- **Evidence-led** — assurance is anchored in artifacts from the [evidence pipeline](governance-evidence-pipeline-architecture.md), not narrative
- **Doctrine-consistent** — language honors [../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md](../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md)
- **Operationally grounded** — claims tie to running system behavior, not roadmap
- **Conservative** — assurance is offered only where it can be defended
- **Categorically anti-surveillance** — assurance never frames Nzila as a surveillance, scoring, or productivity-extraction vendor

The assurance posture is operating-mode, not sales-mode.

## 3. Required Procurement Outputs

### 3.1 Procurement Evidence Pack
A reviewer-facing bundle for an institutional buyer or regulator:

- Scope statement (which Nzila system, which deployment, which classes)
- Certification tier per class (per [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md))
- Latest assurance findings per dimension
- Sampled evidence artifacts with signatures
- Standing review reference ([assurance-readiness-review.md](assurance-readiness-review.md))
- Doctrine references for each assurance claim

The pack is generated, not authored. Manual narration is added only as preface, not as substance.

### 3.2 Governance Attestation Bundle
A formal attestation suitable for certification bodies:

- Governance posture statement
- Compliance review verdicts for the certification window
- AI governance review records for AI-touching capabilities
- Architectural review records for material platform changes
- Reviewer identifications (by role)
- Signing chain for retained evidence

### 3.3 Deployment Legitimacy Report
A release-window report for institutional buyers under change management oversight:

- Releases in window with manifests
- Sequencing and pacing rationale
- Reversibility evidence
- Stakeholder visibility actions taken
- Incident and rollback record
- Continuity posture trajectory through the window

### 3.4 Continuity Governance Summary
A continuity-focused assurance summary:

- Continuity surfaces and their posture readings
- Isolation enforcement evidence
- Aggregation stance evidence
- Retention and portability evidence
- Workforce trust safeguards in operation

### 3.5 Explainability Assurance Summary
For AI-touching systems:

- Capabilities in scope and their explainability surfaces
- Authority surface presence and override path validation
- Anti-surveillance posture evidence
- Prohibited automation absence (categorical)
- Generation indication coverage

### 3.6 Pilot Safety Attestation
For pilot engagements:

- Pilot scope, structurally bound
- Pilot route gating evidence
- Pilot data isolation from production analytics
- Pilot exit criteria and governance plan
- Reversibility plan

## 4. Procurement Language Discipline

Procurement materials use approved language from [../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md](../nzila-governance/investor-and-procurement-narrative-alignment.md). Specifically:

- "Operational trust infrastructure" (not "platform")
- "Governance-safe modernization" (not "transformation")
- "Continuity-safe AI exposure" (not "AI capabilities")
- "Pilot discipline as a structural commitment" (not "pilot programs")
- "Aggregation stance preserves workforce trust by construction" (not "no monitoring")

Materials that drift into AI-startup, disruption, or productivity language are returned for correction.

---

## 5. Evidence Presentation Standards

- Evidence is presented in its reviewable form, with cited sources
- Findings are stated honestly: *Met*, *Partially Met*, *Not Met*
- Gaps are named with remediation paths
- Tier claims are paired with the evidence threshold they meet
- Trend information is included where the buyer requires sustained-attestation evidence

A presentation that claims more than the evidence supports is corrected before delivery.

---

## 6. Anti-Patterns

- **Marketing assurance** — assurance materials that read as product brochures
- **Roadmap-as-evidence** — committing to future capability in place of present evidence
- **Tier inflation** — presenting C4 claims with C2 evidence
- **Selective scope** — narrowing scope to exclude unfavorable evidence without disclosure
- **Manual narrative replacing automated evidence** — descriptions in place of artifacts
- **Surveillance positioning** — framing aggregation refusal as a feature negotiation rather than a doctrinal commitment

---

## 7. Procurement Engagement Posture

Nzila enters procurement engagements as:

- An institutional governance counterparty
- An evidence provider, not a story teller
- A long-horizon vendor where stewardship outranks velocity
- A partner whose doctrine constrains what it will sell

When a procurement requirement is incompatible with doctrine, Nzila declines or proposes a doctrine-aligned alternative. It does not adapt doctrine to win the engagement.

---

## 8. Discipline

Procurement assurance succeeds when buyers can defend their selection of Nzila on governance grounds, not on velocity, fashion, or feature surface area.

This framework is the discipline by which Nzila earns that defense.
