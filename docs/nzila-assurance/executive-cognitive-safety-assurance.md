# Executive Cognitive Safety Assurance

> **Status:** Canonical assurance · **Layer:** Executive surface assurance · **Inherits:** [../nzila-governance/executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md](../nzila-governance/executive-cognitive-governance-standards.md), [../nzila-ip/executive-cognitive-governance.md](../nzila-ip/executive-cognitive-governance.md), [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md)

This document defines the **measurable assurance standards** for executive operational calmness and cognitive governance. It is the assurance counterpart to the executive cognitive governance standards: standards specify *what* must be true; this model specifies *how it is verified, attested, and certified*.

It is the model by which Nzila certifies executive surfaces under class **Executive Cognitive Governance**.

---

## 1. Posture

Executive cognitive assurance is:

- **Surface-bounded** — assurance is per executive surface, not per product
- **Threshold-anchored** — assurance is against measurable thresholds from the governance standards
- **Continuously observed** — calmness is verified at runtime, not only at design review
- **Non-coercive** — assurance is a stewardship instrument; never used to pressure executives or teams
- **Anti-surveillance-safe** — observability honors aggregation stance

A surface whose calmness depends on memory or taste is not assured.

---

## 2. Measurable Thresholds

### 2.1 Information Density
- Distinct interactive regions on primary view: bounded per surface class
- Distinct information classes on primary view: bounded
- Concurrent state indicators: bounded; doctrinally minimal
- Concurrent affordances: bounded; supports primary act
- Tile count on primary dashboard: bounded; each tile doctrinally justified

### 2.2 Escalation Pacing
- Escalation arrival rate per role-bearer: bounded
- Sustained breach window: triggers governance review
- Covert escalation: prohibited (zero tolerance)
- De-escalation parity with escalation: required

### 2.3 Alert Restraint
- Alert classes: bounded and doctrinally defined
- Alert arrival rate: bounded; sustained breach reviewed
- Click-through acknowledgment rate suggesting fatigue: indicator
- Color-only meaning: prohibited
- Alerts surfacing restricted-class individual signals: prohibited

### 2.4 Strategic Readability
- Reading time to surface posture: short, bounded
- Time to act from posture to governance act: short, predictable
- Type hierarchy expresses continuity hierarchy: required
- Quantitative content paired with interpretive context: required

### 2.5 Governance-Safe Visibility
- Per-role visibility resolved by visibility policy
- Rationale availability for every shown signal
- No "show everything" override
- Privileged visibility itself is a reviewed surface

### 2.6 Operational Calmness
- Motion: state-transition only
- Layout stability under live updates: required
- Notification arrivals on executive surfaces: bounded
- Real-time-style feeds on executive surfaces: prohibited

### 2.7 Cognitive Overload Risk
- Page count to comprehensive posture: doctrinally minimal
- Concurrent open governance acts visible: bounded
- Reading complexity (sentence length, jargon density on primary content): governed
- Disclosure depth required for primary comprehension: minimal

### 2.8 Continuity-Safe Executive UX
- Hierarchy: posture → stake → signal → interpretation → act (invariant)
- Composite continuity scoring: prohibited (zero tolerance)
- Authority framing on consequential surfaces: required
- Doctrinal vocabulary used consistently: required

---

## 3. Validation Models

### 3.1 UX Review
Design reviewer applies thresholds at design time and records findings against each dimension.

### 3.2 Heuristic Review
Independent reviewer applies executive cognitive heuristics (calmness, restraint, hierarchy, readability) and records findings.

### 3.3 Operational Pacing Review
Steward reviews refresh cadence, alert arrival rates, escalation cadence, and notification budgets against governance-cadence expectations using runtime evidence.

### 3.4 Governance Readability Review
Steward verifies that an executive can comprehend continuity posture, identify consequential exposure, and act within doctrinal time floors. Findings recorded against readability thresholds.

Each review produces an evidence record contributing to certification under [institutional-certification-framework.md](institutional-certification-framework.md).

## 4. Tiered Executive Cognitive Assurance

| Tier | Evidence Threshold |
|------|--------------------|
| C0 | Unverified |
| C1 | UX review applied; thresholds acknowledged |
| C2 | Heuristic and operational pacing reviews applied; partial automated evidence |
| C3 | Continuous evidence (density audits, notification rate observation, refresh cadence verification) tied to release gates |
| C4 | Signed evidence retention; external reviewer can verify without privileged access; standing review sustained |
| C5 | Sustained C4; suitable as reference for executive cognitive governance |

---

## 5. Categorical Non-Certifiable Patterns

Executive surfaces presenting any of the following do not enter certification until structurally remediated:

- Composite continuity scoring on a primary executive surface
- Real-time-style feeds on executive surfaces
- Color-saturated alert walls
- Notification streams replacing summary cadence
- Dashboards constructed for breadth or showcase rather than governance support
- Click-through acknowledgment without governance act
- Tabular data dumps as primary executive surface
- Covert escalation paths

---

## 6. Anti-Patterns in Assurance Itself

- **Aesthetic assurance** — certifying calmness based on look-and-feel rather than measurable thresholds
- **One-time assurance** — assuring at launch and never again
- **Reviewer rotation insufficiency** — same reviewer indefinitely; calibration drift
- **Threshold relaxation** — moving thresholds to accommodate surfaces rather than vice versa
- **Vanity dashboards in observability** — observability surfaces that themselves violate executive thresholds

---

## 7. External Posture

Executive cognitive assurance is presentable in:

- Procurement evidence packs for executive-touching products
- Regulator engagements where executive governance is in scope
- Board and steward communications
- Certification submissions

It is presented as institutional operating-mode evidence, not as design-quality marketing.

---

## 8. Discipline

Executive cognitive assurance succeeds when executive role-bearers can be relied upon to read continuity posture accurately, calmly, and at governance cadence.

This model is the discipline by which Nzila protects the most consequential cognitive resource in the institution.
