# Customer Proof Playbook

Date: 2026-04-22

## Purpose

Create a repeatable system to capture credible social proof from pilots without overstating outcomes.

## 1) Testimonial Capture Workflow

1. Trigger at measurable milestone (not before).
2. Prepare 5 structured prompts tied to specific outcomes.
3. Capture quote in writing and optionally video.
4. Confirm wording approval from customer owner.
5. Store approved final version in proof repository.

## 2) Case Study Template

- Customer context
- Initial problem and baseline
- Pilot scope and timeline
- Outcome metrics (before/after)
- Operational and leadership impact
- Customer quote
- Constraints and what did not change

## 3) KPI Capture During Pilots

Required KPI set:
- cycle time
- admin effort hours
- reporting turnaround
- user adoption by role

Rules:
- baseline captured before launch
- weekly measurement cadence
- one owner per KPI
- explicit method documentation

## 4) Permission Workflow

1. Internal draft created.
2. Customer reviewer validates factual accuracy.
3. Legal approval for publication scope.
4. Marketing approval for channel usage.
5. Record final approval artifact with timestamp.

## 5) Logo Usage Approvals

- Never use customer logo without explicit written permission.
- Track permitted surfaces (web, deck, one-pager, PR).
- Track expiration or revocation conditions.

## 6) Renewal Evidence Loop

At renewal checkpoints, collect:
- KPI trend continuation
- business-owner sentiment
- security/ops confidence signals
- expansion readiness indicators

Output artifact:
- renewal evidence memo per account, linked to next contract cycle.
