# Responsible AI Controls

## Control Objectives

- AI output is advisory, not autonomous final decisioning.
- Human operators retain approval authority in critical workflows.
- Auditability of AI-assisted decisions is required.

## Implementation Summary

- Confidence and evidence-oriented output patterns are used in platform AI flows.
- Governance and policy controls exist in platform packages.

## Claims Boundary

- No claim of formal third-party AI certification is made in this package.
