## Step 1 — ICP Fit Summary (From GTM Docs Review)

### What the ICP model scores (from `ICP_DEFINITION.md` + `icp-scoring.ts`)

| Dimension | Weight | Sweet-spot signal |
|---|---|---|
| Sector fit | 30% | Municipal ≥ Healthcare ≥ Federal ≥ Provincial |
| Member scale | 20% | ≥ 2,000 scores 1.0; ≥ 500 scores 0.8 |
| Governance complexity | 20% | Multi-unit, active arbitrations, contested elections |
| Tech modernisation need | 15% | Spreadsheet/paper only scores 1.0 |
| Warm path | 10% | CLC affiliate relationship or partner referral |
| Contract expiry soon | 5% | ≤ 12 months to next CBA expiry |

**Tier A** ≥ 0.70 → Assign immediately. High probability.  
**Tier B** ≥ 0.40 → Good fit, 2–3 data points to qualify.  
**Tier C** < 0.40 → Channel/event only.

### Key Union Eyes Advantages That Drive Fit

1. **Canadian data sovereignty** — Azure Canada Central, PIPEDA-aligned, no U.S. data transit. Non-negotiable for public sector procurement and federal affiliates.
2. **Purpose-built vocabulary** — The product knows what a Step 3 is. It knows ULP filings, arbitration-ready bundle exports, seniority disputes, and CA-step deadline tracking. Not a CRM with union labels.
3. **Pilot economics** — $12,000 CAD / 90 days, fully credited to Year 1 subscription. Lower friction than any custom build or enterprise SaaS.
4. **Cognition ROI is auditable** — 50h/month saved for a typical 15-steward local at $65/hr loaded rate = $3,250/month. Breaks even at any licence under ~$3,000/month/local.
5. **Bilingual out of the box** — EN/FR from day one. Mandatory for NB, federal, and Ontario francophone affiliates.
6. **CUPE pilot precedent** — Healthcare, municipal, and post-secondary CUPE locals are co-designers. The CUPE pilot offer (`pilot-offer-cupe.md`) removes trust friction for any CUPE affiliate.
