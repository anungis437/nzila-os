# Union Eyes: Executive Summary

**The Problem Unions Face**

Union reps spend more time managing paperwork than protecting members. Grievances are tracked in spreadsheets. Case timelines are reconstructed from memory. Arbitration prep means digging through email chains. The result: delayed outcomes, avoidable losses, and burned-out stewards.

**What Union Eyes Does**

Union Eyes is a modern operating system for union administration. It replaces fragmented tools — spreadsheets, shared drives, email threads, handwritten notes — with a single system that connects intake to outcome across every pillar of union operations:

| Module | What It Replaces |
|---|---|
| Grievance Management | Excel + paper intake forms |
| Case Intelligence | Manual pattern analysis, gut feel |
| Governance & Motions | Email chains, Roberts Rules of Order notes |
| Member Communication | Mailchimp + group SMS |
| Elections & Voting | Paper ballots, SurveyMonkey |
| Document Vault | Shared Google Drive |

**Early Results (Pilot Organizations)**

- −58% grievance cycle time (Healthcare Local, Ontario)
- −44% admin burden per rep (Regional Council, British Columbia)
- 3× faster arbitration document preparation
- Zero data incidents in 18 months of operation

**Who It's For**

- **Local unions** (100–5,000 members): Full platform — grievance tracking, case management, member comms
- **Regional councils & federations**: Multi-local governance, cross-unit analytics, consolidated reporting
- **Public sector units** (universities, municipalities, Crown corps): Grievance management, election administration, policy documentation

**Why Now**

Labour organizations are under increasing regulatory scrutiny, member accountability expectations, and operational complexity. The unions that modernize their operating infrastructure in the next 36 months will be positioned to win; those that don't will spend those years managing crises.
