## 1. Purpose of the BDC Workstream

This workstream assembled and stress-tested the credit-readiness package for Nzila Ventures Inc.'s $75,000 BDC financing request. The objective was to identify every finding an underwriter would raise, consolidate those findings into a remediation register, and establish the exact gap between current documentation and a submittable credit package.

The workstream produced an evidence dossier, eight internal underwriting stress-test documents, a consolidated remediation register, and a credit package readiness assessment. It did not produce a financeable package — that requires verified inputs from the founders, the company's accountant, legal counsel, and BDC directly.
