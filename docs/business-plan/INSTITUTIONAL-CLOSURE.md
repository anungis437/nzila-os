## What This Transition Means

**Active authoring** is the phase in which the repository's business-plan corpus was being constructed, organized, and structured from first principles. Documents were created, section by section, to capture and evidence the company's commercial thesis, product portfolio, governance posture, and credit readiness.

**Institutional maintenance** is the phase in which the corpus is complete and is maintained through controlled, traceable, evidence-based revisions. No further broad construction is required. Changes occur in response to verified inputs — not in response to further analysis or strategy elaboration.

This repository now represents the **official commercialization knowledge base for Nzila Ventures Inc.**
