### What the company does

Nzila Ventures builds and operates **Union Eyes**, a software platform that helps Canadian labour unions manage grievances, track cases, communicate with members, and administer governance. It runs on dedicated Canadian Azure infrastructure (Azure Canada Central), is bilingual (English/French), and is designed specifically for Canadian labour organizations.

The same technical platform also supports **CIVIC**, an institutional-intelligence tool for public-sector organizations, and provides the foundation for **CourtLens**, a planned legal-sector extension. Union Eyes is the commercial focus of this financing request.

---

### Why this company

Union Eyes addresses a documented operational problem: Canadian labour unions manage complex caseloads on general-purpose tools — spreadsheets, email, and generic project software — that were not designed for collective-agreement workflows, audit requirements, or arbitration-evidence preparation. No Canadian-hosted, union-specific software platform exists at the feature depth Nzila has built.

The company is not pre-product. Union Eyes has received internal production-readiness certification, has a documented controlled-pilot GO clearance, and has a fully published pricing framework, sales kit, and pilot program. A lender can review a deployed system, a written pilot offer, and a defined 14-day go-live timeline before approving this loan.

---

### Why now

The Canadian labour sector is under increasing regulatory and operational pressure: hybrid work arrangements, duty-to-accommodate obligations, and digital-record requirements from arbitration panels are creating demand for structured digital case management. Existing tools are generic, US-hosted, and inadequate for bilingual Canadian requirements.

Union Eyes was built for this moment. The product is ready. The sales materials are ready. The first pilot conversation is the rate-limiting step — not product development.

### Why it will be repaid

The repayment logic is straightforward:

**One converted Local-tier subscription ($28,000/year) covers debt service.**

The assumed loan amortization is 5 years at approximately $1,450/month ($17,400/year). A single converted Union Eyes pilot at the mid-tier Local plan generates $28,000/year — more than enough to service the debt.

The path:

1. Pilot contract signed (target: within 60 days of funding)
2. Pilot invoice issued: $12,000 + HST at go-live
3. 90-day pilot runs; customer uses the system
4. Conversion decision: if customer converts, Year 1 subscription net of credit = $16,000 incremental cash
5. Year 2 subscription: full $28,000
6. Debt service: ~$17,400/year — covered by a single mid-tier account

In the downside scenario, SR&ED tax refund claims ($140,000 claimed in 2025; $220,000 projected for 2026) provide a backstop. These claims are active and on file with CRA.

The company has zero existing debt. Personal guarantee by Aubert is available if required.
