# Stabilization Signals System

> **Status:** Canonical governance operations · **Layer:** Stabilization · **Inherits:** [continuity-posture-review-system.md](continuity-posture-review-system.md)

## 1. Objective

Surface stabilization indicators that encourage the institution to slow down, distribute load, and preserve continuity — never to accelerate, react, or surveil.

## 2. Required signals

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| Operational calmness | Banded calmness across observation window |
| Governance stability | Banded stability of doctrine enforcement outcomes |
| Continuity stabilization | Banded continuity stabilization trajectory |
| Modernization pacing integrity | Banded pacing of modernization changes |
| Deployment legitimacy confidence | Banded confidence in last N legitimacy validations |
| Onboarding continuity | Banded onboarding pathway integrity (system-scoped) |
| Governance-safe rollout posture | Banded readiness of any in-flight rollout |

## 3. Required surfaces

- Stabilization bands — single banded reading per signal.
- Calmness indicators — restrained text + a single muted indicator.
- Governance rhythm panels — cadence of governance acts over the window.
- Modernization pacing summaries — banded pacing across the rollout corridor.
- Deployment confidence summaries — banded across last N deploys.

## 4. Posture

Stabilization signals MUST:

- **Encourage stabilization.** A `concerning` reading suggests slowing, not accelerating.
- **Refuse composite scores.** Each signal stands alone.
- **Refresh on slow cadence.** Default 5 minutes for stabilization signals.
- **Suggest action calmly.** Suggestions are advisory; humans hold authority.

## 5. Discipline

A stabilization signal that triggers reactive operator behavior has degraded from governance into telemetry addiction. The institution is healthier with one calm honest band than ten reactive panels.
