# Continuity Posture Review System

> **Status:** Canonical governance operations · **Layer:** Continuity review · **Inherits:** [continuity-observability-runtime-embedding.md](../nzila-runtime-integration/continuity-observability-runtime-embedding.md)

## 1. Objective

Expose institutional continuity posture in a governance-safe way — interpretive, stabilization-oriented, never judgmental of individuals.

## 2. Required visualizations

| Visualization | Reading |
|---|---|
| Fragmentation trend | Banded fragmentation across the windowed observation period |
| Coordination stabilization | Banded coherence across orchestration boundaries |
| Onboarding continuity | Banded onboarding pathway continuity, system-scoped |
| Governance friction accumulation | Banded friction observed across governance-bearing acts |
| Escalation concentration | Banded escalation density (system, never per-person) |
| Continuity-safe modernization health | Banded modernization pacing integrity |

## 3. Posture

Visualizations MUST be:

- **Interpretive**, not judgmental.
- **System-scoped**, never person-scoped.
- **Banded**, never numeric scores presented as truth.
- **Stabilization-suggesting**, never throughput-pressing.

Visualizations MUST NOT:

- Score employees.
- Rank teams.
- Surface coercive analytics.
- Aggregate person behaviour into a profile.

## 4. Required panels

- Posture review cards — one card per continuity dimension.
- Trend narratives — short institutional sentences, not chart annotations.
- Stabilization guidance panels — advisory only, with a clear human-authority path.
- Continuity pathway visualizations — calm, low-density.
- Governance-safe interpretation helpers — text that translates banding into action language.

## 5. Discipline

Continuity posture review succeeds when reviewers leave the surface having understood the institution's continuity, not having acquired anxiety about it. If reviewers walk away resolved to push harder, the visualization has failed regardless of technical correctness.
