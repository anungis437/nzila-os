# FairCase Governance Realignment

> Canonical realignment of FairCase intelligence surfaces toward **Institutional Equity Continuity Infrastructure**.

## 1. Convergence Statement

FairCase exists to **support investigatory continuity and institutional equity preservation**. It is governance-safe, investigation-supportive, and continuity-oriented.

FairCase is **not**:

- workplace surveillance
- behavioral ranking infrastructure
- workforce optimization
- coercive workplace intelligence
- employee scoring

## 2. Refactor Targets

- Bias intelligence → bounded bias-signal interpretation with explicit human investigatory authority.
- Exposure scoring → equity-continuity exposure interpretation; never used to rank individuals.
- Investigation intelligence → bounded investigation-support cognition.
- Classification systems → continuity-safe case categorization, with human review of record.
- Recommendation systems → bounded suggestion surfaces, never authoritative dispositions.
- Behavioral interpretation systems → prohibited at the individual-employee level.

## 3. Explicit Prohibitions

FairCase **must not**:

- surveil individual employees
- rank individuals by behavior
- score employees
- optimize a workforce coercively
- present authoritative case dispositions without human investigatory authority

These prohibitions are doctrinal and validator-enforced.

## 4. Required Surface Behavior

Every FairCase intelligence surface must:

- present interpretation as bounded
- expose the human investigator of record
- include the canonical bounded-interpretation disclaimer
- avoid scoring or ranking semantics
- avoid surveillance or productivity-optimization semantics

## 5. Authority

This realignment derives from the [Institutional Operational Cognition Doctrine](institutional-operational-cognition-doctrine.md) and the [Global Anti-Surveillance Enforcement](global-anti-surveillance-enforcement.md) policy.
