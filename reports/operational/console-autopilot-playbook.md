# Console Autopilot Playbook

## Objective

Create a weekly recommendation engine that turns operational signals into one-click approved decisions and tracked initiatives.

## Inputs

- Runway pressure and scenario drift
- Receivables aging and collections priority
- Venture ranking spread and score gaps
- Founder focus tax and deep-work score
- Stale execution initiatives
- Existing briefing decision candidates

## Recommendation contract

Each recommendation includes:

- action
- rationale
- expectedUpside
- urgency
- confidence
- owner
- category
- dueDays

## Approval loop

1. Approve in Autopilot page.
2. Write initiative in execution table.
3. Write executive decision with linked initiative.
4. Open decision scoreback record with expected result and confidence.

## Weekly operating cadence

1. Monday: approve top 3 recommendations.
2. Wednesday: update execution status and blockers.
3. Friday: update scoreback statuses for outcomes with measurable movement.
