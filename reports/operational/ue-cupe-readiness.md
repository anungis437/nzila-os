# Union Eyes CUPE Pilot Readiness

## Executive Summary

Decision: GO WITH RESTRICTIONS.

Union Eyes is close to a credible first live deployment, but only if the pilot is deliberately narrow. The strongest parts of the product are the newer case APIs, workflow enforcement, auditability, and defensibility export. The main reason it is not a full GO is that the actual member-facing submission and attachment journey still points at legacy endpoints that do not match the hardened path.

## Scorecard

1. Security and tenant safety: Medium.
2. Workflow usefulness for stewards: Medium to high.
3. Member-facing intake trust: Low in current wiring.
4. Privacy and evidence handling: Medium with restrictions.
5. Admin and operational readiness: Medium.
6. Deployment readiness: Medium minus.
7. Buyer readiness: Medium if sold as a controlled pilot.

## What Is Ready

1. Hardened case intake route.
2. Staff-side assignment, notes, transitions, audit view, escalation, and evidence export.
3. Document governance and defensibility model.

## What Is Not Ready

1. Member self-serve case creation through the current UI.
2. Member evidence upload through the current UI.
3. Broad trust in legacy org-scoped auth helpers.
4. Broad rollout across multiple locals without further hardening.

## Recommended Pilot Shape

1. One CUPE local.
2. Invitation-only users.
3. Steward-assisted intake.
4. Daily review of operational issues.
5. Expansion only after the hardening items in the go-live decision are complete.
