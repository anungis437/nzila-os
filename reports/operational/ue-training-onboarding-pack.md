# Union Eyes Training And Onboarding Pack

## Pilot Audience

1. Pilot lead from the CUPE local.
2. Two to five stewards or officers handling intake and triage.
3. One Nzila operator responsible for cutover support.

## Training Objectives

1. Teach staff which workflow is authoritative for the pilot.
2. Prevent accidental use of legacy or unsupported paths.
3. Set clear expectations for members about what is and is not available in phase one.

## Day 0 Operator Brief

1. Approved pilot path: case intake, assignment, notes, transition, audit, export.
2. Not approved for pilot: legacy `/api/claims` member-create path, `/api/upload` for case evidence, open-ended admin exploration.
3. Incident escalation: if a member cannot submit or attach evidence, route the case through steward-assisted intake immediately.

## Day 1 Staff Training

1. Intake a case through the approved workflow.
2. Assign the case.
3. Add member-visible and internal notes correctly.
4. Transition the case using the allowed next actions.
5. Export evidence and explain what the seal means.

## Member-Facing Messaging

1. Phase-one pilot is supported by trained union staff.
2. Digital submission support may be steward-assisted during the initial period.
3. Sensitive documents should only be uploaded through approved channels communicated by the union.

## Readiness Checklist

1. Pilot staff trained on approved routes.
2. Support contacts published.
3. Member privacy statement reviewed.
4. One rehearsal case completed end to end.
5. One evidence export verified before go-live.
