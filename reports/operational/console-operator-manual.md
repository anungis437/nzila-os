# Console Operator Manual

## Daily Loop (15-20 minutes)

1. Open /today and /accountability.
2. Resolve all overdue and no-owner initiatives first.
3. Escalate any P0 decision without initiative movement.
4. Trigger collections on invoices >30 days overdue.

## Weekly Loop (45 minutes)

1. Open /briefing and approve top decisions.
2. Confirm each approved decision created a linked initiative.
3. Rebalance founder time allocation based on top venture scores.
4. Review /board for risks and board asks.

## Decision Discipline

- Every approved decision must have:
  - owner
  - due date
  - linked initiative
  - status progression (approved -> executing -> done)

## Finance Discipline

- Treat finance-spine outputs as canonical for operating decisions.
- Investigate any abrupt delta in:
  - cash
  - burn
  - receivables aging (31d+)
  - base runway

## Accountability Discipline

- Alert thresholds:
  - critical: >=3 overdue, >=3 stalled, or any open P0 decision
  - warning: any overdue or no-owner initiative
- Weekly target: velocity >= 2 completed initiatives per week.
