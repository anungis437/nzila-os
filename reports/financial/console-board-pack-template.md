# Console Board Pack Template

## 1. Executive Summary

- One sentence: current focus and most urgent constraint.

## 2. Financial Spine

- True cash
- MRR / ARR
- Burn and gross margin estimate
- Base / conservative / growth runway
- Receivables aging and top collections list

## 3. Portfolio Allocation

- Top ventures by score and action
- Cut-review ventures and rationale

## 4. Risks and Regressions

- Top 5 risks rising
- Decision or execution blockers

## 5. Execution Cadence

- Top initiatives for next 7 days
- Overdue and stalled counts
- Owner-load hotspots

## 6. Board Asks

- Capital approvals
- Hiring guardrails
- Escalations required

## 7. Appendix

- Key assumptions
- Data quality notes
- Material changes since prior board cycle
