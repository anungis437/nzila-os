# CUPE National Executive Verdict

## 1. Overall readiness conclusion

Union Eyes is **not ready to be represented as a CUPE National production capability**. It has a bounded, evidence-supported pilot workflow that can be demonstrated with synthetic data, but its current evidence does not substantiate the National organizational, confidentiality, bilingual, accessibility, integration, migration, scale, privacy, or operational assurances CUPE National would reasonably require.

## 2. Generic Union Eyes demo verdict: GREEN_WITH_CONDITIONS

**Why:** Selected intake, assignment, workflow, evidence/audit, and organization-isolation paths have code and targeted test evidence.

**Conditions:** Use fixed synthetic seed data, pilot personas and documented critical paths only; state that the session is a constrained pilot demonstration.

**Allowed claims:** “We can show a controlled pilot workflow and selected server-side organization-boundary tests.”

**Prohibited claims:** complete product authorization, full records management, bilingual accessibility compliance, National scale, or production readiness.

## 3. Tailored CUPE National executive demo verdict: AMBER

**Why:** The demonstration can truthfully explore CUPE’s questions and show a useful representative workflow, but key CUPE questions receive “needs discovery,” “partially implemented,” or “not proven” answers.

**Blockers:** National-local hierarchy/authority, aggregate-only oversight, sensitive/privileged matter controls, French parity, integration and migration proof.

**Allowed claims:** “CUPE-specific configuration and validation would be a joint design/pilot activity.”

**Prohibited claims:** “CUPE National can use this model now” or “National visibility has been safely configured.”

## 4. CUPE National production-readiness verdict: NOT_PROVEN

**Why:** Critical implementation and assurance evidence is absent or incomplete. A deployed app, schema, checklist, or route count does not close those gaps.

**Required remediation:** Complete CUPE-01 through CUPE-10 in the backlog with test and runtime proof, then complete CUPE governance, privacy/legal and operational sign-offs.

## 5. Five strongest capabilities

1. Server-side guards and explicit persona expectations on selected pilot-critical routes.
2. Seeded cross-organization negative-path test coverage for claims, evidence, search, metrics, audit and workflow mutation.
3. Pilot intake, assignment, transition, timeline/audit and evidence-export primitives.
4. Organization-linked operational structures: employers, worksites, bargaining units and committees.
5. Inspected AI summarization guard pattern: authenticated, rate-limited, feature/entitlement-gated, organization-checked and human-review flagged.

## 6. Five most material limitations

1. 1,707 discovered surfaces are outside the current registry, preventing an all-routes authorization assertion.
2. No evidence-backed CUPE National hierarchy, multi-context membership, aggregate-only oversight or cross-structure ownership model.
3. Sensitive/privileged matter, legal-hold, retention and records-management controls are not proven.
4. Full French parity and accessibility conformance are not proven.
5. Migration, integration, National-scale performance, privacy assurance and production continuity are not proven.

## 7. Five highest-risk questions

1. “Can National see aggregate issues without accessing local case files?”
2. “Can a privileged legal or harassment matter be hidden from otherwise senior users?”
3. “Can a file move or be jointly serviced across local, regional and National structures without ownership loss?”
4. “Can we operate equally in English and French and meet accessibility obligations?”
5. “Is CUPE National scale, privacy/residency, integration and recovery readiness proven?”

## 8. Immediate demo blockers

- Any request to use CUPE data or present a live National/local access model.
- Any claim of privileged-file controls, full bilingual readiness, integration, migration, scale, accessibility conformance, privacy compliance or production readiness.
- Any AI demonstration using confidential CUPE content or implying a recommendation/decision.

## 9. Recommended demo envelope

Run the 30-45 minute sequence in `cupe-national-demo-boundary-register.md`: context/boundary, synthetic intake and assignment, controlled transition/evidence/audit, a recorded or live seeded organization-denial proof, pilot operational view, then AI boundaries. Ask the President about authority boundaries, system-of-record expectations, confidential-data classes, French/accessibility needs, and pilot success measures.

## 10. Required next implementation wave

Prioritize National hierarchy/authority, aggregate-only reporting, sensitive-matter controls, universal authorization coverage, cross-structure service workflows, bilingual/accessibility validation, privacy/operational assurance, migration, scale, and AI data-governance controls. See `cupe-national-gap-remediation-backlog.md`.
