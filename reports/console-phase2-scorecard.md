# Console Phase 2 Scorecard

Date: 2026-04-17

## Outcome

Phase 2 shipped the three highest-leverage operator systems:

1. Founder Focus Engine
2. True Runway Engine
3. Weekly CEO Decision Briefing

It also upgraded:

- Today page
- Portfolio ranking
- executive navigation

## Before / After Scores

### Console as Business OS

- Before: 7.6 / 10
- After: 8.8 / 10

Why it improved:

- founder time is now visible
- runway is now modeled from working capital, burn, and scenario assumptions
- weekly decisions are surfaced explicitly, not inferred by the founder mentally

### Console as CEO Cockpit

- Before: 7.4 / 10
- After: 9.0 / 10

Why it improved:

- Today now opens with a weekly directive
- Briefing page compresses improvements, deterioration, cash, risk, and decisions into one read

### Console as Capital Allocator

- Before: 7.1 / 10
- After: 8.7 / 10

Why it improved:

- capital priority is now dynamic
- runway scenarios show what can actually be funded
- spend suggestions now pair with venture rank and runway pressure

### Console as Founder Force Multiplier

- Before: 4.8 / 10
- After: 8.4 / 10

Why it improved:

- founder time allocation is now measurable
- admin drag and context-switch tax are visible
- weekly reallocation recommendations are explicit

### Console as Weekly Decision Machine

- Before: 6.2 / 10
- After: 8.9 / 10

Why it improved:

- decisions are listed directly
- risks are consolidated
- time and spend allocations are suggested automatically

## Highest ROI Wins

1. Shared executive-intelligence layer: one scoring and recommendation engine powers Focus, Runway, Briefing, Today, and Portfolio.
2. Founder time logging: converts intuition about “where the week went” into measurable allocation and leakage.
3. Working-capital runway: the page now models cash reality instead of only infrastructure burn.
4. Portfolio ranking v2: founder attention and capital allocation are no longer driven only by static catalog order.
5. Today page extension: the main landing page now says what to focus on this week in one sentence.

## Real New Capabilities

- founder hours by venture and category
- admin drag %
- context-switch tax %
- deep-work score
- weekly focus targets
- revenue per founder hour
- pipeline per sales hour
- treasury snapshot inputs
- runway scenario modeling
- receivables aging
- hiring affordability
- safe spend threshold
- weekly CEO briefing with decisions, risks, and suggested allocations
- dynamic capital priority ranking

## Remaining Gaps For Phase 3

1. Venture-level attribution for quotes and invoices is still incomplete outside explicit product-linked records.
2. Treasury remains mixed-mode because cash is manual-input rather than bank-synced.
3. Founder logging exists, but automated calendar/time capture does not.
4. Weekly briefing exists as a page, not yet as a scheduled digest or board artifact.
5. Execution initiatives are still static and should move to DB to close the loop between priorities and work.

## Verdict

Console is now materially closer to “mandatory Monday use.”

The biggest remaining distance to elite operator-grade status is no longer layout. It is integration depth and automated delivery.
