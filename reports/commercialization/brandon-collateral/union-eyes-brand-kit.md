# Union Eyes Brand Kit (Canva-Ready)

Last updated: 2026-06-11

## 1. Brand Core

Brand name: Union Eyes
Tagline (recommended): Organizational governance and continuity infrastructure for unions.
Positioning: Institutional, trusted, clear, accountable, Canadian-hosted, bilingual-first.

- Deep text navy: #0F2133

## 3. Color Usage Rules

1. Primary CTA/button: #1F5B84 with white text (#FFFFFF).
2. Primary heading text on light backgrounds: #12324A.
3. Premium highlight or trust signal: #B59B61 (use sparingly).
4. Secondary backgrounds/sections: #F4F7F9 or #FAF9F6.
5. Keep coral/emerald/violet for status and emphasis, not primary brand blocks.

## 4. Recommended Combinations

1. Hero section: #12324A background + #FFFFFF text + #B59B61 accent.
2. CTA block: #1F5B84 background + #FFFFFF text.
3. Light card: #FFFFFF background + #12324A heading + #1F5B84 links.
4. Institutional gradient: #0F2133 -> #123451 (for whitepapers/hero banners).

## 5. Typography

Primary typeface: Poppins
Weights in use: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
Fallback stack: system-ui, sans-serif

### Typographic hierarchy (Canva)

- H1: Poppins 700, 44-56 px, line-height 1.1-1.2
- H2: Poppins 700, 30-38 px, line-height 1.2-1.25
- H3: Poppins 600, 22-28 px, line-height 1.25-1.3
- Body: Poppins 400, 15-18 px, line-height 1.5-1.7
- Label/eyebrow: Poppins 600, 11-13 px, uppercase, tracking +8% to +18%

## 7. Accessibility Guardrails

1. Use white text on #12324A and #1F5B84.
2. Avoid gold text (#B59B61) on white for body copy; reserve for accents and short labels.
3. Keep body text at 15 px minimum in Canva materials.
4. Prefer high-contrast combinations for board and executive documents.
