# Union Eyes — Executive Brief (Customer Zero Pilot)

Audience: Local President, Executive Committee, Decision-makers
Length target: 2 pages
Date: 2026-06-02

---

## 1. The Problem We Need To Solve

Today, steward and leadership workflows are frequently slowed by:
1. Fragmented case and document tracking.
2. Inconsistent follow-up and visibility across active issues.
3. Reliance on individual memory instead of durable organizational records.
4. Limited operational signal for timely executive decision-making.

This creates continuity risk, slower response, and avoidable coordination overhead.

---

## 2. What Union Eyes Solves

Union Eyes provides a practical operating layer for steward and leadership workflows by:
1. Centralizing case progression and document workflow context.
2. Improving visibility into grievance lifecycle and follow-up status.
3. Capturing operational memory (events, blockers, lessons) as a reusable record.
4. Providing structured pilot metrics for adoption, readiness, and success decisions.

The immediate objective is not broad transformation. It is measurable improvement in a bounded pilot window.

---

## 3. Pilot Structure (90 Days)

Pilot format:
1. Fixed 90-day pilot.
2. Scope lock at kickoff.
3. Weekly operating cadence.
4. Mid-pilot correction checkpoint.
5. End-of-pilot decision package.

Commercial structure:
1. Fixed pilot engagement.
2. 100% pilot fee credit-forward on annual conversion.

---

## 4. Expected Outcomes

By pilot close, leadership should have:
1. Evidence of real steward workflow adoption.
2. Clear signal on case and follow-up workflow improvement.
3. Objective opportunity and readiness scoring for decision confidence.
4. A structured recommendation: proceed, constrained extension, or close.

Primary decision question:
Can this improve continuity and workflow execution enough to justify annual deployment?
