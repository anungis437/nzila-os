### 2. Which surfaces remain semantic shells?
None at hard-fail level. Several secondary surfaces (`workbench`, `inbox`, `priorities`) carry warning-only drift — additive Wave 7+ candidates, not Wave 6 blockers.

### 3. Which surfaces remain UI-heavy but substrate-light?
- `dashboard/movement-insights` — partially hydrated; carries strong projections but lighter chronology refs than `chronology` itself. Acceptable Partial Hydration tier.
- `dashboard/sector-overview` — Projection Shell tier; intentional federation-safe aggregation.
- `dashboard/federation-trends` — Projection Shell tier; coexistence-safe by design (no per-org drill-down).

### 4. Which surfaces lack chronology integration?
- Onboarding/admin flows: chronology refs limited to deployment summaries; institutional-memory-safe but could deepen. Addressed by Wave 6 narrative governance (no new copy strings required — fence prevents regression while Wave 7 deepens).

### 5. Which surfaces lack continuity integration?
- `inbox` & `priorities` carry continuity-aware framing but no breakpoint visibility. Wave 6 fences against `continuity orchestration` / `continuity analytics` drift so future deepening cannot collapse into operational-management posture.

### 6. Which surfaces lack provenance linkage?
- Exports/evidence: provenance is preserved in serialization but render-side provenance ribbons are inconsistent across export targets. Wave 6 fences `provenance analytics` to prevent drift; Wave 7 candidate for ribbon parity.

### 7. Which surfaces lack explainability overlays?
- `dashboard/representation`: explainability is implicit (lineage refs) but not surfaced as an explicit "why is this visible?" overlay. Acceptable for current wave; Wave 6 prevents `predictive governance` / `governance scoring` from filling the gap.

### 8. Which surfaces lack topology awareness?
- `committees` surface carries affiliation visibility but lighter delegation-pathway visualization than `topology`. Wave 6 fences `topology analytics` / `topology orchestration` / `topology telemetry`.

### 9. Which surfaces remain disconnected from continuity cognition?
- `claims` carries cognition refs in the timeline but not in the inbox-style intake. Acceptable Partial Hydration; Wave 6 fences `cognition analytics` / `cognition telemetry`.

### 10. Which surfaces create runtime credibility asymmetry?
Asymmetry now lives between **Fully Institutional** (governance-center, chronology, topology, continuity-intelligence) and **Partial Hydration / Projection Shell** (movement-insights, sector-overview, federation-trends). Asymmetry is **doctrinally legitimate** (federation-safe aggregation surfaces must remain projection-thin to preserve coexistence boundaries). Wave 6's job is to **codify** this asymmetry as intentional and prevent drift back toward analytics/telemetry posture.
