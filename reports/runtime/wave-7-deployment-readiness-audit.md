### 9. Which operational surfaces lack institutional stewardship posture?
Primary admin: stewardship-grade. Secondary (`inbox`, `workbench`, `priorities`): partial — Wave 8+ deepening candidates, fenced by Wave 7 against automation/optimization drift.

### 10. Which rollout surfaces create procurement credibility asymmetry?
