## 5. Departure From W12 Strict-Parity Rule

W12 established "FR/IT/PT bundles untouched unless absolutely necessary." W17 deliberately departs from this rule because:

1. The collision was **brand-level** (homepage meta titles + json-LD descriptions visible to search engines and clients).
2. IT/PT held **untranslated English titles** — updating them is parity-preserving, not parity-breaking.
3. FR / FR-CA changes are **doctrinally translated** (`infrastructure de gouvernance` → `infrastructure constitutionnelle de continuité`), preserving translation quality.

This is documented as an intentional, doctrinal exception, not a regression of the W12 multilingual discipline.

## 6. Constitutional Category Outcome

Union Eyes is no longer positioned as a *product within the institutional governance software category*. It now asserts itself as **constitutional category infrastructure** — a continuity substrate that the institution itself constitutes. The "governance" framing is preserved as an attribute (`governance-preserved`, `governance-safe`) and rejected as a category noun (`governance platform`, `governance system`, `governance product`).
