## Boundaries still requiring explicit qualification

- Corporate helpers expose a current rate snapshot. They have no taxation-period input and do not apply transition proration, Quebec employee-hours/sector eligibility, or every provincial business-limit/passive-income difference. New rates must not be described as applying to every whole 2026 fiscal year.
- Personal quick estimates use maximum BPAs without federal/Yukon/Manitoba income phaseouts and omit Ontario surtax/health premium and Quebec federal abatement. Deprecated 2025 names remain aliases to 2026 data, not historical schedules.
- Payroll helpers do not establish CRA quarterly eligibility. Date generators return nominal dates without weekend/holiday adjustments. Quebec EI/QPIP and reduced employer premiums are outside the generic constants.
- Interest verification covers January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2026 only. Legacy fallback outputs outside that period are explicitly unverified.
- Standard sales-tax rates do not determine zero-rated/exempt treatment, place of supply, registration, or quick-method eligibility. QST recovery remains separate from CRA input credits.
