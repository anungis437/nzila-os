# @nzila/civitas

Phase 2 legal-authority substrate for CIVITAS, on the Phase 0–1 foundation. CIVITAS is the specialist for lawful autonomy, jurisdiction, and continuity.

CIVITAS advises and analyzes. It does not deploy, merge, transact, file, or sign, and it is not counsel of record. Personal data is not stored. A legal source cannot grant execution. Later phases in `docs/doctrine/civitas/` are targets.

Evidence references come from `@nzila/platform-decision-engine`. The legal domain label comes from `@nzila/decision-core`. `DecisionAuthority` in that package is permission scope, not a legal source. Entity names used as future targets come from `@nzila/platform-ontology`. This package does not replace those primitives.
