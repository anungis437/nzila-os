# Console — Demo Flow

## Goal
Show operators and stakeholders how the console provides one place to monitor and steer platform operations.

## Demo Steps
1. Open `/console` and walk through launcher destinations.
2. Call `/api/health` and confirm service status.
3. Call `/api/metrics` and highlight request, error, and latency signals.
4. Open assurance and audit routes to show governance context.
5. Trigger evidence export and explain tamper-proof evidence pipeline.

## Narration Prompts
- "This console centralizes operational decisions."
- "We can verify service health and policy posture in real time."
- "Evidence packs make audit trails reproducible and defensible."

## Expected Outcomes
- Faster operator onboarding
- Clear governance visibility
- Reliable incident triage entry point
