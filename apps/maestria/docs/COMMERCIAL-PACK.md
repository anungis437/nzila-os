## Pilot-to-Production Conversion Path

The Maestria pilot is designed to reduce time-to-value to under 48 hours and conversion friction to near zero.

### Phase 1 — Pilot Activation (Days 1–3)

1. **Connector setup**: Connect your Shopify store via OAuth. Estimated time: 15 minutes.
2. **KPI baseline**: The warehouse ingests your last 90 days of order history automatically.
3. **Initial dashboard review**: Your onboarding contact schedules a 30-minute walkthrough (Growth/Enterprise) or shares a self-serve checklist (Starter).
4. **Flow Engine quick-wins**: Two pre-built automations are enabled: low-margin SKU alert and daily GMV digest.

### Phase 2 — Value Validation (Days 4–30)

| Milestone | Success Criterion |
|---|---|
| KPI accuracy check | GMV figures reconcile within 0.5% of your Shopify admin totals |
| First automation trigger | At least one Flow Engine rule fires and is acknowledged |
| Ops time baseline | Staff log actual hours on reconciliation tasks vs. prior week |
| Stakeholder review | Internal sign-off that Maestria data is trustworthy |

Clients who complete all four milestones by Day 20 convert to paid at a **95% rate** in cohort data.

### Phase 3 — Conversion Decision (Days 28–30)

A conversion call is scheduled on Day 28. The agenda:

1. Review KPI warehouse accuracy and automation coverage.
2. Confirm the correct tier based on GMV run rate.
3. Discuss any add-ons required (additional connectors, seats, retention).
4. Sign commercial order form — DocuSign, 5 minutes.
5. Payment method on file — invoiced monthly or annually (10% discount for annual prepay).

**No conversion call needed** for self-serve Starter clients — a single in-app prompt on Day 28 with a one-click upgrade flow.

### Phase 4 — Production Onboarding (Day 31+)

| Step | Owner | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Production credentials issued | Maestria ops | Dedicated API keys, production webhook endpoints |
| Data retention window set | Client | KPI history retained per tier |
| Remaining connectors enabled | Client + Maestria | All licensed connectors live |
| Additional Flow automations | Client | Up to tier limit configured |
| SLA monitoring activated | Maestria ops | Synthetic monitors and alerting enabled |

### Conversion Exit Criteria

If a pilot client decides **not to convert**, the following applies:

- KPI warehouse data is exported to CSV and delivered within 5 business days.
- All OAuth tokens and API credentials are revoked within 24 hours of cancellation.
- No invoice is raised.
- A 90-day re-activation window is available at 50% off first month.
