# Zonga — Africa-First Commercial Model

## Why Africa First

Africa's music industry is projected to grow to $1.2B by 2028. Today's dominant
global platforms charge 30–50% commissions and offer limited mobile money support.
Zonga is purpose-built for African creators and markets.

---

### 3. Genre & Cultural Support

- **40+ African genre classifications**: Afrobeats, Amapiano, Bongo Flava,
  Highlife, Kizomba, Coupe-Decale, Coupé-Décalé, Mapouka, Ndombolo, and more
- **16+ African currencies** supported natively
- **Regional discovery**: Charts and trending per-country/per-city
- **Offline-first**: USSD-formatted content for low-bandwidth markets

### 4. Rights Protection

African creators often lack formal rights management infrastructure. Zonga provides:

- **Split agreements** with versioning and digital signatures
- **Deterministic royalty computation** — verifiable by creators
- **Hash-sealed proofs** — tamper-evident payout records
- **Dispute resolution** — built-in workflow with evidence tracking
- **Audio fingerprinting** — content identification for rights enforcement

### Scaling Dynamics

- Each new creator brings ~50 listeners (viral sharing loops)
- Each new market adds mobile money volume (processing margin)
- Enterprise deals provide predictable recurring revenue
- Event ticketing has highest per-transaction margin

## Competitive Positioning

Zonga is not competing on catalogue size against Spotify or Apple Music.
Zonga competes on:

1. **Economic fairness** — 85% creator take vs. 50-70%
2. **Payment accessibility** — mobile money in 13+ providers
3. **Cultural relevance** — African genre taxonomy and regional discovery
4. **Trust** — deterministic, auditable financial flows
5. **Low-barrier entry** — $0 Starter plan, $1 minimum payout
