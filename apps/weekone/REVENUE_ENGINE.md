# WeekOne — Revenue Engine (CRO Playbook v1)

> **Mode:** Convert launch into revenue.
> **Wedge:** Agencies → first 50 customers → expand.
> **Founder GTM, Calm Monday brand, WOS category — preserved throughout.**
> **The premise:** A repeatable engine that turns one founder's hours into recurring revenue, then turns customers into the next CAC channel.

---

## 1. Founder Personal Brand Strategy — *"The Calm Monday Operator"*

Early-stage GTM buys trust from humans, not brands. The founder is the first salesperson, the first writer, the first product manager, and the first community.

### Persona — *The Calm Monday Operator*

**One-line bio (LinkedIn / X / podcast intros):**
> *Building WeekOne — the Weekly Operating System for owners and small teams. Writing about how to run your week like a real company.*

**Identity rules:**
- Voice: calm, sharp, generous. *Operator, not influencer.*
- Show the work, not the wins. (Build-in-public > announcement marketing.)
- Always speak in operator language: *priorities, retainers, runway, drift, rhythm, Monday.*
- Never punch down at incumbents (Notion, Asana, Monday). Always reframe: *"Different category. They track tasks. We track the business."*
- Never humble-brag MRR. Talk about **customer Mondays completed** instead.

### Weekly cadence (sustainable for 90+ days)

| Day | Channel | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | LinkedIn | "Calm Monday" post — emotional/identity | 30 min |
| Mon | X | Thread version of the same | 15 min |
| Tue | LinkedIn | Operator playbook (utility) | 30 min |
| Wed | LinkedIn | Anti-positioning / sharp take | 20 min |
| Thu | LinkedIn | Customer story / metric | 20 min |
| Fri | LinkedIn + X | Build-in-public — what shipped, what got killed | 30 min |
| Bi-weekly | Newsletter | "The Calm Monday" essay (700–1,200 words) | 2 hr |
| Bi-weekly | YouTube/short | "Monday with WeekOne" — 60-sec real review | 1 hr |

**Total founder media time: ~5 hours/week. Non-negotiable. Block it on the calendar.**

### Audience growth targets (founder handle, not brand)

- Day 30: +200 followers (LinkedIn), +100 (X)
- Day 60: +600 cumulative LinkedIn, first 100 newsletter subs
- Day 90: 1,500+ LinkedIn, 500+ newsletter subs, 1 podcast guest spot/month

### Distribution flywheel
1. Founder posts → drives website traffic and DMs.
2. DMs → demos.
3. Demos → customers.
4. Customers → quotes / case studies / referrals.
5. Quotes → next founder post.
**Loop closes in ~14 days. Compounding starts at day 60.**

### Off-limits content
- No screenshots of MRR charts. (Reads as desperate or boastful — both lose trust.)
- No "look how fast we shipped" posts without naming the customer pain it solves.
- No competitor screenshot dunks.
- No AI-buzzword posts. The brand is calm, not loud.

## 2. 14-Day Case Study Capture System

Industrialize proof. Every customer becomes a case study by day 14 — or we know why not.

### The standard artifact (one per customer, 1 page)

**Top of page:**
- Logo + agency name
- One-line context: *"{Agency}, {N} people, based in {city}."*
- The hero metric: *"Cut leadership alignment from 90 minutes to 15."*

**Middle:**
- **Before:** 2 sentences, in the owner's words.
- **After:** 2 sentences, in the owner's words.
- **The number:** one specific outcome (hours saved / dropped balls eliminated / retainer protected).

**Bottom:**
- A 12-second video clip of the owner saying the punchline.
- A screenshot of their actual Monday review (with permission, sensitive data masked).
- CTA: *"Want a calm Monday too? Start your first week."*

### The 14-day capture timeline

| Day | Trigger | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Sign up | Welcome email + book Setup call within 48h | Founder |
| **1** | Setup call | Capture **Before** state on a 5-minute structured intake | Founder |
| **3** | First Monday | Send "How did your Monday feel?" 1-question email | Automation |
| **7** | Day-7 health check | Verify Monday #1 happened. If not → see §4 (Churn Rescue) | Automation + Founder |
| **10** | Second Monday | DM the owner: *"Quick favor — got 2 minutes for a voice note?"* | Founder |
| **12** | Voice note received | Transcribe. Pull punchline quote. Draft case study page. | Founder |
| **13** | Approval | Send draft to customer. Ask: *"Use it publicly? Anything to redact?"* | Founder |
| **14** | Publish | Ship to weekone.app/customers. Post on LinkedIn. Tag customer (with permission). | Founder |
| **14** | Referral ask | At end of approval email: *"Know one other agency owner who'd benefit?"* | Founder |

### Five intake questions (Day 1 — Before state)

1. *"On a scale of 1–10, how chaotic does your average Monday morning feel?"*
2. *"What's the last thing your agency dropped — invoice, lead, hire, renewal?"*
3. *"How long is your weekly leadership meeting today, and how useful does it feel?"*
4. *"What would a calm Monday look like, in your words?"*
5. *"What outcome would make WeekOne worth $99/month, beyond a doubt?"*

(These are also the **first product conversation**. They install the rhythm in the customer's head before the product does.)

### Three voice-note questions (Day 10 — After state)

1. *"What's different about your Monday now?"*
2. *"What's the most concrete thing WeekOne stopped you from dropping?"*
3. *"If a friend asked, how would you describe WeekOne in one sentence?"*

### Targets
- 80% of customers reach Case Study v1 by day 14.
- 50% give a usable public quote.
- 30% give video.
- 20% become active referrers within 30 days.

## 3. Churn Rescue Automation (when *Mondays-completed* declines)

The leading indicator is **Mondays-completed/customer/month**. When it slips, churn is ~60 days away. Catch it at the slip, not at the cancel.

### The signal ladder

| Signal | Definition | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Yellow** | Customer skipped Monday review for 1 week | Auto-nudge |
| **Orange** | Skipped 2 weeks in a row, OR fewer than 2 Mondays in last 30 days | Onboarding review |
| **Red** | Skipped 3+ weeks, OR Monday-completion rate < 25% | Founder call |
| **Pre-cancel** | Logged into billing settings | Save offer |

### The rescue sequence

**Yellow — Day 1 of slip (automated email)**
> Subject: *Missed your Monday?*
> Hey {{firstName}} — noticed your Monday review didn't run last week. No judgment — life happens. If you want a 15-minute reset, here's a 90-second video on how to bring the rhythm back in one sitting. [link]

**Orange — Day 8 of slip (in-product banner + email)**
> Subject: *Two Mondays. Let's reset.*
> The product works when the rhythm runs. Two questions: (a) is this still the right time for WeekOne, and (b) want me to jump on a 15-minute call to install it back into your week? — {{founderFirstName}}

**Red — Day 15 of slip (founder personal email + Loom)**
> Subject: *Quick honest check-in.*
> Hey {{firstName}} — I'd rather you cancel than pay for something you don't use, so I want to ask you directly: is WeekOne still solving a real problem for your agency? If yes — let me re-onboard you personally, no charge for the next month while we get the rhythm back. If no — let me make the cancel painless. Either way, you decide. — {{founderFirstName}} *(also a 90-sec Loom)*

**Pre-cancel — billing page interception**
> Before you cancel, two options:
> 1. *Pause for 60 days* — billing stops, your data waits.
> 2. *15-minute call with the founder* — free re-onboarding, plus a free month if we don't fix it.
> Or: *Cancel anyway* — one click, exports included.

### Save-offer rules
- Pause is always offered. Pause customers reactivate at 35% in our model assumption.
- Free month + re-onboarding is offered once per customer per year.
- Discount is **never** offered as a save tool. (It teaches the wrong behavior.)

### Targets
- Yellow → Green recovery: **70%**
- Orange → Green recovery: **45%**
- Red → Green recovery: **25%**
- Net effect: **~30% reduction** in monthly logo churn vs. no rescue.

## 4. EOS / Traction Objection Handling

This is the most credible objection in the agency segment. The answer below is shipped as: (a) one homepage FAQ entry, (b) one founder essay, (c) one demo-ready talk track.

### The objection
> *"We already run EOS / Traction. Why do we need WeekOne?"*

### The talk track (verbatim, 45 seconds)
> EOS is a great operating philosophy — Rocks, L10s, scorecards, accountability charts. We respect it. WeekOne is the **digital, lightweight execution layer** that makes EOS run without a $2,000-a-month implementer or a Google Sheet that goes stale by week three.
>
> If you run EOS, WeekOne replaces the spreadsheet, the L10 prep doc, and the scorecard you forget to update. Same philosophy. One calm screen. Fifteen minutes on Monday instead of ninety on Tuesday.
>
> If you've drifted off EOS — which most agencies do by year two — WeekOne is the system that keeps the rhythm without needing the framework police.

### Three positioning lines (use in posts, ads, and demos)

1. *"EOS is a philosophy. WeekOne is the execution layer."*
2. *"We operationalize weekly execution digitally — without the consultant overhead."*
3. *"If your EOS scorecard is in a Google Sheet nobody updates, you don't have EOS. You have a folder."*

### The crossover offer (for agencies actively running EOS)
- **Free Setup Sprint upgrade** ($2,500 → $0) for the first 5 agencies who migrate their EOS scorecards into WeekOne and let us publish the case study.
- Outcome we want to publish: *"How {{Agency}} kept EOS — and dropped the implementer."*

## 5. Agency Referral Flywheel

Agencies refer agencies. Build a flywheel that converts one happy customer into 1.5 new customers within 90 days.

### Three referral surfaces (not just one)

1. **In-product, after first calm Monday**
   > *"That was your first calm Monday. Know another agency owner whose Monday still looks like twelve tabs?"*
   > [ Send WeekOne ]

2. **Email, after day-30 retention milestone**
   > Subject: *You've run 4 Mondays. Want to give one away?*
   > Body: One free month for you, free Setup Sprint hour for them.

3. **Public, on the case study page**
   > *Bring an agency friend → both get a free month and an onboarding call.*

### Mechanic
- Referrer: 1 free month per successful referral, stacks indefinitely.
- Referee: 30 days free + 1 free Setup Sprint hour ($250 value).
- **Bonus**: 5 successful referrals → free Guided Operator month ($1,500 value).
- **Public leaderboard** (opt-in) of "Top Calm Monday Operators" — gamifies and signals to the market.

### Channel partners (parallel to direct referrals)
- **Fractional COOs** for agencies — install WeekOne as their default tool. Revenue share: 20% recurring for 12 months.
- **Agency consultancies** (small, niche) — same revenue share, plus co-published case studies.
- **EOS implementers** open to a digital layer — different rev-share, position WeekOne as their "client homework that actually gets done."

### Targets
- Day 60: 15% of new logos are referral-sourced.
- Day 90: 25% of new logos are referral-sourced.
- Day 180: 40% — referral becomes the largest channel.

## 6. Customer Success Onboarding Scripts

The first three Mondays decide retention. Treat them like a clinical protocol.

### Pre-onboard (within 24h of signup)

**Email subject:** *Your first calm Monday is in 7 days.*
**Body:**
> Two things: (1) book your Setup call here [link, 30 min, founder-led for the first 50 customers]. (2) before the call, answer 5 quick questions [link to intake] — they double as the first product conversation.

### Setup call (30 min, founder-led)

**Minute 0–5 — Reframe**
- *"We're not going to tour the app. We're going to install your first Monday."*

**Minute 5–15 — Pillar walkthrough on their data**
- Focus: type their 3 real priorities for this week.
- Money: pull in retainers, AR, runway with whatever data is available (even if it's a screenshot).
- Risks: name 1 risk drift in their current portfolio.
- Growth: name 1 compounding move.

**Minute 15–25 — The Monday rehearsal**
- *"Now run a real Monday. I'll watch silently. 10 minutes."*
- The first Monday happens **on the call**. Not later. Not "when they have time." On. The. Call.

**Minute 25–30 — The cadence commitment**
- Set the Monday recurring slot in their calendar — together.
- *"Same time, every Monday, for 4 weeks. After that, it runs itself."*
- Send the 5-question intake outcome back to them as their personalized scorecard.

### Day 7 — automated check-in
> Subject: *How did Monday #1 feel?*
> One reply: a number 1–10. That's it. We track it.

### Day 14 — voice note ask
> *"Quick favor — got 2 minutes for a voice note?"* (See §2.)

### Day 30 — milestone email
> Subject: *You've run 4 Mondays. That's the rhythm.*
> Body: Stats card (Mondays completed, hours saved, risks logged, priorities shipped). Referral ask. Annual upgrade offer ("set it and forget it for a year").

### Onboarding success metric
- 60%+ of new customers complete **3 of 4 Mondays** in their first 30 days.
- Anyone below that = trigger Orange rescue (§3).

## 7. Expansion Plan: Agencies → Clinics → Consultancies

Sequence vertical entry. Don't open Vertical 2 before agencies hit 30 paying logos and one repeatable case study.

### Phase 1 — Days 0–90: **Agencies** (current)
- Goal: 30 paying customers, 5 published case studies, 1 referral channel established.
- Exit gate: CAC < $300, week-4 retention > 80%, NPS > 45.

### Phase 2 — Days 91–180: **Clinics** (Vertical 2)
- Why second: similar small-team ops pain, longer retention, higher willingness to pay, but slower CAC. Agencies funded the engine; clinics deepen LTV.
- ICP: independent clinics (medical, dental, physio, mental health), 5–25 staff, owner-operator clinician.
- Channel: associations (state/provincial), referral from agency owners who are also clinic patients/board members.
- Pain reframe: *"Run the practice like a real company — not a clipboard and a memory."*
- Vertical landing page: weekone.app/clinics. Same WOS frame, clinic-flavored pillars (Focus / Money / Risk / Growth → Patients / Cash / Compliance / Capacity).
- Goal: 20 paying clinics by day 180.

### Phase 3 — Days 181–270: **Consultancies** (Vertical 3)
- Why third: highest LTV, longest sales cycle, hardest CAC. Needs the credibility that vertical 1 + 2 give us.
- ICP: 5–20 person boutique consultancies (strategy, ops, M&A, niche).
- Channel: referrals from agencies (consultants buy from agencies they trust).
- Pain reframe: *"You sell rigor. Run rigor."*
- Vertical landing page: weekone.app/consultancies.
- Goal: 15 paying consultancies by day 270.

### Phase 4 — Day 271+: **Trades-with-office-staff** & nonprofits
- Open only when the first three are >100 logos combined and self-sustaining on retention + referrals.

### What unlocks each phase
- **Phase 2 trigger:** agencies hit 30 logos AND CAC < $300 AND retention > 80% week-4.
- **Phase 3 trigger:** clinics hit 10 logos AND verified retention is **higher** than agencies (validates the LTV thesis).
- **Phase 4 trigger:** combined ARR > $500K and >40% of new logos coming from referrals.

### Discipline rule
> No vertical opens until the previous one has a published, referenceable case study and a repeatable acquisition channel that the founder can hand to a hire.

## 9. Weekly Founder Operating Cadence

The founder runs WeekOne on WeekOne. (Eat the dog food. Publicly.)

### Monday — *Calm Monday* (90 minutes total)
- 7:00–7:15 — **Run own Monday review** in WeekOne. (Live-stream or screen-record monthly.)
- 7:15–7:30 — Post Monday content (LinkedIn + X).
- 7:30–8:30 — Triage outbound replies from weekend.
- 8:30–9:00 — Book the week's demos.

### Tuesday — *Sales day*
- 3 demos scheduled (max 5).
- Run script (Launch Playbook §4).
- Same-day follow-up email + plan recommendation.

### Wednesday — *Customer day*
- 5 customer touchpoints: onboarding calls, day-14 voice notes, save calls.
- Update churn rescue dashboard.

### Thursday — *Build day*
- One product improvement shipped (small).
- Demo objections from the week → backlog.
- Friday's build-in-public post drafted.

### Friday — *Compound day*
- Publish: build-in-public post + bi-weekly newsletter (alternating).
- Capture case study artifacts due this week.
- Run weekly metrics review (§10).
- Plan next week's content + outbound list.

### Saturday — *Off* (and visibly off — sets brand tone of calm)

### Sunday — *Operator's hour* (60 min, optional)
- Read inbound DMs.
- Sketch next week's three priorities.
- One personal note to a key customer or referrer.

### Non-negotiables
- 5 hours/week founder media time.
- 3 demos/week minimum.
- 1 published artifact (case study or essay) every 14 days.
- Saturday is off. Visibly. The brand is calm because the founder is.

## 11. What this engine quietly does (the strategic point)

Most "launch playbooks" are a list of activities. This one is a **closed loop**:

> Founder content → demos → customers → case studies → referrals → cheaper content → cheaper demos → more customers.

Each loop tightens the next. By day 90 the founder is doing **less outbound** and **more publishing**, because the customers are now the channel.

The dashboard tracks whether the loop is closing. The Calm Monday metric tracks whether it deserves to.

If both are true, WeekOne stops being a project and starts being a company.
