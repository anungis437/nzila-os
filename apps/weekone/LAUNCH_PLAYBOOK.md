# WeekOne — Launch Playbook (GTM v1)

> **Mode:** Launch.
> **Beachhead:** **Independent agencies** (4–15 people, services/creative/marketing/dev).
> **Wedge product:** *WeekOne for Agencies — the Weekly Operating System for client-services teams.*
> **Preserve:** Too big for spreadsheets · Weekly Operating System · Calm Monday · Anti-chaos.

---

## 0. Why this is structured this way

Broad positioning **publicly**. Narrow positioning **at point of sale**.
The homepage still says "Weekly Operating System for small business." But every paid channel, every outbound, every demo, and every onboarding flow in the first 90 days is built for **one vertical**: independent agencies.

That gives us: cheap CAC, fast referrals, repeatable demos, identifiable buyer, recurring pain, urgent budget cycle, and case studies that compound.

## 1. Beachhead Selection — Why Agencies (not Clinics, Trades, or Nonprofits)

Scored across four GTM dimensions:

| Dimension | Agencies | Clinics | Trades | Nonprofits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CAC speed** (channels available, buyer reachable) | **9** — LinkedIn-native, founder-led, public on X | 6 — gated by associations, slower trust | 7 — local, word-of-mouth heavy | 5 — long sales cycles, board approvals |
| **Pain intensity** (acute, recurring, expensive) | **9** — every Monday, every client, every utilization gap | 7 — ops pain real but masked by EMR | 7 — cash & scheduling pain real | 8 — board reporting pain real |
| **Willingness to pay** | **8** — already buying tools, knows the value of an hour | 7 — buys software but slower | 6 — price-sensitive | 5 — grant-cycle dependent |
| **Referral coefficient** (do peers talk?) | **10** — agency owners are loud, networked, public | 5 — peers compete | 7 — trade associations help | 7 — sector networks help |
| **Total** | **36** ⭐ | 25 | 27 | 25 |

**Decision: Agencies first. 90 days. Then expand into clinics (vertical 2) and consultancies (vertical 3).**

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — v1

- 4–15 employees
- Services revenue, retainer or project-based
- Owner-operator still in the work
- Already uses some combination of: Notion, Asana/ClickUp/Monday, Google Sheets, Slack
- Has missed at least one of: a deadline, an invoice, a renewal, a hire — in the last 90 days
- Owner can name a "Sunday night feeling"

## 4. Demo Script (15 minutes, built for agency owners)

> The demo is the close. Don't tour features. Run a real Monday on the prospect's data.

### Minute 0–2 — Reframe (no screen)
- *"Before I open anything: tell me what last Monday morning felt like."*
- Listen. Mirror back two of their exact words. (Usually: *scattered, behind, anxious, reactive.*)
- *"WeekOne is the rhythm that ends that. Let me show you what your next Monday could look like."*

### Minute 2–5 — Focus
- Open Focus pillar. Type their 3 real priorities for this week, live.
- *"That's it. The whole agency, in three lines. Everyone reads this in two minutes."*

### Minute 5–8 — Money
- Open Money. Show retainer health, AR aging, runway.
- *"This is the screen that costs you nothing — and saves you the renewal you're about to lose."*
- Anchor to a specific recent loss they mentioned.

### Minute 8–11 — Risks
- Open Risks. Add a real risk from their current week. *"Utilization slipped on the X account."*
- *"This is the line item that, two weeks from now, becomes a fire. It lives here so it doesn't."*

### Minute 11–13 — Growth
- Open Growth. Help them name **one** compounding move (pricing change, niche, productized service).
- *"One move. Tracked weekly. That's how plateaus break."*

### Minute 13–14 — The math
- "If WeekOne saves you 4 hours a week, that's a billable day a month. Plan is $99."
- "If it stops one dropped renewal a year, the math doesn't even need a calculator."

### Minute 14–15 — The close
- *"There are three ways in: self-serve today, Setup Sprint if you want it installed in two weeks, or Guided Operator if you want someone running the rhythm with you. Which feels right?"*
- **Don't ask "any questions?" Ask "which feels right?"**

### Demo principles
- Never show the dashboard before naming the feeling.
- Never list features. Always show outcomes.
- Always type live in the prospect's tenant. Never use the canned demo data.
- End with a binary close, not an open-ended one.

## 8. Pricing Close Strategy

The plans are set (Solo $29 / Team Operator $99 / Growth $249 / Guided Operator $1,500+ / Setup Sprint $2,500). The **close** is what converts.

### Default close (use 80% of the time)
- **Lead with Team Operator ($99).** Frame as "obvious choice for an agency your size."
- **Offer Setup Sprint** at the moment they say *"I love it but I don't have time to set it up."*
- **Annual = relief**, not discount. *"Want to make this one decision and not think about it for a year?"*

### Trade-up close (for hesitating Solos)
- *"You're at the edge of Solo. The next hire makes Team obvious. Start on Team and grow into it — same price as one missed invoice."*

### Trade-down close (for stuck Growth prospects)
- *"Start on Team. Move to Growth when seat count crosses 10. We won't let you overpay before you need to."*

### High-touch close (for Guided Operator)
- Reserved for warm intros, founder-led conversations, or post-Setup-Sprint upsells.
- *"You already know you won't run the rhythm consistently alone. Guided Operator is the version of WeekOne that doesn't depend on your discipline."*

### Friction removers (put on the pricing page)
- *Cancel anytime. We'd rather you stay because it works.*
- *14-day free trial, no card.*
- *Export your data in one click. Always.*
- *Less than the cost of one missed invoice.*

### What we will *not* do
- No free forever tier. (Teaches the wrong behavior — that the rhythm is optional.)
- No usage-based pricing. (Operators want a fixed line item, not a meter.)
- No "talk to sales" gate below Guided Operator. (Self-serve is the brand.)

## 10. Metrics Dashboard — First 100 Customers

Track weekly. Review every Monday (eat your own dog food).

### Acquisition
- Visitors → trial signup conversion (target **6–10%**)
- Outbound DMs sent → reply rate (target **15%+**)
- Demos booked → demos held (target **70%+**)
- Demos held → paid conversion (target **35–50%**)

### Activation
- % of trial users who run their **first Monday review** within 7 days (target **60%+**)
- Time-to-first-value: signup → first Monday completed (target **< 48 hours**)

### Revenue
- New MRR / week
- Plan mix (Solo / Team / Growth / Guided)
- Setup Sprint attach rate (target **20%** of new Team+ customers)
- Annual billing share (target **40%+** by day 90)

### Retention
- Week-4 retention (target **80%+**)
- Day-90 retention (target **70%+**)
- Mondays-completed/customer/month (the **leading indicator** — if this drops, churn is 60 days away)

### Loops
- Referral-sourced customers / month (target **15%** of new logos by day 60)
- NPS at day 30 (target **45+**)
- Public testimonials collected / month (target **3+**)

### Unit economics
- CAC blended (target **< $300** in beachhead)
- LTV / CAC (target **> 4** by month 6)
- Gross margin (target **> 80%** on SaaS, **> 40%** on Setup Sprint, **> 50%** on Guided Operator)

### The one number that matters
**Mondays-completed-per-active-customer-per-month.**
Everything else is downstream. If a customer runs four Mondays a month, they renew. If they run zero, they churn — even if they "love" the product. Make this the single number on the founder's wall.
