# WeekOne — Operating Finance & Execution Plan (COO/CFO Layer v1)

> **Mode:** Operate.
> **Wedge preserved:** Agencies → first 50 → expand. Founder-led. Mondays-completed north star. Calm brand tone.
> **The premise:** Numbers, triggers, and contingencies that make the engine *survivable* — not just buildable.

---

## 0. Operating Principles (read first)

1. **Cash > MRR** for the first 12 months. The Setup Sprint exists to fund the SaaS.
2. **Capacity is the real constraint, not demand.** A founder selling alone has a math ceiling. Hire when the ceiling cracks, not when it feels right.
3. **Instrument before you optimize.** A metric you can't measure isn't a metric — it's a wish.
4. **Plan the failure.** Every assumption gets a tripwire. Every tripwire gets a Plan B.
5. **Calm extends to the P&L.** No heroics. No 80-hour weeks. The brand and the operator are one system.

## 2. Founder Salary Timing

The founder's compensation is a leading indicator of company health. Pay the founder *with* the engine, not before it and not after burnout.

| Phase | Months | Founder draw | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bootstrap** | M1–M3 | $0 | Survive on personal runway |
| **Sustainability** | M4–M6 | $3,000/mo | Triggered by: 25+ paying customers AND $2K+ MRR |
| **Market rate (lean)** | M7–M9 | $6,000/mo | 100+ customers AND $10K+ MRR |
| **Founder market rate** | M10–M12 | $8,500/mo | 200+ customers AND $20K+ MRR |
| **Year 2** | M13+ | Reset annually based on ARR | Default: 1.5–2% of ARR, capped at $180K |

**Rules:**
- Salary never increases in the same month as a hire.
- Salary never increases when cash balance dips below 4 months of OpEx.
- Founder takes one full week off in M6 and M12. Not optional. The brand depends on it.

## 3. Hiring Trigger Thresholds

The first founder break-points are predictable. Hire **at the trigger**, not before.

| Role | Type | Trigger conditions (ALL must be true) | Target start |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ops VA** | Contractor 10 hr/wk | Founder spends >5 hr/wk on inbox triage / scheduling / data entry | M4 |
| **Content editor** | Contractor 5 hr/wk | Founder publishing slips below 4/wk for 2 consecutive weeks | M5 |
| **Customer Success Lead** | PT contractor → FT | 60+ active customers OR 15+ support events/wk OR founder demos drop below 8/wk | M7 PT, M10 FT |
| **Demo SDR / second seller** | FT | Demos held >12/wk for 3 consecutive weeks AND demo→paid >40% | M10–M12 |
| **First engineer (after founder)** | FT | Product backlog blocks revenue (3+ deals lost to missing capability in a month) | M12+ |
| **Designer** | Contractor → PT | Brand visual debt becomes a sales objection (rare, but track it) | As needed |
| **Founder COO/Operator** | FT | ARR >$1M AND founder context-switching > 5 areas/day for 4+ weeks | Year 2 |

### Founder capacity ceiling (do not exceed without hiring)

- **Demos:** 12/week sustainable. >15/week = quality drops, conversion falls.
- **Active customers under direct touch:** 60. Past 60, missed touchpoints become churn.
- **Support events:** 15/week. Past 15, response time slips past 24h.
- **Outbound DMs:** 250/week. Past 250, personalization collapses.
- **Content output:** 5 LinkedIn posts/week. Past 7, voice flattens.

> **The founder math:** ~50 hours/week, of which 5 on media, 8 on demos, 10 on customers, 10 on product, 5 on metrics/ops, 7 on inbox/calls, 5 buffer. When any bucket creeps past 1.5x its plan, hire that bucket.

## 4. Customer Support Model

Calm customers are won at the support desk before they're won at the demo.

### Three-tier model

| Tier | Channel | SLA | Owned by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Self-serve** | Help Center, in-app guides, 90-sec videos | Always-on | Founder writes, contractor maintains |
| **L1 — Async** | Email, in-app chat | 4 business hours | Ops VA (M4+), then CS Lead |
| **L2 — Live** | 15-min call (Calendly link) | Same business day | Founder (M1–M6), CS Lead (M7+) |
| **L3 — Founder rescue** | Personal email + Loom | Next business day | Founder always (no delegation, ever) |

### Routing rules
- Any churn-risk signal (Orange/Red from Revenue Engine §3) auto-escalates to L3.
- Any feature request → product backlog **and** templated reply: *"Logged. We ship the demo we needed yesterday — not the feature we wanted to want."*
- Any negative public mention → L3 within 4 business hours.

### Office hours (replaces 1:1 calls past M6)
- 30-minute group "Calm Monday Office Hours" every Wed at 11am ET.
- Drop-in, no agenda. Customers help customers. Founder facilitates.
- Doubles as live community building (Revenue Engine asked for one — this is the seed).

### Key support metrics
- L1 first-response time: <4 business hours (target <2 by M6)
- Resolution time: <24 business hours
- CSAT post-resolution: >4.5/5
- Self-serve deflection rate: >40% by M6, >60% by M12

## 5. Instrumentation Event Schema

Without events, the dashboard is fiction. Ship instrumentation in M1 alongside the product. Use a single event tracker (PostHog or Segment-class).

### Naming convention
- `snake_case` event names.
- `noun_verb` order. Past tense.
- All events carry: `user_id`, `org_id`, `plan`, `created_at`, `client_ts`.

### Core event schema (v1)

| Event | Trigger | Required props |
|---|---|---|
| `account_created` | Signup completed | `source`, `referral_code`, `plan_at_signup` |
| `trial_started` | First successful login | `time_to_first_login_sec` |
| `setup_call_booked` | Calendly booking | `lead_score`, `firmographic_size` |
| `setup_call_completed` | Founder marks complete | `notes_intake_score`, `pillars_configured` |
| `monday_started` | User opens Monday review screen on Mon AM | `local_time`, `pillars_loaded` |
| `monday_completed` | User saves the weekly review | `duration_sec`, `priorities_count`, `risks_count` |
| `pillar_focus_updated` | Focus pillar changed | `priorities_count`, `delta_from_last_week` |
| `pillar_money_updated` | Money pillar changed | `runway_weeks`, `ar_aging_dollars` |
| `pillar_risks_updated` | Risk added/closed | `risk_severity`, `risk_status_change` |
| `pillar_growth_updated` | Growth move logged | `move_type` |
| `teammate_invited` | Invite sent | `invited_count` |
| `teammate_viewed_monday` | Teammate opens latest Monday | `time_since_publish_sec` |
| `invoice_flagged` | Money pillar flags an invoice | `days_overdue`, `amount` |
| `referral_link_generated` | User generates a referral link | `surface` (in-product / email / page) |
| `referral_link_clicked` | A referee clicks the link | `referrer_id` |
| `referral_converted` | Referee becomes paying | `referrer_id`, `plan` |
| `trial_to_paid` | First successful charge | `plan`, `days_in_trial`, `setup_sprint_attached` |
| `plan_changed` | Up/downgrade | `from_plan`, `to_plan`, `mrr_delta` |
| `cancel_started` | Cancel flow opened | `current_plan`, `mondays_completed_30d` |
| `cancel_completed` | Cancel finalized | `reason_code`, `verbatim_reason` |
| `pause_taken` | Pause selected instead of cancel | `pause_duration_days` |
| `pause_resumed` | Reactivation after pause | `pause_duration_actual_days` |
| `nps_submitted` | NPS prompt answered | `score`, `verbatim` |
| `support_event_opened` | Support ticket | `tier`, `channel` |
| `support_event_resolved` | Support ticket closed | `resolution_time_min`, `csat` |
| `health_signal_changed` | Yellow/Orange/Red transition | `from_state`, `to_state`, `mondays_completed_30d` |

### Derived metrics (computed from events, not stored separately)

- **Mondays-completed/customer/month** = count of `monday_completed` per `org_id` per rolling 30 days.
- **Time-to-first-Monday** = `monday_completed.created_at` (first) − `account_created.created_at`.
- **Activation rate** = `monday_completed` within 7 days of `account_created` / `account_created`.
- **Trial→paid rate** = `trial_to_paid` / `trial_started`.
- **Setup Sprint attach** = `trial_to_paid` with `setup_sprint_attached=true` / `trial_to_paid`.
- **Net referral coefficient (k)** = `referral_converted` / unique `referral_link_generated` per cohort.

### Privacy & retention
- All events PII-minimal. No verbatim customer data in event payloads (use IDs).
- Verbatim NPS / cancel reasons stored separately, with consent.
- Event retention: 24 months hot, then aggregated.
- Any AI feature that touches customer data has an audit log event: `ai_inference_run`.

## 6. Pricing Experiment Calendar

Pricing is the highest-leverage variable. Test it on a calendar — never on a feeling.

| Phase | Test | Trigger | Win condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1–M3** | No tests. Hold prices. Build conversion baseline. | n/a | Baselines: trial→paid >30%, plan mix matches model |
| **M4** | A/B annual vs monthly default on pricing page | 25 customers reached | Annual share +10pts, no drop in signup rate |
| **M5** | Setup Sprint: $2,500 vs split into $1,500 / $3,500 tiers | 10 Sprints sold | Total Sprint revenue +20% with attach rate held |
| **M6** | Team Operator anchor: $99 vs $129, with $99 as "Solo+" | 50 customers, demo→paid >40% | New ARPU +20%, conversion drop <5pts |
| **M7** | Add **annual-only** trial path with 14-day money-back | NPS >40 | Annual share to 50%+ of new customers |
| **M8** | Guided Operator: $1,500 vs $2,000 | 5 Guided sold | Conversion held; ARPU +33% on top tier |
| **M9** | Referee reward: free month vs free Setup Sprint hour | Referral surfaces live 30+ days | Higher referee→paid conversion |
| **M10** | Introduce **multi-tenant for COOs** ($499/mo, manage 5 orgs) | 3+ Fractional COO partners signed | First 5 multi-tenant customers in M11 |
| **M11** | Solo Operator: $29 vs $39 | Solo cohort >40 customers | Conversion drop <8pts, ARPU +34% |
| **M12** | Annual price: full price vs 10% off vs 15% off | Annual share >40% | Highest annual cash collection |

### Rules of pricing experimentation
- One test live per surface at a time. Never overlap.
- Minimum sample: 200 unique visitors per arm OR 4 weeks, whichever first.
- Never run a test that could embarrass an existing customer (no public discounts a paying customer can't access).
- Grandfather every existing customer at their current price — forever, on their original plan.
- Document each test in a `pricing-tests/` folder with hypothesis, arms, sample, outcome, decision. The folder is the institutional memory.

## 7. Failure Scenario Playbooks

Each failure has a tripwire and a Plan B. No emotion required at the moment of impact.

### Failure A — Outbound underperforms (reply <8% by day 30)
- **Tripwire:** 2 weeks of <8% reply rate.
- **Plan B:** (1) Switch DM message variants. (2) Cut volume in half, increase personalization. (3) Pivot 50% of outbound capacity to founder content amplification.
- **Cost cap:** Outbound tooling halts at $200/mo until reply >12%.

### Failure B — Agencies churn fast (week-4 retention <60%)
- **Tripwire:** 5 logos churn before week 4 in any 30-day window.
- **Plan B:** Pause all paid acquisition. Run 10 customer interviews in 14 days. Identify one product or onboarding gap. Ship it. Resume only when retention is back >70% on a fresh cohort.
- **Communication:** publish a build-in-public post. Trust is rebuilt by transparency, not silence.

### Failure C — CAC creeps above $500
- **Tripwire:** 30-day blended CAC >$500.
- **Plan B:** Kill bottom 50% of ad creatives. Reroute spend to retargeting + referral incentive bumps. If CAC doesn't drop in 30 days, halt paid entirely and survive on organic + referral until CAC math returns.

### Failure D — Founder burnout signals
- **Tripwire:** Two consecutive Sundays where founder works >4 hours OR Mondays-completed by founder drops to <3 in a 30-day window OR media output drops to 0 for one week.
- **Plan B:** Mandatory 5-day disconnect. CS handoff to L1 contractor. One published "honest update" post upon return. Scale back demo target by 30% for following 4 weeks.

### Failure E — Product under-adopts (Mondays-completed/customer/month <2.5)
- **Tripwire:** Cohort 30-day metric <2.5 for 2 consecutive cohorts.
- **Plan B:** This is the existential signal. Stop adding features. Run 15 customer interviews. Identify the friction at first Monday. Rebuild onboarding within 30 days. Offer free Setup Sprints to anyone in the affected cohorts.

### Failure F — A bigger competitor copies the category
- **Tripwire:** Notion / Monday / Asana ships a "Weekly Operating System" feature.
- **Plan B:** Lean *harder* into category ownership. Publish "Why an OS isn't a feature." Increase content cadence. Double down on Guided Operator (services moat). Vertical landing pages become the new competitive surface.

### Failure G — A customer publicly criticizes us
- **Tripwire:** Negative public post by a verified customer.
- **Plan B:** Founder responds publicly within 4 business hours. Acknowledges, doesn't argue. Offers a 1:1 call. Whatever the outcome, the response itself becomes a brand asset.

### General rule
> Every tripwire is reviewed in the Friday founder review (§10). A tripped wire that goes 7 days unaddressed is a bigger failure than the original signal.

## 8. Churn Cohort Model

> The hidden killer of SaaS economics. Track it monthly from M1, not M6.

### Cohort definition
- One cohort = all customers who became paying in the same calendar month.
- Each cohort tracked monthly: M+1 retention, M+3, M+6, M+12.
- Reported as "% of original cohort still active and paying" — not net revenue retention (we'll add NRR at M9).

### Conservative model assumptions

| Cohort age | Retention assumption | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| M+1 | 90% | First Monday completed |
| M+3 | 80% | First quarter survived |
| M+6 | 70% | Renewal or annual conversion window |
| M+12 | 60% | Strong indication of product fit |

### What we expect to see by cohort (agencies wedge)

| Cohort | M+1 | M+3 | M+6 | M+12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 (founder-onboarded)** | 95% | 90% | 85% | 75% |
| **M3** | 90% | 80% | 75% | 65% |
| **M6** | 85% | 75% | 65% | 55% |
| **M9 (CS Lead joined)** | 92% | 85% | 75% | 70% |

> Why later cohorts dip: less founder touch. Why M9 recovers: CS Lead absorbs the role. **The cohort table is the proof that the CS hire paid for itself.**

### What we do with the cohort table
- Reviewed monthly in the Friday founder review.
- Any cohort below the conservative model row → root-cause analysis within 7 days.
- Cohort survival curves published quarterly (sanitized) on the public engineering blog. Trust signal.

### NRR addition (from M9)
- Track upgrade revenue and Setup Sprint repurchases.
- NRR target: **>105%** by M12 (modest expansion offsets churn).

## 9. Break-Even Roadmap

**Definition of break-even:** monthly cash in ≥ monthly OpEx, sustained for 3 consecutive months.

| Milestone | Conservative ETA | Bull case ETA | What unlocks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OpEx-positive** (cash in > OpEx in a single month) | M2 | M1 | Setup Sprints carry the early months |
| **3-month sustained break-even** | M4 | M3 | Customer base + Sprint cadence stabilize |
| **Founder draw at $3K** | M4 | M3 | Triggered by 25+ customers |
| **Pay first contractor without dipping reserve** | M5 | M4 | $7.5K OpEx covered by Sprint+MRR |
| **Pay first FT hire (CS Lead, $5K)** | M10 | M8 | $20K+ MRR sustained |
| **Founder market rate ($8.5K)** | M10 | M8 | 200+ customers |
| **$500K ARR run-rate** | M12 | M9 | Referral channel firing, vertical 2 contributing |
| **$1M ARR** | M18 | M14 | Clinics live; consultancies in pilot |

### Bridge to year 2
- **Cash on hand at end of Y1 (conservative):** ~$210K.
- **Y1 ARR (conservative):** ~$474K.
- **Decision point at month 12:** raise outside capital (only if vertical 3 needs faster CS hiring) **or** stay bootstrapped (default; the math works).
- **Default policy:** stay bootstrapped through $2M ARR. Raise only when capital unlocks a 5x outcome we can't reach organically.
