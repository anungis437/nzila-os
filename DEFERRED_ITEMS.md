# Deferred Items

Canonical backlog of intentionally deferred platform items.

## Purpose

Capture deferred work transparently without implying completion or production certification.

## Policy

- Every deferred item must include owner, rationale, risk class, and next review date.
- Deferred items cannot silently block critical governance controls.
- High-risk deferred items must be reflected in readiness and release gate outputs.
