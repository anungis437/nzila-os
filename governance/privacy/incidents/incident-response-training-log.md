## Minimum Evidence Per Exercise

1. Agenda and scenario brief.
2. Participant roster and attendance confirmation.
3. Timeline of decisions and communications.
4. Identified gaps and assigned remediation owners with due dates.
5. Post-exercise summary and sign-off.

## Completion Criteria

1. At least one completed exercise record exists for the current quarter.
2. Attendance includes engineering on-call, security lead, and incident commander role coverage.
3. Follow-up actions are tracked to closure in governance backlog.
