## What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.
Similar technologies include local/session storage, pixel tags, and
software development kits.

## Categories we use

| Category | Purpose | Examples (Nzila) | Consent required |
|----------|---------|------------------|------------------|
| **Strictly necessary** | Authentication, session, security, load balancing | `nzila_session`, anti-CSRF token | No (cannot be disabled) |
| **Functional** | Remember preferences (locale, UI choices) | `nzila_locale` | No (legitimate interest), opt-out via browser |
| **Analytics** | Aggregate usage to improve the service | TODO (e.g., GA4, Plausible) | **Yes** in EEA/UK/CA-Quebec |
| **Marketing / advertising** | Personalized advertising | None currently | **Yes** wherever required |

We do **not** sell information and we do not place advertising trackers.

## Consent management

Where consent is required, we present a banner on first visit allowing you
to accept all, reject non-essential, or customize. You can change your
choices any time via the "Cookies" link in the site footer.

For browsers signaling **Global Privacy Control (GPC)**, we treat the
signal as a request to opt out of analytics and any advertising cookies.
