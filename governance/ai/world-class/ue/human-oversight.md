# UE Human Oversight Model

Owner: UE Lead
Last updated: 2026-06-08
Scope: union-eyes-triage

## Oversight Roles

1. Steward reviewer validates AI triage recommendation before action.
2. Supervisor override authority for high-risk or ambiguous cases.

## Required Review Conditions

1. Confidence below review threshold.
2. Tier-1 policy trigger or safety signal.
3. Any recommendation affecting worker outcomes.

## Recording Requirements

1. Reviewer identity, decision, and rationale must be persisted.
2. Override actions require explicit reason and audit reference.
3. Rejections feed monthly calibration and retraining review.
